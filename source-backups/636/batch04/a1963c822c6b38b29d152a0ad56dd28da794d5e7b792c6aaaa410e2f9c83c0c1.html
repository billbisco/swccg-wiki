Commander Nemet
review by Jeff Hansen
[click here to view card]


The Imperial version of the Rebel Romas "Lock" Navander, Nemet is a sub-par pilot, providing a paltry 1 to the power and maneuver to anything he pilots. However, his gametext can come in handy, as the opponent may not react to or from the same location as Nemet. This helps to combat those frustrating Arconas. When playing against a Cloud City deck, the opponent could likely be using Lift Tubes and Lift Tube Escapes. Should Nemet happen to be deployed to Cloud City (preferably right before battling), he will shut down those powerful reacts at a site. This guy is protection.
