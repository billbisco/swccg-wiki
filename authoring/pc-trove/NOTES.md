# swccgpc.com trove — research notes for wiki.swccg.com

Compiled 30 Oct 2026 from Wayback captures of swccgpc.com / www.swccgpc.com (2002–2010 and the 2019 deckbuilder), cards.swccgpc.com and the www.swccgpc.com/forums phpBB board. 631 site pages and ~430 forum pages were fetched; raw copies are in `raw/` (site, forum, forum3, forum_g1). Every finding cites the exact capture. "wiki:" compares with wiki.swccg.com as read on 30 Oct 2026.

Already cited by the wiki (exturlusage, 13 links): `www.swccgpc.com/`, `/interior.htm` (2003/2004 Worlds), `/Decks/dpcchicago/desaiDS.pdf`, `/Resources/DefensiveShields.pdf`, `/Resources/Official%20SWCCG%20Tournament%20Guide.pdf`, `/Resources/tournaments/OfficialSWCCGTournamentGuide.pdf`, `/forums/viewtopic.php?t=2936`; plus Wayback cites on the advocates pages, [[Players Committee Advanced Rulebook 1.7.1]] and [[Virtual Sets (2002-2009)]]. No `forum.swccgpc.com` captures exist in the CDX.

## Answers to the open questions

- **2001 Worlds 3rd/4th (Hayes Hunter vs Brian Hunter):** not found. The site starts in Oct 2002; a 2004 forum "history" thread lists only champions (96 Asselin … 01 FreedomCon Winkelhaus).
- **2015 Worlds 3rd, 2016 places 3–8, 2024 Day 1 standings, 2012 places 3–8, 2013 eighth Day 3 player, 2013 pool (8.3 vs 9.1 Preview):** not on swccgpc.com. By 2010 its news permalinks pointed to starwarsccg.org, and in 2019 the domain was a third-party deckbuilder ("SW:CCG DB"). Search starwarsccg.org captures instead.
- **2007 Worlds Brian Fred vs Mike Honsho seat:** not found. The only 2007 trace is a "Worlds Weekend 2007" topic label in the Oct 2007 Stories Archive; the articles were not archived.
- **2009 Gemme vs "Mike Gerome":** not found. The only 2009 Worlds-related pages are the 2K event ones; Mike Gemme made Day 2 there. 2009 Worlds pages are not on this domain.
- **Field sizes 2002–2005:**
  - 2002 found: Day 1A 45, Day 1B 54, Day 2 52 (Bruun's year-end article says 54); see 2002 section.
  - 2003 Worlds: not found. DPC Chicago 2003 had 25.
  - 2004 Worlds: field size not found, but the schedule says the top 12 advanced to Day 3.
  - 2005 Worlds: not found.
- **Exact 2005 Worlds days:** partial answer. The PC qualifier table ends "GenCon - SWCCG Worlds | 08/20/2005" (20 Aug 2005, Saturday of Gen Con Indy 2005). The day-by-day schedule was not archived.
- **Venues and hotels:**
  - 2003: Dragon*Con at the Hyatt Regency Atlanta (265 Peachtree St NE) and Atlanta Marriott Marquis.
  - 2004: Indiana Convention Center, Gen Con, "in the DecipherCon area".
  - 2008: Twin City Comics, 3954 Central Ave NE, Columbia Heights MN.
  - 2010: Philadelphia Airport Hilton.
  - Not found here: 2005 hall, 2006 (wiki has PasTimes), 2007, and 2011–2020.
- **Hall of Fame enshrinement of Matt Sokol and Chris Gogolen (27 Apr 2019):** not found. swccgpc.com/decklists/halloffame (Jul 2019) is the third-party deckbuilder's decklist "Hall of Fame" listing (empty in the capture), not the PC Hall of Fame.
- **Virtual Block 6.1–9.2 dates / whether 9.2 was legal:** only one date found. Virtual Block 6.3 Mercenaries was released Fri 12 Mar 2010 (the wiki gives "official text 15 March 2010"). Nothing on 6.1, 6.2 or 7.x–9.x.
