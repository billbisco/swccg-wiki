# swccgpc.com trove (2026-10-30)

Research notes from the Wayback captures of the old Players Committee site (swccgpc.com, 2002–2010).

- `NOTES.md`: 163 findings. Open hub questions are answered at the top. Each finding has a quote, the exact Wayback capture link, and a verdict against the wiki (has / missing / contradicts).
- `inventory.tsv`: 10,341 archived HTML pages, with timestamp, URL and section.
- `raw.tgz`: saved copies of the 631 site pages and about 430 forum pages that were fetched.

Not yet applied to the wiki. Photos are handled separately (see `photos/` once added).
