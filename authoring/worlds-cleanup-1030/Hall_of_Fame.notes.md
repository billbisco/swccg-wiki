# Hall of Fame: draft notes

Draft: Hall_of_Fame.wiki. New page, nothing on the wiki edited.

## Sources checked
- PC Awards page, current snapshot https://swccg.com/snapshots/pc/community-awards.html (captured 12 Oct 2026). Lists 16 inductees with enshrinement dates. Brian Fred (2025) is not on it yet.
- Community card list https://res.starwarsccg.org/cardlists/Communitycard.html: the "Hall Of Famer" table lists all 17 with induction years, Fred 2025 included.
- Worlds wrap-ups (swccg.com snapshots) for 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025 confirm the inductions, dates and presenters. The 2021 wrap says the induction was made "by the Advocate Council and Tournament Committee", which is the only sourced statement of how inductees are chosen.

## Brief vs. sources: additions and corrections
- Three inductees were missing from the brief's list:
  - Matt Sokol and Chris Gogolen, enshrined 27 Apr 2019.
  - Chris Schoenthal, enshrined 21 Jan 2023 at the 2023 San Diego Super Open (that wiki page cites the PC article "...Schoenthal inducted to PC Hall of Fame").
- Every other date matched the sources: 2013-08-10, 2015-08-08, 2017-08-12, 2019-09-20, 2021-10-09, 2023-08-12, 2024-09-21 (both Jaap and Hunter).
- Fred's date of 4 Oct 2025 comes from the wrap. It gives the weekend as 3–5 Oct and the induction as "Day 1 Saturday morning".

## Unresolved
- **Event for the 27 Apr 2019 enshrinement (Sokol, Gogolen):** no source names it. The table says "event not stated". The [[2019 Match Play Championship]] (April 2019) is a plausible venue but is unconfirmed. Its PC snapshot is not at swccg.com/snapshots, and web.archive.org is blocked from this session.
- **Photos:** Jonny Chu, Matt Sokol, Joe Olson and Hayes Hunter have no individual Hall of Fame photo file on the wiki, so their community card is shown instead. Two image sources could fill this:
  - the PC's JoeOlsonHoF.jpg at res.starwarsccg.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2022/;
  - the 2013 class photo, which is embedded in the awards snapshot.
- **Category:Players Committee** does not exist, so the page uses [[Category:Tournaments]].
- **[[Tom Kelly]]** in the Player of the Year list (2016) is assumed to be the 2018 Worlds runner-up page. Worth a glance.
- **Lifetime Achievement and Euro Emperor** facts come from the Lingrell and Menzel Hall of Fame write-ups on the awards page.
