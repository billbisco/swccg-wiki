## 2. What the PC called the format at the time

- In 2009 the PC homepage said: "our **standard playing format**, which is **open deck construction** where all cards in the card pool are available for use" (Wayback, 18 Sep 2009).
- Event listings used "Format: Constructed", "Open Constructed Swiss" and "Standard Contstructed" (sic) between 2009 and 2010.
- The 2010 Block PDFs describe optional "Block tournaments" (choose some virtual blocks plus Decipher sets) as an alternative format. A "Classic VBlock 1 only" format also existed. These are side formats; none of the listed events used them.
- On 25 July 2014 the reset was titled "**Reset of Standard Format**". It said: "The new Standard tournament format will consist of all post-reset V Cards … we also will create a new Legacy format".
- So at the time the pool was simply the **Standard** (constructed) format. "Legacy" is a 2014 name applied after the fact. The wiki's "Legacy Open" is this wiki's own name for that 2014 Legacy format.

## Uncertain points

1. **The VB9.2 legal date and whether it was used at Worlds/EC 2014.** The PDF says 14 Jul 2014. The reset post of 25 Jul says 9.2 "will not be released in its current form" and promises a ~12-card Worlds mini set, and 9.2 has 11 cards. The PC slips page during Worlds shows no 9.2 PDF.
2. **Legal dates for 6.1, 7.1, 7.3, 7.4, 8.1, 8.2, 8.3 and the 9.1 Preview.** No legal date was found for these. The table gives release dates, and the deck-usage checks give upper bounds. The 6.1–7.2 practice was 14 days after release. In June 2012 the planned delay was 1 week, and 9.1 was legal immediately.
3. **Missing exact dates.** 2012 Canadian Nationals (≤7 Aug), 2012 EC (Leicester, ≤17 Aug), 2012 SoCal GP (20 Sep–5 Dec) and 2010 Yavin 4 (≤26 May) have no exact date. 2012 Yavin 4 is 30 Jun 2012 on its hub page but only "2012" on the list page.
4. **Transcription anomalies.** Some deck pages link cards from later subsets than the event date allows. Examples are 2010 MPC → Captain Verrack (V) and General Airen Cracken (VB9), and 2012 EC → Dexter Jettster (Virtual Block 9). These are probably links to the wrong card page, and they were not used as evidence.
5. **2010 Dagobah Regionals (25–26 Jul).** The 26 July 2010 CBT changes took effect on day 2. The event is assigned to the 7.1 page anyway, with a note.
6. The forum (forum.starwarsccg.org) search was rate-limited and timed out through FlareSolverr. No forum threads were used.
