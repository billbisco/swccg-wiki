The 2020 World Championship was the 25th Star Wars CCG World Championship. Because of the COVID-19 pandemic the Players Committee moved it online to GEMP, where it ran over four days in December 2020 and hosted 335 games.[2] Day 1 (5–6 December) was a six-game event with 97 players in four heats; the 32 players who went 4–2 or better played four more games on Day 2 (12 December); and the top eight played three rounds of match play on Day 3 (13 December).[1][2] Joe Olson defeated Quirin Fürgut in the Final Confrontation[2][3] with Hunt Down And Destroy The Jedi (V) and He Is The Chosen One.[1]

Top 8 bracket[edit]

Day 3 was three rounds of match play.[2] Only the final (Olson def. Fürgut) was reported; the quarterfinal and semifinal pairings and the game scores were not published. A replay of the Final Confrontation was posted on YouTube.[2]
