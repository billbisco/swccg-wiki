The 2019 World Championship was the 24th Star Wars CCG World Championship and the Players Committee's World Championship for 2019, held in Bochum, Germany, from 20 to 22 September 2019.[1][2] Sixty-nine constructed pairs were published for Day 2.[1] Bastian Winkelhaus defeated Bryan Mischke in the Final Confrontation,[1][2] playing Ralltiir Operations (V) and Old Allies on Day 3.[1] He became the first player to win the World Championship in consecutive years and the first three-time champion (2001, 2018, 2019), and was enshrined in the Players Committee Hall of Fame on 20 September 2019, Day 1 of the event.[2]
