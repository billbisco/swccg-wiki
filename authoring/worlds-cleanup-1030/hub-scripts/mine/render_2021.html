The 2021 World Championship was the 26th Star Wars CCG World Championship, held by the Players Committee at the Hyatt Regency Crystal City in Arlington, Virginia, over the weekend of 8–10 October 2021, with four sanctioned events across three days.[1] (The hub previously gave 7–10 October; the wrap-up gives 8–10 October.) Sixty players entered the main event.[1] Joe Olson defended his 2020 title by defeating Matt Scott in the Final Confrontation, with Agents Of Black Sun and Qui-Gon Communing;[1][3] it was his fourth straight major win. He became the sixth player to win two World Championships and the second, after Bastian Winkelhaus, to win in consecutive years.[1] Olson was inducted into the Players Committee Hall of Fame on 9 October 2021, just before Game 1 of the main event.[1][2]

Top 8 bracket[edit]

Every player at 6–2 or better made the cut. Jared Napolitano finished Day 1 first, followed by the other 7–1 players Tom Haid and Matt Scott; Greg Shaw, Barry Alperstein, Robbie Hendon, Casey Anis and Joe Olson (8th on Day 1) completed the Top 8.[1]

  • Quarterfinals: Olson def. Shaw; Scott def. Hendon.[1]
  • Semifinals: Olson def. Napolitano; Scott def. Alperstein.[1]
  • Final: Olson def. Scott.[1]

The other two quarterfinal results and all game scores were not published.

Side events[edit]

  • Team tournament (Friday evening, 18 teams, glass mug trophies): won by "Stop Batmouse Cold", Dan Tartaglione and Brad Reinhold. "Potential Allies" (Charlie Arlandson and Amar Banger) finished second.[1]
  • Consolation (Sunday, 28 players): won by Anthony Howard at 5–1; Ryan Jellison, Chris Gogolen and Sam Tashima finished 2nd–4th.[1]
  • Hall of Fame: Joe Olson, enshrined 9 October 2021, introduced by Phil Aasen.[1][2]
  • Throwback Event (Friday 8 October, six-game Swiss, Premiere–Reflections II cards only): Steve Skilton went 6–0 to win, ahead of Jared Napolitano, Matt Carulli and Kevin Elia. The wrap-up gives a field of 15; the published results list 16 pairs.[1][4]
