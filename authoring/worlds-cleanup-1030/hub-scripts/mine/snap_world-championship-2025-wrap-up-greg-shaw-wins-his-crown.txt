World Championship 2025 Wrap-Up: Greg Shaw Wins his Crown
October 23, 2025 
The 30th annual Star Wars CCG World Championship took place the weekend of October 3-5 at the Hampton Inn & Suites Hotel in SeaTac, WA, with four different sanctioned Players Committee Events spanning three days. The Main Event saw hall-of-famer and New Allies team member Greg Shaw secure his long-awaited first World Championship title, cementing his well-deserved place among the other great SWCCG World Champions, defeating Joe Olson in the final confrontation. Greg has never been a stranger to the top tables at major events, including two Worlds 2nd place finishes back to back in 2002 & 2003, so this inevitable win to solidify his place as a top player was very meaningful to Greg and his friends and team members in the community!
2025 World Champion Greg Shaw
The Main Event (“Day 1”) included an incredible group of 55 players and had eight games of Swiss play, with a cut to the Top 8. The other top 8 players for Day 2 included: Brian Fred, Matthew Harrison-Trainor, Pat Johnson, Hayes Hunter, Sam Tashima, and Benji Chu. On Day 2, Greg defeated Pat and Brian (who beat Benji) in the quarterfinals and semifinals, respectively, while Joe defeated Matthew and Hayes (who beat Sam) en route to the final confrontation.
Popular decks for the Main Event included a lot of First Order Reigns and a bit of Shadow Collective for Dark, and Watch Your Step, Saga (mostly Luke or Rey), and a good mix of other decks for Light. As usual, the Streaming Team, led by Dan Tartaglione, provided live commentary coverage of the event, with Garrett Larson assisting with commentary duties throughout the weekend. All decklists are available on the PC website and also in GEMP-importable format, and all streams have been cut and posted to the PC’s YouTube Channel in their own playlist.
On Friday evening, the traditional Team Tournament took place, with 11 teams battling it out for the coveted Glass Mug Trophies. In the end, Team “Scott Norwood” (Brian Fred and Jonny Chu) were victorious, with Mike Turner and Nate Davis finishing in second. Additionally on Friday, a Cube event was held with 18 participants, and saw Steve Harpster take first place and Anthony Howard coming in second place.
During the pre-event announcements on Day 1 Saturday morning, long-standing veteran player and pillar of the community Brian Fred was formally inducted in the SWCCG PC Hall of Fame, joining the ranks of the other stand-out players and volunteers that have helped shape and represent our game and organization throughout the years. Brian’s award was presented by his good friend and New Allies teammate Mike Kessling.
New Hall of Fame Inductee Brian Fred
Finally, the Consolation Event took place on Sunday while the Top 8 match-play for the Main Event played out in parallel. Brian Mischke finished in first place with a stellar 6-0 record in a field of 36 total players and won the main cash prize, with Jonny Chu finishing in second place.
The Main Event and all of its moving parts were run smoothly as ever by Tournament Advocate Chris Schoenthal. Dozens of player-volunteers made the weekend a success from every angle, and they cannot be thanked enough for their selfless contributions – contributions of the like that have been made for over 20 years now to keep our out-of-print card game going strong.
Keep an eye out for tournament reports to read up on players’ experiences from the weekend, which included not only competitive and slightly-less-competitive Star Wars CCG, but of course also all the usual components of a wonderful atmosphere amongst friends amidst Seattle ventures, late night gaming and dinners out.
The Star Wars CCG tournament committee is finalizing the major event schedule for 2026 as we look to keep momentum going through the winter & spring, and on into the rest of the 2026 organized play calendar! Stay tuned!
