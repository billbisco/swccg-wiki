The 2026 World Championship was the 31st Star Wars CCG World Championship, held by the Players Committee at the Hyatt Regency Bloomington by Mall of America in Bloomington, Minnesota, from 23 to 25 October 2026; the main event was played on 24–25 October.[1][2] Fifty-eight players entered, and all 58 played all eight Swiss games.[3][4] Brian Fred defeated Justin Desai in the Final Confrontation with ISB Operations and Diplomatic Mission To Alderaan,[5][3][6] a year after his Hall of Fame induction at the 2025 World Championship.[7] Fred also won the Friday team tournament with Phil Aasen.[8]

Top 8 bracket[edit]

The main event was eight Swiss games on Saturday 24 October (strength-of-schedule tiebreak), then a Top 8 of match play on Sunday with 1:15 games. Quarterfinal pairings were drawn at random, top four against bottom four; the higher seed chose which side to play first, and the bracket was re-seeded to the Day 1 standings after the quarterfinals.[1]

Day 1 seeds: 1 Greg Shaw 8–0; 2 Drew Lichtenstein 7–1; 3 Desai 6–2; 4 Joe Horbey 6–2; 5 Joe Olson 6–2; 6 Dennis Reinhardt 6–2; 7 Fred 6–2; 8 Bryan Mischke 6–2.[3] Mischke and Chris Wirfs (9th, 6–2) played a game for the eighth seat; its result was not posted, but Mischke made the Top 8 and Wirfs is listed 9th.[3][5]

The organizer posted one game of each match; the second games and the match totals were not posted. Margins are differentials; lost piles in brackets.[3]

  • Quarterfinals: Desai (Dark) def. Reinhardt by 20 (19–44) in the posted game, and Desai advanced. Lichtenstein (Light) def. Olson by 19 (14–31), and Lichtenstein advanced. Shaw (Dark) def. Mischke by 3 (34–33), and Shaw advanced. Horbey (Dark) def. Fred by 13 (27–35), but Fred advanced.[3]
  • Semifinals (re-seeded): Desai (Dark) def. Lichtenstein by 20 (21–36), and Desai advanced. Shaw (Light) def. Fred by 9 (18–28), but Fred advanced; his game 2 against Shaw is in the YouTube playlist.[3][9]
  • Final: Fred (Light) def. Desai by 5 (28–46) in game 1. Game 2 was not posted. Fred won the title.[3][6]

The Top 8 was streamed live on twitch.tv/swccgpc and twitch.tv/tashi_station_,[3] with Dan Tartaglione and Garrett Larson as the stream team.[10] Eleven games from the weekend were posted to a YouTube playlist; the final is not among them.[9]

Side events[edit]

  • Team tournament (Friday 23 October, teams of two, one player on each side, three games, cumulative differential; 11 teams): won by The Unholy Alliance, Phil Aasen and Brian Fred, with 12 points (+92) without dropping a game, for the mini obelisk trophies. Hong Kong Danger Duo were second (8, +39) and Team S&C third (8, +9).[1][8]
  • Consolation (Sunday, six Swiss games, about 27 players): won by Pat Johnson, undefeated, ahead of Carson Stockman, Sam Tashima and AJ Hatoum.[11]
  • Other events: a Friday Cube event ("Throdo's Cube Spectacular!") and Retro (Premiere–Death Star II) events were also scheduled; their results were not posted on the forum.[1][12]
  • Holotheatre: Brian Fred joined deadbody and DTartagOne for a special Holotheatre episode on twitch.tv/swccgpc on Monday 2 November 2026.[13]
