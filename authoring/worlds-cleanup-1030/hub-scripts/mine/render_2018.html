The 2018 World Championship was the 23rd Star Wars CCG World Championship and the Players Committee's World Championship for 2018, held in Morristown, New Jersey, from 4 to 7 October 2018.[1][2] Sixty-nine constructed pairs were published for Day 2.[1] Bastian Winkelhaus defeated Tom Kelly to win the title,[2] playing Agents Of Black Sun and Watch Your Step on Day 3.[1] It was Winkelhaus's second World Championship, after 2001.[2]
