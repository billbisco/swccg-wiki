The 2022 World Championship was the 27th Star Wars CCG World Championship, held by the Players Committee in Atlanta, Georgia, over the weekend of 14–16 October 2022, with four sanctioned events across three days.[1] (The hub previously gave 13–16 October.) The main event had 51 players.[1] Justin Desai defeated Matthew Harrison-Trainor in the Final Confrontation with Moment Of Triumph (V) and The Galaxy May Need A Legend,[1][3] winning his third World Championship (after 2013 and 2015) and joining Bastian Winkelhaus as the only three-time champions.[1]

Top 8 bracket[edit]

Joe Olson was the only player at 7–1 after Day 1. Nine players finished 6–2, in order: Desai, Ryan Jellison, Greg Shaw, Kyle Krueger, Hayes Hunter, Harrison-Trainor, Jeremy DiPaolo, Chris Wirfs and Sam Tashima. In two play-in games DiPaolo beat Wirfs and Harrison-Trainor beat Tashima.[1]

  • Quarterfinals: Desai def. Krueger; Harrison-Trainor def. Shaw.[1]
  • Semifinals: Desai def. Hunter; Harrison-Trainor def. Olson.[1]
  • Final: Desai def. Harrison-Trainor.[1]

The other two quarterfinal results and all game scores were not published.

Side events[edit]

  • Team tournament (Friday evening, 12 teams, glass mug trophies): won by Justin Desai and Steve Baroni ("Kashyyyk Train of Dominance"); Matthew Harrison-Trainor and Matt Lutz ("Drs. Matt") were second. Dan Tartaglione directed the event.[1]
  • Consolation (Sunday): won by Jonny Chu at 5–1; Ryan Sersen was second. Bill Kafer directed the event.[1]
  • Throdo's Cube Spectacular (Friday, draft and four games, 28 players): won by Phil Aasen ahead of teammate Cal Aldred; organized by Jonathan Murray.[1]
  • Main-event teams: players were assigned to teams for an "event within the event" based on main event performance.[1]
