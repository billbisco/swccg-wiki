The 2024 World Championship was the 29th Star Wars CCG World Championship, held by the Players Committee at the Zu den Vier Winden bar in Bochum, Germany, over the weekend of 19–22 September 2024.[1][3] It was the third World Championship held in Europe.[2] The main event on Saturday had 53 players.[1] Joe Olson defeated Emil Wallin in the Final Confrontation with Endor Operations and He Is The Chosen One,[1][3] winning his third World Championship and joining Bastian Winkelhaus and Justin Desai as three-time champions.[1] Kevin Jaap and Hayes Hunter were inducted into the Players Committee Hall of Fame during Day 1.[1][2]

Top 8 bracket[edit]

After eight Swiss games and four play-in games, Timo Dusel, Patrik Csapi and Wallin were 7–1; Eric Hunter, Olson, Hayes Hunter and Dan Tartaglione finished 6–2, in that order; and Jonas Hagen Nørregaard, the best 5–3 player, took the last place in the cut.[1]

  • Quarterfinals: Olson def. Eric Hunter; Hayes Hunter def. Dusel; Wallin def. Tartaglione; Nørregaard def. Csapi.[1]
  • Semifinals: Olson def. Hayes Hunter; Wallin def. Nørregaard.[1]
  • Final: Olson def. Wallin.[1]

Game scores were not published. The event was not streamed live; recorded games with commentary were posted to the Players Committee YouTube channel.[1]

Side events[edit]

  • Team tournament (Friday evening, glass mug trophies): won by Emil Wallin and Brian Fred.[1]
  • Consolation (Sunday, four games, 14 players): won by AJ Hatoum at 4–0; Pär Birgander was second.[1]
  • Hall of Fame: Kevin Jaap (presented by Chris Menzel before the main event) and Hayes Hunter (presented by his brother Eric Hunter at a mid-day break), both enshrined 21 September 2024.[1][2]
  • Tamás Papp was tournament director; Kevin Jaap handled prize support.[1]
