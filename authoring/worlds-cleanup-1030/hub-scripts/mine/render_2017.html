The 2017 World Championship was the 22nd Star Wars CCG World Championship and the Players Committee's World Championship for 2017, held in Bloomington, Minnesota, from 10 to 13 August 2017.[1][2] The Players Committee awards page gives the site as Minneapolis, MN; Bloomington is a suburb of Minneapolis.[3][4] Fifty-nine players' constructed pairs were published for Day 2 (55 finishers and four drops).[2] Phil Aasen won the title, defeating Jonny Chu in the final,[3] with Endor Operations and Combat Preparedness (V) on Day 3.[2] Mark Walseth was enshrined in the Players Committee Hall of Fame on 12 August 2017, during the event weekend.[3]
