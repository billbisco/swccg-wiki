The 2025 World Championship was the 30th Star Wars CCG World Championship, held by the Players Committee at the Hampton Inn & Suites in SeaTac, Washington, over the weekend of 3–5 October 2025, with four sanctioned events across three days; the main event was played on 4–5 October.[1][2] The main event had 55 players.[1] Hall of Famer Greg Shaw defeated Joe Olson in the Final Confrontation with Shadow Collective and The Force Is Strong In My Family (Luke Saga),[1][2] winning his first World Championship after finishing runner-up in 2002 and 2003.[1] Brian Fred was inducted into the Players Committee Hall of Fame on Saturday morning.[1]

Top 8 bracket[edit]

Day 1 was eight games of Swiss play with a cut to the Top 8.[1]

  • Quarterfinals: Shaw def. Pat Johnson; Brian Fred def. Benji Chu; Olson def. Matthew Harrison-Trainor; Hayes Hunter def. Sam Tashima.[1]
  • Semifinals: Shaw def. Fred; Olson def. Hunter.[1]
  • Final: Shaw def. Olson.[1]

Game scores were not published. The games were streamed with commentary by Dan Tartaglione and Garrett Larson and posted to a YouTube playlist.[1]

Side events[edit]

  • Team tournament (Friday evening, 11 teams, glass mug trophies): won by team "Scott Norwood", Brian Fred and Jonny Chu; Mike Turner and Nate Davis were second.[1]
  • Cube (Friday, 18 players): won by Steve Harpster; Anthony Howard was second.[1]
  • Consolation (Sunday, 36 players): won at 6–0 by Bryan Mischke (spelled "Brian Mischke" in the wrap-up); Jonny Chu was second.[1]
  • Hall of Fame: Brian Fred, inducted on Saturday morning, presented by his New Allies teammate Mike Kessling.[1]
