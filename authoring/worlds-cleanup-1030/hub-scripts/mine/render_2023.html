The 2023 World Championship was the 28th Star Wars CCG World Championship, held by the Players Committee at the Hyatt Regency in Morristown, New Jersey, over the weekend of 10–13 August 2023, with five sanctioned events across three days.[1] The PC decklist page gives 11–13 August.[2] The main event had 69 players.[1] Hayes Hunter won his first World Championship, defeating Casey Anis in the Final Confrontation with Tatooine: Desert Landing Site SSA (V) and Obi-Wan Communing.[1][2] Greg Shaw was inducted into the Players Committee Hall of Fame on Saturday morning, 12 August 2023.[1][3]

Top 8 bracket[edit]

Bryan Mischke and Anthony Howard finished Day 1 at 7–1. Ten players finished 6–2, in order: Anis, Hunter, Conor Britain, Joe Olson, Ryan Jellison, Bill Kafer, Brian Fred, Wayne Cullen, Scott Lingrell and Jonny Chu. In four play-in games Chu beat Britain, Olson beat Lingrell, Jellison beat Cullen and Fred beat Kafer.[1]

  • Quarterfinals: Hunter def. Olson; Jellison def. Mischke; Anis def. Chu; Fred def. Howard.[1]
  • Semifinals: Hunter def. Jellison; Anis def. Fred.[1]
  • Final: Hunter def. Anis.[1]

Game scores were not published. The PC decklist page spells the 5th-place finisher "Brian Mischke".[2]

Side events[edit]

  • Team tournament (Friday evening, 16 teams, glass mug trophies): won by Justin Desai and Jonny Chu ("Kashyyyk Train of Dominance"); Scott Lingrell and Bill Bacheler were second.[1]
  • Retro event (Friday, Premiere–Death Star II, 8 players): won by Garrett Larson (Hidden Base mains and Ralltiir Operations); Joe Horbey was second (TRM and ISB scum).[1]
  • Consolation (Sunday, 36 players): won by Cory Lauer at 6–0; Greg Shaw was second and took the deckbox prize for the best Jawa-format deck.[1]
  • Hall of Fame: Greg Shaw, enshrined 12 August 2023, presented by Bill Kafer, with his parents in attendance.[1][3]
