2024 Worlds Wrap-Up – Joe Olson Earns Third Championship Win
October 11, 2024 
The 29th annual Star Wars CCG World Championship took place the weekend of September 19-22 at the famous Zu Den Vier Winden bar in Bochum, Germany, with several different sanctioned PC Events spanning three days. The Main Event saw Joe Olson secure his THIRD World Championship title, defeating Swedish legend Emil Wallin in the final confrontation to join the World Championships 3-wins club alongside Bastian Winkelhaus and Justin Desai. This also marks Joe’s 3rd championship title in the past 5 years, an amazing accomplishment solidifying his reputation as one of the best players in the game right now.
Winner Joe Olson & Runner-up Emil Wallin
The Main Event on Saturday (“Day 1”) included an incredible group of 53 players and had eight games of Swiss play, leading into four play-in games to finalize the Top 8 cut. Timo Dusel, Patrik Csapi, and Emil Wallin all finished Day 1 with a record of 7-1. Four total players finished 6-2; in order: Eric Hunter, Joe Olson, Hayes Hunter, Dan Tartaglione, and then the top 5-3 record player, Jonas Hagen Norregaard, rounded out the Top 8 cut. On Sunday (“Day 2”), Joe defeated Eric and Hayes (who beat Timo) in the quarterfinals and semifinals, respectively, while Emil defeated Dan and Jonas (who beat Patrick) en route to the final confrontation.
Popular decks for the Main Event included a RotS (mostly Maul but some Vader), Hunt Down, and MKOS for Dark, and then a good amount of HITCO, Zero Hour, QMC, and WHAP decks for Light. Streaming coverage was not able to be performed live for the event, but the Streaming Team, led by Dan Tartaglione, is providing recorded commentary on the video coverage taken at the event, and videos are being uploaded to the PC’s YouTube Channel in their own playlist. All decklists are available on the PC website and also in GEMP-importable format.
Top 8 Competitors – Emil, Dan, Joe, Jonas, Patrik, Timo, Eric, Hayes (L to R)
During the pre-event announcements on Day 1 Saturday morning, long-standing volunteer, veteran player and pillar of the community Kevin Jaap was formally inducted in the SWCCG PC Hall of Fame, joining the ranks of the other stand-out players and volunteers that have helped shape and represent our game and organization throughout the years. Kevin’s award was presented by his good friend the Euro Emperor Chris Menzel, whose presentation is available to watch. Additionally, during a mid-day break, another veteran player and invaluable PC volunteer Hayes Hunter, was also inducted into the SWCCG PC Hall of Fame for his stellar player record as well as his contributions to the community over many years. Hayes’ award was presented by his brother Eric Hunter, and that presentation is also available to watch.
On Friday evening, the traditional Team Tournament took place, with many teams duking it out late into the night for the coveted Glass Mug Trophies, with Swedish Emil Wallin and his American counterpart Brian Fred coming out victorious in the end. Additionally, a 4-game Consolation Event took place on Sunday while the Top 8 match-play for the Main Event played out in parallel. AJ Hatoum finished in first place with a perfect 4-0 record in a field of 14 total players and won the main cash prize, with Par Birgander finishing in second place.
The Main Event and all of its moving parts were run smoothly by Tournament Director Tamas Papp, who did an amazing job running the event, along with support from swag czar Kevin Jaap, to guarantee everyone at the event walked away with a myriad of cool slips, cards, and other goodies. Dozens of player-volunteers made the weekend a success from every angle, and they cannot be thanked enough for their selfless contributions – contributions of the like that have been made for over 20 years now to keep our out-of-print card game going strong.
Amazing swag from Kevin Jaap, Tamas running the show
Numerous tournament reports have captured players’ experiences from the weekend, which included not only competitive and slightly-less-competitive Star Wars CCG, but of course also all the usual components of a wonderful atmosphere amongst friends in and around the Four Winds venue, including late night gaming and dinners out.
Tournament Players in front of Zu Den Vier Winden
The Star Wars CCG community now turns its eyes towards the next in-person major events to wrap up the year:
–2024 Endor Super Weekend (Seattle, WA – October 26/27)
–2024 Ohio “Mega Worlds” Super Weekend (Columbus, OH – November 2/3)
–2024 Online Championship Series (OCS) Playoffs (Oct-Nov – online, qualifying)
The Game will continue its momentum through the fall & winter, and on into the 2025 organized play schedule! Stay tuned for more news on the 2025 event calendar!
