# PC deck-post audit (2026-11-04)
Inventory: Wayback CDX of www.starwarsccg.org/* (HTML, collapsed by URL) -> 4,257 per-deck post slugs (2014-2026).
Compared with every starwarsccg.org / swccg.com/snapshots/pc URL the wiki cites (exturlusage) and with the 17,976 pages in Category:Decklists (year + event + stage + side + fuzzy player name, one page per post).
- 3,121 posts cited on the wiki; 1,136 uncited; of those 945 map one-to-one to an existing deck page (built from GEMP files / PDFs).
- missing.json: 191 posts with no deck page of their own (50 no page at all, 141 extra decks of a player who has one page for that event+side, mostly 2020 TMW pods with two decks per side).
- Not covered: posts Wayback never captured (2018 has only 3 captures) and events whose lists exist only as PDFs/GEMP zips.
