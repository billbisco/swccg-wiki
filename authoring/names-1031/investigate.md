# Part 1: three name calls (evidence for Bill)

I read the wiki scans of the sheets directly. Crops are in the scratchpad `img/` folder.

## (a) "Ben Campbell" (2007) and Ian Campbell (2009): the same person, high confidence

- **The 2007 sheets are not "Ben".** These are pages 27–28 of [File:2007 Regional Bespin.pdf](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2007_Regional_Bespin.pdf), headed "MN State, 6/30".
  - **DS sheet** ([File:2007 Minnesota State Championship Ian Campbell DS.png](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2007_Minnesota_State_Championship_Ian_Campbell_DS.png)): it clearly reads **"Ian Campbell"**, with deck title "Dark Hamdogs".
  - **LS sheet** ([File:2007 Minnesota State Championship Ian Campbell LS.png](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2007_Minnesota_State_Championship_Ian_Campbell_LS.png)): the first name is a hasty scrawl ("Ian"/"Ra"), with deck title "Hamdogs". The transcriber read the LS scrawl as "Ben", and the deck page records that as "Name on sheet: Ben Campbell". The DS half of the same pair settles it.
- **2009 sheet** ([File:2009 Bespin Regionals Ian Campbell LS.png](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2009_Bespin_Regionals_Ian_Campbell_LS.png), Bespin Regionals, 8/1/09): the name is "Ian Campbell" and the username is "Runner". The deck name "Hamdogs" is in the same style as 2007.
- **Same decks.** Both years pair There Is Good In Him (LS) with Hunt Down And Destroy The Jedi (DS):
  - [2007 LS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2007_Minnesota_State_Championship_Ian_Campbell_LS_There_Is_Good_In_Him)
  - [2007 DS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2007_Minnesota_State_Championship_Ian_Campbell_DS_Hunt_Down_And_Destroy_The_Jedi)
  - [2009 LS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2009_Bespin_Regionals_Ian_Campbell_LS_There_Is_Good_In_Him)
  - [2009 DS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2009_Bespin_Regionals_Ian_Campbell_DS_Hunt_Down_And_Destroy_The_Jedi_(V))
- **Wiki.** The only person page is [Ian Campbell](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Ian_Campbell). "Ben Campbell" appears only in the "Name on sheet" line of the 2007 LS deck page.
  - [Colin Campbell](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Colin_Campbell) also played the 2009 Bespin Regionals. He is a different first name and a separate person, possibly a relative, but nothing says so.
- **Other sources.** I found no other "Ben Campbell" on the wiki or in web search. A forum.starwarsccg.org search cannot help: phpBB ignores "Ian" as too short, and "Campbell" alone matches off-topic posts.
- **Suggested tidy-up.** Change "Name on sheet: Ben Campbell" on the 2007 LS deck page to "Ian Campbell (first name hard to read on the LS sheet; the DS sheet reads Ian)". Nothing needs to move.

## (b) "Ethan Taylor" and Brian Taylor (2007 Illinois States): the sheet says Brian, high confidence

- **Source.** These are pages 17–18 of [File:2007 Illinois State Championship.pdf](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2007_Illinois_State_Championship.pdf).
- **The scan.** The sheets are faxed upside down. With the page turned the right way up, the name at the top of both sheets is block capitals **"BRIAN TAYLOR"**.
  - The fax header ("OCT-01-2007 … P. 17/18") cuts through the B on p17 and the B-R on p18. That is where the "?RIAN" / "Ethan" machine reading came from.
  - Scans: [DS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2007_Illinois_State_Championship_Brian_Taylor_DS.png), [LS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2007_Illinois_State_Championship_Brian_Taylor_LS.png).
- **Wiki.** The only person page is [Brian Taylor](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Brian_Taylor), with only this event. "Ethan Taylor" appears only in the "Name on sheet" lines of the two deck pages.
  - [Nate Taylor](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Nate_Taylor) played the same event. He is a separate person, and nothing ties the two together.
  - A Game Players Network deck ([AdmiralThrawn36 Info Agents of the Black Sun Classic Version 2.1](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/AdmiralThrawn36_Info_Agents_of_the_Black_Sun_Classic_Version_2.1)) thanks a "Brian Taylor", but nothing gives a place, so it may or may not be him.
- **Other sources.** I found no "Ethan Taylor" anywhere in SWCCG sources I could search. The forum search for Brian Taylor returns only "Brian … Taylor" coincidences in 2025 Worlds pairings (Brian Fred / Taylor Gosiaco), with no hit for the person.
- **Suggested tidy-up.** Change "Name on sheet: Ethan Taylor" to "Brian Taylor (the fax header covers the first letters)" on both deck pages:
  - [LS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2007_Illinois_State_Championship_Brian_Taylor_LS_Quiet_Mining_Colony)
  - [DS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2007_Illinois_State_Championship_Brian_Taylor_DS_Hunt_Down_And_Destroy_The_Jedi)

## (c) "Kirk Cyland" (2008 NY States), Kirk England, and "Kirk" (2007 NY States): 2008 is probably Kirk England (medium); 2007 is possibly the same man (low to medium)

- **2008 sheet** ([File:2008 New York State Championship Kirk England LS.png](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2008_New_York_State_Championship_Kirk_England_LS.png)): p9 of ny08decklists.pdf, from the [swccgpc.com Wayback copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20080913090820/http://www.swccgpc.com/decklists/ny08decklists.pdf).
  - The player-name line reads "Kirk Eglad" or "Kirk Englad". The capital is an E, not a C, so "Cyland" was a machine misreading. The best reading is **England** with the first n dropped.
  - The sheet has no username or e-mail. Its deck title is "Light", and the deck is We'll Handle This.
- **2007 sheets.** These are pp4–5 of the 2007 States NY PDF.
  - Both are signed only **"Kirk"**, dated 6/30/07. The DS deck title is "Why, Steve, Why?" and the LS title is "OMG, I Can't Believe It's Not Combat".
  - Scans: [DS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2007_New_York_State_Championship_Kirk_England_DS.png), [LS](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/File:2007_New_York_State_Championship_Kirk_England_LS.png).
  - The capital K and the "irk" are written the same way as on the 2008 sheet. The event is the same state championship, held one year earlier. No other Kirk appears in any New York result on the wiki.
- **Other sources.** I found nothing outside the sheets:
  - forum.starwarsccg.org: the search for "Kirk England" gives one unrelated hit (a "New England" Dunkin' post in a 2023 San Diego report).
  - Web search: no hits.
  - Wiki: no other events for Kirk England.
  - I could not open the forum's member list; profiles need a login.
- **Verdict.**
  - 2008 = Kirk England: medium. The surname is a reading, not a printed name.
  - 2007 "Kirk" = the same man: low to medium, resting on the Small World rule and the handwriting.
  - The alternative for 2007 is to SKIP the two sheets (first name only), as was done for the 2008 Texas MPC Day 2 first-name sheets.
  - Either way, the current page [Kirk England](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Kirk_England) should say on its Talk page that the surname comes from the 2008 handwriting and that the 2007 sheets carry only "Kirk".
