## 1. Roy Bordier → **Ray Bordier** (Ray is canonical)

**Evidence that the name is Ray:**
- **Every sheet reads Ray.** I checked a montage of all nine DS sheet headers.
  - Handwritten "Ray Bordier": 2007 MPC ("Happiness"), 2007 NJ States, 2007 NY States ("Ray Bordier – Dark"), 2007 PA States.
  - Typed "Ray Bordier": 2008 MPC.
  - Handwritten "Ray Bordier" with username **Spectre**: 2009 2K Event, 2010 MPC, 2011 MPC, 2012 MPC.
  - The 2010 sheet's e-mail field also begins with "ray". I have not copied the address.
- **The transcribers agree.** The names helper's own column `names-final.tsv` reads "Ray Bordier" everywhere. The 2007–09 batch then mapped it to "Roy" only to match the earlier 2012 MPC page.
- **Forum posts say Ray:**
  - Old Decipher rankings, "Ray Bordier … 2054 … Coruscant" ([p1139533](https://forum.starwarsccg.org/viewtopic.php?p=1139533#p1139533)).
  - 2017 MPC registered players list, "Ray Bordier" ([p1082344](https://forum.starwarsccg.org/viewtopic.php?p=1082344#p1082344)).
  - Clayton Atkin's 2000 GenCon report ([p1236582](https://forum.starwarsccg.org/viewtopic.php?p=1236582#p1236582)).
  - "Who is the best player to never make Day 2/3 of Worlds" ([p1301475](https://forum.starwarsccg.org/viewtopic.php?p=1301475#p1301475)).
  - The forum has 151 hits for "Bordier" and none of the ones I read say Roy.
- **Decipher sources say Ray:** the Philadelphia Squadron Member listing, the DecipherCon results and photos, and a 1999 Decipher Deck Design are all "Ray" (or "R.") Bordier.
- **Conclusion.** "Roy" exists only on this wiki and is a transcription misreading.

**Drafts:**
- **[Ray Bordier](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Ray_Bordier):**
  - I merged the two results tables into one "Tournament Results" table, newest first: 9 Players Committee rows from 2007 to 2012, then all Decipher and DeckTech rows.
  - I blanked the "—" finish cells to match the page's blank-place note.
  - The lead gains a sentence on the 2007–2012 events and the username Spectre, with refs to the 2007 MPC D1 PDF and the 2012 MPC PDF (the existing name `pc-rem`). It ends: "published … as Roy Bordier, a misreading".
  - See also and categories now cover 2007–2012 and Players.
  - Nothing else is removed.
- **[Roy Bordier](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Roy_Bordier):** `#REDIRECT [[Ray Bordier]]`. It has no Talk page.
- **Moves:** 18 deck pages and 18 scan Files, "… Roy Bordier …" → "… Ray Bordier …". After the moves, each deck page's text says Ray throughout, including its scan link.
- **Hub rows changed in:** [2007 Match Play Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2007_Match_Play_Championship), [2007 New Jersey State Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2007_New_Jersey_State_Championship), [2007 New York State Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2007_New_York_State_Championship), [2007 Pennsylvania State Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2007_Pennsylvania_State_Championship), [2008 Match Play Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2008_Match_Play_Championship), [2009 2K Event](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2009_2K_Event), [2010 Match Play Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2010_Match_Play_Championship), [2011 Match Play Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2011_Match_Play_Championship), [2012 Match Play Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2012_Match_Play_Championship).

## 2. Peter Fratjord → **Peter Frafjord** (rename)

**Evidence that the spelling is Frafjord:**
- **Every sheet spells it with an f:**
  - "Peter Frafjord" at 2008 Worlds Day 2, p115. The f has a crossbar, which is the source of the "t".
  - "Peter Fafjord/Frafjord" at 2009 Bespin, p48–49, username galacticsenatebumblegum.
  - "Pete Frafjord" at 2009 West Nationals Day 1, p31–32.
  - "Peter Frafjord" at 2009 West Nationals Day 2, p7–8.
- **His brother's name.** Tom is "Frafjord" on every wiki page and every PC source (2008 Worlds Top 8 history).
- **The forum.** forum.starwarsccg.org has **136** posts for "Frafjord" and **0** for "Fratjord":
  - Bespin history threads: "2008 – Tom Frafjord, 2009 – Tom Frafjord".
  - jimli: "me and Raveling and Frafjord all made top 8".
  - RMC 2015: "Tom Frafjord (first player to 8-0 at Worlds)".
- **Caveat.** The brother relationship comes from the earlier names notes only. I did not find a published source for it, so the draft does not state it in the article. It only lists Tom under See also.

**Drafts:**
- **Page move.** Move [Peter Fratjord](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Peter_Fratjord) → Peter Frafjord. The redirect stays.
- **New page text:**
  - The lead says "also Pete Frafjord; also published as Peter Fratjord".
  - The broken duplicate second "Tournament Results" heading is replaced by See also plus a References block.
  - Category:2008 is added.
- **Moves:** 9 deck pages and 9 scan Files.
- **Hubs changed:** [2008 World Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2008_World_Championship) (Day 2 and Team rows), [2009 Bespin Regionals](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2009_Bespin_Regionals), [2009 US West Nationals](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2009_US_West_Nationals) (Day 1 and Day 2 rows).
- **People lists:** [People/F](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/People/F) gets the Frafjord, Peter row (2008–2009) and drops the Fratjord row. [People](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/People) is updated.

## 4. Joe Wisniewski → **[Joseph Wisniewski](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Joseph_Wisniewski)** (probable, not proven)

**Evidence:**
- **For the merge:**
  - Joseph is the fuller name.
  - Joseph's page is better sourced: 7 results, the organizer's name and handle list, and 10 backlinks.
  - There is no other US Wisniewski in any results.
  - Both names are active in 2025–2026:
    - Joseph = GEMP geraldkw0 / forum geraldkw. He started in 2021 as a new player ("played maybe for a second back in the premiere days"; [p1328983](https://forum.starwarsccg.org/viewtopic.php?p=1328983#p1328983)) and played the 2025–26 Retro League.
    - Joe played the in-person [2026 World Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2026_World_Championship) (Bloomington, Minnesota, #58). The pairings posts say "Joe Wisniewski".
- **Against, or unproven:**
  - Nothing ties geraldkw to Minnesota or to the Worlds entry. The forum profile (u=19520) needs a login.
  - The PC's Worlds deck page spells the name "Joe **Wishniewski**".
- **Verdict.** I drafted the merge as Bill asked, with the caveat on Talk.

**Drafts:**
- **Joseph Wisniewski page:**
  - The Worlds row is added at the top of Tournament Results.
  - The lead adds: "As Joe Wisniewski he played the 2026 World Championship … PC spells him Joe Wishniewski".
  - See also is updated.
- **[Joe Wisniewski](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/Joe_Wisniewski):** redirect.
- **Deck pages are not moved.** The 2026 Worlds deck pages keep "Joe Wisniewski" in their titles, because that is the published name. This follows the precedent of the "2026 GEMPC Pat Johnson …" and "… PMJ …" decks, which keep the source name.
  - Their player links become `[[Joseph Wisniewski|Joe Wisniewski]]`.
  - If Bill prefers moving them, the moves would be "2026 Worlds Joe Wisniewski DS BHBM" → "2026 Worlds Joseph Wisniewski DS BHBM" and the same for "LS Zero Hour". They are not in moves.tsv.
- **[2026 World Championship](https://wiki.swccg.com/wiki/2026_World_Championship):** the row link becomes `[[Joseph Wisniewski|Joe Wisniewski]]`.
