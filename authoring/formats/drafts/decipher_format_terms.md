# Decipher format terms: quotes for the history article

Collected 2026-10-24 from Decipher's own pages on the Wayback Machine (fetched through the linked PC). Each quote gives the original URL, then the snapshot I opened. Spelling and punctuation are as on the page. Raw text of every page is on the PC under `~/fmtdec/txt/` and `~/fmtdec/pdf/`.

## 1. Constructed vs sealed: how Decipher described them

### 1996: "The Decipher System" and non-sanctioned sealed deck
Organizer's Guide, "Tournament Rules System, August 1996 Release" (PDF)
http://www.decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/tournamentguide.pdf
https://web.archive.org/web/19961219040510/http://www.decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/tournamentguide.pdf
- Contents: "PART 2 TOURNAMENT RULE SYSTEMS & STRUCTURES: The Decipher System / Alternative Systems / Simple-Swiss Systems / Elimination Format / Sealed Deck Tournaments"
- "THE DECIPHER SYSTEM — Swiss Style (Sanctioned). This system is Swiss Style (with a twist). In order to make sure all players use both decks (Galactic Empire and Rebel Alliance) equally, we have devised a system of Rounds."
- "Currently, Decipher believes that a consistent format for tournaments is desirable to insure uniform fairness and accuracy in tournaments and player ratings. Thus, holding a sanctioned Decipher System event is desirable."
- "Sealed Deck Tournaments (Non-Sanctioned). Sealed deck tournaments can be a great way to run a tournament for both beginners as well as experienced players. By simply bringing one sealed starter deck to a tournament everyone will be playing with the 'fate' of the cards. Experienced players know that to win a sealed deck tournament you must first have good luck and second have good playing and deck building skills."
- "Any and all cards from the Star Wars™ Customizable Card Game™ universe are allowed in play at tournaments." / "A standard tournament deck consists of 60 cards. Their is currently no limit to the number of one card that a player can have in a tournament deck." / "NOTE: During Sealed Deck tournaments only, players may swap with their remaining unused cards between games."

Non-sanctioned Introduction (1996)
http://www.decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/nonsanctionedintro.html
https://web.archive.org/web/19961219012152/http://www.decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/nonsanctionedintro.html

### 1998: three sanctioned formats; "enhanced format"
Tournaments overview
http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/overview/index.html
https://web.archive.org/web/19980717031138/http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/overview/index.html
- "Three Tournament formats are sanctioned: Swiss, Junior Swiss, and Sealed Deck."
- Downloads: "Tournament Command Card", "Sealed Deck Command Card", "Junior Swiss Command Card"; Tournament Guidelines "(last updated 4/15/97)".

Using the Official Tournament Sealed Deck
http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/overview/sealeddeck.htm
https://web.archive.org/web/19980506110410/http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/overview/sealeddeck.htm
- "So why did Decipher make the Official Tournament Sealed Deck? In a sealed-deck tournament, players do not bring their own cards but instead use unopened packs which they normally purchase as part of the entrance fee. This is a great way to bring new players into the game! However, it has one drawback. Sometimes the basic ingredients for a great deck are not obtained from the purchased mix of starter decks and booster packs."
- "We are going to designate every tournament run using these products as 'enhanced format.' We feel this conveys the higher quality experience players will get when competing in these events! And these tournaments can be sanctioned, thereby contributing toward player ratings."

### May 1999: "Decipher Swiss", "Decipher Junior", "Decipher sealed deck"
Tournament Guide, II. Formats
http://www.decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/overview/swtournamentguide/formats.html
https://web.archive.org/web/19990502214324/http://www.decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/overview/swtournamentguide/formats.html
- "Decipher sanctions tournaments that follow three different formats: the Decipher Swiss format, the Decipher Junior format and the Decipher sealed deck format. These formats are considered separate for rating purposes and a separate rating database will be created for each."
- "In sanctioned tournaments, of any format, there are no restrictions on any particular cards in the game. Both white border and black border cards from the Star Wars Customizable Card Game universe are allowable for use in tournaments as soon as they are released. ... Gold border cards (if and when released) will not be allowed in sanctioned tournament play. Side boards ... are not allowed."
- "This format, based on traditional Swiss-style tournament play, is designed to test players' abilities using both Light Side (Rebel) and Dark Side (Imperial) decks. Each player must come to the tournament with two 60-card decks (one Light Side and one Dark Side)."
- Junior: "exactly the same as the Decipher Swiss format, except ... Participation is limited to players 14 years old and younger / Deck size is restricted to 40 cards / Timed games are no less than 50 minutes".
- Sealed: "A sealed deck tournament is played with unopened Premiere starter decks (limited or unlimited)."
- "Tournament directors may run 'theme' sealed deck tournaments (Hoth, Dagobah etc.) by providing additional expansion packs for each player. ... These sealed deck events will still be sanctioned and rated."
- "B. Non-sanctioned Alternative Tournament Formats: i. Simple Swiss Format ... ii. Elimination Format"

Tournament Guide, I. General (same snapshot series)
https://web.archive.org/web/19990502223301/http://www.decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/overview/swtournamentguide/general.html
- Promotion must state "sanctioned formats - Decipher Swiss, Decipher Junior, or Decipher sealed deck".

### June 1999 onward: "Constructed Deck, Junior, and Sealed Deck"
Decipher Customizable Card Group Tournament Guide, "TD GUIDE 6.99" (PDF, all Decipher games)
http://www.decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/resources/pdfs/tournamentguide0699.pdf
https://web.archive.org/web/20000903022456/http://www.decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/resources/pdfs/tournamentguide0699.pdf
- "Decipher sanctions three basic formats: Constructed Deck, Junior, and Sealed Deck."
- Promotion must state "sanctioned formats - Constructed Deck, Junior, or Sealed Deck plus any particular format within the basic tournament type" and "special rules - scenarios, deck themes etc."
- Junior: "all players must be under the age of 15 years old".
- So the rename from "Decipher Swiss" to "Constructed Deck" falls between the May 1999 HTML guide and this June 1999 PDF, when the guide was rewritten to cover Star Trek CCG and Young Jedi as well.

Official Tournament Guide, II. Tournament Types and Formats (HTML)
http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/resources/tournamentguide/tournamenttypesandformats.html
https://web.archive.org/web/20000109144553/http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/resources/tournamentguide/tournamenttypesandformats.html
- "Decipher sanctions three basic formats: Constructed Deck, Junior, and Sealed Deck. These formats are considered separate for rating purposes ..."
- "In sanctioned Decipher tournaments there are no restricted or banned cards, with the sole exception of the banned Star Trek CCG card, 'Raise The Stakes'. All cards, with any colored border, are allowable for use in tournaments as soon as they are released. ... Some thematic and scenario-based formats do limit card selection to ones that fit within that theme or scenario. In such cases, the allowable card list needs to be available in advance to all players."
- "Constructed Deck, based on traditional Swiss-style tournament play, is designed to test players' abilities using premade decks. Each player must come to the tournament with decks already constructed according to the standard rules for that game."
- "A sealed deck tournament is played with unopened starter decks. ... each person must bring, purchase, or be provided with any deck-building materials required for the tournament, such as expansion packs, Warp or Jedi Packs."
- Same wording in the 2002 guide on tournaments.decipher.com: http://tournaments.decipher.com/tournamentguide-stswyj.asp — https://web.archive.org/web/20020220233154/http://tournaments.decipher.com/tournamentguide-stswyj.asp . The winner is awarded only 1 victory point for a modified win."

## 3. "Type I" / "Type II"

Not found in any Decipher page I read (grep of all fetched pages and PDFs for "Type I", "Type II", "Type 1", "Type 2"). Decipher's own term for an Episode I-only pool was "Episode I Closed Environment" or "Episode I only environment" (see section 1). The wiki's "Type II (players)" for the Episode I Closed Environment needs a player-side source.

## 4. When and why formats were named

- 1996 → 1998 → 1999 names for the standard constructed event: "The Decipher System / Swiss Style" (Aug 1996 guide) → "Swiss" (1998 overview) → "the Decipher Swiss format" (May 1999) → "Constructed Deck" (June 1999 PDF, "TD GUIDE 6.99"; HTML guide by Jan 2000) → "Standard Constructed Deck" (2001). URLs above.
- Junior: "Junior Swiss" (1998) → "the Decipher Junior format" (May 1999) → "Junior" (June 1999 / 2000) → rating category "Junior Constructed Deck" (1999–2001).
- Sealed: "Sealed Deck Tournaments (Non-Sanctioned)" (1996) → sanctioned "Sealed Deck" (1998) → "the Decipher sealed deck format" (May 1999) → "Sealed Deck" (1999–2002).
- "Enhanced format" (1998): "We are going to designate every tournament run using these products as 'enhanced format.' We feel this conveys the higher quality experience players will get when competing in these events!" (OTSD page, above). Later "the regular Enhanced Format Sealed Deck tournament rules" (OTSD Booster Draft rules, 1999): http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/resources/otsdboosterformat.html — https://web.archive.org/web/19990901012813/http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/resources/otsdboosterformat.html
- EPP Overdrive (1999): "Get ready to meet the newest sanctioned Sealed Deck tournament format. Called EPP Overdrive, it uses Enhanced Premiere Product (EPP) with rules adjusted so that every game moves quickly and wraps in 15 minutes." / "a fast-moving alternative to the typical four-hour event" — http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/epptournaments/index.html — https://web.archive.org/web/19991010234206/http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/epptournaments/index.html
- Endor Draft (1999): "The standalone nature of the Endor expansion allows players to take part in an exciting new tournament format. This format is a type of sealed deck event that can be run as a Decipher sanctioned sealed deck tournament." — http://www1.decipher.com/starwars/rules/endorrules.html — https://web.archive.org/web/19990915001027/http://www1.decipher.com/starwars/rules/endorrules.html
- Team events (1999): "The beauty of the Team Event format is that it seamlessly blends with normal Constructed or Sealed Deck protocols." — http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/teamevents/rules.html — https://web.archive.org/web/20000109210353/http://decipher.com/starwars/tournaments/teamevents/rules.html
