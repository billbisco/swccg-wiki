# Cloud City Limited: release day (research note, 2026-10-24)

**Finding:** no source found gives the exact street date. The evidence points to
**Wednesday 5 November 1997** as the most likely day, and puts the release in early November 1997.

## Evidence

| Date | Source (Wayback copy opened) | What it says |
|------|------------------------------|--------------|
| 2 Jul 1997 | Decipher NewsFlash, "Star Wars CCG Cloud City Update" (Rich Loftus) — https://web.archive.org/web/19971212060904/http://www.decipher.com:80/newsandhighlights/cloudsupdate.html | "Cloud City breaks out in late September of this year". This plan slipped. |
| 22 Sep 1997 | "New ships! New characters! New weapons!" (Joe Boulden) — https://web.archive.org/web/19980224002140/http://www.decipher.com:80/newsandhighlights/cloudcityarticles/joeccity.html | "Cloud City is aloft!" A preview piece about the art, not a release notice. |
| 9 Oct 1997 | NewsFlash index item "Jabba's Palace: Star Wars CCG Future Expansion" — https://web.archive.org/web/19980110221528/http://decipher.com:80/newsandhighlights/index.html | Calls Jabba's Palace "the next planned full Star Wars CCG expansion following Cloud City". Gives no release date. |
| **Wed 5 Nov 1997** | Decipher NewsFlash, "Cloud City Easter Egg Contest!" (Tom Lischke) — https://web.archive.org/web/19980223163152/http://www.decipher.com:80/newsandhighlights/ccityeggcontest.html | "November 5 1997 - Welcome to the Cloud City Easter Egg contest! Inside the Cloud City expansion you will find some visual goodies…" The contest runs "today through noon (EST), December 19, 1997". This is the earliest dated text found that treats the cards as in players' hands. |
| 19 Dec 1997 | "Cloud City Easter Eggs and Their Discoverers" — https://web.archive.org/web/19980223111121/http://www.decipher.com:80/newsandhighlights/cceggwinners.html | Refers to "the Cloud City Easter Egg Contest that began November 5". |
| 5–7 Dec 1997 | 1997 World Championship deck pages on the wiki (Jacobs, Riboulet, Alread, Krause, Feldman) | All of them contain `CC-` cards, so Cloud City was in use by early December. The wiki's Worlds page (citing Trandosite) says Cloud City "had just hit the shelves". |
| 5 Jan 1998 | NewsFlash index item "Revisit the Cloud City Articles" (same capture as above) | "Cloud City has been quite a success since its **November release**." |
| 28 Apr 1998 (comparison) | "Jabba's Palace In Stores Tomorrow" — https://web.archive.org/web/19980506103233/http://decipher.com:80/newsandhighlights/jpreleasedate.htm | Decipher's next expansion had a "worldwide street release, Wednesday, April 29, 1998". Its pre-release sealed events were held 25–26 April, the weekend before ([jabbasprerelease.htm](https://web.archive.org/web/19980506105604/http://decipher.com:80/newsandhighlights/jabbasprerelease.htm)). |

5 November 1997 was a Wednesday. Decipher's street dates were worldwide and fell on Wednesdays, and it opened the Easter Egg contest on that date as if the cards had just reached players. Together these make 5 November the best estimate. One caveat remains: the contest could have opened a few days after the cards reached stores. No source found places Cloud City in stores before 5 November, though.

## Searched without result

- Wayback CDX for decipher.com, Oct–Dec 1997: there are no captures before 12 December 1997. The site's "Archives" month pages (`insidedecipher/archives/1997/oct.html`, `nov.html`) are linked from a February 1998 capture but were never archived.
- Decipher's 1997 regional reports and finalist bios (`specialevents/current/97worldfinals/*`) for the 1–8 November qualifiers (Hoth 2 Kamloops, Tatooine 3 Phoenix, Ithor 3 Paris, Ord Mantell Tokyo, Yavin 4 1 Virginia Beach, Borås Open). The bios carry no deck details, and no event reports for those qualifiers were archived.
- The SWCCG Collecting Encyclopedia 3.2, Wookieepedia, CardGuide and Wikipedia all give only "November 1997". Web searches for exact-day phrases found nothing.

## Effect on event formats (event_formats.json)

- Events on 1–2 November 1997 (Borås Open, Ithor Regionals Paris, Tatooine Regionals Phoenix, Hoth Regionals Kamloops, Ord Mantell Regionals Tokyo) are set to **Premiere - Dagobah** as a judgement call, since they came before the most likely street date. For the non-US events, the new English set would also have arrived later.
- Yavin 4 Regionals (Virginia Beach), on 8 November 1997, is left as "[[Premiere - Dagobah]] or [[Premiere - Cloud City]]". It came three days after the likely release, and no source says whether Cloud City was allowed there.
- A decklist from any of these events would settle the question: Cloud City cards have wiki images starting with `CC-`.
