# Format rules research: Reflections II Booster Draft, Progressive Sealed Deck, Players' Choice Sealed Deck, Allegiances

Done 2026-10-24. Decipher's and the old Players Committee site's pages were read on the Wayback Machine through the linked PC (raw copies on the PC under `~/fmtres/`). The Wayback Machine went "Temporarily Offline" several times while I worked, so I retried each request. Every snapshot cited below is one I opened. Spelling is as on the page.

## 1. Reflections II Booster Draft: FOUND (Decipher's own rules page, plus two Players Committee guides)

### 1a. Decipher, "Rules for Reflections II Sealed Deck: Booster Draft Format" (January 2001)
http://www.decipher.com/starwars/expansions/reflections2/boosterdraftformat.html
- First version, snapshots 2001-01-24 and 2001-03-03 (same digest): https://web.archive.org/web/20010124152400/http://www.decipher.com:80/starwars/expansions/reflections2/boosterdraftformat.html
- Revised version (captured from 2001-06-08 until 2005, one digest): https://web.archive.org/web/20010608212408/http://www.decipher.com:80/starwars/expansions/reflections2/boosterdraftformat.html
- The only difference between the two: "Each player is allowed to bring with them any 6 battleground locations." became "... any 6 battleground locations for each side." (The change falls between the 2001-03-03 and 2001-06-08 captures.)

Quotes (January 2001 version):
- "Sealed Deck Booster Draft play is designed to provide a balanced tournament environment, while still maintaining an arena for creative deck construction."
- "Each player is allowed to bring with them any 6 battleground locations. The tournament director must inspect these cards before the drafting of packs begins. Players can then incorporate these battleground locations into their deck after drafting."
- "Each player must start with 4 packs of Reflections II. Players ideally are seated in groups of 4, however, this is sometimes not possible ... and groups of 6 are used."
- "In the case of an odd number of players, the tournament director might need to draft a dummy hand." / "The tournament director should always draft in a group of 6 and draft competitively."
- Group-size table: 8 players = 2 groups of 4; 9 = 1 of 4 + 1 of 6 (with a dummy player); 10 = 1 of 4 + 1 of 6; 11 = 2 of 6 (one with a dummy); 12 = 3 of 4; 13 = 2 of 4 + 1 of 6 (dummy); 14 = 2 of 4 + 1 of 6; 15 = 1 of 4 + 2 of 6 (one dummy); 16 = 4 of 4.
- "Players are randomly determined to play either Light or Dark side of the Force and are never seated next to a player of the same side of the Force."
- "Each player chooses any one card that he or she wants for their side of the Force only ... (Remember: there is no defensive drafting, you may only take cards from your side of the Force)."
- The first pack passes left; the second "this time passing the cards to the right and switching directions after each drafting turn has been exhausted."
- "Once the draft is completed everyone has 10 minutes to builds a 40 card deck. Any cards that the player does not use may be placed aside and substituted into their deck between games, always maintaining a 40 card deck."
- "The tournament is then run using the regular Sealed Deck tournament rules"

Reflections II home page (links to the rules page)
http://www.decipher.com/starwars/expansions/reflections2/index.html — https://web.archive.org/web/20010124152400/http://www.decipher.com:80/starwars/expansions/reflections2/index.html
- "Reflections II Booster Draft Format Introduced"
- "Bring your combo cards and Expanded Universe characters - check out the new rules for sanctioned Sealed Deck that's focused on Reflections II."
- "REFLECTIONS II: EXPANDING THE GALAXY (release date - January 10, 2001)"

Sandy Wible, 12 December 2000 (Reflections II area)
http://www.decipher.com/starwars/expansions/reflections2/121200wible.html — https://web.archive.org/web/20010124151500/http://www.decipher.com:80/starwars/expansions/reflections2/121200wible.html

### 1b. Players Committee "Official SW: CCG Tournament Guide", Version 3 (07/10/2007) and Version 4.0 (04/01/2008)
- v3: http://www.swccgpc.com/Resources/Official%20SWCCG%20Tournament%20Guide.pdf — https://web.archive.org/web/20071026144111/http://www.swccgpc.com/Resources/Official%20SWCCG%20Tournament%20Guide.pdf
- v4.0: http://www.swccgpc.com/Resources/tournaments/OfficialSWCCGTournamentGuide.pdf — https://web.archive.org/web/20080509195802/http://www.swccgpc.com/Resources/tournaments/OfficialSWCCGTournamentGuide.pdf

Both list under "Supported Formats", Sealed Deck: Premiere Sealed, OTSD Sealed, OTSD Booster Draft, JPOTSD Booster Draft, EPP Overdrive, Death Star II Sealed, Death Star II Booster Draft, Reflections II Booster Draft, Reflections III Booster Draft, Secret Squirrel (v4.0 adds Watto's Cube). Both say: "Additional Sealed Deck formats may be approved on a tournament-by-tournament basis."

Entry (same in both):
- "Reflections II Booster Draft / Product required: 4 Reflections II booster packs / Rules: Booster Draft / Additional notes: Each player may bring with him six battleground locations that may be included in the player's deck."
- "Reflections III Booster Draft / Product required: 4 Reflections III booster packs / ... Each player may bring with him six Tatooine, Coruscant and/or Naboo battleground locations that may be included in the player's deck."
- Their Booster Draft rules repeat Decipher's text almost word for word (group table, alternate seating, own-side picks, left then right), and add: "If more than one type of booster pack is being used, packs should be opened in ascending order of release". Deck building: "Players building 40 card decks should be given 10 minutes."
- The 2015 Alternate Format Guide (already cited on the live page) merges the two into "Reflections Booster Draft" with "4 Reflections, Reflections II, or Reflections III booster packs" and drops the Tatooine/Coruscant/Naboo limit; its list of sealed formats still names "Reflections II Booster Draft" and "Reflections III Booster Draft".

## 3. DecipherCon 1999 "Players' Choice Sealed Deck": NOT FOUND for 1999; the name is defined only in 2002

- The pop-up `playerschoice.html` was never archived (see section 2). The early schedule lists '"Players\' Choice" Sealed Deck tournament - cost: $15' for Star Trek CCG on Thursday 6pm–11pm (https://web.archive.org/web/20000122042301/http://decipher.com:80/calendars/event/mainevents/deciphercon/events.html); the final schedule has SW.21 (Thursday 10pm–4am, Friday 8am–2pm, Saturday 9pm–3am) and ST.21.
- Score sheet SW21_3 ("Players Choice", 31 players, 3 games): https://web.archive.org/web/20001219114400/http://decipher.com/calendars/event/mainevents/deciphercon/events/scores/SW21_3.html . Unlike the Progressive sheets, every one of the 28 rows with two or more sides shows the player on both sides (e.g. Rick Gaudioso: Dark, Light, Light). So players did not keep one side as in ordinary sealed deck. Why is not stated.
- Later use of the name, by the Players Committee at DecipherCon 2002 (not evidence for 1999):
  - swccgpc.com, DecipherCon 2002 non-championship schedule: "8-man events SEALED Cost: $15 Description: Chinese menu (Player's Choice) - starter of choice, 4 boosters of choice (availability limited), get together 8 players and go at it." http://www.swccgpc.com/tournaments.htm — https://web.archive.org/web/20021106221038/http://www.swccgpc.com:80/tournaments.htm
