 The Strategy

Note: ?s not edited out in here.
First Turn: Pull Bionic Hand. Activate 4 by yourself. Pull the Training Area, pull Great Warrior, deploy SOS to the training area from reserve deck, deploy the backpack on him from reserve; reshuffle. Get Yoda and Yoda?s Hope. Deploy Great Warrior. SOS pulls Wise Advice. Put Yoda in the backpack. On opponent?s turn, pull A Jedi?s Strength. Second turn: Pull Domain Of Evil. Activate 6 by yourself. Do Great Warrior. 6(SOS)+1(Yoda)+1(Yoda?s Hope)+1(Backpack)+1(Wise Advice)+1(Luke?s Lightsaber)+1(DAG sites)=12, so you need a 2. Deploy A Jedi?s Strength. Signal for Traffic Control. Deploy Traffic Control. Pull Yoda?s Hut. Get Projection/Arconas out. Move to Test 2. On opponent?s turn, pull Size Matters Not. Third Turn: Pull It Is The Future You See. Activate 8 by yourself. Do Test 2. Deploy 3, it?s automatically done.. Deploy Size Matters Not. Deploy a DAG site from reserve. Fourth turn: Activate 9. Complete test 4. Put Bionic Hand back into your deck. Using MWYHL, get regular SOS from reserve, replacing (V)SOS (so you don?t have to lose to use the 7). Fifth Turn: Activate up to the Hand, stack it on Test 5. Now you?re ready to roll. Lose Yoda?s Hope to get Jedi Levitation. Now you are ready for beatdowns. For each weapon you have in a battle, you can select one opponent?s character to be hit. EPP: Han, Leia, Chewie is a great combo! Three opponent?s characters hit, and you have three destiny?7s. So that?s power 34. Needless to say, you should clear the site : )
Use J Levitation to get a character back and kill more. Since you can?t get maintenance, it will be EPP. An EPP kills at LEAST two things. FSYH(V) is obviously getting characters back, AJR lets Qui/Obi kill again! Tank is for something low deploy- like Han or something. Later in the game, drop Hiding In The Garbage and deploy all your lost characters for a final beatdown. I say you have all this force because you took none to a little damage earlier. Retrieve 3 for each test, and Projection and Arconas are awesome. One last note: HCF with two 7s takes out Deal?s Thrawn+Executor, then you lose it. Matchups all play the same.


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     Again, no Arcona protection before you are going offensive. See you other retrieval loop deck. I prefer using the 5 Dagobah site for generation, but Wokling seems to work well too. You have your standard battle package- the persona replacement rule is power/ability at least equal to the replaced version, so once you complete test 2 and SOS (V) is power 6, it won't work. Massa Luke is the way to go if you want to do that. You don't need to pack 4 Signals, or for that reason, any. You can pull Traffic COntrol with Test 4. I think you should include some SAC, since this deck is made for it. Really mess with your opponent- if you can set up the game winning battle by cancelling an interrupt, losing 2 Force is nothing. Plus, if you add Honor (which you should) it is reduced to nothing! ICBW is always fun. HCF is in limited usefullness, since you will be attacking with Han and Chewie on the ground.
   Cam Solusar     One problem I see in your deck is the lack of battleground sites to lure the opponent with. There ARE a lot of strategies where the opponent will supply the battlefield, but I know that at least SYCFA will not give you that opportunity, as well as TTO. (I don't think that 1 HCATF will handle all of that) I would substitute one of the Leia's with DoS if you have it; BHBM could be a problem. Also, I don't think that SoS can replace (V) SoS because test #2 will make (V) SoS power +1. Use Master Luke instead. The beatdown idea is a good strategy for open MWYHL; you have put it together well. Good luck!
