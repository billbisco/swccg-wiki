 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Pure and Simple if you've run with any type of battle deck this is what you need. I've done this with Dark Side Lightsaber Combat and that wasn't really working because I couldn't battle with my Jedi and those are important to me so I was contemplating running it with either BHBM or Hunt Down and well BHBM is a good format for the deck I want to be able to kill Luke if he drops him with Vader and seeing as that I can't cause he get's captured I decided to go with Hunt Down plus the one point of damamge per turn helps as well.

So in note, the deck itself is quite simple 5 Vaders give you a good chance of getting him on the oppnening draw the first thing you do after you activate is get a docking bay prefereably the Executor Docking Bay for the 1/0 site, however if you have 6 force allready and a Vader get one of the other docking bays out and drop him there... If you also have a Maul Strikes you may want to stave off a turn and get a lightsaber for max. beatings... but either way Choke Vader is always good by himself as well.

As for match-ups LightSide Saber Combat is the hardest, but for that all you have to do is go to him with Maul, Mara and Vader and battle on your turn. With you high destinies you should be able to hit them. Then from there you re-deploy if you have to.

Against most anything else you just drop to your site and sit there. If Obi-Wan or Luke come across then Circle or Obsess them. Yes I know that there are only 2 in the deck but you can use Janus to get them into your hand faster and you can draw and drop! them back when you need the destiny.

This whole deck is matter of just playing it smart and not trying to worry to much about the other person. Just remember to make him loose a force each turn and remember the KEY IS TO STACK!!! so that you can win the duels... and with that said don't forget the Dark Rage gets Deep Hatred so you too can Lightsaber Combat your opponent.

Enjoy
-Kubaz Collector-
