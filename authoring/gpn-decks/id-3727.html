***END OF UPDATE***

Here's the EXTREMELY basic strategy: Flip QMC, get the generals to all 5 sites (keep in mind that Obi is a general, too!), drain/direct damage/retrieve, rinse & repeat.

Here's a more detailed strategy:
Turn 1 - pull Rieekan and one other general into hand (Calrissian or Madine preferable). Pull West Gallery (paying attention to what you've got in your deck), use Wokling to pull Launching The Assault (or CC Celebration if they're playing something with fast damage, eg Court, Hunt Down), play LtA on Bespin, and use it to pull Home One. Play both generals to West Gallery. Draw up
Next turn, pull another site, use Rieekan to pull Leia, play her and another character to Guest Quarters. Draw up.
Next turn, pull another site... by this time, you should have drawn one of your last sites, so play that from hand. Play Home One to the system (Maybe put Ackbar or Thaneespi aboard), and flip.
After this, you'll be setting up your generals at all the CC sites for direct damage from the Admiral's Order, and for retrieval with Celebration. From this point on, it's a play-it-by-ear kind of deck...

Card choices -
Feyn Gospic: Pulls Balanced Attack, which pulls Admirals and Generals, and also adds 2 to attrition wherever you've got the Falcon.

Owen & Beru, Tawss Khaa: Characters that draw destiny on their own. If you'd like to suggest something better, please do! >:)

Cyborg Construct: TECH! No more fear of Monnok lost, random card removal, Uncertain Is The Future. Trust me on this one... it's cool!

Effective Repairs: If Celebration is lost (and Wokling's already been POOPed), this is the best way to retrieve it.



This decks pretty new, and I could use some constructive feedback to fine-tune it. Thanks in advance! >:)
