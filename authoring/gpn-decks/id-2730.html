 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I've found that few players can compare to a well defended Power Coupling, especially with it's little technicalities:
1) It lets Visored Vision wreak havok.
2) It works with Recoil in Fear as a Non-Recircualtion (it's shuffling) action to allow you to dump your destiny 5,6,7 hand (face up) into your deck.
3) It allows you to see what you get from Jedi Levitation.
4) It allows the maximum power of Oola and Leesub.

When you track those 5,6,7, grab On The Edge and get almost infinite force retrieval. Drain on Dagobah with a fast start (deploying at least 2 sites first turn). There's so much searching in this deck that you'll get the matching sabers at lightning speed.
Somehow, this deck works like a well oiled machine.
The only enemy I've discovered for it is a Death Star Deck that can blow away planets.
Any comments? What would hurt this deck? I want to know and try and make it better.
Yes, it's a massive retriever and activator. That's almost all it does. During a game I played I managed a Surprise Assault with three destiny 7 cards. Imagine the look on my opponent's face.
Above all else this deck is fun! It's a type of strategy the opponent will never see coming, but be honest if you think it stinks... you can't hurt a Dark Lord of the Sith's feelings... you can just chop off his hand or upper half or something, but that's it.

   superdavis     I give you three stars for some originality and creativity. This is not a 5 star deck however. There are a lot of things that keep this deck from being as powerful as it could be, though, I can't tell you how powerful it could be since this is a untested archtype to me. Here are my thoughts off the top of my head. First of all, the deck needs more damage potential. I can almost guarantee you that in a tournament environment someone is either going to grab your Suprise Assualt or your On the Edge cards. Then what you have is a deck with not enough characters to hold down an opponent's site or enough force drain potential to be a threat. Though your strategy is good, make it a side strategy. You can make Dagobah a force drain powerhouse using clouds and/or Gungans. (Yes, Gungans.) Kaduus react for free if ridden by Gungans and add 1 to Force drains at a Swamp. (There are potentially two Swamps at Dagobah, if you use the <> version.) That will let you spread out on Dagobah rather well with little fear of retaliation. (Been a long time since I saw a Dagobah drain deck, so I'm sure you don't have to worry about a Cane Addis anytime soon.) Your attack force needs beefing too. You need to keep in mind ahead of time that you're almost definitely going to have to drop down on a non dagobah location. Come Here You Big Coward will shut you down in a heartbeat since Dagobah is not a battleground location. Bye bye force retrieval. Want to know how to make room? Here you go. Use the Sense/Recoil combo instead of 3 of each card. Get rid of: Jedi Levitation 1 Projection 2 Encampments R-3P0 Losing Track Away Put Your Weapon Leesub That's 14 cards, by my quick count. Maybe leave 1 Levitation, but you need to trim the fat on this deck. If you keep a Levitation, take out a Visored Vision or It Could Be Worse. Leesub . . . eh, so what? I think you need to worry more about making your strategy work and trying to avoid a Monock. Also consider using Headed for the Medical Frigate and starting with some of the more helpful effects, like Get to Your Ships! and Saitorr to get characters out with matching ships and weapons. Use multiple signals to get your Power Coupling card out. Also, keep in mind, you can NOT pull <> sites with the Dagobah game text. They are not Dagobah locations until deployed on the table and not before. Not a bad effort. =)
