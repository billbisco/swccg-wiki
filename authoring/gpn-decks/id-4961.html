 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Basically your standard blow-and-drain... with a twist. You'll notice that I've included the Death Star Tractor Beam and two copies of Besieged; any space-based light side deck should be running a copy of Advanced Preparations (V) with standard gear for popping the happy fun ball under their shield. If a space force moves towards the Death Star, they've got a LOT going against them: TIE's with guns, multiple battle destinies, and me capturing their starships left and right.
You've got enough named destroyers to give you some hefty power and retrieval, some power-hitters for the ground, awesome defenses on the Death Star, plenty of drains and damage, and an easy shot at Alderaan. What more could you want?

No strat this time, sorry guys. If you can't figure out the basic concept of a SYCFA Flip deck, don't bother reviewing.

David Kangas
Shadow 31
Vancouver, WA

   Cam Solusar     One thing that worries me is the disparity of parsec numbers in this deck. I would try to throw in a parsec = 4 system so the Thunderflare can make it through. Also, why Force Lightning? Most SYCFA decks just drop Palpatine at the DB and let him stay there all game. You could replace it with a Death Star Assault Squadron or Imp. Barrier or something else that could prevent a big explosion. I like the idea of capturing ships but I think a tractor beam for the Tyrant would be too much. Strategy would help, but this deck is pretty self- explanitory. Another possibility for removal MIGHT be the Short Range/ Watch Your Back combo, but I'll leave that entirely up to you. Good luck.
