 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck is more of a standard draw than a pull deck. I can pull AT-ATs, TTMG, Electro Rangefinder, and AT-AT Cannons.

To answer Lord Hoban, the AATs Add two battle destiny (last game I had two at a site, and I was drawing five battle destinies), and AAT Assault Leader can deploy Energy Shell Launchers from Reserve.

Also, with Vader on Blizzard 1, and Electro Rangefinder, I have to draw a 1 to hit the MPG. If I control ANY exterior hoth site, then I only need a zero or greater. That's pretty good odds that I get it.

I use Inconsequential Losses to soak up attrition with my AT-AT Cannons. I'd like another one, plus some AT-ST Dual Cannons, but I don't have those as yet.

Pote Snitkin adds 3 to power, and moves for free. J'quille is power +2 on hoth (for 6 total)

Also, IMPOC/IMPCON says 'opponent loses no force to YMSYL'

   LordHoban     If you're too tired to write a strategy section, then I guess I'm too tired to rate it... :P No offense, but the strategy section determines whether you know how to get the blood of this deck flowing, or whether it gets crushed under any decent opposing deck. So yeah, strategy section IS a good idea. I don't see why you're wasting the deck space with Inconsequential Losses when you don't even start with Blaster Rack (v) which would let you pull your weapons when you deploy your mains. Besides, I wouldn't want those 1s back in my deck anyhow. Also, what about You May Start Your Landing? That would allow for added damage to your opponent, but I guess since you aren't gonna play walker garrison it might be beside the point. I just don't see this deck being very successful in blowing the MPG, the destiny average is too low, especially with only 7 real interrupts and effects in the deck. You obviously pull Blizz 4 and use that to pull Lord Vader, who pilots one of your other walkers with the AT-AT Cannon to blow, so in that case, you need at least an average of 3+ to blow the MPG, just incase you have to use Mara as a backup. You could almost garuntee success if you could pull the 3rd marker and move there, BUT, big problem, you can't pull it until the objective is flipped (which, if you started with You May Start Your Landing, you could pull it when needed). I'd also use Virtual Blizzard 1, since it is cheaper, even though it doesn't add 1 to the total. I also don't see you having enough weaponry/means to get that weaponry out to make Crossfire effective, and it doesn't work with lightsabers. I think you need to focus on what you want to accomplish with this deck. Do you want to make a full onslaught Walker deck with both ATs and STS? Or do you want to make a mains and toys walker deck? In any case, you need to remove something so you can up your destiny average. Why are the AAT's in here? They just don't belong in this deck, remove them and their weapon and put some more interrupts and effects in. J'Quille and Pote are odd choices for the deck as well. You need more varied strategy possibility. Ok, that's all for now.
