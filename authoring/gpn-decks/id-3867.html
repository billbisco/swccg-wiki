 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Basically I try to get all my effects out as fast as possible. Simultaneasly, I pull Jodo with audience chamber. I then try to get him, with Vader's Cape, armed and ready to capture the enemy however possible. The other bounty hunters are there to take on characteristics of Mandolarians. Once I get the Objective flipped, I feed everybody to the Sarlacc.

This Deck has really had next to no success the few times I played it. Please Help me. I have a small pool of cards to work with. Not much after Special edition and none after Reflections II.

   Shewski     Many valid points below my comments. I think that you need to get some better locations, I'd suggest keeping a few docking bays (pullable by Ket Maliss) and the sail Barge. If you use the JP Jabba, you can get some more activation by draining for 0 with aliens to activate 1.
   LordHoban     No offense, but how did this deck get a 3? He wants help, not pity. First thing, Feltipern Trevagg's Stun Rifle works best with Jodo... uncommon weapon from Special Edition (SE). It fires for free, is +2 with Jodo, and captures, but the Assault Rifle (which needs to be Virtual) is a good alternative as well. Next, it's KAST, not Cast. Jodo Kast. Take out Miiyoom onith. Your character selection is too weak as it is. Court decks usually require 15-20 characters to be effective. Otherwise, you won't be able to hold off against an LS mains deck, even with your weapons and Hidden weapons. No version of Boba Fett in it? Jet Packs aren't that great, you shouldn't use more than 1 in a deck, if that. I never knew Jabba's Sail Barge had a Bassanger Deck... You should also put this in Theme/Fun, then I might be able to be a little more lenient. The main purpose of a Court deck is either to battle, or capture. You've chosen the capture route, obviously, but you need IG-88 with gun. Using Hidden Weapons, you should also use a version of Fett. You need more interrupts to back you up. 4 Twileks? With only 4 effects in the deck? That's wasted space. Also, you're using all aliens, but NOT Scum and Villiany? Why is Dantooine in here? You need protection from Fallen Portal, so I recommend They're Still Coming Through... especially in this deck, that 1 interrupt, which is pretty common these days, will kill you. You don't have enough characters to survive losing to Fallen Portal. You might want to consider obtaining a Colo Claw Fish if you want to go the weapons route. Use the Virtual Greedo, not the normal one. Then use Oo-ta Goo-ta (v), would be good to start with Ket Maliss (v) or Power of the Hutt (which pulls the Sail Barge), to allow you to get key characters out of your deck. If you don't use Scum and Villiany, you gotta have Vader in the deck with his saber. 12 weapons in a deck, ack... way too many, and those antipersonal guns are too expensive. Double Laser Cannon might also be good, it is a destiny 7 vehicle weapon for the Sail Barge. 'An Entire Legion of my Best troops' Endor uncommon effect, would make your Assault Rifles (read the lore) and Blaster rifles destiny + 2 and deploy -1. It basically comes down to the fact that, you need more punch, no matter what direction you take it. Inconsequential Losses i've found doesn't work all that well since they only forfeit for 3 apiece (if they forfeited for thier destiny number, then I could see it being useful). I'd rather have 3 more characters in a deck, than 3 more weapons in most cases. Weapons may shoot and hit, but characters provide special game text, power, and ability, which is why there are only a few dedicated weapons decks that actually work. You can look at my Destroyer Court (under Theme/fun) for an example of a weapons deck, and that's mainly due to the Fish, Destroyer Droids, and Blaster Rack (v) to get my mains their weapon of choice. And that deck revolves around 19 characters, 15 unique characters of which are all useful in battle. Double Back was a suggestion listed earlier, which I'd recommend, as if Jodo dies, currently there's no real way to get him back. One last thing, how fast can you set up? I'm betting not very, and that is probably the biggest element that kills this deck. If you could get out all the characters you wanted with weapons, then yeah, I'd say you might stand half a chance, but I don't see that happening. You need some more tricks up your sleeve for a Capture deck to work. Which is why I tend to make battle my first priority in Court, with capturing being a bonus. You see, I know Court better than most people, since it's my favorate objective (I've worked a version of a deck with it since it came out, minus my time out of the game inbetween). And with the cards available now, as opposed to when it came out, it can be vicious in the right hands. Fast and brutal. You need to find your opponent's weakest link, and assault them there. You need to outlast whatever characters they throw at you. You need to consistently go to your opponent. You don't want them to come to you. At least, that's how I play, and that's how I've seen Court to be most effective. I've played capture Court, and it tends to be slow, and very demanding, strategy-wise (at least, if you build it right). If this way of playing doesn't sound too appealing to you, you might want to consider another decktype. I've talked long enough... so.. Look at my latest Jabba's Hit Squad deck below for suggestions and possibilities (just for insperation, mind you). Hope it helps, thanks for listening to my late night ramblings. http://www.gameplayersnetwork.com/card/decks.asp?gm=starwars&id;=3782
