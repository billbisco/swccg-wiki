Sim Aloo adds a destiny with the emperor

Stormtrooper Garison Adds a destiny with lord vader, emperor, tarkin, or even thrawn, and prevents them from being targeted by weapons

Moment of triumph adds a destiny with a lone alien, and 2 if tarkin(3if also emperors power)

Maul strikes adds 1 or two destinies if lord maul vs Qui-Gon 3 destinies +3

Vaders cape and darth vader add one to each destiny,

After her adds one to each destiny, and if u wipe out the opppnent you retrieve 4 force and put a destiny 5 back in the deck
Prepared defenses adds one to a destiny

Colo claw fish lets you cycle out the lowest destiny,or save a realy high weapon destiny,

and tarkin still lets you cancel an oponents destiny if with vader

with 3 sevens, if you didnt lose any(remember the phantom menace goes back if maul is lost)gives you reasonable odds of turning luke with only 2 on insignificant rebellion

if the emperor is lost you can usually get him back with imperial reinforcements

always take death star II docking bay on first turn with arrest order

I'd appreciate any comments or suggestions
