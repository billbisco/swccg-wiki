 The Strategy

Okay, I submitted this once already, and for some reason it didn't work, so I'm resubmitting it. I hope this strat section doesn't seem trite, because my last one was enormous and really well thought out.
Notes: I keep forgetting things. Sorry. :) Starting effects are Squadron Assignments, Insights, and Strike Planning.

General Strategy:
Keep doubles of high destinies and your recyclables in the deck at all times. Keep Mirax alive, and she will be your savior. Put Qui-gon w/ Saber and EPP Obi at the Forest, which can be gotten via Control/Tunnel Vision if necessary, and as long as you keep those high destinies stacked, you should be relatively fine. The new Luke + On The Edge = money. You use the On The Edge during your Activation or Control phase, and retrieve it with Luke as your next action, or your first action of your Control phase. It can really make some problems for opponents who have already pulled out all of their shields. Your drains in space at Kessel and Naboo will usually trump their drains on the ground (especially when you get your retrieval engine set up), so nuts to them. It Could Be Worse + It's A Hit for Podracing, if anyone still plays that. Save the Rebel Barrier for when they come down on one of your guys that looks weak (maybe Tantive with Corran Horn piloting or something), then next turn you release the hounds. When you're not retrieving towards he beginning, you can use the new Luke to pull your All Wings combo (hopefully they're not running Black 2. I know I wouldn't. :) which can get you another ship, so you don't have to wait to use Squadron Assignments again. The Supply Tanks are there because they add a single power and a single maneuver to every ship in the deck. I don't see why anyone wouldn't add them in a space deck. Throw them on the Tantive or Spiral, and you're a beast. Retrieve your Control/Tunnel Visions early on, using the new Luke as well, because they are really helpful to this deck. It's an engine that is really hard to stop, since you should keep Luke and Wedge together, cancelling destinies and Darklighter Spinning for destinies of around 6-8 usually, with A Few Maneuvers. System pulling goes Endor (they get no activtation, which is always a good thing), Chandrila, and then Tibrin usually. This allows you to go get a lost system, if you top deck one (it's not supposed to happen, but it MIGHT still happen).

Problems this deck will encounter:
Decks that can stop you from cancelling their drains, or decks that cancel your drains more than you cancel theirs. So this comes down to Senate, MKOS, and DS Dueling, basically. DS Dueling is virtually non-existent in open environments because LS Dueling is so much better. MKOS has made a big come back, and it will be a really hard deck to beat. Set up Menace Fades and hopefully Battle Plan shield, and you should be able to outdrain them, eventually. Senate will be everyone's toughest match up again, I think. You should be able to drain more than they do though, since they can only usually get rid of one a turn, and you can still cancel their drains. You can easily beat them up in space unless they're playing Senate TIEs, and the grabber you start with will help out against that version. Don't be afraid to pull Battle Plan early on! I can't stress that enough. MKOS may have a tough time finding a big enough space package to take you on at both places, or even at one place, effectively, so make sure you slow their drains down so that those don't cut you up too quickly. Hopefully you can set up Menace Fades on them once you throw down to the Forest, as well as Battle Plan. Legendary Starfighter is a given. Also, as a little note, if you want to go get a card out of lost pile with Tibrin, and you haven't used Luke yet, put a high destiny down and retrieve it. It's just one of those little things you can do that will help you win the game.

Hopefully nothing is out of order for this, my first submission, since I changed the deck from my first pseudo-posting.

   HuntaWarya     This is pretty close to the VSC light deck I posted. Similar theme, and several common card choices. Here's a few things that I think might help: Use at least one more system. I can't overstress how important it is for HB flip to use more than 5 BG systems. It speeds you up, makes you harder to probe, and it means you might not have to pitch 3 cards to tibrin if you lose a system off the top. Also, if you're using strike planning, then it would make more sense to use the mini-falcon, instead of playing scoundrel lando (who will VERY rarely team up with any of your epps to attempt a beatdown) You're missing evasion interrupts in space. Just because you're playing HB doesn't mean your ships are invincible. One barrier won't protect you for long. No Alternatives to Fighting? No Hyper Escapes? No Houjix??? (Houjix is a card you desperately need. could save you in space or on the ground) Other problems: you have one spy and no transmission terminated, so visage will eat you alive. (obviously Honor won't last long without No Escape coming down) Using only farmboy luke will give you big problems against AOBS or BHBM. You are over-reliant on him with this deck, and you'll be in trouble since neither of those decks will let you use luke in space
