 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Use the objective, Pote snitkin, Scum & villainy and Jabba T Hut to give the skrillings some crazy advantages.

They deploy -1, are forfeit 4(or 6)and power 4. Retrieve 2 force when i initiate battle...
Battle destinies are +2...GR8!! Retrieve a skrilling every turn(and thus a destiny 4).

The podrace is here to do some early damage and to retrieve some force when i have a slow start(or a bad one). I should be able to win the race after 4 rounds. Since the opponent doesn't draw race destiny first turn, it'll be hard to come back.

The disruptor pistols should be targetting opponents characters with weapons first because the skrillings still have low defense value. Sail barge is to cross Desert hart if necessary.

Power of the hutt gets 3 destiny 1's out of my deck and is supposed to count as duplicates for those characters(to pull them faster).

Abyssin ornament is extra retrieval and destiny 5 is better than 4 so...

Guess that's about it.

ENJOY!!
