   icebreath     As stated before, Ralltiir Operations would be a good objective to use with this deck. Throw in some copies of High-speed Tactics to add battle destinies with the bikes, swap the Blaster Rifles for some Assault Rifle(V)'s... You may also want to try throwing in Blizard 2 (V) and a copy or two of Walker Barrage (V) or Trample. That'd give you some more ground power, and a chance to pound away at some of those annoying LS characters. Take a look at my RalOps Speeders deck if you want some more ideas, and feel free to ask me any questions about it.
