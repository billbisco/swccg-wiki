 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Starting:
Carbon Chamber Testing
[deploy carbonite chamber, carbonite chamber console, and security tower with a rebel (opponent's choice) from opponent's reserve deck (if possible) imprisoned there.]
Each turn deploy one of the sites allowed by CCT from reserve deck for free. Get a bounty hunter out on the first turn to escort the rebel to the carbonite chamber. Use the 1 force from Carbon Chamber Console to bring a Carbon Freezing into play. Use 1 force on next turn to take 1 Prepare the Chamber into hand and keep using this ability to get both Ugnaughts at carbon chamber also. Then move the bounty hunter towards the carbonite chamber. When he reaches it attempt to freeze the character. If successful begin to move towards the East Platform.(keep playing other characters and ships to back him up) When he gets to the East Platform transit to Docking Bay 94. From there head to Audience Chamber and flip CCT. From there you should be able to kick some pretty major ass by being immune to attrition <4 with most things in the deck and also being able to retrieve force each turn. I realize that Vader with lighsaber has no place inthis deck but when theopponent starts to completely overpower me its nice to mow a few characters down. Also questionable is the star destroyers. I use them because the starfighters that belong to the bounty hunters aren't powerful enough to go up against someone with a few decent light side ships. Overall it works extremely well against anyone I play against.(tha isn't a whole lot of people because in my high school of a couple thousand students only about eight play star wars)


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     Good comments below. Be sure to use Jabba's Prize. Why, you ask? Well, you can knock out all personas of Han (including HCF) while you may get a wimpy rebel like Corran Horn otherwise. Also you need to use DS Lando, for the same reason as using the PRize. No Angry Lando, as long as you use him in space or in a vehicle. Good luck!
   Darth Hoth     I agree with Gaffer Gamgee, you need better Bounty Hunters (like the V version of Dengar and IG-88). I would drop the Vader, Maul, Blizzard 1 and the Star Destroyers and turn this into a Scum deck. I don't like that many multiples of weapons, drop some. You can start Blaster Rack (V), lets you deploy a "matching weapon" on a character you just deployed. Drop the starship weapons. You have Mandalorian Armor in here, but no Hidden Weapons? That's a must with Mandalorian Armor. I don't like the Freezing aspect of the deck, and I think it would be better to just use Jabba's Prize, but it's your call.
   Gaffer Gamgee     At first glance, you need to use some better bounty hunters. This doesn't mean you need to buy more cards (although a Boba Fett, Bounty Hunter is a choice addition). Rather, go to www.swccgpc.com and check out the fifth Virtual Card set. There are some tasty new game texts for some of your bounty hunters. Drop some green cards. You don't need that many weapons. The ion cannons are wimpy. You need some more burly interrupts. Look into Jabba's Through With You (to take advantage of weapons), Hidden Weapons, Release Your Anger, End This Destructive Conflict, Imperial Barrier, and Defensive Fire (V) among others. Have fun.
