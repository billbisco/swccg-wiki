 The Description
A Quite Mining Colony deck. Mainly the goal is to keep using the Epp with sabers over and over

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Turn One-Use one force get 4 Cloudcity sites, because of Insurrection, Keeping The Empire Out Forever and Aim High. Deploy Leia if Path of Least Resistance and Leia in hand.

Turn 2- You should be Activating like 10+ force this turn. Get another site and if opponent has no characters on cloudcity, get objective flipped.

Turn 3- if the obj. hasn't been flipped yet get the last site and do so. Once there is enough space to hold Bespin Deploy Cloudcity Celebration.

turn 4- If celebration in play, all couldcity sites in play and you occupy, retrieve 7 force and possibly drain your opponent for 11 force from Cloudcity locations. Also Ultimatum conditions are not hard to meet, only requires one more site than the Celebration.

Card Choices

Talon Karrde-since 5 of the characters in the deck are smugglers with defense values of 3 or less Karrde will increase that defense values. With the objective flipped, if Lando With Blaster Riffle, Dash Rendar, and Karrde if at the same site they are difficult to remove.

Sorry About The Mess & Blaster Profiency- 4 Characters with blasters helps them get rid of more of the opponents characters.
