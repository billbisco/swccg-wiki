i think we all have an idea of how this deck plays, so i'll go through specific card choices

non V maneuvering flaps
with luke and garrison i dont think the V maneuvering flaps is needed and the non-V adds to power, forfeit and maneuver of your speeders.

EBO and Haven
i know death squadron cancels haven, but holding Hoth is the key to keeping the retreival going, so being able to deploy cheeper, adding a destiny, +3 to power and +2 to drains i think is worth the two card slots

Ion Cannon
can be pulled from reserve with rieekan and can help hold the system

non combo Insurrection
quite simply adds more to the forfeit of my speeder pilots, if DS retreival is a problem then i'll use a sheild

Attack Pattern Delta V
can be pulled from reserve with Nick of Time and having a 7 for battle destiny should be enough to knock out those pesky AT-ATs

Landing Claw
stops DS controlling Hoth system if they become too powerful to beat off

leia, lando, quiggy and obi
use for hit and run tactics against any easy targets

Two medical droids
makes it easier to cycle the mains for those hit and run beatdowns

Red 6 and Artoo in Red 5
high destiny and i have other ability 4 pilots which can pilot them

Boshek, Melas and Corran Horn
to use with above and the other starships

Dadonna
pulls navander from reserve so that you can use T-47 battle formation and pulls the admirals order so that you can cycle your starfighters to the docking bay when lost from the system

i've not had a chance to play this deck yet so i'm not sure how well it would do, i think this deck type will have problems because i think it is too card intensive, with all the cards that can be pulled from reserve deck you could run out of cards very quickly if the dark side gets any good drains going

build it, try it, let me know how you get on
