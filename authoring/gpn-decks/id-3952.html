 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The strategy is to Play Hidden base, and use Rendzevous Point to pull Kessel at the start of the game. Heading For the Medical Frigate allows you to pull Haven and Squadron Assignments. The Signal Allows you to pull Kessel Run. Rebel Fleet is used to help take away from a force drain. Once the Falcon is out deploy Han and Chewie onboard to make the kessel run. Wedge teams up with Hobbie and Red Leader to cancel a battle destiny, while the intruder missles played on one of the B-Wings takes out an Imperial Cap ship, which is first targeted by another weapon system. This adds 4 or 5 to the destiny draw depending on who fired it. Intruder missles also have the honor of boasting 7's for destinys. Luke, Leia, and Qui-Gonn help on the ground defense and Chewbacca can always be used to help too. Squadron Assignments helps because it allows you to pull the matching pilot or ship for your hand.

Note: I do not own any of the EPP cards which is why I don't have them in the deck.
