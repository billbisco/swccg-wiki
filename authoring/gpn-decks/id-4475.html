 The Description
It’s 11:00 PM, and I’m trying to close my eyes. Han and Leia won’t be quiet in the Guest Bedroom. Luke and Obi think it’s funny to spar at midnight in the Lower Corridor. Dash, Lando, and Figrin D’an are running the slots. Chewie’s busy protecting at the Downtown Plaza, waiting with open channels on the Wrist Comlink. Ackbar has everybody else running military maneuvers in orbit with his Home One. Doesn’t anyone sleep around here?

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I’ve posted this deck once before, but it’s been awhile and I’ve taken heed to some strategy.

I’ve noticed that in a QMC deck you spread thin and then duck n’ cover as you go. Interrupts such as Path Of Least Resistance and Keep Your Eyes Open can do that for me. I don’t use interior sites enough to use Lift Tubes. I debated on whether to use Dodge in this deck, and it is still being considered for final draft. If I don’t want to run, I’ll just ring up some of my buddies with a Comlink.

It has been suggested that I play Strike Planning with Gen. Calrissian and Gen. Solo. To tell you the truth, I don’t like it. It took away from my fun element of Sabacc because of CC Lando’s GT, and I like CC Han’s GT better than General Solo’s. That’s just me I guess…

The key to this deck is activation. Within five turns I can be activating up to 10+, which will come in handy for reacting and high deployment for the Home One. I also debated It Could Be Worse for this deck, but decided on better effects with higher destiny draws. May change that as well…

About midgame I ditch YISYW for Wokling (v) or Squadron Assignments, whichever is necessary in the moment. If need be, I can then ditch Wokling (v) for Launching The Assault, CC Celebration, Lightsaber Proficiency, or Reflection. I also try to spread back out if I think I have handled the beatdowns. This way I can retrieve unmercifully with CC Celebration. I also start draining pretty heavy to get my opponent to come and get me. Sometimes they get me, and sometimes they don’t!

I can pull what I want with SA and SKF all day long, and if I flip quickly I can pull from my force pile once per turn. Cards such as A Jedi’s Resilience, Keep Your Eyes Open, and Wrist Comlinks (thanks AdmiralThrawn36!) are just to try out some new tactics. They are also high destiny!

Everything else is self explanatory. I appreciate your ratings and any advice you can give me. I think I know what changes I want to make (I also think I know what changes YOU want to make), but I have no idea how to make them; I really don’t know what to cut out of this deck. I like it all! Thank you for your time!


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     Why not start with the Guest Quarters? Almost every other QMC deck I see uses that for the early deploy bonus. Exterior sites= bad news. With IO running around, all your exterior sites are prime beatdown areas. I'd stay away from them, and it would be safer to ditch the sabacc retrieval and focus on Celebrating. I'd run 2 in case you get it cancelled, ditch reflection. A little more red could not hurt. I think you should take a little bit of advantage of the new EPP Classic rules, and save some space by playing a few of those. Since you like CC Lando, consider using Lando's Not A System from R3 for some battling fun.
   swcironecg     Your goal should be to flip ASAP. When I play QMC, I flip fast, then don't draw, saving up force and using KTEOF to get all the cards that I need. With the saved up force I can drop beatdowns or counter-attacks on Cloud City. I would take out some characters, you only need about 14-16. That is the number I find reliably gets you one or two characters in your starting hand. Drawing your force from turn 1 activation, and using Squadron Assignments to get out a good ship at Bespin (add Mirax and the Pular Skate, good for tracking, draws a battle destiny, and only deploy for 3 force once you flip), I can usually flip on turn two or three. Then it is just a matter of pulling evasion cards while you drain and retrieve on CC. Add Pucumir Thryss if you have him (adds 1 to your drains on CC), and get him if you don't. Characters that you could drop would be the droids and some of the smugglers. Acronas are good for CC, since they can react away all by themselves, but watch out for Stop Motion (V). Use Luke (V) instead of LSJK and add All Wings Report In & Darklighter Spin OR drop the Red 5 (V), R2 (V), and add Wedge RSL, Red Squadron 1 and an X-wing Laser Cannon (to take care of Zuckuss). You probably only need 3-4 sites, so you could drop some of those. I like the Wrist Comlink idea, you could expand on it by playing lots of mains and adding all the team interrupts (Life Debt, Skywalkers, etc.), reacting characters together so that you could play an interrupt and draw 3 battle destiny! I hope this review isn't too long, and I hope that it helps. Good deck.
