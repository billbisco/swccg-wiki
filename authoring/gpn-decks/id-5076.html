 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Star with Rebel Planers, Strike Planning, get to your ships or squadran assignments
and play with an unusal amount of fear.
Hoth Main powergenerator and pll 4th marker if no hoth site stared.

Pull Ranken and Dodona with strike planning And on your turn pull hoth db play Raiken to pull a new sceret base and pull the two outher hoth locations or pull 1 & Ebo. Pull systems form under your sheild's to drop ship's to them and use the epp jedi to hit and run on the ground.

First with rebel Planers virtual I don't have to put systems in my deck that oppens up 3-5 card slots in most decks. also allowed me to drop some of the pullers that I was using to get systms.

Hopping mad (V)is fun to play with and is intresting to try and pull off.

Princess Leia (V)Replaced EPP Leia most of the same skill set but there are outher uses of her game text that are cool in space or ground a good fit in this deck.

All of the spoilers for these cards are on the players Commite Web site in the forums area.

There are some deck that will give this deck problems a true speed deal deck will slow it down and walkers will defently give you fit's if walkers is big in your area thou you could tech it out for them tho.

Well enjoy and please give advice on how you think I might be able to improve this deck idea.

thanks

   Black_wraith     i see youve got the bacta tank, ever think of running docing and repair facilities so you can save pilots and their shpips at the same time? I also like the ideas of rayc and the kessel runs.
   Shewski     I agree this is a neat way to run EBO, but I'm not sure if the reliance on having all your systems under your SE is the way to go. If you had ANSB and RP (V), you'd get 2 a turn, 3 with Lock (V) if you split some between your SE and your deck. I'd use Rayc (V) since he is a great pilot and you can use him to get some retrieval with him pulling a Kessel Run. The 3 PO stuff is neat, but doesn't seem to be gamebreaking. I'd include Strikeforce so your drains can't be stopped. I'd run some hyper escapes, hojix combos, and the like to get some staying power. Finally, I'd remove Leia's V gun and use her SE gun, since it makes things lost, including vehicles.
