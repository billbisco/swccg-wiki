there are almost to many red cards! destiny is rather hi in the deck, and there are so many destiny adders its crazy!

its a rather simple deck and your activation is crazy!

turn 1 pull up the docking bay and the farm, put the docking bay betwean the junkyard and the outskirts, use wokling V to pull secure route, draw.

you need to get some one to the docking bay and deploy secure route there, so you can get your majer drains started early! deploy any warrior to the junkyard, my self i like leia there alone with the saber, light saber pro, meditation and warriors courage! wich is all pull able. she will have three battle destiny and she will make some one forfit zero! i must be allowed to speek is very good with this deck becouse i activate so much force and then i git to look through my used pile and dig stuff up!

just play your game and get those drains going fast. if they come to you then clear the site with sabers and as many destiny adders as you can! if they are playing Carbon freeze then pull han with chewbaca V and lose him to a drain or something, if you free han then you will get him back for free any way.
