 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First off, there aren’t any specific locations as to where characters and/or starships should be deployed. The Emperor should just sit at the Imperial Square. Him and Xizor can move around, but keep them together for the attrition bonuses. Remember, Power 9 and some immunity to attrition is a terrible thing to waste. Give the Naboo Blaster Rifle to Djas Puhr, the Assault Rifle to Jodo Kast, and use the Blaster Rack to exchange them. Lastly, your best effect in the deck is Scum And Villainy. Luckily with your objective out, only The Emperor will cancel it, and he’s not that important in your deck. Just loose him, and then retrieve him with the 2 force retrieval.
