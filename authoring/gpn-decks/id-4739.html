 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First Turn: Against Deal, play don?t Do That Again shield. Pull Rieekan. Activate. Deploy Rieekan to the Throne Room, he pulls the Hoth War Room. You should have a site or site-puller in hand, so put it down or use it for another site. A Tragedy Has Occurred should come out. Second Turn: Activate, Rieekan pulls Navander, Navander pulls Endor, where opponent doesn?t generate?unless EOps on table ; ). After that, track for On The Edge and beatdowns. Grab all his interrupts. Signal for all your cool effects. After your characters have dwindled when you reach the later game, drop Garbage (V) and do some more smacking. I?ve come to love Monnok/Grimtaash. In case you don?t know what the trackable interrupts are, they are Escape Pod (V), The Signal, Wesa Gotta Grand Army, and Bith Shuffle combo. Matchups:

CCT: Smack the escorter of Jabba?s Prize. And everyone else, too.

Hunt Down: Revo the Meditation chamber, if he has no bgs (unlikely, but it could happen), you can deploy there (since Holotheatre is immune to Revo, right? If it?s not, you can Revo that) and move to the Holotheatre to drain, unless that?s the site that drains ?1. I?m so confused. Anyways, Revo SOMETHING so you can drain. If Holo drains ?1 AND is immune to Revo, then Revo the Med Chamber to deploy there, then Revo back to drain. Anyways, smack Vader.

ROps: Invade Ralltiir. Revo Ralltiir. Revo the Forest for some immunity of your own!

ISB: Revo the Coruscant site and raid it. If it?s Coruscant system, Revo that, too, and just choke him.

EOps: Assuming this is a D2 deck. Revo the bunker AFTER Moff Jerjerrod shows up there, then beat him down.

BHBM: Revo the D2 db, and invade the Throne Room.

AOBS: Revo Coruscant system and choke him.

Watto: Totally fight at Mos Espa, fighting at the junkyard isn?t much use.

Senate: INVADE THE SENATE! Too bad it isn?t the U.S. Senate, but anyways, use destiny adders to wipe out the Senators.

Combat: Kill the DJs.

TDIGWATT: Revo a couple CC sites, and just fight him off CC.

COTVG: Revo the AC, if you can turn 1, so he doesn?t get that alien in. Dungeon also makes a good Revo. It shouldn?t be too hard to kill BHers.

IO(V): Revo his sites, so his limited activation becomes even worse.

SYCFA: Lose, I reckon. In case you?re thinking of saying this isn?t a good matchup strategy?you?re right. But who plays SYCFA, anyway? >:-P

   Shewski     I am concerned about you plan of letting Rieekan sit at the Throne Room. That sounds like you are asking for a beating. I'm not a fan of the Strike Planning start. I like using naked 3po (pulled by Were you Looking) to cycle through and getting your twixes out. Plus that would help set up OTE draws. (where is that in your decklist?) Since you are using some Tunnel Visions, I'd recommend using Yoda MOTF and Control & Tunnel Vision instead. I'd use Rendezvous Pt as one of your twixes, maybe instead of Endor since you have no space. Character selection is your main crew, but I recommend using Corran to break UC spies. Buddy interrupts abound, but if you use 3po you can run fewer (and run some space) and still track your high destinies. Run another Goo to lock down quicker. Still a good effort overall!
