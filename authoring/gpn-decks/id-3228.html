 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Dressilians will help you flip the objective. The squadrons, weapons, troopers, and mains are for beat down. Base yourself on Dantooine where if you can force drain, and if opponent gets card out the deploy +2 for imps should keep him from getting lots of cards out, and if he does you can take him out. I really don't have more than that.

   tyranus     This is an objective I've used a lot. The wookies sre good, maybe another one or two of them. I'd change Chewie w/ gun for V-Chewi (pull the wookies). Instead of the troopers, I've alway found scouts to work well with DBO (put Strike planning as your third effect you start with). Madine pulls the scouts, and some of them are real useful (Kensaric adds 2 to the deploy cost of anything to his site when with another scout). I'd probably swap han and leia for Luke w/ and Obe w/ (Just my pref). I'd swap the B-wing squads for more xwing squads too (3 cheaper makes alot of difference. I wouldn't use a docking bay myself (you can do without Dark jedi and Garrisons just taking the bus into your base). I like the whole 'fire weapons, add a destiny, forfeit useded for 3' thing. A cool deck. Hope you stick with it and keep tweaking for a while. Its a versatile deck, open to a few strategys (speeders work). I'll always reveiw any DBO deck, I rate it highly. Nice one Spideyguy.
