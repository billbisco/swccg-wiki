 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Start the aforementioned stuff, and initiate a race. Pull your hand, and play any sites you have. Try to get a dejarik to the holotable (most people wil either forget about it as you drain away, or will not know the dejarik rules). Kill Luke and Obi-Wan with duels, and drain away. Always remember visage, even if you dont really want to lose the force, it still is one force for your opponent too. TRACK!! I cant stress this enough, ive seen people (more experienced people) botch the game by forgetting to track. Try to set up all the 5s and 6s that are in there, and dueling, battling, racing, and pretty much everything else will be a piece of cake.

Card Choices:
Vaders: Monnok protec.
Boba Fett: May not fit with this deck ,but he is damn powerful and really helpful.
Double Saber vs Single: If you need me to answer this, then you need serious help.
Aurra's Blaster: Not a bad weapon, destiny 7.
Ships: Basically the best perm pilot ships.
Imperial Holotable: Extra drain most people are reluctant to send saomething there.
