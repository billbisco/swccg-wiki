 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Pull CC docking bay, Lando, and JP: Audience Chamber. Put JP on Lando, or another guy, if I can play him. If not I'll just put him on Lando and leave him at the prison. I'd save force to play another warrior to escort JP. Then move JP to CC:DB.

Pull Tatooine: DB 94, and move JP there. Also play Boba Fett to AC, and also move him to T:DB 94 for backup.

Move both Boba and JP to Audience Chamber to flip CCT.

After flipping I guess just pound away with force drains and battles, all the while me retrieving a Force every control phase.

By the way, Bubo prevents opponent's character's from moving from his site if at a JP site.

Darth: What are the odds of a guy playing Lando on the first turn, AND my not having another warrior.

I don't have any of the cards you mentioned. Because I am now into the Lord of the Rings TCG, I won't be getting any more SWCCG cards, except perhaps Bring Him Before Me, DSII: Throne Room, and Mara Jade, TEH.

I'm trying to do the best I can with the cards that I have, and if a two is all I get, so be it.

   Shewski     This has the potential to improve, you just need a post flip strategy. This doesn't look like CCT, since you aren't trying to freeze anyone! =) That being said, this may work better in another objective, like Corran mentioned. Try to get EPP bounty hunters, as it stands now they are tons better than the normal ones. Destiny seems incredibly low. You have too many locations- I would ditch all those you can't pull, and add in some pullable ones, like the Flagship Bridge and even Jabba's Sail Barge and Passenger deck. BTW if planning on using Bubo, use Krayt Bones (V) to strengthen him and pull him from under your starting effect.
   Darth Hoth     it's always been my dream to build a good CCT deck, but it's not gonna happen soon. With this in mind, I give a 3 to what I would normally give a 2 to. Who are you using to transport JP? I'm assuming Lando, since he's the only one you've ensured yourself of getting. That's a bad choice. The LS just replaces Lando and then Han goes free. I would start using Any Methods Necessary, get 4-LOM w/gun or IG w/gun (add him in), and Jabba's Palace: Dungeon. Then you deploy them, and they can't be battled, so Han is safe. I would try to turn this into a Scum deck, it would be much better that way. That would mean dropping the Imps and Imp starships. Use Ket Maliss (V), and the cards that he pulls, they're surprisingly good, and use Rodian (V). Address these problems, and I'll go a little more indepth.
   Corran Antillies     I give this deck about a 2.5, but only because I don't know much about cct. Take out Bubo, unless he has some secret power that I don't know. isn't he the one who doesn't attack your characters? If you want to use a creature use a tough one like rancor or sarlacc. I don't even think creatures are necessary at all, unless you have a specific reason to use them. I like Myo, but I don't know if he's the best character to use in this case. Try to start all tied up if you can. JP dungean is always nice if u can use it. You definately need more interupts. 2 just isn't enough. If you don't know what else to put in, at least put in multiple copies of the ones you have. Twileks are always good. Add Blaster Rack (V), because you have so many unique weapons. Can u pull your systems. I like to be able to pull all my locations if at all possible. I can't see too much more wrong with it. It looks pretty good overall.
