 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The strategy isn't really that hard to figure out so I'm not going to make a lengthy section for it.

CARD CHOICES
Executor DB-Deal decks always give away alot more force than is healthy, and this helps a little. DB transit is free with Brangus.

Bad Feeling Have I- what can I say? the cards just good, especially in Dark Deal where it helps make up for all the force you give away.

2 Dark Deals-in case one gets canceled, or lost from reserce accidentally.

Ghhk combo-get yourself out of a really big mess.

Bane Malar-alien(for flip side), immune to Goo Nee Tay, draws destiny by himself, and his gametext. but mostly his gametext.

Brangus Glee-draws destiny by himself, immune to goo nee tay, alien, DB transit is free. also he is a character who is not destiny 1(which is mostly all that is in this deck).

Captain Sarkli-i dont know why I have to give a reason for using him, hes a pimp, but everybody always asks. he stops activation at opponent's non-BG locations.

Lathe-hes an alien. this guy is HUGE against good players(aka ppl who track), b/c he almost completely shuts down tracking. otherwise he sucks though.

4 obsidian TIEs-well they're cheap, great with Talon roll, but most of all TMNALTC lets me take them into hand instead of lost pile.

Sebulbas racer-with so many BGs in the deck, this stops nearly all loss from the race.

If you have any questions AIM admiralthrawnMT. Thanks!
