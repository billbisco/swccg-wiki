 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
same strategy, free han, then battle, drain, and flip side objective until they are depleted.

with a lack of han (V) this deck is a lot weaker than it could be, so i've tried to get around that through talon karrde and the EPP han.

also dont have rycar (v), but qui-gon can retrieve his own lightsaber, and obi should have some staying power with the old bens.

space is a lot lighter now that i have removed the tat celebration, and the droids and podracing have also been removed, taking away some retrieval.

adding qui-gon, R3 version gives some combat protection, but i have his tat lightsaber for drains and such.

   Shewski     Looking better, but you've created some new problems. You know, even if you don't have cards you can still type what you'd like to put in (and then actually play what you have). Get Han (V) if you want to do Profit, they are made for each other. You can't start HotJ, it isn't immune to Alter when you deploy it. Why are you playing Insurrection? You have no DBs to pull. Massa Luke should make an appearance in here as well. I'd say you'd be safe with only one spare Han. Use TAT Qui, unless combat is big in your area. Talon Karrde is a strange choice. For space, I'd not go with matching pilots, and you might want to leave it altogther. Tat outskirts are good, but you have no way of getting it out of reserve. You activation stinks- you won't last one 5 force a turn for very long. I felt your previous version was a 2.5, and went high- this one is much the same, but to balance it out I am going low.
