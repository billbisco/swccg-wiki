 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The idea behind the deck is to deploy the Vader, Maul, and Mara Jade to locations where the opponent has a small force. Hopefully they aren't play Insurrection to get good use from Elis Helrot.

Basically one should be drawing 2 to 3 battle destinies at a single site.

The Average Destiny of the deck is kind of low, but there are things in the deck to modify the lightsaber weapon destiny draws, Darth Maul, Young Apprentice and Sniper & Dark Strike as well as lowering there defense value with You Cannot Hide Forever & Mobilization Points.

Turn One-Deploy the Emperor from reserve deck even if one is in hand. If Emperor's Power was in Reserve Deck deploy it with the game text of the Throne Room and deploy Death Star 2: Docking Bay.

Turn 2-Activate at least 5 force and deploy another docking bay. Coursant: Docking Bay Preferably. Save Force Unless no dark jedi in hand then draw. Deploy Darth Sidious to the Death Star 2: docking bay if in hand and you have enough force.

Turn 3-Activate at least 6, 8 if Sidious also in play at docking bay. Play Endor: landing platfrom if possible and Vader and saber to there. Draw any remaining force.

Turn 4-Force Drain at the Landing Platform. Play Maul to Coursant: Docking Bay (Special Edition) with his saber. Play Tatooine: Docking Bay 94.

Turn 5-Play Mara Jade and her saber to Docking Bay 94. Use Elis Helrot if opponent deploys too many characters to your site.
