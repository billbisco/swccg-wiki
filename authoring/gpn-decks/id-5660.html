 The Description
Either the opponent comes down to face you or they take huge drains… This is a versatile deck that requires you disrupting the opponent while keeping your eyes on Naboo… Retrieval from racing helps keep that differential up -

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Strategy:

First action – Take I did it into hand (no need to have a floating 0 if you can have it)

Don’t forget to start race on opponents first control phase and remember to race each of your opponent’s control phases…

Turn 1 – Get Jedi down to naboo 3/2 site (preferably Qui-Gon as he is the best combat man out there) w/ matching weapon, if you feel like it draw…

Turn 2 – Yoda down to 2/2 site, draw…

Deploy Graxol Kelvyyn as soon as you get him to set up retrieval every turn…

From her you are going to have to play very reactive… Keep your jedi on naboo backed up with evasion, and don’t deploy more than 1 Combat card to each jedi (if you need to you can put one down on your opponent’s deploy phase… if they are playing the anti-combat shield, don’t deploy any combat cards until they deploy down to you)… remember you can pull artoo in r5 with all wings combo in a pinch for a combat card…

Pick and choose your battles in space and on the ground… Do not go after their dark jedi that aren’t on Naboo, as they cannot drain with them… Finish the race when you feel you have lost enough force, but save up 6 force just in case they don’t pop the shield (which is good for you because they lose 6)… As soon as you finish the race, back up Graxol, as he will be a sitting duck… Deploy I did it, pitch BEP whenever you need the 4 force…

Space in this deck is best suited to Malastare as the gametext favors you heavily since you won a podrace… this will keep you from having to pay for drains, set up menace fades, and keep you retrieving and messing with your opponents force pile… Your space will usually consist of wedge in ship and corran horn and dash in the outrider… save HC&F; for if you need it… as an emergency, luke can come down with artoo in r5…

You can get the extra retrieval from On the edge, you can use flash to make sure it’s big, and then get the OTE right back with flash…

Also Edge works great with threepio… if you have a bad destiny on top, switch it…:D You can also take down star destroyers with an intruder missile on the flacon as long as you’ve got that 7 showing…;)

I think this deck’s toughest match ups are against DS LSC and Walkers, so I’ll outline the matchups…

Walkers:
Best bet here is an early Corran and Lando to the 5th marker ASAP… Kill their walker and keep the drains on Naboo… You can handle the generators getting blown up, but if you can postpone it, it’ll help… after that, just hold a sense for Walker Garrison, because these hurt… You can probably take out their space, as it’s usually pretty weak… If you just keep battling on the ground, you should be okay… If you fear walkers in your area, you might want to run a chewie protector to stop general veers from drawing multiple destinies… Your race retrieval will really help in this match up… and I did it keeping them from saving up force will help too… All in all, it’s a winnable game if you keep the pressure up and stop garrision…

LSC:
This game is rough… They will be packing high destiny too… and have combat cards too… your best bet is keeping your jedi for naboo and going to space and the podrace arena after the race… You really need to keep qui-gon alive as the +1 bonus from his saber and additional force loss will help you… Strike blocked will help you in this game as well… It will basically come down to keeping your 7s and 6s for combat cards, or if they are using ability x3 (V) then you will have to track, hope they don’t track, or get really, really lucky… Retrieval helps a bunch...

This deck wins by large margins, because you don't have to commit a lot to the table and you retrieve bunches... good for tournament play... In my last tourney went (2-0) +50something, beating deal (slow) and walkers... both by over 20...
