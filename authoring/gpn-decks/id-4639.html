 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Dueling: Drop Vader, track some destinies, duel. Rinse, repeat.
Draining, etc: Palpy and his entourage will move on through the docking bay system, draining and battling wherever needed. Kir Kanos and Myn Kyneugh with sticks are pretty nasty with the tracking you'll be doing.
Tracking: Play MM to grab a Visage, play Operational to drop the Visage on top of MM. You've now played 3 cards onto your used pile; a 6, a 7, and a 5. Add insult to injury by playing Omni Box combo on top of that (shuffle their Reserve to screw up THEIR tracking), tracking another 5. Rinse, repeat.

The rest of your stuff is just your basic Classic-format beats. Play smart, time everything right, battle like hell. It's a simple design that does a LOT of damage with battles, duels, and a bit of drains.

***UPDATE***
Response to reviews:
slim1256 - Regarding bounty hunters & ships: these are beat-and-forfeit cards, not hold-a-location cards. They drop, take out some characters and cause some overflow, then forfeit away.
Regarding BFHI(V): if I had room to start it, I would consider it. HOWEVER, since I don't have the room in starting (no, I'm not pulling IAO, Mob Points, or Crush. Period. End of discussion), I guess I'll just have to be content with what I've got in, eh?
Regarding Premiere Vader: Try telling me he's not good when I drop him to Jedi Knight Luke's site, battle, play I Have You Now, and add 1 to each of my destiny draws (which will be a 5, 7, and 6). That's three more overflow... which, quite honestly, could be the difference between a win or a loss. I could toss him and the EPP Vaders out in favor of another Lord Vader and another Choke Vader or two... but he's useful, he's unexpected, and he can win a game if I need him to.
Regarding Evader: I'm not really planning on using the Used text on Evader, but rather the Lost text (getting a just-lost Vader back into my deck so I can play him again). Also, Mr. Play-high-destiny-interrupts, the original Evader is a destiny 4 that I can choose not to pull with Crush if I'm REALLY desparate enough to forgo my tracking and try to blind draw for a dueling destiny. So there.
Regarding Mara's Stick: yes, she can pull her own stick. Which is why I'm not including 3 copies of it. Mara is, and always will be a beatdown character... it doesn't work quite as well if the saber ain't swingin'.
Regarding Palpy & Crew: yes, I suppose I could've worded that a bit better. Pardon me for writing deck strat at 2 in the morning and getting a couple of words off... I'm surprised I did as well as I have with it...

Shewski - Congrats, man!
As for the Empy/Palpy crisis... I don't own a Palpy, so I had to play with what I've got. Oh well. >;)

   slim1256     Good deck, but several changes could be made to make this deck leaner and more efficient. Bad Feeling Have I (V) was made for this type of deck... so use it! I appreciate the Grimtaash protection that using all of Vader's personas provides, but original recipe Vader isn't that useful (though I realize that you may have him in there since you don't have any more of any of the others... but Bad Feeling Have I (V) will eliminate the need for at least one of those Vaders, and that'd be the one to pull). BFHI(V) would also allow you to reduce the dueling interrupts to one and one. I would change at least one of the Evaders to Evader & Monnok; it’s just TOO useful against so many decks, and it can be a game breaker against decks that play multiples of the same personas. I would take out BFiS1, since you’ve got EPP Fett in there (Same for DiPO and EPP Dengar). Space in this deck is meant to disrupt, not go toe to toe, and it makes things that much tougher when you’re using character and starship personas in different theatres in the same deck. IMHO. Also, keep in mind that Palpatine can’t DB transit to a DB where your opponent has presence (your strat makes it sound like you intend to do that). Reduce Mara’s Saber to x1, since she can pull it every game. All of the open slots should be used for various high destiny or usefull interrupts, methinks. A Force Field (or two) would be good for keeping Vader alive, and Sniper & Dark Strike would be a good addition. Focused Attack would also be something that would do well in this deck. Other than that, I’m sure you can find some cards you’d like to add to fill up the open slots. Hope all my rambling helps somehow!
