 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I can pull my space locations much faster than I can my docking bay locations. Quick manipulation of my deck will be necessary to pull my other locations out. This is something I do need some advice on…any assistance would be great.

I wanted to incorporate a strong Space and Land deck with a theme of ISB so that I can drain everywhere. Tagge (v), Tarkin (v), and Motti (v) let me do this by pulling troopers, ISB Agents, and Kuat Drive Yards. I wish I had a couple copies of Imperial Command or The Empire’s Back. These three are crucial to the deck’s success.

I know that my interrupts are trooper based, but I do have more troopers than anything…and if well played I can place a beatdown and/or hold control of my docking bays so that Imperial Decree can come out. I’ll need to stop heavy draining quick, and I'll need strong activation for my SD's.

My choice of Star Destroyers lies mainly in that they are wonderful for Turbolaser Batteries. Once Motti is out, I’ve got some powerful SD’s!

I already have in mind a few things to drop out of my deck…so changes will not be difficult to make. Any feedback would be appreciative. Hope you like the deck! Thanks!


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     Good comments by Ricky. I think that ISB should be a space deck more often than not, but your balance is pretty good, but a little slanted towards space. Activation will be difficult. If playing you, I would first pull my Dont Do That Again Shield to kill our activation and planet pulling power. You will be hard pressed to get out an ISD with only 2 Force. AELOMBT and Tagge don't work. The effect gives them armor, and Tagge V can only pull troopers w/o armor. I'd get some better ISB agents, like 5D6, maybe and ORS or two, and Cmdr Merrejk (who can get systems). I'd try to use some of the unique troopers since they are 2/2 and you don't need 4 to get a battle destiny set up. Also consider using COR blaster rifles, for their better hitting or destiny.(or assault rifles V).
   Rickynumber24     First, if you have ANY of the other Special Edition Coruscant locations, use one of them. Two of them are twixes, which gives you more activation, and Imperial City, the 1/0 one adds a destiny to power at all battleground locations if a General (read Tagge in this case) is there. Wakeelmui might be good for activation, but it seems a little hard to get out if you grab Kuat with Mobilization Points. I would suggest starting IAO if you can, and putting Lt. Suba in along with Executor and/or Death Star docking bays for free troopers. He would make Snowtrooper officers (who are ISB agents as well) actually worthwhile to play. It's really annoying to not have something like The Empire's Back, since all 4 of the characters it affects are in the deck, which could really bite your opponent if you get them all out. I think it is common or uncommon, though, so you should be able to get your hands on some. One final thing, if you can get the Admiral's Order Intensify the Forward Batteries into the deck, especially with some Spaceport Docking Bays, it would really help because you could capitalize on both bonuses it provides. BTW, I am happy to see this deck because it inspires me to go try to make one of my own of these decks with my (also limited) cardset.
