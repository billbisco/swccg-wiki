STRATEGY

It all pretty much speaks for itself.

First Turn ( 5 force) Play Scum & Villainy from hand. Use Jabba’s Palace to grab the Audience Chamber – having a look for Jabba and pull him with the locations game text.

In subsequent turns I continue to pull locations and aliens to flip my Objective (Usually done at the latest on my second turn.)

I pursue force drains with the locations and the Expand The Empire – baiting my opponent in and beating up anybody who comes along with a continuous flow of Chevins backed by leaders and bounty hunters.

Also; Boba, Jodo, Mandalorian Armour, All Wrapped Up and the Dungeon keep invaders at bay and retrieve a little force. If the pressure is on at Tatooine sites I like to zip around on the Skiffs using reacts for tight situations and then regroup.

Xizor, Guri and Chokk are a little hit squad that I usually arm with the Naboo Blasters and interfere with my opponent’s agenda on other planet sites. If I need to probe or take to the skies I load Xizor and Guri up on their ships.

Jabba’s Twerps and Abyssin Ornament provide a little life boost if necessary.

It’s got a 50% loss rate at the moment but I can’t find the weak links – like my other deck it seems good on paper but I can’t quite get it to work. Any helpful suggestions appreciated.

   sithborg     Cam pretty much hit it on the head. I would have Jabba's Influence (V). Boosts Bounty Hunters and lets you pull non BHs. Hutt Bounty (V) is really good, adding to your Force Generation and makes it lethal to attack Jabba, Xizor, Gardulla, and Vigos.
   Cam Solusar     Your use of Chevins is unique; I like it. You should try to get Jabba's Sail Barge and run the Passenger Deck instead of Lars Moisture Farm. Locations that you can't pull from you reserve deck are bad because they just float around and get drawn for destiny (Murphy's Laws) Also try to get [v] Jabba's Space Cruiser and good pilot/ship combos like [v]Zuckuss in [v] Mist Hunter and [v]Bossk in [v]Hounds Tooth. You should be using [v] Jabba The Hutt because he can double the power of your aliens in battle. One thing that you might consider trying is using different Reps. Abyssins are really good because you can send them to your used pile when you forfeit them, and Skrillings are fun because of their high destiny (four) and their ability to steal stuff. I'd try different types of strategies and just see what works best for you. Good job and good luck!
