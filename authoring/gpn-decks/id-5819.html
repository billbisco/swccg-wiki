if you dont know how to flip quick, see my previous CCT deck.

some card strategy:
Desilijic tattoo: makes bounty hunters, especially ones with weapons, good

jabbas influence: makes BHs more powerful and lets you deploy non-BHs from reserve. also lets Palpy be used with Scum

ket maliss: can deploy DBs, can take some good people, and while Palpy's not on table, makes black sun agents(some of which are "created" with Xizor) better

special delivery: can deploy effects and makes use of Carbonite Chamber Counsle

one of my favorite combos is Jodo Kast, a naboo blaster rifle, some other random alien, hidden weapons, and Jabba's Through With You. almost garunteed hit on anyone, two battle destinies and (hopefully)a hidden weapons capture/hit/lost

   Cam Solusar     Looks like a solid start. I would recommend trying out the IG-88 start for the Tunnel Vision effect that you get with him. It's less reliable than Boba Fett's Rifle, but potentially more flexible. Plus, you'll be able to get low-destiny cards like the Tatooine system and your weapons out more easily. That's my opinion, but of course the decision is entirely up to you; both starts have their strengths and weaknesses. Regarding your Jodo Kast strategy, he's deadly with Feltripren Trevagg's Stun Rifle. I'd recommend that over the Naboo Blaster Rifle. Lastly, I would think about including Prepared Defenses so you can do a 3 effect start against Profit. That's again just my personal preference, so I'd playtest and decide what works best for you. Good job and good luck; feel free to respond on the RFT.
