 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
-First Draw Roche using your Obj. because it is central to one form of your retreival, Slayn and Korpil Facilities. Your other source is the minimal from Virtual Luke.

-Keep chewie and Han together aboard Independance to add battle Destiny.

-if there are any really tough starfighters(bounty hunter ships) Use X-wing Cannons and Steady Aim together and blow them away.

-hit as many ships as possible as it will retreive you two more for your use...

-If they refuse to come and fight, drain all the the life force out of those cowards
