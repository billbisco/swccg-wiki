(first off i can't spell for the life of me so bear with me)

These are the first decks I've mad in three years and this is the first of my four deck series.
Your starting System is Ralltir and you should try to deploy that first and Dantooine last. Dantooine is the only system that dosen't have a dark side force icon and this will hopfully cause your opponint to think this is your hidden base(little bit of phsycological warfair). Use REBEL FLEET and IT COULD BE WORSE to slow down force drain and help absorbe battle damage. once you have all the X-WINGS out use ALL WINGS REPORT IN and get back that eight force.

Try to get VERRACK on the DEFIANCE and if you can manage it don't deploy S-FOILS until RED LEAER IN RED 1 is on table. Use luke to get DARKLIGHTER SPIN in to hand and have him be retriving 1 fore a turn as much as possible.

Since this is one of my first decks that I've made in 3 years I would appriciate as much feed back as possibel. BE BRUTAL I CAN TAKE IT!


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     Rebel Planners (V) is a must for a modern HB, even running swarm. I would focus primarily on the X Wings and not worry about the other ships. I think you should run some EPP characters for ground, or do matching speeders/Sandwhirl on Kitonak to get around the drain tax. Doing this also open us using On the Edge/Flash (V) for more retrieval. Run more X wing guns only. I would use some space evasion like Alt to Fighting or Hyper Escape or good ol Barrier so you can counter attack with a horde of X wings. Also run some Houjixes in case you get beaten down when you spread out.
   Cam Solusar     Looks good, but some changes would make it better. It's better for HB to be strictly fighters/matching pilots with a few capital ships, due to the flexibility. Check out virtual set #6 at swccgpc.com for some HB helpers, including one that helps you get your planets out faster. Most of your non-unique fighters should probably be unique ships and matching pilots like Dash/Outrider, Mirax/Skate, DSII Wedge/Red Squad One, etc. Those guys are absolute murder in space. As far as weapons go, heavy turbolasers are practically useless as are the torpedoes, unless you're planning on blowing the Death Star. X-wing cannons are excellent, but you should get some more X-wings into your deck. ;) A great start, and a potentially powerful deck. Good job!
