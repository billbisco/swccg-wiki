 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Strategy
First Turn I Try to deploy Luke to Tatooine system with red 5 I almost always have AWRI or Luke or Red 5, I do that to get his game text working. I also deploy Corellia for the Reserve deck and hopefully you get Ord Mantell Early.

2nd turn I deploy the Falcon with Chewie 1st choice Han 2nd choice not Lando, along with kessel. I will try to deploy A2 but unless your opponent gives you some force you have to wait.

3rd I use Chewbacca V text to go get some cards:

Han with Heavy Blaster
Chewies' AT-ST
Chewbacca's Bowcaster
Life Debt
Rug Hug
Millennium Falcon

I also will use AWRI to get more smugglers to space via Luke V.

Card Selection
Ord Mantell - With Theron Net He adds to a force drain there and red 10 deploys free

Rycar Ryjerd - To make Luke V a Smuggler so you get all the added benefits for the objective and it is a destiny 4 which is not to bad

Chewie's AT-ST - Well I have some problems with battles on the ground so if I put Yotts in there I get some descent power, and a better defense. The thing is easy to get into hand with Chewbacca V's game text but it's deploy restriction is some time problematic. It is a Destiny 4 so it is not a complete waste.
