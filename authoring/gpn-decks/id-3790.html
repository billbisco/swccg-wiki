 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
On my first turn I deploy Mara Jade at the audience chamber using it’s text, her lightsaber can come to using blaster rack (V) but only if the opponent gives my 1 force to start with ‘cause her stinking lightsaber costs a force. If that works out I use Maras text to pull Dr. Evazons gun for Jodo Kast. After that its go with the objective except that if I could get scum and villainy up it’s a nice retriever. If you haven’t noticed my space fleet is puny though it can do some damage, it’s pretty much there to keep the other guy on his toes in space and I can pull them all up with the objective. The non-bounty hunters (especially the droids) are there for some extra punch. Hutt smooch can be handy for taking out spies, I just used the combo for the ability to get it back in my deck.

Shewski: those cards are fillers so take it easy! but thanks for the suggestions. usualy i capture one hi forfiet character and thats enough incentive to come to tatoine.


 The Last 5 Comments
   LordHoban     Looks like it could be a fun deck... it should be in that section, as it seems pretty straight forward. I'd still recommend using Search and Destroy, for added damage. I'm almost tempted to say go for Ket Maliss (v), so you can use Greedo (v) (forfeit 6 with objective, pulls Oo-ta Goo-ta) and pull him, too, as well as Reegesk. Krayt Dragon Bones (v) is also a nice card to have in the deck, and put your creatures under your starting effect, like Bubo, One Arm and Wampa (to mess with your opponent with LS Hoth, start with this), as well as your Sarlacc. You aren't using the starting effect? That's like a must. Dantooine is a waste. Kashyyk and Kiffex are good for space drain, otherwise put in another docking bay instead. It isn't Cast for Jodo, it is Kast. Don't duplicate the characters you have, try and get a hold of IG with Gun and 4-Lom with gun. Either use Dengar or Dengar with gun, not both. And if you use Dengar with gun, take out the Blaster Carbine. Bossk with Morter Gun is also best if you can get ahold of him. Largely what Shewski said, like the fact that you don't need quite so many weapons. Docking Bays means you need to play with at least 2 They're Still Coming Throughs!, uncommon card from Coruscant.
   Shewski     It seems like you want to play a pretty straightforward COTVG. I think you need to get more incentive for the opponent to come to Tatooine. Your objective damage of 1 is not enough. Try cards like Search and Destroy and Mosep/Gailid to make your drains hurt. First Strike/COlo CLaw Fish would be good additions, especially with S & V. You have very few chracters- I would add more heavy hitters (jabba, xizor) and focus less on weaponry. Run at least Jabba's Sail Barge and Power of the Hutt so you can pull it (and the passenger deck to get generation up.) I'd add another pullable site, like the Blockade Flagship Bridge and put in We Must Accerates to pull it. Get rid of the rest of your locations. If you can, get stronger versions of your hunters, like EPPs and R3 Fett. Also use Krayt Dragon Bones (V) to hide your creatures and make them stronger when you need them. Ditch most of your weapons, all except Mara's stick and Vibro Ax. Some of you interrupts make no sense. Focus them on the ground mroe than space- I recommend running only Bossk in Ship, Iggy in Ship, Suckuss in SHip, and Jabba's Ship. Good luck!
