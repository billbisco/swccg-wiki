 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
It may seem like I have to many systems in this deck, but in my opinion the more systems the more confusionthere will be on which planet to probe. The Outrider, Pulsar Skate, and the Falcon allow me to jump from Ren. Point to pretty much any system on the board, while my other ships ship can spread to the more convenient ones. I can’t see why everyone doesn’t have It Could Be worse in their deck, its AWSOME, if you must lose force for any reason, reduce that amount by using “X” amount of card instead of losing them. Aquaris in force generation +3 if controlled with a starfighter present, this will help in getting ship deployed and having access to useable force for It Could Be Worse. When Heaven I deployed on Ren. Point starships deploy -4 and pilots -1, thus your fleet may mobilized very quickly at a low cost.
The overall game plan is to pull out all the sytems, yet simultaneously pulling starships and their pilots. Once all systems are out, flip the objective, this will hopefully slow the opponents strategy and allow you to set back a force drain and battle at your pleasure.
