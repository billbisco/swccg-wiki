Strategy Overview:
These are optional on which to use if need be! Use Strike P. to get out Han and Crix- Crix to get Chewie (and other
scouts), to get wuta, to get sites, to flip the Objective! ( I need to get a Crix Madine Fit him in for another scout)
On the first turn get Madine, Han, Chewie, and Wuta.(Madine and Han before activating or on opponents turn and
yours !!!!!!) Now that the first turns are over CONTROL ENDOR!!!!!!! Get out scouts with Crix! And get out The
Bunker. If opponent is force draining, you lose ability 1 or 2 scouts you can get them back if you flip, DO NOT lose
too many though! I know comes in handy when Han ””accidentally”” is left alone at a site! Hi Leia!
Independence may be changed for Liberty (it goes to the used pile if lost in a battle).
Keep R2-D2 at any scomp link. Why? R2 says: If at a SCOMP LINK when opponent draws ANY destiny of: 1-3
Activate 1 force or 4-6 may draw the top card of reserve deck into hand. Can’t argue with that! He IS a SPY so he’ll
deploy anywhere, leave him alone at an interior site to guard against TRAMPLE. Remember because he is a spy your
characters may deploy where he’s at! If there are any questions about a card D-MAIL ME FIRST!!!! I will explain
any questions. No question is too short or long No question is too stupid or too smart! Please ask before you rate!
