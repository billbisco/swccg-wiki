if u got two force left pull they will be no match for u

visage
pull another location

keep pulling your dueling cards as you need them

Use conduct your search (pulls endor: back door and blast door controls) and the docking bay puller (use to pull coruscant DB (1/0), DS2 DB (1/0), and endor: landing platform (1/1)) in addition to BFHI(V)... Use 2 maul w/ sabers and you don't have to worry about finding his... when playing HD, you need higher destiny... plus you need insurance in case your visage goes away... Run at least 2 more visages and at least one more phantom menace... run a second palpetine and a second sith infiltrator... it's a good ship and can help you big time against Hidden Base... and you need to pick up a janus, as he is key to tracking for your duel draws... drop the imperial artilleries... you only have one weapon they will work with and there are better 7s for hunt down...
