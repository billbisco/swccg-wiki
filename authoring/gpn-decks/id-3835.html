 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Well... this is QMC, so just run. Path Of Least Resistance should cover that even if it gets grabbed. Never draw cards once you flip, only use Keeping The Empire Out Forever's search feature, along with Mirax and The Signal, to get the cards you need. With Bacta Tank and A Jedi's Resilience, Luke and Obi-Wan just don't ever seem to die. A Step Backward can retrieve a key character, at a cost. Honor Of The Jedi and Menace Fades limit damage. Sorry About The Mess combo and Clash Of Sabers make Luke and Obi-Wan (and Leia if she has Anakin's Lightsaber) a force to be reckoned with. The space should be plenty strong enough to hold Bespin and protect Cloud City Celebration.
