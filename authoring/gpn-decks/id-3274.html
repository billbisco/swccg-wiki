 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
A basic Echo Base Operations deck. Use A New Secrect Base to pull all sites and systems, mains to control Echo Base and get EBO on table. Once its out, deploy starships
like mad, using Organized Attack to pull three fighters or one squadron. The ‘squad’
fighters have good ability and power, the premire versions have cheep deploy costs so
you can get the squadrons out quickly. Corvettes have good deploy/forfeit ratio.
Projection Of A Skywalker can limit some of the damage you take from drains. Deploy
Haven at Yavin 4 to add a battle destiny and deploy most of your starships for free
(corvettes will still cost one force). Destiny adders and A Jedi’s Resilience help support characters, and once you establish a defense at Echo Base you can use any leftover
characters to attack your opponent at their sites. Traffic Control for destiny stacking and reducing cards in your hand (maybe I’ll change it to Thrown Back). X-wing Laser
Cannon to deal with bounty hunter ships. Once EBO is on table this deck moves fast and
drains big. Thanks for checking it out and please let me know what you think.

   tyranus     I would beef up your starting stuff. I posted this in a conversation on the PC site the other day so I'll paste it here. I start with V-wokling (pull ANSB), Insurrection (forfeit boost, anti ellis),Strike planning to pull Madine and McQuarrie (or Riekan if I have one of the others in my starting hand). Mcquarrie goes in the War room ,adds 1 to power at 3rd marker, doubles Hoth Sentry, which now adds another 2 power, and makes deploy +2, and opponent needs ability 6 to draw destiny). Use madine to pull Delevar, Kensaric, and another ability 2 power 3 scout (I like Brooks). Use Ebo to pull Echo base sensors. Deploy Caldera Righim and a couple of EPPS with Sabers to 4th Marker (you can build them up at 3rd). At 4th Marker (with Hoth sentry on it), opponent need ability >5, but <8 to battle, they cant move away after deploying at +4 (!) for everything (!) from sentry and Kensaric, your characters cant have their forfeits reduced (put Delevar in the med lab) and you are power lots +3 (sentry and Mcquarrie in WR). And you're set up for Under Attacks (use junkin as one of your scouts maybe for if theres no walkers). Your ships can now drain for free cos you have a BG site.
   catman421     This deck is good for a first attempt. I agree with karrde. You need more systems.    karrde     looks good, but there are a few things i would do to make it even better. first off, more systems. almost any good dark side deck has 4-6 power starhips (bounty hunters, etc). with only 4 systems, it gets awefully crowded and u really dont want to get stuck battling. have at least 6 maybe even 8 so u can really spread out and do some drain damage. to protect your spread out fleet, use alternatives to fighting and hyper escape. also, maybe a little less power on the ground. u only need to occupy 3 sites for one turn, and after that the mobile effect ice storm does an excellent job of protecting the base. lastly, maybe toss in a couple high power starships to surprise your opponent if he tries to spread out to stop your draining (han, chewie, and the falcon is an excellent choice). sorry to take up so much space but with some work i think this deck could be really good.
