 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Well, ya see...I've been workin' on this deck for almost two years...and I have it almost the way I want it. I already have some changes in mind, but I have to get the cards first. Any feedback would be most helpful.

As you can see, I am well fitted for Order to Engage. I even have a strategy article about it. I absolutely love this card. It can do so much for me in the late game when I have my force out.

As you can tell, I am a big fan of effects. They seem to withstand gameplay much longer than interrupts. And plus, not many people play Sense/Alter decks anymore. But I do need to watch out for them. Effects like I Feel The Conflict and Eyes In The Dark are used to REALLY tweak the opponent off (I love doing that). Effects such as K'Lor'Slug and Lightsaber Proficiency are used to bolster my power. I love Jar Jar, so BMM is a must...and the other effects are just more "pisser off-ers".

I use The Signal to pull Sai'torr Kal Fas (v) so that when I get my mains out they aren't defenseless.

My force retrieval seems to be well placed, though any other ideas would be helpful. Target a Jedi on Tatooine and get to Alderaan, and retrieve ALOT of force (depending on the characters I have on the table).

Guys like Caldera Righim, Deneb Both, and red cards like Traffic Control and Tunnel Vision can help me cycle through my deck to get what I need to flip and wreak havoc.

I have alot of fun with this deck...I have won once with this deck, and I've only played it once. I hope to win much more with it. Feedback please! Thank you, kindly...
