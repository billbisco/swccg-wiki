 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck does not incorporate too much space so it may be easily beat down in that department. This deck basically aims at trying to beat down your opponent on ground. You can quickly begin deploying your characters with the use of Ket Maliss (V) wich allows you to take out Beedo and Greedo pretty quickly. You can also get out Docking Bay 94 so you can move around quicker. You can start the game with either Power of the Hutt or No Bargain depending on which type of deck you would be playing. For more Rebel heavy decks I suggest No Bargain. You can quickly occupy space with the Objective if there is a system down on the table. Force generation may be a problem but with the amount of sites people are playing these days it might not. Protecting you hunters are an amalgam of cards that include Oo-ta Goo-ta, Solo (V), Jabba's Through With You and Human Shield. Shocking Revelation is there as a quick 5 recycler but works both ways for those scanning decks. I also recommend taking out Prince Xizor with JP: AC pretty quickly to deter any type of Rebel threat early in the game. Any suggestions would be appreciated and thanks for looking.
