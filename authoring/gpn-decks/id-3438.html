 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
You start Endor system with Operational as planned to get Jerjerrod, DSII, and Desperate Counter in hand. On your first turn, you play Desperate Counter, use it to pull Imperial Arrest Order from reserve deck, use it to play DSII: Docking Bay, play Jerjerrod there, take a DSII sector from reserve deck, play DSII, Combat Response from Reserve deck, and any sectors you may have in hand. Each turn afterwards you try to get out a sector. When you have the Reactor Core out you play That Thing’s Operational from reserve deck, and then move to the planet that you opponent has played. Meanwhile, play the <> docking bay to the opponents system if you can, and play your characters to sites to battle him where he is. Try to occupy as many Battleground sites on their planet without spreading yourself too thin. You have six imperial leaders to use with the Garrison that will let you draw an extra destiny, but Davod Jon won’t let you draw two (he is only ability 2). Igar in Tempest 1 also adds a destiny. Deploy your TIEs to either DSII or Endor, with their pilots of course, and then try to get at least two with cannons on them. Before you move DSII, get your TIEs to it so that they move with it. If your opponent tries to blow up DSII, follow him in and do your best to shoot him down. Mianda in Scythe 1 will move twice and/or fire during your move phase and he adds 2 to his weapon destiny so make sure you use him effectively to shoot ‘em down. If you get Twi’Lek Advisor, use it to get We Shall Double Our Efforts so that X is increased by one on That Thing’s Operational and it makes opponents starfighters maneuver –5 at DSII sectors, thus making them sitting targets. Hebsly also subtracts 1 from maneuver of an opponents fighter. If you still don’t shoot them, their movement destiny are going to be –9 if you have three armed TIEs, minus the 5 maneuver from before so they are going to need a destiny of 10 to get a 1. Use Maul’s Infiltrator if they are playing with a lot of space but otherwise try to leave it in your deck to get better destiny draws. The Dark Jedi saber can go on either Sidious or Sing, or anybody else that needs one. Just try to take control of their planet and drain, direct damage, and/or battle the heck out of them.

 The Last 5 Comments
   EchoBaseTrooper     You definately should start with Endor Operations. If the light side player is using Strike Planning (and several decks do), then you only get 1 force on your first turn. The bounty hunters aren't really necessary, you should fit in Imperial Command (at least 2) Admiral Piett and Commander Praji(V) to pull the pilots. Or even Come With Me(V). Also add in a few more Twi'lek advisors to pull your effects faster.
   pointy-eared green dude     I give you a three for it's well thought out battle strategy and organization...but it could be better. Why the bounty hunters? I mean, they are powerful, but in an all Imp deck, why have them? It just seems a little out of place, especially when you could throw in some destroyers or something to protect your DSII.
   swcironecg     Do not start Endor Operations. If you have it, pull the Mobilization Points & You Cannot Hide Forever combo with Operational As Planned. Use it to get out a system and then sack it for Desparate Counter. Use Desparate Counter to pull Combat Response and Imperial Arrest Order (combo if you have it). Imperial Arrest Order pulls the Death Star II: Docking Bay. Deploy Moff Jerjerrod there and he will be safe. Other than that your basic strategy is good. I suggest Mob Points because you cannot rely on your opponent to deploy a system. You can also include a <>Spaceport Docking Bay to deploy to the system you pull with Mob Points so you can do more damage with That Thing's Operational. You need more space in my opinion. Take out the Black TIEs and put in Saber Squadron, maybe the Onyx fighters too. You also may want to look into bounty hunter ships like Dengar In Punshing One and Zuckuss In Mist Hunter. Take out the Blaster Rack (V), it is only good if you start with it. Use EPP versions of Vader and Maul instead, it is more card efficient and you need the room in a Death Star II deck because building the Death Star takes up so many cards. Boba Fett, Bounter Hunter is better than Cloud City Fett, but if he's the only one you have then that's what you have to work with (I only have Boba Fett w/ Blaster Rifle). Take out Fett's gun. Might need more ground, Prince Xizor and Guri would be good additions. Building large space stations of death is always fun, post again once you have made some changes.
