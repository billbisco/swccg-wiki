 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The basic rescue Han, persona replace if possible, then spread out some and drain/flip side objective and battle until they are done. use podrace and tat celebration to retrieve and last longer.

some of the card choices could be better, but i am working with limited amounts of certain cards, and lacking a few that should prolly be in here-individual card strategies welcomed

the two landos seems like a problem to me, but maybe one that is unavoidable-i am looking for good combined ships and pilots with immunity to attrition and ability to draw destiny by themselves-again, any suggestions welcomed

   Shewski     I'd go matching weapons and LS PRof. Also, I hope She's Allright will increase the DS's damage. I'd have a little space, but not too much and forget about Celebration. The droids might not be needed. Use Bacta Tank and 21B. Maybe Beggar for some beatdown fun. Maybe Draw Their Fire since you can be offensive. Run Luke Run is good as well.
   swcironecg     Rycar Ryjerd (V) doesn't pull weapons Rev, only effects and devices. But Sai'torr Kal Fas (V) does. I agree that you should drop the podracing. Use HFTMF to deploy Sai'torr (V) and two other effect. Include Obi's Lightsaber and Anakin's Lightsaber, Qui-Gon's too, if you have Qui-Gon. It seems like you may need more sites. Add City Outskrits and one other site. Add Mindful Of The Future x2-3 to pull the sites. Or if you don't have that, add Geezum and Take Me To Jabba Now x2-3, which may be better since you can pull the other aliens in your deck. Don't do Tat Celebration, focus on damage on the ground from the objective and drains. But if you really want to do Celebration, add more copies of the system, Mindful Of The Future to pull it, and start Wokling (V) to pull Tat Celebration (and to retrieve it if it gets canceled). Outrider, Red Squadron 1, and Pulsar Skate are all good figher/pilot matches. I would also add Han destiny adders, Don't Get Cocky, I Know, and Life Debt, in addition to or in place of the ones you already have, since you can be sure that Han will be on the table. I usually don't write reviews this long, I hope it helps.
   Revelation     This looks good. I would reccomend starting HAn (V) instead of HAn Solo, it's just better. Then, instead of the pod start, you could start HFTMF and Rycar Ryjerd (V)(+2 other effects) to pull his gun and something else. Also, I reccomend Old Ben, and you should play Ben Kenobi instesad of one of your other Obi personas. Also, play with a few It's Not My Fault! to protect Han, and maybe one more backup Han. He is the focus of your objective, after all. Good luck!
