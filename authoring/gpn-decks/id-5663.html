this is a fun deck to play and really bugs the other player!

lets say you dont have the cards in hand, and you only activate the force from your locations.

turn 1,
pull a sleen, activate 4 force, pull any site, drop the site then drop the sleen(for free) to the same site to cancel the LS icon, then pull the sleen from under your effect and put it on the swamp to cancel that one too, now you can pull Capt daultay dofine and put him on the flag ship and then pull Activate the droids into hand. leave one or draw one.

note: the other player dose not get any force from you :). and can only deploy spy's to your sites.

and wile it is the other players turn pull into hand another sleen.

turn 2: pull into hand a creature, activate at least 6 force, pull and drop your last site and then put a sleen there. now you can start working on flipping your objectiv. and you will be activateing at least 8 force each turn(the droids are cheap any way).


the creatures cant attack your droids so you wont need to worry about loosing them to that, and if you do lose a droid then no worry! he gose to the racks and you can pull him back into hand. The sleens arent ment for long term, just there to upset and slow down the other player and it works great! i like to use the other creaters to go to the other players sites and goble them up! drop the wampa from under your effect to really mess them up if there at Hoth, and for those dirty little MWYHL decks drop the dragonsnake there to eat Luke/leia or Yoda V if not then you can still drop it at the Naboo:swamp

i know that force drains arnt that great, but just get a battle droid to the swamp and you can still drain a little or get your jedi with saber there to drain for a bit more and haako or gunray to add more drain power.

ability[3] V helps you agenst the Senate decks and you can pull your characters (that dont go the the droid racks) to hand from your lost pile! and you can retriev your cards (non character) with Activate the droids.


space is well taken care of i feel, useing Fighter cover you can really make them hurt in space and ground, if you flip the Obj then it really rocks for your battle droids makeing them immune to attrition less then 5! and you can hit those jedi rather easy useing You cant hide/mobl points, and if you track destiny then you will have no problem.

i know the destiny in the deck is at a all time low but cant really do much about it with the droids.

Please post and let me know what you think of the deck. and what you think would be better or at least some thing that i can use. i am really wanting ot stick with the battle droids as much as i can to really use the droid racks.

In order to use Kryat Dragon Bones(V) to pull a creature they have to be "ferocious" the definition of a "ferocious" creature is defense value > 2. A sleen only has defense value of 2 so the entire first part of the strategy is completely mute. Also you can't pull Vine Snakes with Kryat Dragon Bones(V) either.
