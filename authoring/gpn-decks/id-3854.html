 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Capture Luke with Vader, and let Vader take care of the pesky small characters that bug him. Leia, Han, Lando, Chewie, etc. What, then is the threat to Vader? It's them stinkin' Jedi! Darth Maul, Young Apprentice takes care of Jedi nicely. If you are not fortunate enough to have a YA in hand, simply use the EPP one with Maul Strikes, and you still should have no problem. Again if you are lucky enough to have Darth Sidious down, place the Jedi out of play so they will bother you no more. Let's see, Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon out of play, Luke captured?who is left? Leia, Chewie, Lando, and Han are the only mains. CHOKE THEM! YAB them! Dark Strike and Sniper them! They will all die at the wrath of the Sith lords! Beat them down and cross Luke! Any smaller characters should die quickly, as well. karrde: Your fixation with space seems a little abnormal. You should see someone about that. Shewski: ACTIVATION IS NOT LOW!!! AND WHAT FREAKING DIFFERENCE does it make whether I play with DS2 or R2 Palpatine??? Huh? It's never and never will make a freaking difference!
