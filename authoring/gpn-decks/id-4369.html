 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Use the City Outskirts to deploy Quigon on turn 2 from reserve deck. if you get a Speak with the Jedi Council on the first turn or Jedi Council Chamber you are now in luck. an addition 3 force per turn and you get to deploy dispatcher Yoda.
Most of the deck revolves around getting alot of extra force and being able to spend it.
The maxium production of this deck if your opponent gives you no force icons is 28 force. 29 if the objective is Flipped.

The Strategy is simple, use the Control & Tunnel Vision, Jedi Levitation, Speak With The Jedi Council and Sence & Recoil In Fear to get the cards you need.
Once Toshce Station is in play the game will go much faster. I have yet to play this card and not have my opponent not try to come at my force there immediately. The pullers should be used to get the needed sites. Obi-Wan's Hut is in the deck because Obi-Wan deploys for 1 force. Quigon can hold down the Junkyard by himself if Obi-Wan is at his Hut and you have 2 force to spare.

Use the Blaster Deflections and Queen Amidala to prevent your opponent from offing your characters and the game is won.

*If it's legal with The Hyperdrive Is Gone, I'm debating using Chewbacca who is both an alien (to get by The Hyperdrive Is Gone Restriction) and a rebel for Our Most Desperate Hour for some massive retrival.

You can run Dash/Ship, Han (V), Chewie (V), and Falcon, and some Bravo squad instead of the generic Naboo Def Fighters. I think you can survive w/o needing to pack all those sites for generation.    icebreath     You're NEVER gonna get the objective flipped without undercover spy protection... why are you playing such heavy SAC? I know you've got a ton of Jedi in here, but 3 each of Sense and Alter combos? That's a bit overboard... you'll want to toss 2x Alter and 1x Sense in favor of Lando w/ Vibro and a couple SATM & BP's. You need to seriously overhaul your character selection... by my count, you have 4 different personas that can deploy straight to Tatooine without costing an arm and a leg. Yoda's 'teleport' is cool, but you can't rely on it to back up your characters... at the very least, put a Bacta Tank in! At best, throw in some high-forfeit senators (swap Insurrection for Security Control), some unique Naboo Security guys, and a few aliens. After that, put in some battle destiny adders to take advantage of your flipside retrieval. This deck needs a LOT of work before it's seriously tournament worthy. Take a closer look at my THGG, which is currently undefeated in tourney play... maybe you'll figure out how to patch up the holes in this Swiss cheese deck.
