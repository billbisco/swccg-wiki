 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
To start off, DO NOT GO TO FIGHT THEM ON THEIR TURF. Hold Tatooine at all costs. If they take up the offer of deploying 2 aliens to the chamber, hopefully you will get Rebel Princess in hand and use someone who loves you to free Han early. The 2 locations you need held down for this to work are Tatooine, and The moisture Farm. Get Luke up in the sky on Red 5, and Owen/Beru at the farm. Play Harvest, and next action, retrieve it with Luke. Never ever keep Luke on the Ground (unless you have 2), and stockpile your ships as needed at Tatooine. Replace Han Solo with Cappy, and deploy the Millenium Falcon to the DB or Farm, move him on and take off. For the most part, this is mains n toys, but the retrieval engine works nice if you get it going. If they play a lot of space, you be the judge about if its worth it to stockpile up there, with three harvests, you can just retrieve another Harvest if need be if you can't get Luke up there. If they place Harvest out of play o well, you can use it 3 times then.
