 The Description
Okay guys, this deck is a little bit different. Just imagine it as a slice, dice, and serve ‘em nice kind of deck, and I think you will enjoy it. I chose Fondor as a base of operations to begin my Imperial campaign of terror. Let me know what you think!

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
So this deck has no objective…so what? I wanted to try something different. Maybe you guys think I am nuts…maybe you guys think me genius. Whatever, I just wanted to be different. Here goes nothing… :D

First Turn: Pull Gall or Executor (whichever you may not have). If you do not have either, I suggest pulling Gall for the namesake of having some more room for space deployment. You can get the Executor later. Also pull <> Spaceport Docking Bay and deploy it to Fondor.

Second Turn: Pull another docking bay. I suggest Endor: Landing Platform (Docking Bay) for the namesake of free transit. Quickly take over the two planets you should have out with some space that should be provided with Combat Response, DS-181-4, Commander Brandei, or Short Range Fighters/Watch Your Back!. Make sure you get control of a planet sites and two systems so that you can play Imperial Decree.

The Rest Of The Story: My choice of interrupts is mainly just so I can really annoy the opponent. Ever try to Tallon Roll Saber Squadron? How about after they have been Darkly Maneuvered? The Watch Your Backs and All Power To Weapons are more beatdown insurance. Snipers, Trooper Assaults, and End This Destructive Conflict greatly enhance my chances on the ground.

Reactor Terminal is a great cylcer/tracker card for some high destinies and wanted cards. It also gets the garbage out of your hand. Pinned Down could really kill a QMC deck if used in good timing and Imperial Training Academy just further boosts my use of troopers. Drop D’an and Onith in the Executor and let them do their thing. I know retrieval could be slow, but it is better than no retrieval at all.

My use of Gall just really puts the mortar to the bricks of demolition in my deck. Blasters, two sabers, and Snipers, along with a mysterious M’iiyoom Onith can really put the hurt on the opponent’s characters. I’m hoping for an onslaught. :D

I hope that you at least enjoyed my deck. It may get a one, but I have really enjoyed piecing it together. I wanted to build a deck WITHOUT a purple card objective. Any feedback would be appreciated. Thanks!

   swcironecg     First off, you're not crazy for using no objective. I think alot of people forget the pre-objective days where creativity was key and your entire deck wasn't planned out for you. I would drop the whole trooper weapons package and use Mob Points to pull Rendili or Caridia instead for more activation. Sorry to burst your bubble, but TIE interceptors can't Tallon Roll, only TIE/ln can (check the glossary). In place of the Tallon Rolls, and maybe the Dark Maneuvers, I would more TIE Cannons and a couple I Can't Shake Him! to pile on the battle destinies. Add in some more Vaders, Mauls, or other good characters to hold your docking bays so you get extra activation from Mob Points. I would turn this into a battling deck, where the focus would be to control some docking bays to up your activation, then drop space beatdowns on your opponent's main system, disrupting their strategy by cancellling Celebration cards and fliping back objectives. If your opponent doesn't play a system, then you could either add Admiral Chiraneau and some destroyes and drain in space, or add Fighters Coming In and drain at the docking bays. This deck looks good, you just need to focus alittle more on what you're going to do with your characters/starships and how you can hurt your opponent.
