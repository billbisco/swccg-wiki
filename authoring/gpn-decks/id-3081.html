   spideyguy0     Looks like a pretty standered BHBM deck. You need to switch Emperors. You need more force generation. You need Maul on the ground. Also, I know it is standered in most BHBM decks, but I don't like Imperial Arest Order. The forefit +2 is nice but not essential. You don't need docking bays if you have lots of Elis Herlot (which Arest Order stops). If you do use docking bays Expand The Empire helps you use docking bay transit to go straight to the throne room, and bypass a turn where you would loose one force. Elis also does this, but more efectivley. This is a pretty standard deck so it gets a 3, but nothing really different.
   Admiral_Sjet     I give this deck a two for a few reasons: Force Activation: Maximum you provide for yourself is 5 force a turn on your own cards. If the opponent plays a force choke deck, you're screwed. Character Selection: The Vaders are nice, but in order to be the most effective, the aliens would need to go. Based on force activation, the Anamanan is almost ineffective. It looks like you have a good rares colection, but the original Emperor is almost a staple for this deck. Lastly, you could lose some of the other characters and some of the ships for more Bounty Hunter in Ship combos. Some big bad characters are needed to win some battles, so put some Mauls in. Interrupt Selection: The 9 Imperial Barriers are just insane. 5 is good for most of the time. I dont know exactly what your strategy is for this deck beyond what the objective calls for, since you didnt detail it in the section provided. Therefore, i am based on using my own experience. Elis Helrot is less effective now becuase of Insurrection, but its a nice card if they dont use it (i love the one-turn beatdown with those guys....Endor really screwed that up). LOL.....but anyways, some more battle-oriented cards would be nice, or some undercover spies to block drains.
