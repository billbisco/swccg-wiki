 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Han: Right now all i have is a general solo, but im gonna get a han(v) for those secret plan-less decks out there.

The Camp can be a stressor on anyone using mob points, in combination with uh oh it can make deploying anything to Jabba's Palace difficult for the opponent.

Jar Jar can go into the throne room, then i can slap down luke and his gun, as well as other characters, possibly with weapons. I chose luke's gun because it can fire twice, meaning clearing the audience chamber can occur quicker.

In Space, The order combined with weapons can be used to destroy tough capital ships, like the executor. Armor -4, fire turbo lasers, then add 3 to destiny of missle for being second weapon, and extra 1 if its on a fighter and you can destroy most anything if your destiny draws do not suck.

Side Note: Often times people recommend me cards, which I do not have. I know only a few other players, who usually dont have or are in other decks. I do frequently use cards in multiple decks, but it gets hard to manage after a while. Some friends and I are ordering boxes of reflections to share, and if you would like to help the cause, see my posts in the trading thread!

I gave in to mixing decks, so I put the spiral in there, obi wan, and some spies, and other sites. Often the first thing my opponent does is move an alien from the chamber to the palace, meaning i only get 1 force on the first turn, so i needed a way to get force (Cloud city cites, Blue Milk, Either way you win) so that my opponent doesnt deprive me of almost all activation.

   Corran Antillies     Not a bad start. I'd use more mains, particularly epps in exchange for at very least the ithorians and Klatus. Also, take out most of the space. Stick with Bright Hope V x2 and Spiral x2 and don't play any planets. Teamwork cards are a must in this deck. Particularly with chewbacca v, since his are pullable. How are you getting your sites out? Figure out some way to pull them. I've got the same problem with my profit and am looking into figuring it out. I assume your Bionic Hand is Virtual. Here's a combo that you may want to consider in this deck that somebody played in a tourney recently. LSJK(or master luke)+luke or anni's saber+Courage of a skywalker+Lightsaber Proficiency+Bionic Hand V+losing Owen and Beru Combo during opponent's turn+Jedi Presence(when he's with a jedi)=a power 40 luke. Add any and all those cards that are needed for that combo. Also, Artoo with a gift is good for this deck. You won't be able to win against a strong court deck without Leia Rebel Princess to free Han, or at least Princess Leia V(pullable with Seaking an audience V) with her gun and someone who loves you. I think that's probably it. feel free to refute any of my suggestions in the RFT.
