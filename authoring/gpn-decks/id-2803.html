 The Description
This is a BHBM deck designed using Theed Palace cards stop enemies on Endor or other planets while you worry about sending Vader to find Luke and "Bring Him Before Me". This is the best BHBM deck I could come up with by myself, and I'd really like some feedback as to how I can make it better. NOTE: designed before release of V set #1.

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
A good combination of space and ground attack power allows you to have a good overall defense against whatever strategy your opponent decides to play. I messed up thinking that Vader, Maul, and Mara can all take Luke to Palpy but instead of having 4 of the same character in your deck for a better chance of getting one, all 3 of these guys can be on the table at once, helping you win those battles you need to turn Luke. I will fix this in a new BHBM deck that I'm working on now. Many people think BHBM is worthless since there's a good chance of the light side not having Luke in their deck, but with this deck you also have the ability to battle and force drain your opponent.

   Cam Solusar     Good combination of Episode One and Classic, with a lot of heavy hitting characters. I would add one or two more systems so you can force drain and gain more force.    Bantha Bob     You were definitely thinking along the right lines when you built this deck, however I don’t agree with the combinations of cards. Adding destinies to win battles is a great idea, but if you are intent on using Emperor’s Power to do that, I think some Force retrieval might be a good idea, I think one of the following would be your best choice: First Strike, Gardulla The Hutt, or Barquin D’an in that order. I saw the footnote about the deck being built before v-set I, but the good news is we are at v-set III so let’s use them. My choice to add destinies on the ground would be to use bounty hunters with permanent weapons and both Jabba’s Through With You and virtual Oo-at Goo-at, Solo. This will allow multiple destinies with very few characters and also give you some high destiny red cards in your deck which will come in handy when you draw destiny to turn Luke over to the dark side, and, one of the reds is a used. I would concentrate more on the ground since you will probably have a better chance of getting Luke there rather than space. For your space I suggest cheap capitol ships like dreadnaughts and victory destroyers, with maybe Grand Admiral Thrawn x2 or x3 to give some destiny adding there, and virtual Besieged can help you grab Luke from space. Now for what to start with: Prepared Defenses with, virtual Ket Maliss (lets you get docking bays and two bounty hunters) Conduct Your Search (lets you get Back Door, good Force generation and good draining for you) and the combo card Mobilization Points & You Cannot Hide Forever (lets you get Rendili system for more Force generation and a battleground system and then go after IAO). If you do that there will be at least 8 Force on the table for you by turn number 2, and then you can get 2 Dbs per turn. For the Dbs I suggest two generics, a Tatooine, Cloud City, Coruscant, DS II, Endor, Executor, and possibly Yavin. This way you can take Luke from almost any planet through DB transit to DS II and you will get a lot of Force in a short amount of time. I could be wrong on this, but I don’t see how Mara and Maul are going to get Luke and take him to Palpatine, it doesn’t seem possible with this deck, maybe you could show me something I am not looking at, I personally would prefer more Vaders in this deck rather than having the other two. By the way, make sure you get Young Fool in this deck. You don’t want to be ready to turn Luke and have your opponent play NOOOOOOOOO! and put your deck back with one card. I was going to give this deck a one or two out of five only because it is way out of my comfort zone for playing the game, but what works for someone else may not work for me and vice versa. Also , as I said before, the line of thinking for this deck was good, but it relies on luck a little too much for my taste, but the right combination of cards at the right time could make for a victory.
