 The Last 5 Comments
   DarthVader,AOE     This looks exactly like the type of deck I would have built when the game first came out. I would definitely recommend buying some boxes of Reflections 1,2, and 3. If you got a box of each you would come across almost everything you need to make a really great deck. It's an interesting deck because I can tell what sets you bought from :)
   Shewski     Two for a beginner's deck. This looks a lot like decks that were made when the game first came out. It would get eaten alive in a tournment, but for a fun game between friends, it should do just fine. My biggest advice would be to look at some of the strategy articles on this site- there is a good one on deck making, and one I wrote about my experience in getting back up to speed after being out of playing for a few years. If these cards are the best you, have then good job! Most cards have a purpose, but I would not pack multiples of Yavin. Take out Rebel Guards and Pilots and use "named" characters instead, if you have them- they are typically more versatile than those characters.
   annakinstarkiller     I gave it a two for a biginner. You have no movement from Yavin so you might want to add a docking bay there. Why two Yavin 4 systems though? Beggar seems kinda useless at this point. Instead of using Rebel Pilots, which in my opinion are pretty useless, try getting some uncommon pilots from SE, like, Hol Okand and Lieutenant Naytaan which should be easy to come by, I have a whole bunch. I'd also toss Don't Underestimate Our Chances, I don't think you have the power to pull it off against a good deck.
