 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Turn one: Activate 6, get a CC site. Wokling gets CC Celebration. Draw if you want, I don?t recommend it. Turn two: Activate 8. You now have 11 force. Get last CC site. Third turn: Activate 11. Flip with some people. Pick a card. Get db (Arcona should go here). Fourth turn: Activate, Deploy CC Celebration, pick a card. Fifth turn: Activate, pick a card, retrieve, beatdown. Just keep repeating the process. This deck is all about recurring characters. First of all, you?ve got the Found Someone You Haves (V). Obviously a good thing. After you are in a battle, next turn you pick this up, get your character back, and take it to your opponent again. Bacta Tank is obvious. You drop Hiding (V) later in the game. As for locations, it?s just for extra generation so you can pick out of a bigger pool, and have more for beatdowns. As for what to pick off the bat, I would recommend Path, Flash Of Insight (V) (obvious why this is a good card), SATM & BF, Bacta Tank, Garbage, 2/0 sites, or Clash. Basically, just set up on CC a few people (Arconas if you can), and beatdown with the other people. A few responses to some comments for sure:

'Only 3 Paths???'
Yes.

'More CC sites would be in order.'
I had two more CC sites, but then decided to cut them because I didn't want to waste resources defending them, when I could be using my characters for beatdown.

'Off The Edge, dude!!!'
Nope.

Matchups:

Dark Deal: Sorry bout this. Lose.

COTVG: You definitely have beatdown capabilities playing against this deck. But watch out for the BH ships!

Hunt Down: Avoid the duels, obviously. That makes it harder to beatdown, so mainly stick on CC.

CCT: You know- kill the person escorting Han.

ROps: He probably won?t come to you, and it will be hard for you to go to him, and he has the capability to shut down all your drains. So go to him.

BHBM: YOU win the battles. Your characters are coming back- his aren?t.

AOBS: Make Xizor sautéed toast.

Watto: Totally dedicate your resources to creaming Watto. Spend 8 to use him, then beatdown his backup if you want. Beatdown vs. Beatdown. Good luck.

Combat: No Jedi, whatever you do! That leaves Luke, Lando, Han, and Chewie to take care of his DJs. Hmm, maybe you better stay on CC. But play the Affect Mind shield, anyway.

SYCFA: Defend Bespin so he doesn?t blow it away. That would be bad.

Other things- do your thing.

You open yourself up to a lot of pain with those fellas. Character selection is your standard, but a plasma said where is Pucimir? Interrupts are pretty good, but with KTEOF you can include some other cool ones to set up beats in space and on the ground- see the worlds QMC for ideas. Hiding is the Trash might be really good, but it can also kill you since you are running multiple mains. I love the use of Flash (V). Instead of Hiding, i'd want to run on/off the edge, then use Flash or CCC t get them back. East platform is a bad idea, it opens to door to walkers. The other CC sites are a good idea, but I don't think you need all the twixes. A little extra activation is good, but you should have plenty and can use the card slots elsewhere.
   swcironecg     Recurring characters? You forgot what could be the best card in such a strategy: A Jedi's Resilience. Also, I think that at least one or two more Paths would help, especially since they will probably get grabbed. Then your opponent plays Stop Motion (V) to halt those Acronas and your evasion is evasion no more. Your beatdown force is good. Personally, I would try to find room for several team interrupts (Life Debt and the like). Add an X-wing Laser Cannon to take care of Zuckuss, since you have so many X-wings already. And where do you find room for all these changes you ask? take out those extra twix sites, or at least a few of them. By using KTEOF, you never need to draw cards, so your force pile will just gradually grow bigger and bigger. Also, using KTEOF to pull the sites would be wasted pulls that you could be using to get beatdown stuff.
