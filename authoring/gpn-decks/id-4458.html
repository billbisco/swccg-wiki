 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The same strategy as before, battle and rescue Han, then spread out and drain/battle/flip side objective until they are depleted.

The addition of Someone Who Loves You means you don't even have to battle Han to free him, and the virtual Han removes a need for It's Not My Fault.

I added Ben Kenobis and 2-1B which I just acquired, to take out the two Old Bens. I also replaced R3 Qui-Gon with Tatooine Qui-Gon to flow with the deck more.

I removed Wedge and his ship because I would have needed Squadron Assignments, and added some main capitals.

Using Lightsaber Proficiency and I Must Be Allowed to Speak on Luke, also using Rycar (v) means his is powerful and protected on Tatooine.

I hope the revisions made this deck a little better.
