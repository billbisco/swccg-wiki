 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I don't really know what this deck does. Surprisingly, initial play test have been pretty good. Since it has no clear focus, except Senators arguing (usually with themselves) in the Senate (and that not very important), there's nothing to disrupt. I've just been waiting for my opponent to reveal their game plan, then doing whatever I can to mess it up. And with 3 lightsabers, as many a 5 battle destinies sometimes and 17 Star Destroyers, there's a lot of messing up power in this deck.

So, what do you think? How can it be improved?


 The Last 5 Comments
   Fush     I am sorry man, but I just don't see how this deck could be a threat to any decent light side. It has some major problems, and I'll list a few of them here for you to review: 1. Activation. You activate 4 force from the beginning, with the possibility of pulling one more location to give you a total of 5. If your opponent doesn't give you a lot of icons, how do you plan on getting your ships and characters out? With 5 force a turn there's not much you can do. Getting the Death Star and Imperial City out will help some, but you have no way of pulling them. You need more locations/icons, and consider Senate Hovercam to help activation. 2. Destiny. The overall destiny of this deck is terrible. Even with Oppressive Enforcement and flipped, you're bound to draw low. Swapping out characters and ships for high destiny interrupts and effects will help here. I would also start Colo Claw Fish for insurance. 3. Game plan. This is probably what you need to think about the most. What are you trying to do? Wait for you opponent? What if he plays on the ground, without giving you much force (say a Profit deck). You don't have enough powerful characters to stop him. What if he plays a fast space deck, like Hidden Base? He'll swarm the systems before you'll be able to react, because of your expensive ships. If you're planning to win with this deck, it needs to be more focused and faster. You need to be able to set up early and cause more damage. Finally, a few questions for you to consider: 1. Why do you choose the combination of senators and star destroyers? 2. How do you plan on making your opponent lose force? 3. Why do you need 17 starships and all those pilots? 4. Why do you play one of each senator? 5. Why don't you use any political effects? 6. Why do you choose Lord Maul and Lord Vader over the other versions? 7. Why don't you play any useful, high destiny interrupts/effects like: Maul Strikes, I Have You Now, Squabbling Delegates, Imperial Command, The Phantom Menace, etc... Again, I am sorry if this sounds harsh, but this deck needs some major work to be competitive. If you would like to see a senate deck that is, check this out: http://www.decktech.net/starwarsccg/decks/decks.php?deck=30348. I recently won the Norwegian Nationals playing a similar version. If you want to play star destroyers, have a look at this: http://www.decktech.net/starwarsccg/decks/decks.php?deck=27718. So, good look with improving your deck. Please don't hesitate asking, if you have any questions. And if you have CardTable, I'd be happy to playtest against you with some competitive decks :) Peace!
