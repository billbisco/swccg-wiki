 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Politics-

Orn Free Taa-
May take just drawn battle destinies into hand, he also deploys for only 1. Opponent can use all their force (deploy phase) to put him in used pile.

Yeb Teb Adem’thorn-
May peek at top card of opponent’s Reserve Deck; return that card or lose 1 force to make it lost. Works well if opponent is playing epic events. Get rid of any important card for something in your hand you don’t need.

Lott Dod-
Very important senator, you can pull him with the phantom menace. May search your force pile and take a card into hand.

Passel Argente-
Killer against a Hidden Base deck. He make all their non-unique starships deploy +2 and power -1 and he is politics +1 for each of opponent’s senators at same site.

Edcel Bar Gane-
Opponent may draw no more than 1 battle destiny at battleground sites.

Tikkes-
Makes my force generation +2 at battlegrounds I control, and Honor Of The Jedi is suspended. Another good trait about him is you can draw up 1 card from bottom of force pile into hand during your deploy phase.

Toonbuck Tora-
While in senate majority, once per turn may add 1 to your total attrition in battle for each character with an ambition agenda at Galactic Senate.

Aks Moe-
Makes force drain +1 at battleground systems.

Political Effects-

This Is Outrageous-
If no senator here, may place a senator here from hand to cancel a force drain. If a trade agenda here, once during your turn, you may place a card from hand in Lost Pile, shuffle that pile and take top card into hand.

Motion Supported-
Add 2 to your next force drain at a battleground, and if wealth agenda here my lose your just drawn battle destiny and replace it with a card from hand.

Our Blockade Is Perfectly Legal-
Add 3 to battle destiny just drawn, and if blockade agenda here, once during your turn you may cancel a ‘react’ or an attempt by opponent to move away from a battle.

Accepting Trade Federation Control-
Subtract 3 for any battle destiny just drawn. If taxation agenda here, during your draw phase, you may activate 1 force; opponent must first use 1 force (if possible)

So know you know what all my political cards do. You can now see how good a senate deck is. The abilty to subtract 3 and add 3 to destinies and cancel force drains is priceless. Even the objective has some crazy things like use 3 force to randomly take 2 cards out of opponent’s hand. It also makes senators destiny +2 or +3 if they have ambition agenda. Otherwise this deck is used to try to force drain and beatdown your opponent. I could describe every other card in this deck but I'll do that on v.2
