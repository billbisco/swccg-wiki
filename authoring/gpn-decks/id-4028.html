 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First Deploy Fondor.
Attack, Destroy, Kill.
Use Defenders to 'React' and just watch your opponents face fall when a planned attack fails, and turns out very costly indeed.

Use Lateral Damage at either Kuat or Wakeelmui, so they cannot go there.

If playing against a space deck, then Assault fully against space.

If playing against a ground deck, then Drain in space and full assault against the ground.

Either way, Make chaos, make confusion, and above all, make Casualties!!!

Yes I know it has no retreival, the fun is in killing, not returning from the dead...

Need another starting effect, suggestions anyone?

Need a staring objective too...


 The Last 5 Comments
   Corran Antillies     I'd say you definately either need better, pullable sites, or none at all. I'd say take out the tempest scouts. They help a little, but your big firepower on ground comes from the AATs' 4 battle destiny. Start with Firepower as your other effect. I like to have a good number of turbolazers and lose them from force drains and stuff, and then only pay two force to deploy. Defenders are nice, but I think that, unless you pack the defenders with wepons, Interceptors are better. If you are going to use defenders maybe put in a few Dark Manuevers. That way your defenders won't get their power reduced by high manuever fighters.
   Shewski     As Hoth said, you need to refocus this deck. Ivasion would be a good platform for if you wanted to go ground heavy, and I think that SYCFA/TIEs would be good if you want to go space. See Hoth's decks, he has made some solid TIE decks recently. You should probably start YCHF & Mob Pts so you can get your activation up.
   Darth Hoth     This is in between a 1 and a 2, but I'm in a generous mood, so I'll give it a 2. Defenders are ok, but there are better TIEs. With the usage of TIEs, I would suggest using SFS and Short-range Fighters. I would start Mobilization Points combo so you can get a system (probably Wakeelmui), and then you can send it to your lost pile to search for an effect. DS-181-3 is a bad pilot, DS-181-4 is much better. This deck is very unfocused. Either get a stronger focus in space or on the ground. I would start Kuat, that way when you pull Wakeelmui, you get the +2 bonus. The <> locations are bad, with the Ounee Ta defensive shield, the deploy for opponents characters is too cheap. This is if you choose to go for space. If you want to stick to ground, I would use Invasion, but I'm not good at that deck, so I'll let someone else explain a good way to work it. If you get this better and more focused, I'll give you a more indepth review.
