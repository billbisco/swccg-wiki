Strategy: Rescue Leia then let your opponent kill her, then put down one of your backups at the War Room to flip. If he doesn?t kill her, kill her yourself with On The Edge. Once you flip, you place his characters out of play by winning battles, and also with Out Of Commission. I was thinking about putting Gift Of The Mentor in. Is that a good idea? I?ve got strong space here, but please don?t say I have too much, okay? I have killed many bounty hunter ships with these starfighters. So once you flip, go roaring into battle. Trust me- once enough of his things are out of play, he can?t fight you anymore. Remember, Boussh is safe from beatdowns as a spy. bigchief: I want to kill Leia so I can put another one down to flip. I can pull space with tunnel vision, and also deploy to my opponent's systems. So what if Out Of Commission gets grabbed? I'll only have to pay 1 force to use it, and it's a trackable 5. If he grabs OOC, that means he won't grab Rebel Barrier, which suits me fine. BTW, those fancy starships? I don't have them. MareTino: Thanks. colinhunter: I don't have Red 5 or R2-D2. I have two EPP Lukes and Obi-Wans, but what's the point of putting in two more destiny 1s, when I can pull them with Tunnel Vision? Does anyone know the SE pilots add one destiny to total power? BTW, I want to play strong space. This deck has done fine without EPPs, but I might put in another Luke and Obi-Wan if it doesn't go undefeated again next tournament.
