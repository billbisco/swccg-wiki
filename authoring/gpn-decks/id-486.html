 The Description
This deck can adapt to any Light side deck
and beat them. It takes advantage of Lott
Dod's ability to get anything you need from
your Force Pile!

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
OK first, here is the starting stuff:

My Lord, Is That Legal?/I Will Make It Legal
Coruscant: Galactic Senate
Tatooine: Desert Landing Site
Fear Is My Ally
Twi'lek Advisor
Senate Overcam

Note that if you are playing against a Light
Senate deck, you better start with The
Phantom Menace instead and make use of your
The Point Is Conceded to the fullest before
taking advantage of the Senate.

DURING THE FIRST COUPLE OF TURNS:

Getting Lott Dod in the Senate should be
quite easy with 4 of him and 4 Squabbling
Delegates in the deck (or the 2 Masterful
Move, The Phantom Menace and 3 Darth Maul
With Lightsaber in there).
If you take control of the Senate, you will
be activating a minimum of 7 force on your
second turn and only giving 1 to your
opponent. Even Hunt Down cannot do that with
its 2 starting locations!

After start, just use Lott Dod to get more
activation going (the 2 Tatooine sites than
you can pull with Neimoidian Advisor,
Blockade Flagship: Bridge that you can also
get with We Must Accelerate Our Plans, and
finally, Tikkes combined with Tatooine:
Marketplace and Watto's Junkyard).

THE RETRIEVAL OF THIS DECK:

I wanna take a minute to make you realize
that the retrieval in this deck is really
great. Gardulla The Hutt combined with
Yeb Yeb Adem'thorn is an easy 1 force per
turn, and so is occupying the Tatooine:
Marketplace. You can also retrieve 4 force
with Squabbling Delegates when your 4
senators will have made it to the Senate.
But the huge card in there is the new
Immediate Effect named After Her! It cannot
be grabbed, nor canceled and even boost your
battle destinies when you drop your squad to
take control of the location. 4 force is big...
and the effect goes back in your Used Pile! WOW!

HOW TO BATTLE WITH THIS DECK:

First of all, you should always take control
of the Marketplace with Maul since he will
deploy free at the Desert Landing Site and
then move there.
But, if you do that, keep Vader and the
other guys (Evazan, Boba Fett, IG-88, the
battle droids and 4-LOM) in your hand
until you are ready to battle...
The character removal is also big in this
deck with Evazan, Sniper & Dark Strike, Luke!
Luke!, You Are Beaten, IG-88 (combined
with the new battle droid 3B3-888 this guy
is even nastier). Also, if the opponent is
playing ground, don't forget to get Edcel
Bar Gane out as fast as you can to limit
your opponent to 1 battle destiny...

THE DROIDS:

Why the Droid Racks you say? Because the 3
new battle droids included in the deck are
awesome and they never will die. Imagine
Corran Horn sitting somewhere with EPP Han
and EPP Leia. Your opponent thinks this site
is safe...WRONG! Put Luke! Luke! on a
Tatooine site targeting Leia and drop EPP
Vader and SSA-306 to battle them. If you
hit Leia with Vader (shouldn't be a problem),
she is immediatly lost due to Luke! Luke!
and both Corran Horn and EPP Han cannot use
their ability toward drawing battle destiny
because of SSA-306 (which is the bomb against
Watch Your Step). SSA-1015 is good against
Jedis and 3B3-888 is a walking Force Lightning!

IN SPACE:

Well Chimarea and Thrawn should be enough
(with Imperial Command) to hold on in space
with Zuckuss In Mist Hunter and 4-LOM.
But beware Power Pivot! This is why I have
included Unsalvageable...

THE POLITICAL EFFECTS:

Cancel drains with your extras Lott Dod or
subtract 3 from your opponent's battle
destiny (can be huge to save your EPP from
dying if he draws 3 or lower).

THE SIDE GAME:

You can also get his hand low with the game
text of side 7 of your objective combined
with Defensive Fire (I included the combo
because it is my only way else than Sniper
& Dark Strike to take care of Undercover
spies). You can nail him easily with 2 cards
in hand during your control phase and then
play Limited Resources (and get it back
after using Gardulla or the Marketplace)!
Finally, Watto's Junkyard is a nice place
with Watto sitting there since the opponent
cannot draw battle destiny!

THE DESTINIES:

They are pretty great. You can easily
recycle the Point Is Conceded by activating
1 force, Masterful Move, Squabbling Delegates
and even We Must Accelerate Our Plans to
make all your weapon targeting successful!

As for the match-ups, well like it says in
the title, it adapts to anything...and beat
it "MOST" of the times since no deck is
perfect! Have fun with it guys!
