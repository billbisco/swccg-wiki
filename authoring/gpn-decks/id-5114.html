 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck is a really good deck in the hands of a master (of DS senate)...

Podracing isn’t hard to pull off and its more force retrieved (and maybe lost) It is also a good counter against a LS podracing Lightsaber Combat deck.

Politics-

Orn Free Taa-
May take just drawn battle destinies into hand, he also deploys for only 1. Opponent can use all their force (deploy phase) to put him in used pile.

Yeb Teb Adem’thorn-
May peek at top card of opponent’s Reserve Deck; return that card or lose 1 force to make it lost. Works well if opponent is playing epic events. Get rid of any important card for something in your hand you don’t need.

Lott Dod-
Very important senator, you can pull him with the phantom menace. May search your force pile and take a card into hand.

Passel Argente-
Killer against a Hidden Base deck. He make all their non-unique starships deploy +2 and power -1 and he is politics +1 for each of opponent’s senators at same site.

Tikkes-
Makes my force generation +2 at battlegrounds I control, and Honor Of The Jedi is suspended. Another good trait about him is you can draw up 1 card from bottom of force pile into hand during your deploy phase.

Aks Moe-
Makes force drain +1 at battleground systems.

Political Effects-

This Is Outrageous-
If no senator here, may place a senator here from hand to cancel a force drain. If a trade agenda here, once during your turn, you may place a card from hand in Lost Pile, shuffle that pile and take top card into hand.

Motion Supported-
Add 2 to your next force drain at a battleground, and if wealth agenda here my lose your just drawn battle destiny and replace it with a card from hand.

Our Blockade Is Perfectly Legal-
Add 3 to battle destiny just drawn, and if blockade agenda here, once during your turn you may cancel a ‘react’ or an attempt by opponent to move away from a battle.

Accepting Trade Federation Control-
Subtract 3 for any battle destiny just drawn. If taxation agenda here, during your draw phase, you may activate 1 force; opponent must first use 1 force (if possible)

   Shewski     Challenger hit the high points. I think you have been going in circles with every incarnation of the deck- you take a few suggestions, but you cut out (IMO) strong points to implement them while keeping weak things like the Marketplace.
   challenger     The first thing I see is your activation. It’s not good. You get 6 by yourself if you can get out the Marketplace. That’s not enough. So to fix that, either start IAO/SP (for dbs) or maybe Mob Points to pull a system (or both), or play several WMAOP for the Blockade Flagship Bridge and a couple A Dark Time For The Rebellion (V) for the Hoth Wampa Cave. That’ll bump it up well. Secondly, check out Chu’s KTOD Senate (or Desai’s, actually). You may want to try to copy that one as far as the mold goes and cut the pods to start Combat Response, IAO/SP, and Mob Points/TPM. Moving on to characters, I think I would play another Maul, and if you can get your hands on them, use EPP Mauls. I thought it was impossible to get them, but now I have 3 (!). So, you should be able to get some, too…anyway, I don’t agree with some of your character selection here. I would take out Reegesk (V), Piett, Keder, Thrawn, and Aurra Sing, even if she is AI foil : ). Then I’d put in some good battling dudes, like The Emperor (V) x2 (will help in Senate vs. Senate matchup), BOBA FETT BOUNTY HUNTER (x2?), and, if you’re going to go the Combat Response route I advocate, you’ll need some pilots. As far as Senators goes, I think it would be wise to cut a Passel Argentes, a Tikkes, and add a Toonbuck Toora and another Orn Free Taa. Moving on to starships: I think this is bad selection. You don’t have force for all these expensive destroyers, especially currently. Again, I’d go the KTOD route and play Black 2 (V), Mist Hunter (V), and Saber 1. If you think you’re going to run up against Home One or some high immunity ships, you might also want to add Punishing One (V). Btw, what’s with all that space stuff when you don’t even have your own system? Sites: I already told you what I think would be good, either cut the Marketplace or put in a puller (Voyeur (V)/Neimoidian Advisor). Interrupts: If you can, of course, Twi’leks should be WMAOP, but aside from that, I don’t think you have enough interrupts. You don’t play enough interrupts and you’re going to be seriously hurting when it comes to destiny. Add a couple Stop Motion (V), Ommni box combo, Control combo, if you use matching starfighters a couple Short Range Fighters/Watch Your Back!, if you have enough room maybe Masterful Move x2, Monnok, and Ghhhk. If you don’t do that, at least run a Ghhhk combo. That card’s great. I’d also play I Have You Now and Maul Strikes. Effects: Cut Decree for Decree (V), Reactor Terminal is awful, consider adding Blast Door Controls, I’d definitely add Bad Feeling Have I, and maybe Ability Ability Ability. Perhaps even Ability, Ability, Ability (V), because it can retrieve your Senators : ). Weps: Look fine. Poli effects: Of course fine. So, to wrap it up, the main problems I see are activation, podracing, and your starship selection. Good luck, and I hope to see you at Madison soon!
