 Back to Top 
I try to focus on retreiving Han fast, first or second turn. I put a lot of Lukes in here so I have a better chance of getting him first turn. I know I am missing some good cards, such as Leia Rebel Princess but im doing what I can with what I got. I also need to through in some more Hans, and I thin ill put in Tantive IV to beef up my space. I also need to change the space site I have in there. Kiffex had good activation and good drains so I through it in. This is the first deck Ive posted on GPN. Any suggestions would be most helpful :).

   Corran Antillies     This is very close to a 3, but it's just not pollished enough. And the only reason I'm being so elaborate and harsh is since I know you. Lets start with the character sellection. You need at least 2 Hans and or Han's back v. You can do nothing if they kill han. Plus, almost any other personas of han are better. My favorite for this style is Han v. Master Luke x3 is good, but take out Epp luke. I'd play Princess Leia x2 instead of the epp leia. Chewbacca V x2 would be best(since he pulls lots of stuff), but epp would be ok too, since you don't have the bowcaster. Quigon Jinn x2 is necessary if possible. If you play Artoo, a gift helps a lot. Naked threepio is better than the one you're playing. And since you're playing unique weapons, baragwins are very nice. On to effects... Take out Battle plan, restricted deployment, and probably the camp. Which ones are you starting? I like Rycar Ryjerd v to pull LS proficiency and Bionic hand v for Luke. Bacta Tank is something you really need. Reflection (I think that's the card) would be nice too if you feel like it. On to the Interupts. Rug Hug(most important because chewie pulls it), Don't get cocky, and I know are the missing teamwork cards you need. Choke really doesn't do much for this deck. Your space package isn't bad, but I like Spiral x2, Bright Hope v x2, and Tantive IV v. Weapons... Maybe consider Chewbacca's Bowcaster, and DEFINATELY TAKE OUT the second Obi's saber. Finally, the worst part of this deck is your locations. I like using Inserection, but Wessa gotta grand army with battle plains and Boss Nass chambers is probably best. Then you can stack at battle plains for a big drain and then nabrum leids (something else that this deck could use) to whereever your opponent is.
