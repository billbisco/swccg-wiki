 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
To begin with, you have 15 destiny's of 7. You also have 8 destiny's of 6 and 9 destiny's of 5. That is 32 destiny's of 5 or better. Very useful! You only have two destiny's of 0, one of which you get into your hand at the start of the game.

The basic idea is to duel the crap out of the dark side, and to find a weak spot and jump on them. Jar Jar and Boushh are the key's to any site in the game. Need another force drain site or two, just go to where they don't want you.

Use Mace, Qui-gon, Yoda, and Obi to duel at Naboo. Leave Luke to beat down with some of his friends. You dont have to worry about battles much, chances are you will draw good destiny, and attrition alone should clear off most sites. The trick is to keep your jedi on Naboo from being jumped on and beat down. To do this, use It's a trap to get out of any combat you don't want to particapate in. Use Out of Commission/Trans. Termin. to keep them from just losing characters from one site, after all you beat them sinceless, you want them to lose big! We don't have time for this adds two destiny's when Amidala & Panaka get's together, so get them together and kick butt. Gift of the Mentor can be used to either get that much wanted lightsaber, or to add two destiny's when the dark side just tryed to attack poor Obi-Won all alone, now depolyee Luke and baam, three destiny's and big power.

As you can see there is not much this deck can't do. If you see any improvments that can be done, please let me know. Thanks.
