 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This is still in the building phase right now, so I'm mainly just looking for some impartial advice. There are 8 cards I'm trying to fit in (listed at the end of the decklist)...... and I can't figure out what to remove to get them in! Any other comments are appreciated. On to the strat:

Turn 1: pull Executor and FlOps with Mob Points combo. You should have a Twi'lek in hand, so pull TMNALTC. Draw up.
Turn 2: hopefully you'll have another Twi'lek to pull Flagship (V) with (or already have a copy in hand). If so, drop the Executor to Bespin for free, play Flagship (V), pull the Ex: Docking Bay with IAO, pull Piett with Flagship (V) and deploy him, pull Merrejk with Piett and deploy HIM, pull a system (Wakeelmui's fine). Draw up.

Keep pulling Executor sites with Flagship (V), and systems with Merrejk. Once you get FlOps and Seinar Fleet Systems played, you can start deploying your TIEs for retrieval. Spread out your Star Destroyers and drain away. Maul, Vader, Dengar, and the P-bots can drop and do some nasty beats with a bit of tracking (TIE Assault Squadrons = destiny 9, Empy's Shield/Sword = destiny 8, Scythe Squad TIE's = destiny 6), and your squadrons have super-high forfeit values to keep your star destroyers alive in space (note: Vengeance adds 2 to the forfeit of each TIE/ln at same system... in other words, another +6 to forfeit of the squadrons).

So, that's how it goes. If you've got any questions, go ahead and contact me in the ratings feedback thread. Thanks!

Enjoy!

   Cam Solusar     I would juggle the order of the main effects that you are trying to get out, (V) Flagship, SFS, and FlOps. Get SFS out first to get retrieval going early, that way you won't flip out if your Executor gets KOed early. Then set up (V) Flagship to pull the pullable sites and/or Gherant. After that obviously you would do FlOps. Don't forget that your destiny draws are +1 in every battle at a system where you have a TIE. Check out tyranus's SYCFA SFS FlOps. It might give you some more ideas. I'll post a link on the RFT.
