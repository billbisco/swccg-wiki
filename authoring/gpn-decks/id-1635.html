 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I’ve seen several invasion decks posted recently, and I wanted to add mine as another point of comparison. I’m not about to say that it is a perfect deck; there is no perfect deck. However, I think that it is a fast deck with some distinct advantages. The strategy goes like this.

On the first turn, you should activate and then pull Dofine with Blockade Flagship. Then pull Activate The Droids with Dofine. That gives you a power 9, armor 7 starship that gets a battle destiny and is immune to attrition < 5. Deploy a Naboo site (preferably Throne Room) from your reserve deck. After that, draw the rest of your force into hand. (You’re looking for a neimoidian and trying to draw lots of battle droids.)

On your second turn, do much the same as on the first. Play any droid starfighters that you have, and draw to get a neimoidian if you don’t already have one. Also, don’t forget to play another Naboo site (I prefer the Generator for the force activation and drain potential. Also, Maul is power 9 there!)

By turn three, you should have a neimoidian and at least five or six battle droids. First, play the other Naboo site (Battle Plains or Generator, whichever is not on table yet). Then, play your neimoidian, flipping your objective. Now, put at least two or three battle droids at the throne room for some protection (and a blaster rifle, too, if you have it). Put the other droids at a good drain location, and draw the rest of your force.

The next turn, you should have a drain of at least five or six for your opponent to deal with. You will not have to worry about Battle Order, because for your objective to stay flipped, you have to occupy the Throne Room and Naboo system anyway.

Next, just some general strategy comments. The idea of this deck is to force your opponent to come to Naboo and battle you. If they don’t, your drains will be too much for them. (I’ve had drains of twelve per turn before!) However, this deck was meant to battle, and not to force drain. It is entirely possible to hold down a Naboo site with only two battle droids and a weapon, particularly if Nute is in the Throne Room, making your battle droids forfeit +1. After all, you get two battle destiny, you can fire a weapon, and you can forfeit your weapon to the used pile. Also (and this is one of the most powerful mechanics of the deck) you have droid racks; all of your dead droids get to come back the very next turn. Finally, if you look at the objective, you can place an interrupt battle destiny out of play each turn. This is great for people who track high destinies late in the game. If they draw that interrupt, it still has its effect, but then they have to lose it. This can be a deciding factor in the endgame. Also, there is a built in force drain cap on the objective (how nice).

One thing to watch out for is Fallen Portal. Since it targets ability, it can be devastating to your droids (ability 0). Just be prepared with enough characters when you attack at docking bays.

Next, some reasons for card selections.

Naboo: Theed Palace Docking Bay – If your opponent doesn’t come to you, you should feel free to come to them. Just play it with the objective instead of one of the other sites. Otherwise, you can lose it to a force drain.

Security Battle Droid – Don’t like your opponent’s characters moving away from you? These guys combined with Battle Order will ensure that they have to use a lot of force just for normal game functions.

Battle Droid Officer – They are probably one of the most useful droids in this deck. The deploy minus one in their text means that cards like 3B3-888 and 3B3-1024 go down for 1 apiece, and 3B3-10 doesn’t cost anything. Also, the fact that droids move there for free means that you can move all your droids at a site for only one force (the cost of moving the officer).

Maul’s Sith Infiltrator – If you need a little extra boost in space power, this is a good one. Also useful for transporting Maul across the galaxy (hyperspeed 6). Destiny 6 doesn’t hurt either.

DFS Squadron Starfighter – This is one of my favorite cards in the deck. While they may not have as much power or defense value as the regular Droid Starfigher, take a look at their text. While they are at the same system as your battleship you can deploy laser cannons on it from your reserve deck. Therefore, you can forfeit the cannons, then pull up another one from your deck next turn.

Imperial Artillery – Destiny 7. Make opponent lose force while you use weapons. Enough said.

Weapon Levitation – Makes the following possible. Forfeit a weapon to used pile with Inconsequential Losses, then take it back into hand with Weapon Levitation, playing it next turn.


Some cards you may want to add.

Another Nute Gunray (I like the original one because he adds to the forfeit of your droids)

Naboo Occupation (The downside of this deck is that it gives away lots of force icons)

Take them away (Undercover spy protection)

Another SSA-719 (His text is invaluable)

Note: I know that I have not included defensive shields, but I do not have the starting effect yet. I hope to get one soon. I would include Fanfare (to deal with Order to Engage), Come Here You Big Coward, and Secret Plans (both anti-force-retrieval). Other defensive shields would be up to the individual.

Finally, here are some of the common matchups that you may encounter.

There Is Good In Him / I Can Save Him
This shouldn’t be a problem. It is impossible to capture Luke. (You can’t play any imperials with this objective.) Therefore, feel free to stage a droid invasion of Endor.

We’ll Handle This / Duel Of The Fates
This is not too hard, since they have to fight you (you are both competing for the same planet), and since your characters come back after battle, while theirs don’t. Just be careful what you do with Maul, and be careful of the high destinies.

Rebel Strike Team / Garrison Destroyed
Take control of the bunker and force them to use their explosive charges to eliminate your droids. You can recycle your characters while they can’t. A few good drains on Naboo wouldn’t hurt, either.

We Have A Plan / They Will Be Helpless And Confused
A tougher matchup since there will be major battles for the throne room. Go for an earlier flip (turn one or two) if you can, and stock the throne room with droids. Also, you can play your Theed Palace: Generator between their hallway and throne room. Don’t attack them at the throne room; wait for them to initiate the battle. If you start the fight and lose some droids, they may be able to clear the site on their next turn. If you wait, you can salvage characters from Droid Racks before the next battle. Try to spread out on Naboo and drain. If you can keep them out of the Throne Room, you should be OK.

Hidden Base / Systems Will Slip Through Your Fingers
This is probably the hardest deck to go up against. Don’t worry about flipping your objective. Just try to get a battle destiny at each site and drain on the ground. Give Blockade Flagship all the space support you can, and run to a different system. Keep them running after you. Battle order shouldn’t be a problem for you unless you lose all your starships, but it will be for them (you should be able to control the ground). Use the Fanfare defensive shield to counter Order To Engage.
