Well, here it is, the uber bounty hunter beats. Unfortunately, the destiny is rather low. Fortunately, most of the stuff you'll be pulling will take away most of the 1's and 2's, and you have a lot of cards to help them anyways.

Playing is fairly easy, the tricky part is remembering what all the cards do, and pulling the right things at the right time. First turn, pull Rendili, Coruscant: DB and Greedo. Deploy and move to the DB to flip (hopefully no Luke yet ^_^), and peek at force and grab it if ya need it. On following turns, get a decent-sized force togther, pull what you can, and go balls to the wall.

Card selection:
As I see it, there are two do-able kinds of AOBS: the Prepared Defense, non-Jabba version, and the Any Methods Necessary, Scum and Villainy version. I think Prepared Defenses version is better, because it doesn't limit your options on the first turns, and allows you to react to your opponent. Also all the effects aren't inner-dependent on each other, and they take up a lot of space.
-4-LOM (non V): With four droids and a bunch of aliens, he will get big, and can be pulled by Zuckuss (V).
-starships: 6 pilot slots, 7 pilots, basically all you need for any space package.
-Jabba's Through: Pull my weapons, and add to a draw if I need it.
-Ability x3: Only 3 characters it can't retrieve, so why not?

The matchups are basically covered in the effects section of the decklist, since it's pretty self-explainitory after then. Any criticism/praise/donations welcome.

   Darth Hoth     Looks pretty good. I like it. First off, is there any particular reason why you start Ep1 Coruscant? I much prefer the SE version, since it's a drain 1 twix. Character selection looks pretty good. I think I'd rather go with Bossk with gun over Bossk (V), but that's more of a meta call. Other than that, the selection looks good. I'd say add another Naboo Blaster Rifle, since that is a pretty nice gun to use with both Jodo Kast and Dr. Evazan and Ponda Baba. I'm not so sure about the Stinger. I don't think Guri does much good in space and the Stinger isn't that good without her. I suppose she's good for the destiny limiter, but she can be put on the Mist Hunter for that. The location selection is a little weak. You don't have any real drain locations. You're relying a little too much on your opponent to give you places to drain at. Also, I'd like to see a <> docking bay so you can get to most planets where your opponent will be playing. Since you're playing docking bays, a good card to consider would be Take Evasive Action (V). It kills Fallen Portal and hurts speeders a lot. I'd like to know what effect you're planning on cutting to start the others you have there. I'm hoping it's Desilijic Tattoo (V). Also, you don't need to start Combat Response against heavy space, unless you're paranoid about Don't Do That Again. You can ditch MP combo for Combat Response, and since you aren't ditching it for anything else, why not? It's a very good deck.
