 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I know I need to add Def. Shields and Luke's Backpack. I plan on adding a couple more retrival cards ((V) Rebel Reinforcements, maybe a couple On The Edge). Prob gonna add another Stone Pile too.

I'm looking for some constructive critisim I would like to get this deck tournament ready.

You all know how Training decks work, so I won't waste your time explain that. I will however, go and explain some of the odder card choices. Here we go..

Hiding In The Garbage + Jedi Levitation + Tunnel Vison = You can always get the cards you need, this becomes more true with MWYHL/SYIC and the completion of Jedi Test #4

Anger, Fear, Agression = I plan to add more of these (up to 4). The idea was to play an avoid deck while I train Luke, then go on a beatdown. While I was training I might as well do some damage. Since DS cannont easily deploy to Dagobah, this should be an easy 4 force loss. (Resistance Def. Shield cuts this to two)

Oola = use Encampment to get her on Dagobah, then keep putting opponents characters back in Used Pile. Annoying.

Other Characters = these are almost all of my heavy hitters, suggestions about better ones, would be greatly appreciated.

It Could Be Worse = pretty obvious. If captured the remaining can be used to satisfy force drains or you may wanna keep a couple

Bog-wing = If DS happens to get to Dagobah, these critters will keep them busy for a while (carries character up to two sites away)

Vine Snake = God sends these little buggers are. Drop them on some low-ability character of your opponents and have lunch. These little buggers can chew up guys pretty quickly.

Nudj = another staple in this deck (planning on adding a couple more prob just 2 or 3). These little buggers can prevent your opponent from occupying his own sites. They cancel a force icon on the DS side of table. If they have no Force icons, then they cannont deploy, as a lack of force icons, makes the site a non-battleground. Nice for trapping characters at a site by blocking of the adjacent site(s).

Electrobinoculars = track and get cards you need. Filler for now. Don't really need it.

Disprutor Pistols = nice little weapons, prob gonna get replaced in the next version. For now, they are just nice to stick on warriors. (destiny > ability target immediatley lost)

Open to suggestions. Thank for reading. BTW if you have any of the cards I need just email me @ highgturn@yahoo.com Thanks Again

   LordHoban     First of all, I don't like the idea of the deck... Second, if you want a tournament deck, don't play Jedi training decks (at least until the PC gets through with the Dagobah set, maybe). I will try to offer some helpful insight as to why I think much of this deck is wasted. From the top, your creatures are wasted slots. Why? What are the best Darkside places to set up these days? Hoth, Death Star, Cloud City, and Tatooine. 2 of these areas contain mobile sites, which, I'm pretty sure none of your creatures can deploy or move to. Strike. Your Nudjes can't go to either Hoth or Tatooine, nor can your Vine Snakes go to Hoth. Strike 2. No Escape also battles the Nudj idea. Your best creature is Worrt (which isn't even in your deck), and only with the new Tauntaun Bones (v) which adds 2 to its ferocity. Lightside creatures just aren't that effective. Bog-Wings, no more than 1, if that... Most decks will just set up and aim to drain enough of your deck away that when you do get finished training, you don't have enough for it to really matter. If you're so worried about them coming after you, you've got at least 2-3 turns before they can get to you. Beat them down as soon as they come to you with your backup characters using Encampment (which is why you need much more characters). It takes 1 turn, deploy a ship, fill it up, next turn, probably deploy some more, then move it over, then land, then they have to come after you. I've done it in the old days, back when the set came out. One time I had Blizzard 1 and Blizzard Scout running around on Dagobah, with Vader/Tarkin and a few Bounty Hunter/Aliens to back them up. Talk about hunting down the last remnants of the Jedi. Therefore, Hit and Run tactics become your primary defense to slow down the enemy. EPP characters usually are the better choice with A Jedi's Resiliance so they can last longer. Keep them occupied off Dagobah. You don't have enough characters to really put up much of a fight. Inserts, I strongly look down upon any player that has to resort to inserts, especially more than 1... and secondly? A shield kills inserts. Most likely, your inserts will die. Combine the Recoil in Fear with your Senses (since they do have a combo which includes both, and I use 2 of the Sense/Recoil or Uncertain combo in just about every LS and DS deck I play with now). Now that we've dumped the 'most of the time' useless creatures, fill those 6-7 slots with characters. Dump Oola, unless you have a way of reducing your opponent's handsize other than Stone Pile... You don't activate enough force to use it effectively, and most of all, it's just a very cheesy strategy that only newbies and advanced players use (or try to use)... Players that use this decktype tend to have 1-2 Artoo in Red 5s (one to track for test 5, the other to maybe use, or not -0 or 7 is a might nice tool, especially for Sense/Alter). I'd dump the Stone Pile, personally, and look for interrupts and effects with a harder punch. How well your deck fights in a hit and run scenario, and delays your opponent from setting up, or keeping them from being able to do too much direct damage to you, will determine if you survive long enough to be able to win. Most of all, Training Decks are usually not very interactive and they don't tend to be very fun to play against. I hope I was helpful, even if a little biased against the decktype. You still have severe flaws in the deck, in any case. Try looking at what decks are being played in your area, see what LS decks are used and why, and what to incorporate into your own strategies. What kind of Dark Decks are running around? If Ralliter Ops, then yeah, some of your creatures may hurt if they aren't going the Walker route. Try looking for cards that will be consistently useful in your games. For example, in my decks, most of your creatures would have little effect at all, if any. That's out of 5-6 decks, either based on Death Star (mainly a space deck, anyway), Hoth (2-3 different variations of the new Hoth Objective), or Tatooine (these 2 are just for fun). You might also consider a Yoxgit, cause he can retrieve your weapons, and he always rules! :)
