I would GREATLY appreciate comments. One thing I'm worried about is not having enough people capable of rescuing the princess. What do you think? The rest of the strategy is basically outlined on the objective! Flatliner: If my opponent doesn't use ground, Sometimes I Amaze Even Myself still applies. Opponent's starships lose immunity to attrition (the Imps anyway) and attrition against opponent is +2, they need to use +1 force to force drain, and their starships lost from table during battle are placed out of play. I can fight. Thanks for the HCATF suggestion.
