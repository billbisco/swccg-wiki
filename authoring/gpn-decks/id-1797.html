 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Just your average defensive WYS.

- Setup WYS.
- Use Luke to retrieve or get AWRI&DS.;
- Setup Menace Fades
- Limit DS drains with PoaS/ICBW/IAH!
- and/or go for the drain on Kessel if possible

If you don't like the [V] Fusion Generator Supply Tanks exchange it for a Ralltiir Freighter Captain. Plus one maneuver to all my starships helps against cannons and cancelling Lateral Damage is good news for Dash.

Martijn Peeters
