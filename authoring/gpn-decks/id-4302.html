 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Any advice for this deck would be apprecitaed. The goal is to have a heavy drain at Tatooine: Desert Landing Site as quickly as possible. Bounty and then Hutt Smooch any Undercover Spies that show up hopefully with Boba Fett and retrieve the Hutt Smooch. And continue to drain at the Desert Landing Site forcing the light side to engage you there or be drained to death by this time you ought to have a large enough force that the light side cannot win the battle.
Also, there are so many bounties in the deck that you should be able to get one into hand from drawing, but using Nal Hutta's game text saves from having to draw many cards to get to the bounties. Keep Boba Fett with binders at the Desert Landing Site as often as possible only letting him run to the dugeon to drop off captives.
The other main site of attack for the Lightside would be the Audience Chamber. If
things went according to plan, Jabba, 4-Lom and IG-88 can hold it down.
