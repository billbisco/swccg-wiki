Creative deck… Hurt them 3 force every turn…

There is a huge HB meta around Wisconsin (5 out of 8 people at the last tournament I was at played HB). I also saw a discussion on the GPN discussion boards saying that maybe there was some other strategy out there that wasn’t found yet. So I was looking through my effects and found this. It might not be new to use bombers but im pretty sure that nobody has used them in a BHBM deck lately. BHBM just doesn’t normally do enough damage to make them deploy Luke so I added some fun. I know there is a shield but most people don’t play it, and if they do it doesn’t hurt me too much.

Ive Lost Artoo – Little known text. Knock out a hyperdrive.
Location, and Ability ^3, maybe an extra 3 force lost on top of the 3 from Your Destiny. (definitely 1 a turn to HB after flipped) Kessel is my Renezdous Point
I’ll go right to the deck matchups since you should know how everything works pretty much. They are also key to playing this deck.


Hidden Base – Let them make the first move and then use Emperor’s Power and some of the other space cards to find their HB and then Secruity Precautions should hurt them. Location ^3 after they flip.

Rebel Strike Team – Use Bombing Run if you have to collapse the bunker before they do and you have an easy win.

Profit – Blow up Jabba’s Palace. Do damage. Bombing Run might be needed. One of the harder matchups but I don’t see many of these decks around anymore.

LS Combat – Try and blow up there sites. Kill their space and they are out of sites. Direct Damage.

LS Senate – Haha. Collapse Senate Chamber. Do damage. Stay beatdown safe.

WHAP – Collapse Throne Room. They can’t flip. That hurts them. Drain at Endor.

More of a fun deck but I’ll see what people think. Cheers.

Your right challenger...It's not like I was going to take this to a tournament in Madison where 5/8 people play HB...it was more of a fun deck...and frankly I'm not afraid of playing HB anymore, I can beat it 80% of the time (i lose when the time runs out and I have 18 cards left and Evan had 19) and I still had retrieval chances with my DS senate...
