 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This is classic so I can't use any epps. My open version of this deck basically just uses the epp versions of the hunters.

Get Mighty Jabba and his pals Ephont Mon, and Boelo out as well as the Sail Barge and its site out in the first three or four turns for some serious fun!

Drain on Tatooine for 2-3 force per turn plus the one force loss for them not coming to Tatooine. For around four force loss per turn. Should convince them to Tatooine. Then slam them with the big three listed above which cancels a destiny. Other destiny canceling cards include Oota Goota Solo and Release you Anger. Only start a battle when you get more than one destiny, but that shouldn't be too hard.

Once you have a captive feed him to either Sarlacc or Rancor for some serious direct damage. (I left them both in their for convenience. Whichever one that I am closer to will get the food and if I am on the barge I won't have to worry about the Sarlacc eating my guys.)


In any of your comments please help me by telling me which LS objectives I need to worry about and how I can better defend against them.

   swcironecg     Rev, if you paid any attention to anything you would realize this is a classic deck, so no EPP hunters or broken Fetts. A good idea might be to start Blaster Rack (V), add more matching weapons, and add more Jabba's Though With You. You also look low on force generation, so maybe more docking bays. A good trick with Court is to play Hoth and Yavin docking bays, then use Imperial Decree to cancel force drain bonuses. More Oo-ta Goo-ta Solo? (V) too. My friend played Court with an interesting strategy. Deploy deserts, then when you opponent deploys characters deploy Sandwirl. Use Sandcrawlers or Desert Walkers to protect your characters from Sandwhirl. Once the Sandwhirl leaves the table, play Hunting Party to capture your opponent's missing characters and then feed them to the Rancor or Sarlacc.
