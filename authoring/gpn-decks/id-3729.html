 The Description
Slightly more bizzare than the usual Player's Preconstruct. The harsh habitat of Tatooine has some even harsher denizens- with surprising abilities. Almost every card in this deck works in a superpowered combo with another.

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck has a decent amount of force production and some very powerful characters. The extra sideboard cards and already vast array of combos make this deck more flexible than any of the other Players Preconstructs to date. Should be fun to play.
