 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Drains/Beats/Retrieval deck with some nasty tracking tech. Standard QMC retrieval and drains (you know it, you love it, you don't need to hear it again). Beats are a new thing for QMC, in my opinion... the mains & toys can either stay at home and help hold the fort vs. an offensively based deck, or roam through your opponent's sites with ease, beating the crap out of stuff left and right.
Tracking tech: use Keeping The Empire Out Forever to pull Levitation (V) (aka Tracking For Dummies, light side), use Lev (V) to pull Artoo, use Mirax to drop Artoo to bottom of Used pile. This should all be done as the first few actions of your turn after activating... this helps you bottom-track a couple of destiny 6 cards very easily.

Read the decklist, check out what the cards do, give it a try. It's a simple design with some of the oldest concepts in the book. If you need me to explain in further detail, go to the forums and throw out a question, or give me an email (kdavid@pacifier.com).

   slim1256     The biggest weakness I see with this deck is that it doesn’t play to QMC’s strengths. First off, 25 characters is way too many… you need the quick flip, especially against the Deal, but basically half your deck is characters once you remove your starting stuff and locations! This pushes your average destiny waaaaay down. Second, Sal’Tor is somewhat redundant in this deck, since you can 1) Tunnel Vision every turn and 2) pull Chewie’s Bowcaster and Luke’s Saber (and can’t pull X-Wing Cannons), that leaves only three weapons you’d pull – which should be easy enough to pull with KtEOF. Second, the SquAssign route for space is very inefficient for this deck. I’ve seen occupying Bespin done very well with the Home One and lots of rinky dink, high forfeit pilots… but either way, you’ve dedicated 14 cards to just HOLDING Bespin… that’s way too many, IMHO. Yes, you do need a low cost option for flipping, and that might best be done with All Wings combo, Mirax and ship, and Escape Pod for a Houjix until you can continue your setup. I’d swap out the Combo Paths for regular Paths, the destiny is SOO much better. In summary, the way this deck is designed, I think you’re going to find that you’re drawing lots of character/ships to battle with, but you’re gonna get beaten because you can’t draw high enough destiny to hit most of the DS’s baddies (or crack their immunity to attrition), and your characters are gonna get knocked off one by one because you don’t have enough tricks up your sleeve (in the form of Interrupts and Effects). The strength QMC has is that you can use all those tricks to maximum effectiveness, because you can “tunnel vision” for whatever you need whenever you need it. Hope this helps!
