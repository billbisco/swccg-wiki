 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Ability^3-I make all of my deck around war mongering, this one being no exception and with Scum and Villiany ever time I decide to fight I activate one force for firing my weapons, get a character back as that none of my characters have perminate weapons or maintance costs, and retrive 2 force. How can fighting be so benifical? And thanks to the Ability*3 I don't play with more than one copy of any but my most cruical characters saving my card selection for better things.

I realize the deck is not space friendly, it's ment to be more useful as go ground grab and go. If Confronted in space Flee with this deck the only reason there is any space in it at all is to try to keep Occupation in play.

Boba Fett (V) and Boba Fett's Blaster Rifle (V) allow you to get most of the important effects and keep yourself in those wonderful bounties. As much as I hate to say it, if Boba is not in your starting hand you have to use Audience Chamber to get him as well as his weapon.

Krayt Dragon Bones (V) only because I never liked having Sarlacc in my deck now I don't have to.

Interrogation Array speak for itself, and the Binders well once I do get all of my necessary effects, Boba Fett moves fastest and he can get the most people.

Docking Bay 94- With Mara Jade=Anti There Is Good In Him. Capture with Mara Jade, then feed Luke to the Sarlacc.

Other Card- Feltipern Trevagg and stun rifle become a great combo when at Lar's Moisture Farm. The lightside might see it as a waist of 3 force to go there and if they do, you can sniper one for a captive, hopefully giving the captive with bounty to Boba with Binders and then try another durning your opponent's battle phase. Feltipern is just cannon fodder, but Boba will be getting you some nice retrival out of the trade.

Between Scum And Villiany, Dugeon, and Bounties there is a good bit of force retrival. I also like the Jabba's Through With You since I will be using several character weapons and it is an easier battle destiny adder than some other dark side cards.
