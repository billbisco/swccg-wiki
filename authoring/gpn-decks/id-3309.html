 The Strategy
 Back to Top 

The objective of this deck is not the best but it lets you pull bunker and deactivated the shield generator and "the shield is down" {lets you pull back door to relocate}so your ready to blow up the shield generator. You can basically let most of your weaker scouts die in attempt to hold down the bunker because you can retrieve them once you blow it up. You should have drew up 2 explosive charges or "throw me another charge" by your thrid turn, you need 2 explosive charges on the bunker to blow it up usally(you can track a 4 and 3 or something like that). It stuns your opponent when he has to lose 8 force mostly from his reserve deck because he has to put his hand in used pile and you get to retrieve 8 force. Even if your losing really bad you can turn the game around in just 2 destiny draws. Later in the game force drain a bunch from endor because if they come to kill you "close air support" lets you draw 2 battle destinies (on to total power only)(when you have tala 1 and 2 in the air)(junkin with a concussion grenade is really good)(crack shot makes all of they're hit characters lost immediately, and restricted deployment w/ goo nee tay will make them pay lots)(you can also activate one extra force for each endor. site) (even if your weaker you can kill them). In this deck there is enough characters to attack them AND hold down endor. You can retrieve a scout of ability less than 3 every turn which means chewie is invincible.
Evenally after a couple battles and force drains they will die. Be kind with the ratings and any suggestions just let me know.
