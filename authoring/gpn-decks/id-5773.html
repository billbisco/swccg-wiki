 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck has worked really well in playtesting vs Deal and MKOS and did well against my friend’s AOBS. What I really want to get is feedback on character and interrupt selection, as well as the starting effects.

I want plenty of weapons on the table without having to rely on EPP’s that I don’t own. That is pretty much what keeps me from starting Sai’torr instead of (V)CD, so I would like to hear some opinions on what I should do in that regard. Once I acquire Chewbacca’s Bowcaster I will likely replace Vibro-Ax and then swap C, Protector for (V) Chewbacca. Right now I’m using Control & TV + Yoda’s Hut as an alternative to the JCC and 2x Speak With The Jedi Council, but I like being able to subtract 2 from attrition with Yoda at the Hut, so I’d like some advice in that direction. Activation has been fine, so I’m not sure whether or not I want to change, especially with AOBS likely to be more prevalent. I want to try to fit in a couple copies of Sense, as well as 1x Inconsequential Barrier and Desperate Reach Combo each to counter Imperial Barrier. Any advice regarding my character selection would be of great help as well.

<Strategy>

There are basically two scenarios that this deck will run up against: Decks with Imperials/Vader and decks without Imperials. Against Scum decks the objective is pretty simple. Concentrate on just winning battles, consistently saving characters with (V) CD, (V) 2-1B, and Bacta Tank. As long as I have characters to deploy I can intitiate battles which means I can get more characters back. Just battle to the end. Alternatively, there will be decks with Imperials which will eliminate Luke as a fixture in my battle plan. In this situation a bit more finesse is required. I’m going to want to concentrate on winning battles to stack on I Feel The Conflict, so I want to concentrate on ways to win battles. This is what Obi OOP does. Once I use Skull to place him OOP I can add 5 to power in all of my battles with I Can’t Believe He’s Gone. If I have Higher Ground in hand I can use it to reduce an opponent’s character’s power by 4 instead of hitting him/her. This can net me an advantage of 9 in power even without my characters’ power and a destiny draw. The Claw Fish can help me tweak my destinies to what I exactly need, and Merc Armor also helps in this regard. The Vine Snakes are there for a bit of fun, and since they reduce characters’ power, they work towards my objective. A fun combination is attaching a Vine Snake to a character and then using Higher Ground to make them power -4 (Remember that a Vine Snake makes them lost when their power is zero). In the game against my friend’s AOBS I killed the Emperor with this method.

<Combos>

Vine Snake + Higher Ground- Explained this one already

Wedge + Corran- Cancel a destiny both in space and on the ground

Obi-Wan+ Skull + I Can’t Believe He’s Gone- +5 Power in battle for just one force

Luke + Lightsaber Proficiency + Bionic Hand- I pull these at the start of the game with (V) Rycar to make Luke power +5 and Immune to attrition < 9.

Flash of Insight + Colo Claw Fish- I have nothing to hide (and my tracking’s got much to be desired) so I can figure out my destinies in advance

So that’s the rough strategy. I would appreciate any constructive criticism that anyone would be able to give. If anyone has any additional questions or comments just respond in the Ratings Feedback Thread (RFT).

I'd like to credit Shewski for the Skull idea and Statman for the concept of Vine Snakes as a weapon of mass destruction.

Thanks for reading, God bless, and MTFBWY

Even with it, you don't have the beats TIGIH needs...and I did read the strategy section where you said you didn't have EPPs. It looks like Sai'tor (V)s only use is basically for Han's stick- it is a rare situation where you deploy Leia and would rather have her stick than her gun (and she can pull her gun). 2 Nabrun Leids is overkill since so many DS decks play IAO, cut at least 1. Higher Ground is an okay card, but I'd be tempted to use better cards with better destiny (e.g. OOC, TBS, etc.) We Don't Need Their Scum doesn't do much, Bacta Tank is useless-ized by CCT and is also too expensive for the force generation you're getting if you can't find Redemption (V) (and a system to deploy it to, and backup for it, and assuming it doesn't die). Speaking of which, I would put trading for JCC and SWTJC near the top of my priority list (alongside the EPPs...why aren't they on your wants list?).    Darth Hoth     It's been a while since I last did this, but here it goes... The Dejarik site could cause some problems for you. You have it, but no dejariks or holograms. All it takes is a simple Monnok to get a force drain of 2 every turn. Even with the 2 Escape Pods (V) in there. You don't have many good interrupts in there, so if I controlled the gameboard, I'd just grab that and hold the 2 drain. There's one problem with the whole Vine Snake idea. Even when they're attached, they can still be attacked by characters. It has to be a one turn deal: deploy the creature, attach, cause the character to be lost, end turn. If it goes to the opponent's turn and the Vine Snake is still attached, a total of 3 (ferocity+defense value) isn't going to hold up against much. You never really said what Nabrun Leids is in there for, and unless it's a good reason, I'd take it out. IAO, which is still a very common card kills it. Your character selection seems decent, along with the weapon selection. I'm guessing the Bionic Hand is V. It's a rather good deck, just not quite a 4.
