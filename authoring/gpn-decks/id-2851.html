I put in binks to counter visage of the emperor, along with brisky morning munchen for him, and put in 2 electropoles for the 7 destiny, and because when he throws it it recycles a 7. Bionic hand also got in as a destiny. Reflection and on the edge replaced our most desperate hour and rebel reinforcemens. Electrobinoculars is in because it lets you see what the next destin in the reserve deck is, helpful with on the edge, and also useful when you can only use one combat card do to I find your lack of faith disturbing. Han, chewie and the falcon replaced captain han and the falcon, and let me take out get to your ships, and without get to your ships, i couldnt effectivley play wedge and red squadron 1 so they got taken out also. I added alter, 2 qui-gons,blaster deflection, honor of the jedi and luke rebel scout.

The biggest strategy in it is that you can get prety much whatever you need. Kal fas gets weapons, Rycar Ryjerd can pull Bionic hand, Electrobinoculars, Reflection, lightsaber proficiency, weapon of a fallen mentor, and mercenary armor, and it puts effects and devices back in the deck, and Yoda can get a sense or an alter when you need it.

All my urchins is great because since my objective limits my ability to initiate battle, it lets me punish my opponent for initiating a battle

Artoo Deeto in red 5 not only does it have the best destiny ever, if you use it as a starfighter, its a power7 maneuver 6 hyperspeed 7 immune to below 5 and a destiny

Insurrection and Aim high prevents any movement to Nabboo, because both sites are interior, and cancels the free stormtrooper garison + Elis Herlot strategy

Mercenary Armor on Leia its a free +3 power, on lando it makes him 9 with armor 4 and immunity below 3


Weapon of a fallen Mentor besides just recycling lightsabers, it also lets you put lightsabers on yoda and mace
