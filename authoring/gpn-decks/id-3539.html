 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Standard TDIGWATT deck, nothing fancy. Just drop and drain. Crush The Rebellion and Bad Feeling Have I help for holding the sites, while Early Warning Network can deploy TIEs from reserve deck as free reacts. I should be getting They Must Never Again Leave This City in a trade soon, and will add it in with Executor for some awsome power and TIE recycling at Bespin. I use all interior sites so I can avoid a speeder beatdown, though I may include one exterior site once I get Blizzard 4. Colo Claw Fish sets up a perfect destiny for All Too Easy, which is great once you flip the objective, causing 8 force loss. It may seem like too many starships, but it's easy to hold Bespin and some TIEs can be moved to Bespin: Cloud City for some power boosts at the sites. Let me know what you think.

pointy-eared-green-dude: Thanks for trading me the I'm Sorry, TDIGWATT is one of my favorites and without I'm Sorry I just couldn't run it right. Glad you think it's good.

Shewski: It's hard to argue with common sense, all points well taken. I would add Dreaded Imp Fleet, but I don't have it. I've been thinking about the low character, too.

catman421: Adding a system would be good, but I think I might opt to drop a few ships, add some characters, and add another site instead (bigger drains). I'm revising right now and will take everything into consideration (dark lightsaber sounds good), but I'm finding that I'm having to cut out a lot of different ideas of both my own and others due to limited deck space. I'll be posting an updated version in the next few weeks, tourney's coming up.
