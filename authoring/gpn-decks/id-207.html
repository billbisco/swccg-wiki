 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First off, winning the podrace should be a sinch. With 4 Podracer Collisions and 1 Hit Racer in this deck that should slow them down considerably. And if you have neither of those in ur hand when you need them, there’s We Must Accelerate Our Plans to get them out. I really like Hit Racer because not only does it cancel their race destiny for that turn but it damages their racer. The Podracer Collisions should slow them down enough should they happen to catch up from Sebulba’s fast start. Once you win the race the game shouldnt be that hard to win, as its pretty hard to come back from a 22 force swing. And should u happen to lose the podrace, you should have pulled You've Never Won A Race to help ease the losses. All of the D-shields are pretty self-explanatory.

For an easy flip just drop a PotF at the imperial city or wait just elis him over somewhere. I have included The Emperor because he adds to the attrition against ur opponent when the objective is flipped which is always nice. Both Empy’s also deploy free to the Imperial Square. Aurra Sing is there to steal lightsabers(not really) and shes good against Jedi. A good idea would be to plop TC-14, Aurra Sing, 4-LOM with gun, and Guri against some Jedi, use TC-14 to reduce power of one, cancel the text of another with 4-LOM and then use Aurra to steal a saber if theyre not using EPPs. I really like using Oh, Switch Off to save ur all-important droids, plus its got a high-destiny. The Incinerator will also get back some of ur droids like P-59. Occupation Shouldn’t be too hard to set up, but with that big force swing from the podrace you might not even need it. You shouldn’t have too much trouble controlling space either. The rest of the deck is pretty much self-explanatory. I hope u like this deck and thanx for taking a look.

Sorry if this aint good enough strat for u, im not good at writing it, thanx for looking and i hope u like it.
