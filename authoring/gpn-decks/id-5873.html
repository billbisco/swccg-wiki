 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
STRATEGY
Rieekan is your friend, pull him with strike planning and swamp with your objective, use swamps text to pull a nudj and then drop the nudj and rieekan to the same site, don’t worry he won’t get eaten. Next turn use Rieekan to start getting out your snow speeders and maneuvering flaps for the matching pilots. Flip your objective next turn by dropping nevander who is also pullable with Rieekan and use Rieekan to pull out kessel, start dropping squadrons and when you feel you have the strength move them to kessel. You should put Rieekan in a speeder and keep him there. Get out the rest of your characters and work on holding the sites while pulling haven to make liberty drop for two less and x-wing squadrons for one. You have enough ground strength to fight off whatever comes to you and with a drain of 4 at kessel and 8 on the ground with the added help of T-47 battle formation you should be ok considering all opponents drains are minus 1. all wings report in for retrieval, the destiny adders for ground support, chewie and leia pull their own weapon, chewie pulls the destiny adders. Artillery for direct damage, and sandwhirl to lock down the desert makes this a pretty solid deck
