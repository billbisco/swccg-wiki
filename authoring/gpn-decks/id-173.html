 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
In the early game activate your forces ASAP. You should be able to get out a ton of X-wings early on. use the S-fiols to pump them up and after you have enough out(like 4 at key systems) make sure to hold a organized attack to make them immune to attrition and pow+1. with 4 beefed up X-wings one armed with a cannon no one will go near the site(power 24 and can shoot stuff down). use the undercover spies and the objective to cancel drains. also use projections, the planet its farthest from to lower the drains. If theres a site you need to take over for whatever reason the jedis can do that for you. use jedis resiclance to keep them going if ya loose one to attrition. The deck plays its self for the most part. It has really good destinys draws but you should still do some tracking before you battle. Use the squadrons to recycle your x-wings and up your differential. Also this helps with using all wings report in b/c you can up the amount on the table by recycling your ships. Even though its a simple desine it works quite well in tournys(well not to sure witht e new set coming out now)
