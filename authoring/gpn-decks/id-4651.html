 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
---Strategy---
Basic strategy is to flip TDIGWATT as quickly as possible. Use walkers to control sites, and starfighters to control sectors. Once flipped, consolidate space forces at Bespin, and build up strength on the ground. Drain and damage (with CCO) until opponent begs for mercy. Then finish em off.

First Turn-
Use TDIGWATT to pull a card (preferably a location). Play any sites in hand. Play a Nemoidian Advisor if possible. Also, if possible, play a walker.

Second Turn-
Repeat turn 1.

Third Turn-
Pull with TDIGWATT. If all 4 cards that can be pulled by TDIGWATT are in play or hand, flip objective. Consolidate forces to prevent absurd beatdowns.

Forth Turn-
By this time, Dark Deal and Cloud City Occuptation should be in play. Inflict the largest amount of damage possible. Build forces and try to occupy all three sites (but make sure the beatdown threat is minimal).

Fifth Turn-
Drain/damage again. Drains should be at least 3 per site. If the decide to come fight, show them why they don’t want to. If they don’t, they’ll be buried in the force drain casket.

---Card choices---

Kitik Keed’tik (V)-
He’s a beast. What more is there to say?

Darth Vader, Darth Lord Of The Sith-
Vader version that’s to stick around. EPP is for beats.

General Veers (V) / Blizzard 1 (V)-
A beast when paired together.

Trample-
Squashes a few characters before battle.

Nemoidian Advisor-
This card is key very early in the game. Grabs otherwise unpullable sites. Enough copies so it can be grabbed by opponent. Also recycles mid to late game.

Imperial Artillery-
Makes weapons hurt more. High destiny too.

Tallon Roll & Dark Manuevers-
Great destiny and starfighter eliminator.

Blizzard 4-
Gets 2 copies cause of its ease of forfeit and its character pull.
