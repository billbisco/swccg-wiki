 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck used to be classic but I changed it to non-classic for states.

First thing pull the executor if you didn’t get in your hand with mob. points. Then during your deploy phase deploy the Executor DB with IAO and any characters you get there, and any systems. Next turn pull the endor DB and if the have battle plan put a walker or vader there. Get an admiral out and put come with me on him to pull pilots. Set up with a Star Destroyer or two at every system and drain. Put Chiraneau at Sullust to maximize drains. Retrieve ISB agents and keep on draining.

Card Choices:

OE+Sense+Alter: Why not the combo OE/TINT? I like sense and alter so I play them a lot.

Mob. Points: I like the early activation and pulling executor.

We’re in Attack Position now: The cheaper deploy for pilots and the direct damage from General Veers.

Ion Cannon: In my area a lot of people run X-win and B-wing decks so i like to disarm them, and i like the forfeit =0.

If you want any more explanations just ask and ill post it up here.

Please be constructive.
