 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
My brother likes to pull off a retrieval 11, lose 11 with Boonta Eve Podrace and Watto's Box.

3/4 of Interrupts deal with podracing and become good destiny only after the podrace. What does he need?

After that, it is pretty much a power struggle on Tatooine...

Main Question: What should I be able to do after the podrace???

He needs some help, and I know you guys can give it. Any feedback would be greatly appreciated. Be kind with the rating if it is to be horrible...this is my brother's deck, not mine. Thanks again!


 The Last 5 Comments
   swcironecg     A three, not because it is good, but because TUSKEN RAIDERS RULE!!! That is definately the direction I think this deck should take. Don't use Gamorreans, put in more Raiders instead. Use one of the unique raiders from A New Hope or Special Edition as your representative. Include another copy of Taym Dren-Garen. Take out the Watto cards, the Wounded Wookies, maybe a few of the Podracer interrupts, the extra Podracers, the weapons, and just a few of the locations. Put in Bantha (V), Den Of Thieves, Twi'lek Advisor (to pull Den Of Thieves), maybe 1 or 2 Gaffi Sticks, Chall Bekan, and DEFINATELY 3 to 5 Batha Foders (destiny 4 used interrupt that pulls Tusken Raiders, Banthas, and Taym Dren-Garen!). If you have them, use either Jabba The Hutt or Jabba and put in Scum And Villany. With a little work a Tusken Raider deck can be huge fun and pretty good.
   AdmiralThrawn36     i think he's trying to pull off to much at the same time. Get rid of the podracing and focus on controlling the game-add high destiny interrupts, etc.
