 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Here I start with one of the two starting interrupts, putting down either S-Foils or We Didn't Hit it, Battle Plan, and Civil Disorder (tell me if I can't put down Civil disorder, cuz the card text says "on opponent's side of table.")

Anyway, starting on Coruscant, I go in the air, to control it in order to lessen the Imperials hold. Then, I try to put down the generic sites, which will allow me to pull out Coruscant Celebration.

I also have my Dresselain Commandos, and I try to put those in the Cantina, where they have a boost against non-unique aliens, plus they have +1 power.

Finally, I many cards to recover from my lost pile, including Noble Sacrifice and All Wings Report In. Finally, I've got my two Capital Star Ships, one which I can really stack for power, and the other, the Liberty I can forfeit to my used pile.

   Shewski     Bluealeta, you can edit your decks once they are posted. It is usually looked upon more kindly if you do that instead of reposting it after a few edits. But you're new, so we'll let it slide this time ;) Start HFTMF- it is far more versatile. Civil Disorder is non startable since the effects need to say "deploy on table" or "deploy on your side of the table", nothing else works. As it is, this deck is kinda a pile in today's environment- no real overriding strategy or focus. You basically are hoping to draw all the stuff you need. You can be oh so much more efficent. Run this as a Hidden Base X Wing Swarm- we have lots of good examples on the site, or you can follow the objective for a pretty good deck outline. Nowadays you should be able to get your sites out of the deck ASAP- no one likes floating 0s. Check out the virtual cards at SWCCGPC.com, or the welcome to GPN/FAQ thread on the boards for some more ideas how to bring this up to speed. Or as I mentioned before, you can ask for more ideas in the Feedback Thread.
