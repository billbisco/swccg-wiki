 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
How well would this deck work as it is now? I know the dueling side is probably too weak. When the new Bad Feeling Have I (V) comes out, I'll pack Deep Hatred, and ditch a couple of Vaders. Preferrably, I'll have two Lord Vaders. And I'll probably have to get a They Will Be Now Match ForYou to let Maul duel.

Do the effects in here make any sense? The Twi'lek's will let me pull them, but I'm not convinced that Crush The Rebellion is worth the slot. And is my space package OK?

Which other points do you see? At least, the beatdown potential in here should be pretty good.


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     Crush is definitely worth the slot. I would axe the Spaceport DB and go with another mobile one: CC DB. I would start Crush-MP combo, and Rack. Pull Carida, pull normal version of IAO by ditching combo, and then go to town! I think one extra Visage is sufficent, but maybe I'm just a cheapskate. I don't like Young Fool at all. Use WMAOP instead of Advisor (and pack BF: Bridge) for more generation. I'd throw in some You Are Beaten to max the beat down. (and I monnok combo)
   Cam Solusar     This deck looks pretty good. Although I've never played HDADTJ, I've heard that the beatdown version is much better than the dueling version, so you might want to concentrate on the battling. I would also add 2x Some Rescue as protection against Out Of Commission. The last (and only time) I played against Hunt Down, I placed Vader OOP and that pretty much ended the game for my opponent. This deck looks good. MTFBWY
