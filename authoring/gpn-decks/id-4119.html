 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The strategy is pretty simple... Just initiate a bunch of battles, this lets you retrieve a lot of force (with some cards in the deck- I forget which, I know Scum and Villiany does it...) Even if your gonna lose the battle, you can always thermal detonate the whole thing. And my retrieved force lets me keep getting my thermal detonaters and timer mines back.

I use Jungles and Forest because they're compatible to lots of planets (I guess?) And I start with Death Star cause it kicks ass!

Thats pretty much it, just suicide bomb, then retrieve what you lost.

[Bounty Hunter... whats an index?]
[Revelation, why do you score me so badly? Ever heard of a combo deck, you see some decks play differently than others, like... in mtg, there are some decks that you just play creatures and try to kill, but they're are others that don't kill with creatures, but instead with other cards. Thats what this is, you just based your score of 1 on the fact that I dont have high enough destiny! ARG! I DONT WANT HIGH DESTINY. Reason 1: Character's usually have a destiny number of 3 or lower... say an opponent has three characters at a site, and I have 1 with a thermal detonater. I use my detonater and draw three destiny. Lets say I had a bunch of high destiny in my deck, that would mean I have a greater chance of drawing destiny's like 4, 5, and 6. That also means that I dont detonate anything they control! NOT COOL! Now, If I had more 1's 2' and 3's in my deck, I would have a greater chance of pulling those numbers and killing lots of cards with thermal detonater... Reason 2: I dont need destiny since my deck is not based on winning battles, its based on killing cards with a luck factor, and then force draining... I also use lots of cards that make them enable lots of force to do things like initiate battles. Come on now Revelation, my deck at least gets a score of 2.
