 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The idea of this deck is simple, keep everyone on the Death Star, and just sit back, and let CPI just start blown everything to oblivian. I mean, think about...this deck is virtually unstoppable:

…No one plays Attack Run anymore, so you won't have to worry about it getting blown away itself. You can screw over people badly who play lightsaber combat decks, cause all you'd have to do is blow up Naboo. Blow up Tatooine to get rid of any Profit podracing decks. Blow up Coruscant to stop Senate decks, and kill all of your opponent's Jedi council members. You can force drain big time on Alderaan, and no one is going to try and stop you. Also, the only access I'm giving my opponent to the Death Star is at the docking bay...and with all of the presence on the Death Star, he won't stand a chance.
