 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First Turn: Pull Carida, then ditch YCHF & MP for Aratech Corporation. Use LLL, IAO & SP, and your obj. to get three Ralltiir sites. Deploy Carida and Aratech on it. Second Turn: Use your obj. and LLL to get two Ralltiir Sites, IAO & SP to get the Y4 db. Third Turn: Use LLL and your obj. to get last two Ralltiir sites, you can put some stuff down and flip. The idea is you have a bunch of Power 5 Speeders reacting around. If you don?t have force for react, play Combat Readiness?that?s why there?s 7. You need to send somebody after the Necklace- guess who?s elected? Good ?ol Palpatine. Ok, then you set up Overseeing It Personally and the Necklace, and all your drains on Ralltiir are 3. That?s a good thing. Presence makes Ralltiir a bg system for the purposes of Battle Plan/Order. Once all your bikers are down, retrieve with Abyssin. Basically, you?re untouchable on Ralltiir (you have total battle destiny +7, also), and except for your brief expedition to Yavin 4, that is where you stay. He doesn?t drain you much elsewhere, and you drain him a lot on Ralltiir. The 'Super Chimaera' should hold Ralltiir nicely. You can afford two bikers per Ralltiir site. That's Power 10, and if you react only two more (you can do more, of course) to a battle your foolish opponent (props to Shewski!) initiated, you have Power 20 and a destiny. That's a good thing. Matchups all play the same.

   Shewski     I don't think you need all those locations. 3-4 should do fine. I also think you can free up a startable effect slot and ditch LLL. Think about it, you only get 2 force plus whatever the opponent gives you with this deck, you definitely don't want to flip until turn 2. At those 3-4 sites, ditch YCHF for Sec. Precautions (V), which adds 1 to generation. As a bonus, it counteracts Ounee Ta in the sense that if you give your opponent more force, at least you get some as well. Have you head of Grimmtaash? You are asking for serious trouble by packing so many multiples. You're probably going to be holding mutiple Combat Readiness, BST, and Bikes.... it could get very ugly. You can also use Mosep to bring on the pain. I would recommend toning down your BST and Bikes (maybe 7 each), and cutting Readiness down to 3 or so. You only need one good battle to work with. In their places, you have tons of good biker support cards including: Accelerate (bike Dark Maneuvers), Scout guns (BS Gear, Compact Firepower, Freeze), Unique BST, High Speed Tactics (huge against LU), Relentless Tracking (uses Aratech to its fullest), Sneak Attack (more BD for your scouts), and Search and Destroy (always great to cause more damage). This is a good start for a Biker Scout deck, but there is much more you can do with it. Icebreath made a decent bike deck for classic a while back. The main reason this is a low rating is a Grimtaash at any point in the game can kill your entire hand.
   Darth Hoth     I like this, and it's well thought out. Makes opponent come to you, but you can still win even if the opponent doesn't. I would include Imperial Reinforcements (V) for some extra retrieval. I don't think you have very much multiple destiny stuff, so I would add Imperial Domination (V).
