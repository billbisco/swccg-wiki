 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Basically this deck should include An unusual amount of Fear and 10 defensive shield’s but I don’t have them so I didn’t include them. The idea of this deck is simple deploy on naboo and use massive drain to beat up your opponent’s The key card here is Padme with her ability to deploy both Qui Gon and Obi from reserved deck and use Sai tor to deploy their weapon’s… then use insurrection to deploy naboo DB and secure route (take it out of your deck using Wokling) and you have a drain of 10 from other two naboo sites. Use Yoda and Ki adi Mudi to control the DB and remember to keep Qui gon on the middle Naboo site because you will use him to defeat any dark jedi that dear to show up. When this happen remember to move obi to the Lightsaber combat place to allow Qui gon to use his combat card’s. Use Luke , Leia and Mace to fight away from Naboo. And Han Chewi and the Falcon to cancel Bright Center of the Universe. When you play Dark side Duel deck’s use Strike Blocked to cancel pesky Maul Strikes and try to deploy Lightsabre proficiency on obi to Keep him safe from The circle is now complete with power 9 no Vader will be able to kill him:)You can activate 7+ force on the first turn that sufficient for Padme and Obi:)and if you draw you can have a 4 force drain on the second turn:)) i think thats a good result:) I Think this deck is a good one able to defeat any dark side deck. I hope you give me some suggestion and tip’s and the most important thing “HAVE A GOOD TIME PLAYING IT”!!!!!

   Wedge231     Why do you play Ki-Adi-Mundi? Your Leia should probably be Leia (v) for anti-senate or R3 Leia to cancel drains. If you're going to play Insurrection, it should be the combo version, and you should be playing Home One: Docking Bay for even more activation. Also, just so you know, the Naboo: Docking Bay can't go in between the two interior sites (see Naboo location deployment rules), so Secure Route will add 2 to one drain at most. However, the darkside will be playing the anti-combat shield anyway to cap your drains to 2 apiece. As a final suggestion, I would use at least one or two more ships for space. Paying for Battle Order can be a real pain, and you have no way to supply 7-ability to get around Zuckuss in Mist Hunter, which is probably the most popular ship these days.
   Shewski     I don't think the Naboo DB is needed, instead use Speak to pull the JCC. That frees you up to use Rycar. I'd use Jedi Luke (if you have him) x2 over Mace x2. Use a different Leia, like (V). Add Angry Lando and some other ability 4 characters like Corran, Phylo, or Tawss. You can use them to start fights and then use Run Luke Run to send jedi Luke over to beat on the opponent (plus jedi presence). Remove multiple sabers, and the generic one. I think that you can run some SAC. Add another LS Prof. For your 7 destiny cards, I recommend some r2 in Red 5 (good for SAC and combat). Nine seems a bit much, you could well with less and some good tracking (naked 3po?).
   Darth Hoth     Looks good, but could be better. Instead of Insurrection, why not the combo or Rayc Ryjerd (V), lets you pull Lightsaber Proficiency. I would stick to interior sites only, not the docking bay, that way dark decks need to deploy to Naboo or supply the docking bay. The character selection is a little slim, I would suggest trying to expand it a little. You're using Sai'torr Kal Fas (V), but have multiple copies of the weapons? You don't need to have multiple copies. Control and Alter aren't very good cards, IMO. If you drop the docking bay, drop the Secure Routes too, but I would drop 1 either way (you're using Wokling (V) to pull it). For the destiny 7 cards, use weapons, and Baragwins to retrieve them.
