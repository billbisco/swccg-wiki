Pre-game:
Please remember this is a CLASSIC format deck. Otherwise there would be more defensive shields (Including DDTA to pull Decentóof which there would only be one in that caseÅE, etc. This deck is yet untested in tournament play. This deck is not designed to ìwin,ÅErather it is designed to complete an Attack Run during the LS playerís first turnóthat is itís ENTIRE purpose; as such there is no endgame in this deck (and hence no Lando for use with Lobot (V)). Also please see my (FUTURE) accompanying article on statistical analysis for reference to all statements regarding probability.


ìAdvanced preparationÅE
Draw your hand. You should have a Lobot (V) or Tunnel Vision in hand (90.6% chance of getting one; 45.6 % chance of getting both).
Activate 5. If you didnít get a lobot in opening hand, use your TV to search your force pile for a lobot (66% chance heís there). If you fail to get lobot you either (a) suck at shuffling, or (b) you should not play the lotteryóever. There is a 3.2% chance of failure to get a lobot via this technique. If you didnít get a lobot, you loseÅEorry.
Use MBOís text to pull a Yavin site (either one). Use Insurrection to pull Cloud City: DB. If youíve activated any of these it doesnít matter. Only use a TV to pull the Docking Bayónot the Yavin sites. Put lobot at the CC:DB (free). Use any computer interfaces you have. If you donít have a computer interface use a TV to look in your force pile. If you donít get a CI (V)ÅEgain you lose (and you suck at shufflingóthereís a greater % chance to get a CI than lobot, as youíre running 12x CIs and only 8x lobotóso Iím not going to bother with the % here). Use 3 force to pull DWTE. Keep using CIs (and NOT TVís) until you run out. At this point you should have most of what you need (Luke, Red 5, Advance prep, Fly casual) or some TVís in hand. Proceed to next part.

ìAnalyze the information in this R2 unitÅE
Your hand should be roughly 12-25 cards or soóand you should have decent in hand or be ready to find one. Deploy a Droid merchant to Y4:DB. Use Cell 2178 (V) to deploy R2 from reserve (Free); activate 1 force. Play spaceport speeders. You should draw destiny somewhere between 4-8. If youíre not satisfied, deploy an R2 from hand and play another spaceport speeders. Use TVís at this point to pull CIís. If you see something ìvitalÅEto the attack run in your force pile no problemóuse the force until itís in your used pile. Proceed to next part.

ìAccelerate to attack speedÅE
Cards to deploy if you have them: Great Shot kid, Menace fades, DWTE, The other Y4 site. Deploy DMís to other Y4 sites. Flip MBO once you have 3 DMís down. Once you have so few cards in your reserve deck that you canít play CI or Spaceport Speeders anymore, use Decent to recycle and begin anew (this will happen every game). Hopefully you were smart and got a Decent with a TV if you didnít get one with a CI. This sounds complicated so far, but you should have be using CIís like crazy by this pointóyou should have like 60% of your deck in your hand. Proceed to next part.


ìHits away!ÅE
Deploy D* from hand or reserve now. Use Fly casual and advance prep. Remember to lose those extra copies of cards to get out your defensive shields. DWTE should be out. You should have either been (a) tracking or (b) left a CI in your hand to make sure you succeed with an Attack Run. Most of the low destinies (1ís, 2ís) should be in your hand. Most of your deck should consist of CIís, Lobots, and R2s. Now blow it up.
Enter the ìsurpriseÅEfactor: Your opponent will be blown awayÅEiterally and figuratively. Watch their facial expression as you proceed to make them lose 14 force on your very first turn.

Post-game:
Computer Interface (V) is an incredibly perfect card to abuse like crazy. In classic there is no grabber (at least from their D-shields). DWTE brutalizes decks like walkers or SYCFA. If youíre opponent is playing aliens, well, the 14 force is still crazy too. CI is the ultimate tracking card. Youíll be surprised how many CIís will pull 1 or more CIís from reserveócreating a practically infinite loop. After your first turn, you should be able to ìcycleÅEthrough your deck VERY easily and stack all your high destinies on your reserveóthere are several 6ís. Your battle destinies for the remainder of the game should be consistently 6.

If you donít believe any of my claims, build this deck and play it by yourself. You donít need and opponent to test the first turn.

Advantages:
Average destiny after second turn is 6
(A dummy could track a 6 in this deck)
Massive number of Controls means you win SAC wars
Decent retrieval (Great Shot kid, Nar shadda [track a 1 or 2], luke (V), advance prep)
Never loosing vital cards to drains, etc. (the extra 7x lobots or 4x R2-D2s are easy drain fodder)
Reducing drain bonuses/satisfying battle plan after turn 1
Weapons display (V) in effect after turn 1
+8 deploy of all imperials/imp starships
Imps and imp starships loose immunity (i.e. executor dies quickly)
[and finally] The ability to cause a 14 card force loss on the first turnóthey have NO choice but to lose cards blind off the top of their reserve. With a little luck they will lose a card which is ìvitalÅEto their deck.

Disadvantages:
It sucks to not have a plan for the game beyond your 1st turn.
It sucks if they play aliens.
It sucks if they play BHBM and get the emperor out their first turn.
It sucks if they manage to play any dark jedi their first turn.
It sucks if you canít pull lobot or CI.
It sucks if they battle you on the ground (see 51st cards).
It sucks if luke dies.
It sucks if they successfully get a set for stun off.
It sucks to try and find 12 or more copies of Computer interface.
