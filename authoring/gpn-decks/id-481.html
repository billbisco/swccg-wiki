 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
WELL, this is a pretty easy one to learn.

EARLY GAME--you should be able to flip within the first 2 or three turns once you've done that grab your poitical effects and start recycling senators. the poly effs act like a reactor terminal and you get back those high destiny cards. Get your ground forces to Naboo and believe me its pretty easy to hold with your guys.

MID-GAME-- Hold 1 or two Naboo sites, if playuing against Invasion get control of The Throne Room and the site before it, You want to keep them out at all costs. Get AMidala to the senate to set up your retrieval engine with her. Remember to keep track of your senators bonuses, you can loose track easily check every battle and plus the poly effects are just great for that. Use your ships to hold the Naboo system.

END-GAME-- Battle and retireve, repeat until done

I know the strat isn't very big but there really isn't much of one but here are some card explinations:

LEIA? WHAT'S THE DEAL?!?!---cancel a force drain, by retieving one force?! sure its only twice but damn it good!
young Obi is cheaper to deploy to Naboo
Qui-gon's saber can deploy to Quiggy or Obi so good things there
Secure route is just great drain booster
Phylo is quick ability and forfiet fodder
WHY NO COR SYS?---protection from bombers and TIES----I don't have the shield, or the effect for that matter
Depa is a decent Jedi,,,,actually considering replacing her---any advice?

MATCH-UPS

DS seatne, great game but should win, just keep control of the senate and its over

LS Combat, You base obn Naboo, you have the Jedi AND the sabers, but no combat cards only do it if you have to battle them out of the area

Invasion, See above but if you're lazy---Keep control of the throne room and get rid of their droids when they come to you

Hunt Down, Weaker version of Combat again let them come to you and battle your way out

WATTO, deploy heavy to the junkyard and keep watto of the table

TIES, tough win---but just hold Naboo, drain on the ground and pick your battle in the air, wait for them to spread thin then go for the jugular
