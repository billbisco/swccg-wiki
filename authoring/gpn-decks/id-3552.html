 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Using Hidden Base, I deploy Rendezvous Point and another system (different every time). HFTMF grabs squadron assignments and superficial damage. Since this deck uses alot of weapons, superficial damage is important. Of the 3 admiral's orders, Concentrate All Fire is the most important one. Because of this AO, there have been battles where I can add well over 20 more power. I use Home 1 and Defiance as transportation (i use defiance because it adds to HTBs. )Red 7-10 are used because they draw an additional destiny. The signals grab the remaining effects. DARF allows for the recovery of a starship each turn. Haven reduces deploy cost significantly and rebel fleet cancels force drains (one a turn). I use several copies of a-wing based weapons to add power and elimanate other starships. I use more conncussion missiles than cannons because the cannons can only target starfighters. I have used this deck twice. One time, against a Sienar retrival deck, i won by a huge amount. The other time was against a senate deck. I lost, mainly due to misplaying the deck (I flipped the objective too soon).
