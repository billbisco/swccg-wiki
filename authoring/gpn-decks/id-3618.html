 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The strategy is deploy Artoo at the beginning of the game, find Han and deploy him with his blaster. Shoot during control phases and cancel opponent's battles with Fire Extingushers. Cancel out drains while controlling the ground using Leia and Harc Seff if needed. Battle Plan will make opponent have to use alot of additional force each turn to drain, while you use little force to retrieve and cancel drains (provided you control the battlegrounds). You should only need to control one site to give the needed drains each turn, just use Lightsabers and Lightsaber Proficiency. Move Han and Artoo when needed with Nabrun Lieds (usually only once per game). Brainiac gives an amazing surprise to your opponent in this deck when he deploys cheaply to a battle initated against your lone Han using Out of Somewhere. It's a combo that really works. I suppouse you could use space in this deck, but I've found that it's better to let your opponent temporarily control space and ground so he will deploy his Battle Order Defensive Shield (which almost everyone plays with). When he does this, you lay out your Battle Plan Defensive Shield and optionally Wars Not Make One Great to make drains cost +7 force to initiate! Don't worry, I've never come up against a battleground I couldn't control- and I dbout you would either. This is the deck that caused me to redesign all of my Dark Side decks by adding 3x Sniper.

   Shewski     While not a beginner deck, I do feel this is not up to tournament standards. Your idea is good- cancel battles with r2 and pick them off with natural sniper Han. My concern is generation- and the lack therof. You have many strategies that need Force to work- Harc and ICBW- and there does not seem to be enough to go around. Also, if you get your +4 drain modifier, you will have to pay that as well. The lack of a small space fleet will hurt as well- if you could get a space presence you would not have to pay your own tax. Nabrun will not work in most tourneys due to IAO. What is your strategy if your opponent avoids you? I think you would lose in a straight drain race. You probably will not be battled anywere, but that's the way you want it. My main concern is generation- you would easily get a 3 with more. Secondary concern is your offensive punch. If the DS avoids you completely, can you win? I'd recommend holding on retrival until you get enough activation to pay for it (Secret Plans) and adding in a little space. This may work as a QMC deck. Instead of all the evasion interrupts, R2 can be them all in one!
