 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Ok, start the two sites (be sure to show them combat readiness before and then shuffle it back in your deck), if they are doing racing play sebulba's racer. Drop maul early to the landing site for free, pull your other sites, and set up drains. Feel free to drop a beat force to wherever the opponent is playing. Set up your destinies, and use omni box to kill the opponents tracking. Use the signals to pull your effects, play the phantom menace to back up maul. Generally rape your opponent, but be sure to have fun. (lol)

Card Choices:
Different Mauls: Monnok protection, coruscant maul is not bad against jedi.
Sith Infiltrator: Completely anti-Hidden Base. Cloak, probe, and repeat.
Security Precautions: 'nother anti-hiddenbase. the extra force is not bad, either.
Vader's Obs: if you track correctly, knocking off luke at an oppertune moment is great.
Sebulba's Podracer: Destroy any podracing fools, even the most dedicated ones.

This deck completely destroys all it has come across (WYS, MWYHL, HB, QMC), and the lowest win out of 8 games is by 27.

Emerson "Sparkz" Sklar
Ulaire Cantea

Note: If the area anyone is playing in is big on a certain deck type and not another, feel free to add/subtract cards to modify it.
