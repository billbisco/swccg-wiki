 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I have tuned it up. I don't have that bones card everyone seems to be talking about, so don't put that in response.

I put some bountys in there because if you can't flip the objective you can still retrieve force through bountys. I also put them in there because Bossk w/mg receives a special bonus.

I added a whole all the trap doors I have. I also put Sarlaac in there.

This objective is not too hard to flip, I don't think.

   yodaindahouze     here's what needs to be dropped: P-59 P-60>these can be effectively played in an imperial deck not a "feed 'em to the sarlacc" deck kitik keedkak>if you're going to use him, use the virtual. den of thieves disarmed imperial artillery what to add: definitely more than 1 boba fett. he is key with the hidden weapons(which you also need about 4 of) it also could use more bounty hunter cards: another bossk, ig-88, 4-LOM and their guns scum and villainy can be cancelled so you need another one. other than that, the deck looks good. you may want to add some starships(i.e. boba in ship or bossk in ship) and tatooine and the docking bay to get to your opponent's characters. don't worry about force generation, the bounty hunters can take care of themselves if you have enough of them. my friend plays this deck and it is very effective, so stick with it. good luck.
   Shewski     Still needs some work. Locations can't be pulled, so activation is tight. You should start a 3rd effect w/ Prepared Defenses- I previously suggested Power of the Hutt. I think this deck will be served better removing the Gamorreans and focusing on BH-related interrupts. Quietly Observing too. Interrupts I recommend are Oota (V) and Jabba's Through With You. Locations should be the passenger deck, and then use WMAOP to pull the Flagship Bridge or use A Dark Time (V) to pull caves. I also think you'll be ok with 1 Rancor. You have a fine amount of Trap Doors.
