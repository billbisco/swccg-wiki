 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     I'd go with something else instead of Insurrection combo, since you really are getting out only the DB. You want to stay inside to avoid AT AT beats. I'd replace one Dash/Outrider with Wedge and his Ship. The Bespin Defense Fighters are not that good, even with immunity. I'd much rather see stronger space presence. Too Close for comfort isn't needed, especially with Blaster Prof. Pick one or the other, I think. You don't need weapon Lev, you have KTEOF. I'd add some more evasion stuff, like Blast the Door Kid, Rebel Barriers, Dodge, Run Luke Run, etc. If you want to do buddy interrupts, run 1 copy of Gift, Skywalkers, Warrior's Courage and then pull the one that makes the most sense. 2 Life Debts are great!
