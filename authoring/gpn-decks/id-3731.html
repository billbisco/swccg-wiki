 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Strategy: Leave Luke to Vader. The rest is in Maul?s hands. Qui-Gon been bothering you lately? No problem, drop down Maul and play Maul Strikes. The YABs and S & Ds are, of course, for Vader?s self-defense?and Maul?s warpath. Come now, are you REALLY having trouble stacking on Insignificant Rebellion? I didn?t think so. Oh, yeah, I Have You Now is cool, too. M?iiyoom Onith is well, cool, except when someone plays Goo Nee Tay. Thank you, SalaciousCrumb. However, My TEBs are to get Vader out when I want him, so I don't think I'll take them out. Your Force Field suggestion-great. I just never have had the guts to take out stuff to put in a card that (gasp!) only protects my characters from being hit. I just can't get it through my head. Imp Dom (V) isn't so cool, because then I can't use my non-imp characters. I really don't think I'll put space in- it just takes up too much room in a deck that's ground. I have a customary six space cards (Thrawn, Chimaera, Executor, B:Hound's Tooth, Z:Mist Hunter, and D:Punishing One). I think that would take up too much space. Also, I do not have either of the combos you mentioned, and if someone suspends my Mob Points, it would always take out YCHF, which is like, uncool. Shewski: I don't actually have Crush The Rebellion. I also think S&D; is great or stupid, depending on whether you mean Search & Destroy or Sniper & Dark Strike! I don't mean to bash, but may I point out that even if Mob Points is suspended, this deck still generates eight force- by itself.

   LordHoban     Definitely take M?iiyoom out... You just don't have the force to use her effectively. I'd also consider packing This Is Some Rescue! (6 destiny) and/or Unsalvagables (5 destiny). Why, you may ask? Because those are the 2 used interrupts that cancel OOC (Out of Commission, or combo, which many people are packing now), which could probably take your head off. Say you lose Vader to a force drain early on, or something happens, and all of a sudden, a lucky OOC start bringing your characters out of play. Sure, you may have done quite a bit of damage in the meantime, but with no characters, they can ping you to death for the remainder of the game. Also, You need at least 2-3 ships. AT LEAST, because space could very well kill this deck, and if they realize you have nothing to back up you in space, they spread, and take you out. Best case scenario, assuming they leave you alone and don't put Luke on some faraway unreachable planet (which they can easily do now with Reiken pulling the <> sites, so again, that <> DB will still allow you to go after them), I see a potential drain of 5-6 (6 with double bladed saber doing 1 damage to you as well), + 3 for Your Destiny, so 9 is not bad, but probably won't win in a drain race, though I detest those.
