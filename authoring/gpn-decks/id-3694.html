 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The really short strategy is to get your sites out, flip, and then blow away the Death Star as quickly as possible.
I really don't know what to put for a strategy for this because it seems so obvious to me. You pull Dodanna (and later Mon Mothma) with Strike Planning. Mothma gets your techs and Dodanna gets your ability-2 pilots, including Rhomas, who gets your systems.
There are so many Attack run bonuses that I only need some of them to destroy the death star. I regularly get a bonus of +16 including the pilot's ability before even drawing destiny.
There is a certain amount of low destiny and reaction to low destiny, so destinies are often hit-or-miss.

There is a lot of stuff in here that is probably redundant and/or not used to its full potential. I would like people to tell me what should be replaced with what to use everything to its fullest potential. Thanks in advance.

   Shewski     Looks pretty good, but I see some cards that can be removed for efficency. I like Angrr Chewie better than Protector, but it appears you have an r3 bias, with your inclusion of Toryn. Play with r2 in red 5 and save a slot- also, red5 can't fly anywhere without the plucky Astromech. I'm guessing Advance Prep is V'd, and you forgot. Extra proton Torps are not really needed- if you want weapons, why not use more X wing Cannons? Targeting Computer does not really help, you get enough without it, same with the Briefing room. I personally would remove the ion cannon, change to all red squad ships (SE pilots) and remove Wyron and Toryn. Add some protection for pre-emptive Y4 beatdowns (barriers, ghhks, etc) and some effect pullers so you can get your two out quickly.
