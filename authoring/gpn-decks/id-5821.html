 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Average Destiny: 2.6

Strategy: In addition to benefiting from TDIGWATT and Dark Deal, use massive beat downs and enhanced Force draining against opponent to cause Force loss.

Before drawing (it is legal), pull Cloud City: West Gallery, Cloud City: Carbonite Chamber, They Must Never Again Leave This City and Executor. The key is to do this before drawing so you will have more options available to you once you do have your hand drawn.

Turn One: Pull and deploy Bespin, deploy Cloud City: West Gallery, Cloud City: Carbonite Chamber, They Must Never Again Leave This City and Executor. If possible, pull and deploy a cave. Draw.

Turn Two: Pull and deploy Cloud City. Deploy to Bespin, Cloud City and its sites; the star destroyers are used to control Bespin, the starfighters (Obsidian 1, Slave 1 etc.) are used to control Cloud City. I added Atmospheric Assault (V) to be able to clear out Cloud City against, say Luke in Red 5 or something.

Use Palpatine, Maul, Vader, Mara and Tarkin to control three Cloud City sites. Accompany them with garrisons. Admirals may pilot starships. This usually takes 2-3 turns.

Pull and deploy Dark Deal, flip objective. Drain until you are provoked to start using battles as a means of force loss.

Then, start grouping starships/characters together at one location. Starships at Cloud City can move to Bespin to accompany their star destroyers, if safe and possible. Use the concentration-Lana DoBreed (or Elis Herlot!)-Dark Jedi Presence-Set For Stun-All Too Easy and fleet-Gravity Shadow combos to cause massive beat downs. That’s an ideal combination, but card-intensive. The meat and potatoes are concentration-Elis Herlot-Dark Jedi Presence.

Use Monnok to ensure more successful beat downs. SAC any counter to your massive beat downs, if possible. Use Alligations Of Corruption to grab opponent's SAC.

After beat downs have commensed, continue Dark Deal draining. Repeat cycle. The act of trying to cancel Dark Deal will make opponent vulnerable to massive beat downs by wearing down his line of defense. Likewise, preparation against a massive beat down will result in more Dark Deal force draining, making opponent come to you.


 The Last 5 Comments
   Cam Solusar     The deck is good on paper, but I don't know how reliable the Elis strategy is these days. A couple copies of Houjix will stop you in your tracks, and then all of your characters could get stuck together at some isolated location while you frantically draw up for Elis. Remember that Emperor Palpatine can't move to a location that the opponent occupies and Lana Dobreed can only transport from one exterior site to another. Decent mains decks like TIGIH and TRM will deploy to CC early before you are able to condense your characters, so a couple copies of Imperial Barrier might be useful to hinder this. Having played Deal quite a bit in the past, I can confirm that it is very difficult to play against those style of decks, especially when you aren't guaranteed 2nd or 3rd turn Dark Deal w/ Ket Maliss. Space looks good and is the strong point of your deck. Gravity Shadow is fun. Good luck and feel free to ask any questions on the RFT.
