 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Now, I know some of this deck looks a bit unorthodox, and maybe it is, but there is some sense behind my madness.

I think Daughter of Skywalker is better than Leia, Rebel Princess because she activates more force, and she can hold her own against the likes of Mara Jade and other Imperial lackeys. She can also pilot if necessary, and she's immune to attrition alone.

General Solo, IMO, is more versatile for land and space than Captain Han Solo ever wished to be. No deployment restrictions, cancels battle destiny, and he even carries a little bit of immunity with him.

I chose LS,JK over Luke Skywalker (v) for the sole purpose that LS,JK is a powerhouse. If played correctly, I can clear a site with just him. High power, high immunity, and a great weapon make this guy a dangerous foe. I like to drop him at the Lower Corridor with an Arcona, his saber, and Lightsaber Proficiency and drain for four a turn. Luke alone there is a power 13! Plus, I pack AJR and FSYH (v) so that I can keep him and his buddies comin' back for more.

I like it that I can take my characters to other battling locations so that I can attempt to counter objectives that draw me to their locations.

With YISYW, I can pull Wokling (v), Launching the Assault, or Squadron Assignments. Each choice presents me with other choices. Wokling (v) allows me to pull Lightsaber Proficiency or CC Celebration. Launching the Assault allows me to pull Home One. Squadron Assignments allows me to pull my ships/pilots a wreak havoc in space and at docking bays.

I have a heavy retrieving mechanism with CC Sabacc (which I usually win), Figrin D'an (which I usually win), and CC Celebration. I have a heavy draining mechanism that, if setup correctly, can drain for near 12 force. OTE is a nice tech to drop after I have secured CC just to prevent further invasion. I have evasion that includes a scouting force of Arconas and Path of Least Resistance. And for safety, the only exterior location I play is the Casino. Don't ask me to drop it, 'cause I ain't gonna do it. ;)

Other tidbits...I don't play Sai'torr Kel Fas (v) because I usually flip by turn 3 or 4, so I can pull any weapon I want from my force pile. I also don't play destiny adders because of my Lando's Not A System, He's A Man game text. I try to keep plenty of force activated to add to total battle destiny. Oh, and the worst this deck has EVER lost is by 20, and that was against Bryan Battenburg's DS Senate. I'd like to be able to beat Senate...thus the thought of adding Scrambled Transmission (v).

I've thought about adding Wedge, RSL...some Taking The Initiatives (7 scouts in this deck)...and Scrambled Transmission (v). Do you guys have any other suggestions? I hope you do! Thanks for taking time to read this!

   Cam Solusar     I'll call this deck a fixer-upper, and here's why: Like challenger I agree that you need to augment it a little bit. That means fixing up your character selection and getting a little more Red in. Unlike challenger I will give you my recommendation from a classic perspective. First things first. For QMC you really want to start KTEOF, Squassign and Wokling (V). YISYW is a nice idea, but I think you really want to stick to those three. For most QMC decks two things are critical: Early flip and CC Celebration retrieval. Those last two effects both help get that done. Once you flip you can just pull your critical cards from your force pile, so I don't think that Launching the Assault is necessary. Instead of Figrin D'an and one of the CC Sabaacs I think you should use Off The Edge. Once you learn rudimentary tracking it should be pretty easy to draw good every time, barring the always annoying Omni Box. ;) Instead of the CC DB as challenger recommended, I would try to add 2x Out of Nowhere/Houjix combo, which both add destiny in space and keeps you from being wiped out both on the ground and in space. As for characters, I would advise using EPP characters over the Mains and Toys which you are currently playing. Maybe keep LSJK over EPP Luke, but replace Lando and Obi-Wan with the EPP versions and get rid of the weapons. I realize you may not have the EPP's, but the matching weapons are just too hard to pull without (V) Sai'torr. (another option might be to get her in somehow, but QMC is limited in what effects it can start) Taking out the low destiny weapons would also give you more space for some high-destiny cards that you could use primarily for tracking. Farmboy Luke would be good, since he is a great anti-Zuckuss weapon in his ship, and Captain Han would also be good for the Falcon. This is just a general overview of what I saw, but I'll be happy to help you out more if you respond in the Feedback Thread. Good luck!
