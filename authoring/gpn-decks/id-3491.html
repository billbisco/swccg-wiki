 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Ok, this is basically a Gammorean deck that I think can hold its own either battling or draining on the way to victory.

From the start, you are making it difficult to enter the palace. Rebels are +2 (No Bargain), while Aliens are also +2 (His Name Is Anakin).

Use T: JP's GT to pull out the other Jabba's Palace sites. A key to this deck is using the multiple Twilek Advisors to pull Expand The Empire. You'll want to use it on the AC to make it so you can download any alien to the three JP sites once per game. There are so many aliens in the deck that you should be able to have a few in your hand and you can use the expanded GT of the site to get what you need at that time. Also, try to get the Den of Theives out early to absorb drains.

POTH is a nice card that lets you get 5 cards in this deck, the most handy being Hutt Influence, which makes your Force Drains unmodifiable and uncancellable where you have two different aliens. That shouldn't be too hard in this deck!

Mosep and Gailid crank the hurt up drain-wise- It's very nice to be able to park them in the AC and let them do their thing. Ephant Mon is good to sit there as well, as his forfeiture can help against a hit & run on the dynamic duo.

The bane of this deck would be Leia, Rebel Princess (thanks Chief!) and to counter her, I have 4-Lom and P-59.

The Gammoreans can become pretty fun cards after you get some of their helpers out. Ortugg makes them forfeit +2, No Bargain +1 more, Jabba gives them +2 forfiet and -1 deploy, and S & V makes them another deploy -1. If by some sheer miracle you are playing another Tatooine deck, once you flip the Piggy's are another forfeit +2.

All in all, if you have the above and Bib on the table and some extra force, you can react by deploying Gamorreans for 2 force who are forfeit 8. Nice =)

S & D is nice- you know you will have people out- but will your opponent? Make him pay if he doesn't. AAA also brings the hurt out. I think I could easily take anyone on in a AAA race with 21 characters w/ ability.

POTF is in to add to the Entrance Cavern, which makes at least 3 JP battlegrounds, nicely letting Tatooine Occupation be effective.

Stunning Leader works in any JP site- Somehow I don't think Gamorreans pack stun blasters, but who's complaining =) NSP instead of Barrier since many pack anti-barrier stuff.

I was thinking that Profit would be a tough deck for both of us. Start the AC instead of the Palace, they will replace but you still can use Nemoidian to pull your T:JP get the sites out that way. I'd start with Jabba and Boelo, then bring out Ephant on your turn. Hopefully you can bounce out enough Rebels to get a foothold and then just fight fight fight! On the plus side, it will be easier to flip and your Gammoreans are +2 forfeit from the get go!

Please rate and let me know what you think!
