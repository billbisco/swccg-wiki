 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck is pretty simple to grasp, using lots of Starships, Effects, and Sites to make the most of your many force draining opportunities. It uses quite a few rares, but the rares aren't really essential to the deck, but are really good to have to enhance force drains and to strike general fear into the hearts of the puny rebels (as soon as you whip out "The Executor" against "The Millennium Falcon"). The basic design of the deck is to put plenty of power and ability onto a few sites to hold off the rebels, and to drain as much as you can.

This deck revolves around the power to crush your opponents in battle, then instead of your opponent losing the charaacter, use "We Have A Prisoner" to send them to the "Detention Block Corridor" to help "IT-O" with his force drain work. Also, to add even more characters to the "Detention Block Corridor," deploy "Tractor Beam" and "Ion Cannon" to the "Tyrant." If you hit a ship with the "Ion Cannon," the "Tyrant" never misses with the "Tractor Beam" because it adds 1 to destiny draws. As you might notice, I included a lot of sites with the game text "force drain +1 here" for obvious reasons.

When you play "Expand The Empire" to one of those sites, you end up with an additional two adjacent sites with that text. With the kind of power this deck has, you should be able to control them and have a huge force drain. With the lightsabres, you have an even larger force drain and can literally slaughter characters at will. "The Executor" was placed in this deck as sort of a game board patrol ship that moves around and crushes the rebels wherever it goes.
