 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First all, I have no clue on how this will shake out, but conceptually, it seems fun.

You have the two battlegrounds to cancel CHYBC and BO right off the bat. First priority is to get contol of Aquaris- you should be able to use SA to find a starfighter that (depending on force the oppponent gives you) can either deploy directly to system or to the DB (at -1 or -2 for freighters). Next turn Aquaris is a 4/1 system! If you have the force, use Cell 2187 to deploy R2 to the docking bay (make sure he is in there when SAing), and use How Did We Get Into This Mess to cycle through your deck to get the cards you need.

Use the cycling function to get out Dagobah and set up test #1. Once attained, you should be able to handle other Force drains.

Use Ithorians to enhance drains on Dagobah, use cloud sectors to enchance things even more.

Hopefully, the mains should be able to hold Aquaris and the DB with little difficulty.

The main problem I see is if the opponent grabs How Did We... I am hoping that 4 copies of the card will allow for enough cycling to get things moving quickly.

Now for card choices!

LO- I wanted a bit of misdirection, and I also wanted to start with a battleground site and system. Plus, I should be able to draw a defensive shield before I reveal my true strategy.

Bravos- Bravos are cheap to deploy, and I envision using the generic ones to drain in the clouds. Plus the GT on Ric Olie, Bravo Leader is enhanced by more Bravos in a deck.

Redemption- To keep the DB force around. Forfeit them, Tank em' put them back on the ship and shuttle them back for round 2, 3, 4 etc...

Please let me know areas to improve- I would love to here some opinions as I am building this.

Thanks!

EDIT- I had this designed around QMC, but I like the objective LO better- plus I don't have to use a force to scan my reserve deck (and potentially deploy a cloud to Dagobah). With LO, my deck scanning is free and worst case I have to deploy my Forest to Aquaris.
