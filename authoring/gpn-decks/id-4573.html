 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This is my first attempt at a MWYHL deck. I hope that you enjoy it!

First, I’d like to thank Cam, tyranus, Shewski, and Rev for the props on this deck. They have all contributed ideas and/or cards in order to make this public announcement possible. Now, on with my strategy…

Since I don’t use SOS (v), add a couple extra turns in the “6 turn sweep” to compensate for quick pulling of Jedi Tests. I'm trying to keep this themed, so I am willing to take the extra time to train Luke w/ Saber to have EPP Beatdown later on...I know...it's a gamble. But I like it!

This deck holds two sevens (R2 in R5, Screaming R2). I hope that is enough. Tracking involves Polarized Negative Power Coupling. I got Flash of Insight (v) in here! WHEE!

I’m sure you guys see the error in no “A Jedi’s Resilience”. The destiny six is nice, but I find Bacta Tank to be more universal in the fact that I can heal all of my EPP characters, not just the two with sabers.

I thought a veritable cornucopia of destiny adders would provide many ways for late game beatdown. I am vulnerable to Sense, however. We're talking lots of destiny, though. :D And with sevens smiting the wicked (and of course making them forfeit zero), that looks wonderful.

My only battleground is the twix Tatooine: Lars Moisture Farm. It basically invites the opponent to melt the Lars combo so Luke can go fury on everyone. Just make sure your timing is correct in this measure. Luke being +6 on Dagobah is pointless, obviously…

My space is minimal…just enough to hold off those pesky fighters.

I already have some changes in mind, but I wanted to get this on the boards to get some feedback coming on this deck. Make any suggestions you want, other than suggesting that I put multiple copies of the EPP’s. It won’t happen guys…I don’t have the card base to trade for them. Thanks for reading and thanks for the reviews!

Hope you like the changes!!!

   Cam Solusar     I'm glad that I was able to help you as an inspiration for your deck, so hopefully I can help you more. You probably only need Skywalkers and Gift of the Mentor out of all your teamwork interrupts. Skywalkers' ability to cancel Imp Barrier is useful and Gift is good for protecting Yoda and Luke on Dagobah. I suppose WHAAAAAAAAAOOOOW! might be good if you run into a capture-style Court deck, but Yoda's Gimer Stick is better for protecting against those ever-annoying creatures. Instead of using Harvest and the Grans for retrieval which require Owen & Beru on the table and defending battles, respectively, use On The Edge. Tawss Khaa is good for retrieval, and Reflection is O.K. I have found that (V) Flash of Insight is pretty good for using On the Edge before the completion of test five. I would get rid of Areil Schous and add a ability four character such as Corran Horn who can hold a site by himself against weak attacks. If you want to fit in a Jedi's Resilience in there, you can get rid of one (V) Levitation. I would also add a 2nd Luke w/ Saber, just in case you don't get the Tank out in time. Probably the biggest challenge to playing this deck will not be building it but learning to play it. My first two real games with my MWYHL were abyssmal failures, but I'm starting to get the hang of it. The key is to being able to get characters out during the training to keep your opponent honest and prep for major beatdowns later. I found that not getting anyone onto the table before the completion of test five led to situations that not even a stacked seven could resolve. Good luck, and have fun.
   Shewski     Why are you not using SOS? He can pull many things to help him train and thus frees up a starting slot. The buddy effects are extremely difficult to use. Who will fall for attacking you with them? The smart opponent will avoid the fab four and drain you to death. Some battle adders are OK, but not all the ones you have. You need some other interrupts, like some of the new Leviations card, ICBW, or On the Edges? Using all EPPs is ok, but Chewie Protector is better than EPP Chewie, IMO. Why Grans? Talz only works for hit characters, stick with the more versatile AJR. Noble Sac won't work for Obi... who does DS play that is power 5 besides CC Fett? I'd use Landing Claw to hold off space damage, since the Rebel leaders won't do too much to a space deck. I feel bad for rating this since I was an inspiration, but I think you can improve this to a 3 or 4 pretty easily.
