 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
In addition to MBO, start HFTMF with Insurrection, Squadron Assignments, and Launching The Assault. Don’t flip until you have all your sites out; the last thing you want to do is draw a zero for Attack Run, plus more locations add to your total. Obi-Wan, Leia, and Lando will hold Yavin 4 for you, plus Luke can help if it starts to get hairy. Also, having Obi-Wan out protects you from A Day Long Remembered. The rest is pretty much self explanatory: pull Death Star and Rebel Techs, blow up the Death Star, etc. Force drains are now +2 at systems where you have a piloted starfighter, so use that to your advantage (Elyhek Rue on Red 7 at Ralltiir will drain for 4). Also, your force drains with Home One are +1 if a pilot is aboard, i.e. Ackbar. That’s a potential drain of 5 at one system. Also, Kessel is an automatic 5 drain (2 icons, +1 game text, +2 MBO), or 6 if you put Home One there.

This deck is pretty straightforward and isn’t really flashy, but it’s fun and it wins.

   ben_ethus     Gotta agree with Shewski on this one. The deck looks pretty decent as is, but could almost definitely be improved. "Great Shot Kid" is a great card to have in there, and can speed up the beat-down process significantly after the DS gets blown away. It also couldn't hurt to have some more early-stage defensive cards, for holding Yavin IV, just in case something bad happens. I can't tell you how many times I've seen my strategy blown because Mara Jade came down and beat on one of my lonely rebels, or beause an AT-AT went crazy at the docking bay. You've got Rebel Barrier in there, and I recommend "Fall Back!", or "Houjix & Out of Nowhere". In my experiences with the deck, You're All Clear Kid doesn't come into play all too often, unless it's a scenario game, plus with Luke = ability 5, all the bonuses from Gospic and Techs and sites, you shouldn't even need that extra one bonus the card gives you. I'm also not a huge fan of giving my X-wings laser weapons, but that's more a personal choice, I guess. It might not hurt to sub-out one of your Red Squad pilot/ship combos for Tiree/Gold 2. Cheaper than an X-wing, gives a bonus to Attack Run when he's in the trench, and can even be used as a decoy in case there actually ARE sharp-shooting TIES in the trench. Assuming you can hold Yavin IV system, Haven may be a good effect to have, because you can then get cheapy ships/pilots, and use it as a staging point. (Y-Wing/pilot combos deploy together for all of 1 force!) Tantive IV or Fly Casual could potentially be useful ways to sneak guys to the Death Star, so you don't have to sit around for a turn. Besides those minor critiques, it looks pretty decent. Similar to mine in some ways... I use a lot of the Red Squad guys, too. :)
