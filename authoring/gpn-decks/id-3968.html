 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Use the Sniper/Dark Strike (binders) especially with IG-88 w/ riot gun because he could capture a bunch of people and they couldn't even battle him...

I’m still learning more about how to play this deck… So any tips would be nice…

This deck started out as an AOTBS and then changed to a COTVG and now I think it’s going to stay put at Carbon Freezing…

First of all you can pull Ugnaught and Carbonite Chamber and Prepare the Chamber...

The intruder missiles, and other 7's are never going to be played(maybe), if they are in your hand then you can play them on Colo Claw Fish lose them as lost force and if in your reserve deck they might just pop up when you need them to... The simple concept to this deck is to capture and freeze your opponents characters resulting in a force loss of 8... All the characters are destiny drawers or bounty hunters... you can retrieve force and pay less with scum and villainy which is played when you flip...you can also deploy docking bays and AC from reserve deck to flip easier (with the help of Jabba’s Prize)
Try to get Search and Destroy out and hurt your opponent (especially when he is playing a hidden base deck) If I forgot to add something tell me so more people don’t see my silly mistake...

   Shewski     I still think this is a 2 deck. You don't have Jabba's Prize, which is often more painful that stealing a Rebel out of the opponent's deck. Your Mob Pts can be easily suspended since you don't have a battleground system. I'd start Power of the Hutt instead, pulling some characters as well as the Sail Barge (and deck). I don't think the JP Dungeon and Chasm Walkway are needed- find other means of generation. What do all the CFs and DHes mean? Try to flip quickly- a Lift tube could take you to the DB, then use the Sail Barge to transit to Tatooine, then walk the prisoner to Jabba. Visage and the Artilleries seem to be just there for high destiny, why not use more useful, slightly lower destiny cards like Jabba's Through with You to the most of your deck?
