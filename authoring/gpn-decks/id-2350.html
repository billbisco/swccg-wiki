 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Very Weird Indeed. Here's The Main Strategy:

Forced Servitude is In Play. Try to Deploy Your Mining Droids to that location. Drop one to two timer mines their if you expect deployment from your opponent. From then on, its basically a preventative deck. Deploy EV-9D9 to the Incinerator with a few mining droids and force drain away, making sure the opponent never get s a foothold by deploying your timer mines.

Since the opponent cannot battle you (you have no presence there) all they can do is force drain. Thats where Chief retwin, your spies and Imperial Code Cylinder Come in. They can each block or prevent a force drain, and Ghhhk is in just in case something goes bad.

Mining Droids satisfy 4 force loss if lost while Forced Servitude is on table. Imperial Code Cylinder can cancel force drains. You can retrieve one droid each turn, and when you play Abyssin ornament with 8 or so minig droids on table, you can retrieve your Timer Mines. Control can cancel that nasty Alter trying to get rid of it as well as its secondary function to cancel force drains.

3,720 to 1 is an interesting card that Im playtesting with. the average character destiny is 3, so unless your up against a WYS deck, chances are likely that your oponent will lose 2-3 force from them.

Ive tried playing this and the deck actually works. It has gone 3-0 so far, and the reason for such a small ratio is becuase I am embarrased to bring it to tournaments where many people play (the shame). I prefer my Death Star II decks, but this deck is insanely fun.

The New Mining Droid rules also add some spice. You are no longer limited to laying timer mines. You can lay regular non-mine cards, (a minefield). The Timer Mines do not jsut explode on next turn, but whenever an opponents character deploys or moves there.

The Bluffs is just an interesting twist so that Chief Retwin can have some fun....

Now, the next deck is to use Mouse droids.....
