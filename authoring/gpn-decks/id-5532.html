 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Why did Luke cross the Vader? To get him to the other side! :P

... now that that's out of the way, let's get on with the strat, shall we? Basic MWYHL test-to-six, except for one BIG thing: you're going to cross Vader. If you need a breakdown of how to do MWYHL turn-by-turn, take a look at the previous version of this deck.

After you finish testing to 5 and have won at least one battle (stacking a card on I Feel The Conflict), pull Luke off table and drop him to Vader's site to do test 6. Remember: test 6 is a duel, meaning that the opponent needs to stack a card for Conflict. After you pass test 6, use On The Edge targetting Luke... and purposely fail it. Immediately play Anakin Skywalker (about to be lost at Vader's location!), use the stacked 7, and cross daddy over to the light side. Give him his old saber, bring Leia into the mix, and personna replace Luke with JK Luke at the first opportunity.

Card choices:
Nabrun Leids - Just giving it a try. Having a bit of mobility in a deck that focuses on using the family to beat the crop out of the Empire just makes sense.
Thrown Back - Yet another method for putting back that destiny 7 in your hand and tracking for test 5.
Civil Disorder (V) - DS Senate is still rather popular in the area... if I see a Senate start, I'll pull this instead of Battle Plan combo to screw them over. DS LSC is also occasionally seen.
Imperial Attrocity (V) - Why not?

Enjoy!
