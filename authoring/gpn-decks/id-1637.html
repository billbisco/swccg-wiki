 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Okay, unless you've been under a rock, you know about the whole Strike Planning pulls Madine->Chewie->Wuta combo to get out your sites. Be aware that Wuta needs to be at an exterior site, so if you do not get any sites in your opening hand, you'll be going down to the Landing Platform. Be careful.

Strike Planning is also going to be pulling out General Solo too. Madine can also pull some of the Ewok Scouts a well for some furry fun. While using Madine to pull the scouts, look to see what weapons you have in the reserve deck. If you have the matching character in your hand, use the new V effect to deploy the character and weapon at the same time.

Generation may be a little rough in the begining if they drop someone to the landing Platform. DoS, Romba, and Endor Celebration can help with this. The Endor Celebration will also coming in handy to cancel those pesky Occupations too.

You have the Jedi in there to hack and slash if you need, Resilience can revive Luke, Obi, Jinn and DOS as she'll be using Anakin's Stick.

Rebel artillery can be used with Han And Chewie (if you want to go further, you can replace Anakin's Stick with leia's SE blaster) to deal a little damage if they do not come to you on Endor.

Were You Looking For Me is just fun tech in this deck. Threepio's text is great, because he not only suspends Bad Feeling Have I for most of your mains, but he can go to the USED pile if lost from a site. Artoo is sweet as well because you can send him to used pile to cancel a battle destiny. Were You Looking For Me gets BOTH of them back. Wash, Rinse, Repeat!!

I'm using Lando In Falcon because it can be pulled with Life Debt. dash is tto good not to include. Spiral is in there for the Fusion Tanks (V).

Corran is my back-up pilot for my ships, but can work well on the ground too if Arica comes to the Landing Platform.

BoShek (V) is my other back up and his new text pulls the Claw and the tanks (V).

Everything else is basic filler but with obvious purposes.

What is It? helps with duels.
Goo Nee Tay adds to DS deploy.
HOTJ just in case.
Run Luke, Run/I Know for a little surprise back up.
Ewok Celebration makes my characters even tougher.
The Combo's are staples.

Might consider taking out Lost In the Wilderness for A Vergance In The Force or possibly replacing second LSJK with Rebel Scout.

Sixty-first card list:
Punch It!
A second DoS or Romba


Thanks for reading this far and please provide some feedback with the understanding that I INTENTIONALLY put in Ewoks (please do not say, take out the ewoks for more rebel scouts). ;)
