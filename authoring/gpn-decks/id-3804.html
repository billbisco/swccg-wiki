 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     I agree with many of the other comments, I would recommend going to SWCCGPC.com and checking out some of the world champion Watto decks to get an idea of what some excellent designs are like.
   Revelation     I agree with everything Mr. Hoban said- except Ket Maliss. Use Imperial Arrest ORder to prevent sneaky Nabrun Leidsing. And then play with the Executor, Coruscant, and Death Star II docking bays for activation, as well as your Mos Espa db you already have for movement. And then perhaps the Spaceport db to get to planets LS is hiding on. If you're NOT going to play IAO, by all means use several Elis Helrot, since most LS players do not play Insurrection. To clarify Mr. Hoban's 'three-Watto' comment...most players try to kill Watto with Qui-Gon, Obi-Wan, etc. When he's gone, what are you to do? OR your opponent can just use 8 force if he's played you before and know you're only playing with one Watto. Yes, character selection is good, but a bit unfocused. Carbon Chamber is a stinking objective, if you want to play aliens use Vile Court. Imperials- use Ralltiir Ops. But you don't have to be all of one or all of another, a nice balance, some more Wattos, different starting effects, ditching the capturing theme, more focused interrupts, and this deck would work just fine.
   LordHoban     First off, I gave you a 2 because there isn't any strategy section, and your deck looks a but unbalanced. Why are you using a Tractor Beam? You don't have enough Bounty Hunters in the deck to make it worth it. Most Players tend to start with Ket Maliss (v). I'll let you read that card to figure out why. Take out Quick Reflexes, remove Flagship (if you're gonna put in a Star Destroyer helper, you might as well put in Deflector Shield Generators (v) from Hoth). I'd also axe Fett's Rifle and Evanzan's blaster... there's not enough focus on the Capture element to really make it worthwhile, and you need cards like IG-88 with Riot Gun and 4-Lom With Gun to truly make things like that effective. In the end, if you want to make a Capture Deck, if you're going for Imperials, use Set Your Course, if Aliens? Go with Court of the Vile Gangster or Carbon Chamber Testing. Watto is not a good objective for Capturing. Also, ditch M'iiyoom Onith, you don't have enough locations for generation to make good use for her, and she's just cheese in any case. If you can get a hold of one, go for a Boba Fett, Bounty Hunter from Reflections 3, or at the very least Boba Fett with Blaster. Another thing, if you don't have at least 3 Wattos in the deck, its probably a bad call to play a Watto Deck. The deck needs a little bit tighter focus. The character selection looks pretty good, except, you'll be hard pressed to get them out since you have a lack of locations at your disposel and no real way to pull them. At least with Court, you can pull that docking bay and your Independant ships. Next order of business, if you're going to play docking bays of any kind, you need at least 2 They're Still Coming Through!'s from Coruscant, uncommon card that makes all the difference against Fallen Portal. You also can not build a nonImperial Domination dark deck anymore without Zuckuss in Mist Hunter. Don't use Admiral Piett unless you have an Admiral's Order to pull or a Commander. If you're gonna use Piett, use Captain Piett (v) from Hoth, as he can get your cards back in your deck and some nice added force for your next turn. If you're gonna use Imperial ships, try for Chimera (Thrawn's ship) and Thunderflare (7 for 9 power). Tyrant is also cheap, so ok. I don't see it likly that Executor will come out in any real game unless your opponent is giving you an insane amount of activation (don't count on it), it costs too much for you to manage. I guess I'll wrap this up... just, keep at it, but I hope you find a different objective. Court is fun, it has plenty of bonuses and works well with Bounty Hunters (adds +2 to their forfeit, which is good for CC Fett). Don't give up and you'll get there eventually. Most of all, have fun while you do it.
