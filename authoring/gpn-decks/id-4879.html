 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Ok the strategy:
Star with the Starting Cards.

1st turn: Play IAO Combo with Desperate Counter and then the DS2 Docking Bay and Moff JErjerrod there.

2nd, 3rd, 4th turn: Play the first, second, third sector with Jerjerrod and use Neimodians / Twi'lek's to search for effects systems you need...
Importiant take out We shall double our efforts !!! to add force loss and make maneuver lower...

Dont forget to take out the Epic Event with the sector.
In the 3rd turn you should have transparanted opponents deck a bit. then decide, if he has a ground deck flood the space each location one TIE with one weapon, the Admirals Order makes your TIEs which had fired a weapon Power +3 and I can't shake him then let's you add a destiny our the DS2 System. Also you can surprise him with the EPP. When the Home One comes take Relentless Pursuit. On starfighters make them lost with 3 force shot...

Move the DS2 System to Kashyyk to make opponent lose 3 Force each Time... but beware Local Uprising (V) flipped !! GRRRRR only one force loss...

Make sure when opponent has a drain deck to get Hoth and Yavin with Decree. Control Kashyyk and Endor --> 2 Drains of 2 (most time for 3 Force) and a Force loss of 3 and some turns the artilleries too !

Any time in the game use Wakeelmui, Sienar Fleet Systems and the TIE Interceptors, but more the Emperor's TIEs to retrieve Force !! and Cancel Drains with Dreaded Imperial Starfleet..

With the much options in this deck you have a great chance against many decks, difficult is a Hidden Base One.

But Do not Underestimate the Power of TIEs !!! Take One TIE with Weapons, has Power 3 (6 with the Admirals Order). For 3 Force he makes (often) a starfighter lost , and adds Destiny. So very good for one TIE huh ? and for the last Rescue you have the Ghhk ;-)

If you want to vary this deck you can choose to begin with Combat Reponse instead of IAO and play with the unique TIE's with pilots (The named Scythe TIEs are versy good for defending DS2 and you can Tallon Roll with them and they have Destiny 4 !! *GG*) think of it...

Do not forget to use Def Shields , they are VERY important, for stopping drains, sense alter actions and so on !!!

Thanks for rating....
I hope you have fun to play this deck one time...

   Shewski     I am concerned with your non Ops start. If someone starts Strike Planning, you activate 1 Force your first turn, which is not bad, but it does hamstring you to an extent. I prefer to use MP combo to get out Desp Counter, and then use it to get out IAO combo and Comb Resp. A little riskier, but it eliminated the need for getting out planets to TTO at. If worried about drains, I would consider using the DBs since you can pull them with IAO, but that might require more ground. Why are you not using Inc Losses with all those weapons? Superlaser is too hard to use to be worthwhile. If doing uniques, swap to Fighters Coming In. I prefer that, since you can get bonus drains at related DBs and increase TTO damage by 1. I'd also recommend using uniques since it is so easy to blow away the DS2. Mianda can catch up a shoot down a troublesome invader easily by moving twice. A decent deck but your choices limit it's effectiveness.
