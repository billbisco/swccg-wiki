 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
It's the basic Court stuff. No Escape keeps you from getting screwed up by Honor of the Jedi. I hate Honor of the Jedi. With the added loss from the objective and Ability, Ability, Ability they'll eventually have to come. When they come, and everyone eventually does, just wipe out your opponent's characters and feed them to the Rancor to retrieve force and cause their loss. Hidden Weapons makes doing this ever so easy. The average destiny of this deck isn't the greatest, but the good old Colo Claw Fish makes this okay. With so many weapons you can get rid of the pathetic destiny draws while you're using your weapons, and Bossk with Mortar Gun is quite the fan of those sorry destiny numbers you hate to draw the rest of the time. There are a few cards in this deck just for destiny. I find that with the Colo Claw Fish, all you need is a few large destiny circulating, and Reactor Terminal makes it all possible. I don't use Scum and Villany because I like being able to use Mara, Vader, and Maul if necessary. I try not to use them, except for Maul, so I can play None Shall Pass, but at some point in the game I usually have to play Mara and Vader. Prophetess makes it easy to mess with your opponent's destiny, so......yeah.
