 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Use I’d just as soon kiss a wookie to get rid of a JUST deployed jedi and retrieve.... read the CRD to verify you can do this... i am SOO @#$%ing angry right now...... maybe i will edit this later..... the deck kicks @#$% the submission wizard doesnt!!

OK this is a deck that i have tweaked after going up against the lightside version and a space deck, which are the only 2 that would be huge problems for this deck... the lightside combat objective was my only loss (by 10) but i believe i got all the neccessary changes to beat that deck without giving up the room to beatdown other decks.

Basically, I use the NON-combo of YOu Cannot Hide forever since the new Shield for lightside can kill mobilization points and i don’t see any battleground systems in my deck....

This deck did quite well at local tournaments and in playtesting..... I truly believe that with direct damage cards (Search And Destroy) draining for 3 (with sabers) at both starting sites is a major victory against most decks since you cannot cancel the drains.... sure they can put a Jedi down and then run but since the Obj. lets you combat during your move phase you can move then combat... and with stacked high destiny you are almost assuring a loss of 2(4 with lord maul) and loss of the Jedi which in turn gives you 1 force back.... Also The Ebb Of Battle when placed under a Jedi can Cancel a force drain.... SO save those for big drains late game against an HB and EBO deck....Remember you don’t HAVE to stay on Naboo.... My lightside version of this deck invaded Tatooine with a Jedi and kept the others on Naboo... That is why i choose Spaceport Docking bay over anyother Unique (*) one, for universal application. I can put the DB on Tat, Naboo, or, Coruscant (Even if they have a docinking Bay there..... I personally like The Ebb Of Battle and Blow Parried for there diversity in Used Interrupts.... they rule against Duel Of The Fates but work for recycling against Non- Combat decks.
I love Tat Maul since he can duel every time you hit your control phase.. and maul strikes lets you hit em again if need be.... and all the 6's and 7's are awesome!!
