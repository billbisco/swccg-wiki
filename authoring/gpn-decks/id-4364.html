 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The point of the deck is to say away from space except to control Nal Hutta. The reason i am using bounty instead of Hutt Bounty is that Bounty can target more characters. For the most part this deck relies on multiple ways to capture. All Wrapped Up. Bossk With Mortar Gun. IG-88 With Riot Gun. And Hidden Weapons. All this deck is for is my own enjoyment of trying to capture characters. I hope i did well
