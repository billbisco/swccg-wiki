 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Your goal is a turn 2 EBO using Rieekan, Dodonna, and Navander. Turn 1, pull Rieekan and Dodonna, pull the docking bay, drop Rieekan to the DB and use him to deploy ANSB from reserve, pull War Room and Echo Base Sensors from reserve with ANSB and deploy both, play Dodonna to War Room. Turn two, pull Corridor and EBO with ANSB, pull a pilot with Dodonna, drop the Corridor, pull Navander with Rieekan and play him to Corridor, play EBO.
After that, just pull systems with ANSB and Navander for the next two turns, drop Y-wings for free (-1 from EBO) and give them some cheap pilots, and drain the heck out of your opponent. Speed is key.

Arconas - ground holders. Drop them to an opponent's drain site, and react away.

Noble Sac - use with Wedge or Chewie if possible, as they have the highest forfeit values with the Insurrection bonus.

Reflection - why not?

Hol Okand - drain +1 at Kashyyyk.
