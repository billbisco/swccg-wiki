 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Hidden Base with a twist; use Insurrection to pull docking bays to deploy at systems, then
use operatives to get drains. I’ll Take The Leader adds to drains at the docking bays as
well. I’ve included a good group of characters, though I wish I had a Leia, Rebel Princess
or another Qui-Gon, but if you have suggestions for different characters (I don’t have
many Mains) please make them. The Fallen Portals help protect my docking bays, and
Clash Of Sabers is always good in a pinch. Gold 4 and 6 match the systems I’m putting
operatives at, adding 1 to my force drains at the system, allowing me to do maximum
damage by occupying as few locations as possible, thus allowing me to heavily defend
those locations. BoShek can fly either Gold 4 or 6 and use its immunity, and he pulls the
Fusions Generator Supply Tanks. The Forests won’t get the drain bonus from I’ll Take
The Leader, but they are a safer place for your character to make a stand at, giving them
total immunity to attrition. This is only a first draft of this deck and suggestions are
welcome, and necessary.
