 The Description
The strategy of these 60 cards is to ensue false security in the Light Side player, then, use surprise tactical combos in just one battle to totally break the Light Side player's moral and deck!

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
With Prepared Defenses, deploy Battle Order & First Strike, Oppressive Enforcement and You Cannot Hide Forever & Mobilization Points. His efforts to force drain become ever increasingly difficult because of tactics coming from effects like Battle Order, and defensive shields like Come Here You Big Coward and Resistance. The beauty of Battle Order is that it sets the stage for this deck and the entire game: combat.

Concentrate Maul, Vader, Tarkin, Garrison and Mara at one site without suffering too many force-drain breaches. And I know that unexpected battles do occur. Use a couple or so imperials to clean up any counter-invasion, and then shuttle them to a nearby Star Destroyer, and get them back with the concentration of imperials. If that doesn't work, and the battle is dragging on, move your characters to an adjacent site and set up shop there, beginning to concentrate
characters at that site, if possible.

Interrupting with Elis Herlot transports your concentration of Characters to the site of a soft spot; a location where the Light Side lacks power and forfeit. Ideally, both deficiencies are desired at the soft spot. Interrupt with Focused Attack to double Vader's power once, making Vader's power 12, then interrupt with Dark Jedi Presence, using Maul are your dark Jedi to double the power of all imperials present, including Vader’s again.

If you interrupt as described in the following example, you've already accumulated a power of 59 plus battle destiny!

The second part of my strategy is to cause an entire-deck losing battle in space relatively quickly. The Executor doesn't necessarily have to be the first starship deployed,
but getting used to being able to deploy it free on the first turn, by pulling and deploying Bespin on the first turn, motivates me to lay it out as soon as possible. I compliment the Executor with an Imperial-Class Star Destroyer, and I've pretty much dominated space on the first turn.

Deploy a Star Destroyer, if possible, to the same system every turn. This accumulation of Star Destroyers will be your fleet. After accumulation, move your fleet to confront Light Side starship(s). Being overpowered, even with Home One, your opponent will choose to move. Your opponent will think he is safe for the remainder of the turn because you may be able to move to the space/system his starships are at, but
you won't be able to battle him there this turn, and by the next turn, your opponent will be halfway across the galaxy, leaving you to eat his space dust. To ensure this won’t happen, interrupt with Gravity Shadow to deny movement of a single starship, preferably, a non-unique starfighter, there at that location. The average destiny draw is greater than two, therefore, a successful Gravity Shadow will likely occur, and stop a single ship in its tracks, forcing it to battle your fleet on your next turn. If you have all your ships out, your power is 79 plus battle destiny! Hyper Escape, Alternatives To Fighting can be Sensed. If your opponent uses Houjix, in your next confrontation, use Gravity Shadow to begin the cycle again.

Starships can regularly only move once per turn, so the Light Side can’t regularly move his starships back to back-up the stranded starfighter. Oppressive Enforcement gives + 1 destiny to all your starships. If your opponent is playing Quite A Mercenary, wait until he hasn’t the force to use it, and then strike. I am addressing the lack of speed as best I can.


 The Last 5 Comments
   Cam Solusar     I'm giving you a 2 to balance Shewski's 3. There's something to be said about being proactive. Dark side really doesn't function well with defensive strategies; you need to either inflict steady damage and force the opponent to come to you or go after them from the start with your characters/starships. You will probably want to stick to the former, so force drains, direct damage from CC Occupation and other forms of damage would be good to consider as your main strategy. I think your initial space strategy is effective, but to pull off damage/drains your going to want a space force that locks down on one system and stays there. I would recommend taking advantage of the TDIGWATT objective and starting I'm Sorry, Mobilization Points combo, and (V) We're The Bait. That way you can get Executor out first turn for free if convenient and pull a couple battleground CC sites. Setting up Dark Deal is an excellent source of damage with +2 drains as well as the +4 bonus to power in battle. Your present strategy is certainly effective and looks good on paper, but against a decent player with Houjix or Insurrection it's unfortunately equivocal to sitting there and doing nothing. Good luck!
   Shewski     I've expressed my feeling in general before on that thread, but here's some specifics. Note what Bluethrawn posted on your thread about illegal moves. I might use another deck type to set up, like SYCFA. You can use KDY (V) or run Imp Commands/Merry to get out Bespin if you want to do the TMNALTC trick. Use the good Executor. If wanting to do a game winning beat in space, I'd use some admirals/Imp Commands/Lat Damage. If wanting to play a Force Choke, use A Dark Time For the Rebellion (V) to get twix caves, WMAOP to get the Blockade Bridge etc. SYCFA will even grab the War room, another Twix. If you're relying on Sense so much, I'd run more. You should either rely on generation or Imp Supply, not both. You need some controls to stop sensing of your interrupts. More like a 2.5, but you've put good effort into it so I'll round up.
