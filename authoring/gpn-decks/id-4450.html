Stratagy:

First turn:
First, durring your opponent's turn you pull General Rieekan & Dodonna with strike planning. Then durring your turn you should have at least three force (more if your opponent is generous enough to give you some) put rieekan down and download a new secret base, use that to pull EBO the echo DB, also deploy any systems. If you have enough put Dodonna down, other wise draw up.

Early-game:
You after you get dodonna down, pull gospic, use gospic to pull commander luke or willard (The Luke takes precidence). Luke: use him to pull Icom Corp. Willard: Pull Princess Organa who pulls reflection for somelight retrieval. Third or fourth turn you shouldhave EBO out and a couple systems.

Mid-game:
Put haven out with Rieekan and put X-wings for free at haven and Rendezvous point. Put at least three x-wings at each system (Two at Keffix), then start force draining.

End-game:
There isn't very much end-game. Just force drain and the occasional battle. Use All wings report in to get some last minute retrieval. If the go to hoth bunch yourcharacters up (except for some leaders, leave them at the war room for the power bonus.) and just hold it. It's no big deal if they play battle order because you'll be activating so much.

Please be constructive with your comments. I need a lot of help with this deck, and i dont have any new cards(nothing after D*2)
