 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
At the latest, assuming u don’t get any ds2 sectors in hand, u should be able to set up TTO by the 3rd turn. Heres how:

1st turn: pull IAO (with desparate counter) which then pulls ds2 docking bay. Deploy jerjerrod there, pull 1st sector and deploy it.

2nd turn: pull combat response and deploy, pull the 2nd sector and deploy.

3rd turn: pull/deploy 3rd sector, pull/deploy TTO

rest of game: get mianda in his ship at the star w/ the 7.2 cannons. This pretty well stops any attempt to blow it up. Being able to make 2 moves and shoot during the move phase he can avoid battle while still shooting down starfighters in the sectors. The onyx defenders do a really good job protecting the actual star. (really fun: superlaser + flawless markmanship + relentless pursuit= 4 + 3destiny =blow away home one/any capital ship and make it instantly lost; and remember it can target at the symstem the death star is orbiting too)
occupy bespin with saber squadron, and the cc sites w/ the powerfull ground forces. W/ the 3 battleground sites plus we shall double our efforts your opponent is losing 6 force per turn from TTO alone. You don’t even need to control the battlegrounds! Add in any force drains and your opponent is hurting.

Selections:
SPACE: as afore mentioned, mianda is killer at the sectors (especially w/ we shall double our efforts= opponent's starfighters are maneuver –5 and my shot is +2). I chose onyx and saber squadrons over scythe ties b/c space is pretty popular in my area. they do a much better job surviving against serious space forces (b/c immunity and game texts). All power to weapons makes each tie ridiculously powerfull and immune to attrition; I cant shake him (adds destiny or grabs guns), relentless pursuit(reduces opponent’s power or makes his ship lost) and flawless markmanship(ensures hits) do a lot of damage with the guns.

GROUND: pretty obvious, everyone’s pretty powerful. I chose bespin b/c I figured it would be a system fairly easy to occupy. Hoth would be insane w/L(V) so popular. Plus tarkin (V) is awesome at the mobile sites. Most QMC often concentrate on avoidance, but I still kept plenty of people to protect against hit and runs.

this is much better than my first TTO try, but i would much appreciate any comments to make it better

responses: swcironecg, i know bespin is kinda slow, but if i do the mob. points combo i cant get combat response out. if i were using scythe ties thats what id probably do. endor ops would end up taking up to much space. also the sites would be free drains for my opponent as TTO doesnt work there. my people would be on another planet for TTO

   Shewski     If you are able to pull off the MP combo, it is far superior to just holding onto Desp. Counter, but it is not without risk. You are able to get both Comb Resp and IAO out, there is no once per turn limit. Re-read Desp Counter carefully. I'd recommend looking at my Power TIEs deck to see the EOps starts. Do you really want to risk generating one force on your first turn? Axe the Bespin stuff- I went with Gall. Instead of the two Mauls, I'd use EPP Maul if you have him. Where is Hebsly and Scy 3? Superlaser Mk2 is not needed- who plays dedicated LS Capitals AND will attack your built up DS2? Use IAO combo if you can. I'd pack more effect pullers to get the ultra handy Double Our Efforts out faster. With no EOps start and using the MP start, this deck is a 2 but can easily be improved to a 3 or more.
   swcironecg     This looks good, but getting out all that Bespin stuff might be troublesome. Instead, it is much easier to start with Mob Points combo in place of Desperate Counter. Use Mob Points to pull a system (Gall is good), then ditch it to pull Desperate Counter. I'm not sure, but I think that you can use it to deploy Combat Response and IAO in the same turn. Then, after using IAO to get the DSII: Docking Bay, use it to deploy a <>spaceport docking bay to the system you pulled. That way you are set up with a site early and don't have to draw for the locations. Also, start with the Endor Operations objective. Without it, you might lose your force generation at Endor due to Strike Planning. It also gives you some extra activation from the Endor sites.
