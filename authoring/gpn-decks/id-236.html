 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck's strategy is simple, get control of the Senate and get Lott Dod in there.
After that start getting your systems out with Lott Dod, this removes your "0" destiny's and gets your force generation going.
Watch your opponent and see where they are strong. This will help to guide in the direction you should go. If they play with very little space then go ahead and begin to spread out. With Admiral Chiraneau on a ship at location 6. You can get a drain of 11 plus just in space. If they are playing Tatooine then occupy a site, and with Aks Moe you are adding a plus one there as well.
Force drains are not the only way to go with this deck. A Darth Maul and Dr. E/PB combo will make mince meat out almost any opposition and all the extra destiny cards work on at least three characters. With Lott Dod in the Senate, you can get any card in your hand you need and should be ready for anything.... However, a well built light side dueling deck may be able to put a hurting on this deck. It would turn out to be a drain game. A good light side space deck(seen any of these lately?) Might give you trouble as well. How be it; almost all of your ships have immunity of some kind and most all of your characters are pilots. With the senate bonuses to help you should be able to hold your own against any light side deck out there.
