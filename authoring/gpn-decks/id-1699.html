 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Any Shields can be used.
If you do not get a Leia, use sabers instead. You will most likely get a Leia and a Someone Who Loves You in your hand.
First turn: play Docking Bay 94. Deploy Leia, Anikin’s Saber, Someone Who Loves You, and Flip. Get Ben (and saber) to Chamber ASAP. He keeps Han alive. Also use Bacta Tank with Han. If you don’t get Leia, deploy any character + weapon to the Audience chamber. The opponent may leave their two aliens aby themselves. Battle, fire the saber, make an alien forfeit =0, and kill the other to attrition, and free Han. Same if you don’t get a Someone Who Loves You and there is a non-alien character at the Audience Chamber. Otherwise, play Leia and flip.
Second Turn: Opponent will lose at least 3 force at the beginning of this turn (only way they won’t is if you did not get Anikin’s saber, or didn’t draw Leia). Guard Han with Leia (or Ben if deployed). Deploy a docking bay + any characters wanted.
Third Turn: Deploy a docking bay. You will now be generating eight force + whatever the opponent gives you. Beat up the opponent's characters using mains with sabers (via Sai'Torr Kal Fas). Destiny is low so I put in Smoke Screen and Colo Claw Fish to boost destiny. Battle, destroy the opponent, and drain.
If you happen to get an I Know, don’t use it when you don’t need to.
Saitor Kal’Fas is amazing. It lets you boost your drains with sabers, and each of your mains receives a saber.
Han’s pistol is also amazing. If the deck works, you are guaranteed to have him on table. The opponent will think twice about attacking Han, Leia, and weapons. Possibility of three characters + one force lost.
If this deck is up against a space deck, you will only be able to hold one system at a time (if any). There are three ships and matching pilots: Queen's Royal Starship & Ric Olié, Lieutenant Williams & Radiant VII, and Bravo 2 & Officer Dolphe. Sabers will increase drains by a lot, along with profit. Opponent should be loosing about 3 force a turn per location once set up (One Reg Drain, one saber, one Profit).

Card choices:

Lieutenant Williams- Better than Madackor. He will often subtract three from a destiny.

Mantellian Savrip- Lets you use characters to satisfy battle damage and attrition.

Sai'Torr Kal Fas- The Squadron Assingments for weapons. Lets you pull your five weapons with characters.

Senator Palpatine + Supreme Chancellor Valorum- Anti-Senate cards.

Traffic Control- Recirculate high destiny. Also can be used with tracking.

Somersault- Exchanges your low destiny with opponents high destiny.

Help Me Obi-Wan Kenobi- Make your Rebel Move as a react for one force? Definitly in my deck.

Honor Of The Jedi- Reduces drains, any force loss.

Matchups

Hunt Down: Battle. Use Your Sabers! They will be important. Watch where you deploy Obi-wan and Luke. Your Profit Damage should give you an edge along with retrival. It will be a close game (mains against mains). Choose battles carefully.

Dark Deal: Control Cloud City Sites, and Dark Deal won't be established. Use sabers to add to your drains. Your space will not do much, but deploy starfighters to Bespin: Cloud City and Home 1 to Bespin.

Bring Him Before Me: Keep Luke away from Vader, run away, and you will be fine. Otherwise, free Han and fight.

LSC: Deploy your Jedi to Naboo, and initiate battle against dark Jedi emmideatly- Kill Them with sabers. Try to keep their dark jedi numbers down. Use non-Jedi characters on Tatooine.
Combat will be hard on you. Very hard game.

Senate: Use Senator Palpatine and Supreme Chancellor Valorum at the Senate. Attack with characters every turn, and you will eventually kill many senators. Otherwise, follow their characters.

Invasion: Get to Throne Room First Turn with a weapon. Once you have built up a Throne Room, they will either avoid you or attack and probably be beaten.

Carbon Chamber Testing: There are two versions:
Jabba's Prize: Do Not Lose Han. If you lose both, you will not be able to replace The Prize. Leia Rebel Princess will hold a site full of aliens, to free Han. But Someone Who Loves You does not work. Flip, and place their objective out of play. Fight their characters. When you deplot Han, give him his pistol.
Non-Jabba's Prize: Give them Chewie. Leia Rebel Princess will hold a site full of aliens, to free Han. Flip and free Chewie (or keep Leia at Chamber) to stop their flip. Not too hard a game.

COTVG- Leia will definatly hold a site against aliens, hard game. Your characters (especially Leia) will be captured and fed to the rancor or sarlac. Keep Han away from the interior Palace Sites and Pit of Carkoon. Try to kill Rancor with sabers. Some decks only have one.

Thanks for reading this article.

   The Drop Out     Know your cards and what they do. Squadron assignments does not work with PILOTED starfighters, let alone PILOTED CAPITALS. Your BoShek combo (assuming it is the Virtual version, though it's not marked as such) will not work. This mistake alone could end up killing you. You go to pull Home One hoping to get a big power out to a system, opponenet says you cannot with BoShek (TD agrees), so you have to pull one of the other ships. Now you've got to deploy a weak ship and hope to avoid the beat down. Small distinction, but could really cost you the game. I agree with Moron: Master Luke and Ben Kenobi would fit well in this deck, as well as Lando with Ax. Space is very weak, might consider Landing Claw. It will help block drains and can be pulled using BoShek (V). Good tech, especially in decks with light space (like my Endor deck, all fair ratings welcomed). A good start though, Leia IS awesome on this deck and the new V effect makes her rock with Anakin's saber. Might consider a few Jedi Resiliences since you've got four characters running around with sabers.
   moron     You know how the objective works, but your force activation is next to nothing. Locations my friend. Obi-Wan's Hut would be great for you. Might want to use Ben Kenobi and Master Luke. A Lando with Vibro Ax is always a plus. You seem to be going out of your way to prepare to fight other decks, but not to accomplish your own goal. You don't really have enough sites to pull off this objective all that quickly. A lot of your strategy is based on the opponent only having aliens at sites, what with your choice of Leia, but that's a pretty easy thing for the opponent to take care of. This objective is nothing without sites for you to get your characters at, so throw a few in. Beware a COTVG deck because a good one has no problems with Profit.
