   Elis Helrot     Nice beginning, so try to get the Han from the Jedi Pack (or do you have one ?) And look at the virtual cards... Another must is to get some common / uncommon cards from a friend... Look for Get to your Ships (Theed Palace) or Squadron Assigments (Death Star II) Then morph Boshek to the virtual card. Voila, you have the following matching pilots: - Lando, Chewbacca (and maybe Han) for the Falcon - Jek Porkins and Red 6 - Wedge and Red 2 (never give Wege away, some of the best cards !!) - and Boshek (virtual) is a matching pilot for every ship !! This kind of strategy will give you a faster and powerful start (cause they all made bonuses on their matching ships) The Fusion Generator Supply Tanks are also available as a virtual card... And look for (Organized Attack) from Special Edition Set. With this one you can enhance your X-Wings. Modern Decks will have some problems at the beginning, cause they are overwhelmed on the mass of Characters ;-) Look at the cards above and you have a good beginners deck, then play with it many many times (only chance to get experience) and after the first games (where you learn the rules exactly and strategies ?) try to filter these cards you do not use to get better ones... Much Fun...
   bounty hunter6     good for your first deck (i think)...you have good cards but no strategy, a rebel snowspeeder is a vehicle not a starship, id suggest getting an objective...this deck has no interupts or objective(s) or effects they are what bring the whole game together...try looking at someone elses deck and making one the same but with the cards you have(might have to get a couple of cards)...not bad for a beginner i would give you a II maybe if this deck had interupts and effects that went with the deck...if you have any questions ask me on the discussion...sorry for the harsh rating but you'll get better...
   swcironecg     Take out all the weapons except the lightsabers and the X-wing Laser Cannons, then add in more locations (force generation is key), interrupts, and effects. Good interrupts would be Organized Attack (adds to power of X-wings and Y-wings and lets you pull 3 non-unique ones from your deck) and some interrupts that add 2 battle destinies when you have two mains together in battle (Luke and Obi=Gift Of The Mentor, Luke and Han=Don't Get Cocky, Luke and Chewie=Rug Hug, Han and Chewie=Life Debt). Squadron Assignments (Death Star II set) is an effect that lets you get the matching pilot for a fighter from your deck (or visa versa) and deploy both together. Use Cloud City sites instead of Yavin 4 if you only have Captain Han Solo. Take out the Echo Base Troopers, their deployment is too restricted. If you have them put in a few good non-unique aliens like Eloms (Jabba's Palace set). I could go on forever, but I don't know what cards you have. Put up a message on the disscusion boards with a list of some of the cards you have and I'll try to help some more.
