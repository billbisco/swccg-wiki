   Shewski     Good inclusion of the JCC, but I'm not sure if the Council Members are worth including. I think generation should be fine, you are at 10 with all your sites and Yoda out. Leia's Gun is an inspired choice, since it targets vehicles and makes them lost. Sense is definitely needed- maybe Sense & Recoil x2, and one Alter. Nabrun should not be relied up on at all, like Hoth said. Spell out your destiny 7 cards (again, you can make do with less)- also think of using Bionic Hand. You need Jedi Luke x2, and really only one Mace. Luke has more support cards! You need characters who can fight on the flip side, see my rating on version 1 for examples. Do Contol & Tunnel Vision. I still think you should add another LS Prof.
   Darth Hoth     looks a little better, but still not quite a 4. It's a little light on force icons. Maybe add just 1 site (like Theed Palace: Throne Room). You still have a lack of characters that can battle if the objective gets flipped. I would use Anikan's saber instead of Leia's Blaster. Use Sense so you can cancel Walker Garrison. Remember that Nabrun Leids gets really limited by IAO. I still think you should add Baragwin and use weapons (Intruder Missile, Jar Jar's Electrople and Ewok Catapult) as your remaining destiny 7's.
