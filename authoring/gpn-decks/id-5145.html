   Shewski     Again, challenger reviewed it quite well. I would simply not try to copy KTOD Senate but would try to fit in cards that you have to strengthen your best Senate deck. I'd probably swap in strong characters in place of Vigo, Reeg, and the Cor Guards. I'd run Sec. Precautions (V) if using MP combo to get away from all the Senate deploy hate in V6.
   challenger     First, your start cards: It’s not KTOD. You need Combat Response, and a mix of IAO/SP, YCHF/MP, BLYT, and Crush. If you don’t start YCHF/MP, you should start a system. If you do start YCHF/MP, you should start Maul’s Hut. I don’t think two systems are needed (besides, why are you starting MP without anything to pull with it?). Locations: Once again, your activation is terrible. Only 7 by yourself. That ain’t gonna work. Senate should be getting 9-11 by yourself. So, either add a system, some dbs, WMAOP/BFBridge, or ADTFTR(V)/Wampa Cave. Fighting Characters: You don’t need the Vigo. You should be draining 2 at a system and 1 and the Senate, which is quite a substantial ping. Your fighting characters should be just that: fighting. The KTOD package seems to have worked for them: EPP Maul x2 and EPP Vader x1. Now, personally, I would run a little more than that. Cut Reegesk, the Coruscant guards, Thrawn, the Mauls, Mara, and Vigo. Add an Emperor (V), two EPP Maul, an Arica (for the incredible amount of Profit decks there will be post-V6), perhaps a Dr. E Combo, Zuckuss, and DS-61-2. Weapons: Cut both, since you should have EPP Mauls and Arica instead of Mara. Starships: Cut all but Saber 1. Add Mist Hunter (V) (for Zuckuss), Black 2 (V) (for DS-61-2), and Maul’s Infiltrator. Interrupts: There aren’t enough. Add IHYN x1-2, SRF/WYB! x1-2, Stop Motion (V) x1-2, possibly Dark Maneuvers/Tallon Roll, possibly Masterful Move/Endor Occupation, Ghhhk combo, and Limited Resources. Senators: They look fine. If you can, you might want to add another Lott Dodd. Just because. Effects: Reactor Terminal doesn’t belong in a deck, if you want recycling play Janus. CHYBC? That’s a shield! Ability^3- you may want to play it (V), because if you see hit ‘n run Valorums in your Senate that can get your Senators back. Add Blast Door Controls and maybe another Senate Hovercam, and First Strike (since you’re taking it out of starting). Also play a Phantom Menace. I can’t believe I’m giving advice to one of my future opponents…: )
