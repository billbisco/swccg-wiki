 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I modified this deck quite a while ago but never posted it. If you want to see previous postings of this deck go to EBO deck- Draining+ Retrieval+ Speed= Win under my profile if you wish to see the changes.

General Strategy
Using Wokling [v] pull A New Secret Base and start pulling Hoth Sites and Echo Base Operations. Throw down a couple of people to get EBO set up and start pulling the systems: Hoth, Sullust and Mon Calamari. Set up Millenium Falcon, Red 5 etc, and start draining like crazy. You should be able to drain for about 9 per turn by turn 5 or 6, with early drains of 3 or 4 by turn 3 or 4. There shouldn’t be any trouble controlling space between the Falcon and other ships, but just in case someone drops a ZiMH where I can’t handle it, I’ve added a Hyper Escape. I’ve added some decent ground to hold Hoth and hurt ground drains if necessary.

Card Selections

Tala 2/ Blount
Make Hoth even harder to deploy to.

Medium Transports
I’ve decided to keep the transports and retrieval mechanism- it has worked during play testing and makes this deck a little more unique.

A Few Maneuvers
An easy to track card with decent game text at certain times. Mainly I use this for tracking where I can draw 2 or 3 of these for battle destiny.

Owen & Beru Lars
Decent characters with high forfeit, draw battle destiny alone. Enough said.

Liberty
I decided to keep this because of its ability to be forfeited to the used pile.

Match Ups

Hunt Down
Well, Considering I’ve only got one Jedi in here and I don’t care about sense and alter, I should be able to just set up and drain space. Should be a fairly easy win in my opinion.

Scum
Harder match-up but I should have no trouble dominating space early on. If I do, then I can slow their drains by deploying to Tatooine and messing with them a bit while I drain them to death in space.

Court
Depending on their extent of Space, I could actually lose in space but they’ll have to come to Hoth if their trying to flip.

ISB
I’ve had trouble with this decktype- which is why I added some extra ground. Drain where they can get at you and go from there

Imp Occupation [v]
Set up as early as possible to get the drains going. Try to slow them down as much as you can while you keep draining them in space. Hopefully you can destroy them before they take you down on the ground on Hoth.

AOBS
slow em up with corran horn and the others while draining in space.
These are the main decks I see this going up against
