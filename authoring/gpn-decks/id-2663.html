 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First Turn: Pull A Naboo Site, usually i pull the generator core(unless playing LSC, then I pull Battle Plains. Get Dofine situated with activate the droids on the flagship. Deploy a fighter if you can.

Subsequent Turns: Deploy droids in hordes. Once you get a nemoidian (Rune Haako is my fav to stick in the throne room), flip and hurt your opponent.

Card Strats:
Presence of the Force - get to theose hard to deploy to areas, or enhance one of your force drains.
After Her - Only Retrieval
Dioxis + SSA-1015 + OWO-1 = Dead Jedi
Droid Racks is the Shiznit.
U-3P0 - Undercover spy (Nuff Said)

   Ultima1     Looks good, but here is something you can do for your starting effects. Use Prepared Defenses to play (in this order) Inconsequential Losses (for all your guns), Naboo Occupation(It'll be placed in the lost pile right away, but trust me), and No Escape(pulls Naboo Occ. back into your hand). It's sneaky, but rules gurus have said that it works, so I'd go for it.
