 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Strike Planning pulls Gen. Crix. If possible he pulls Chewbacca of Kashyyyk who pulls Wuta for free (or else just Wuta). Wuta can pull sites to Endor. Crix can pull everybody else that is essential. Panno and Romba bring up the force generation a lot very easily (can be pulled(Crix)). Kazak adds a force drain everywhere there is an Ewok. Ithorains add one force icon to each side of the sight he is at: effectively increasing the power and forfiet of each ewok at his sight and adding to force drains. Beezer can be pulled, who pulls E-binoculars which can allow me to look for those all important high destinies. Nar Shadd Wind Chimes can pull any non unique alien + they can retrieve + they are 5 destiny, and I can pretty much find one with E-bino's whenever (or Signal). Off the Edge helps retrieval (especially because I can search for the destiny with e-bino's) and It Could be Worse is their to protect me from large force losses. The pilots/ships/Squad Assignments are not a really important part of the deck. I have heavilly considered taking the out, but in quite a few games they have slowed down an opponent in space and/or helped w/ battle order (although I can pay for it). The other caharacters (those who can't be pulled) are there for power. They reenforce my Ewok from fights and if I am secure enough, they can invade for good results. The best thing about this deck, and why I play it (even competitively) is becuase it is consistant. Any thing that is needed can be pulled, and I never have to rely on the opening hand or draws to get something done (I rarely draw at all).

   Pete1138     It looks to me like you have a limited card collection. If you have them then I would highly recommend Daughter of Skywalker (add power to ewoks by adding force icon) and There is good in him(start with much better force generation and Chirpa's Hut). Also, Nar Shaddaa Wind Chimes can only be used for force retrieval once per game. I would swap four copies of NSWC for Take the Initiative as it allows you to do huge attrition. Nice to see a less conventional deck on here.
   Darth Spam     I noticed a few places where your deck really, in my opinion, doesn't come up to scratch. First, there is Heading for the Medical Frigate. Great card, I use it myself, in fact, but it looks like you're only pulling two effects and not fully implementing the card. Second is force activation. You've got a lot of stuff and three or four sites isn't going to cut it. I also saw that you're trying to put some space stuff in there too, but you only have three starships. The problem with this is total annihilation from space-based decks and the only time just three starships will come in handy is when you're playing an all-land deck where there's nothing to hurt your starships and you're just using the ships to drain, but even then, you don't have a whole lot of systems to drain with. My advice is to get an objective to base your deck on instead of just having stuff here and there with no real focus.
