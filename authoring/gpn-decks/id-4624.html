 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Alrighty, obviously this is a non-Objective deck. I really like it! It needs work, but it does pack a punch!

First off, there are changes that immediately need to be made. I would like to drop the Bounty Hunters with Guns for EPP Bounty Hunters. Blaster Rack and the guns seem to be too card intensive, and I’d like to drop in some Jabba’s Through With You to add some needed power on the ground.

I tried to split my red down the middle, half land intensive and half space intensive. I plan to keep it that way.

I can pull and hold my docking bays for a solid 10 activation…add Fondor starting and we’ve got 12, and if I pull the Executor early, I can pull Gall to make it an even 13. If I get Sullust quick enough through drawing, I’ve got a steady activation of 15. Not bad, eh? Just gotta control the docking bays…

After reading Slim’s strategy article, I wondered what beatdown I had for this deck. I’ve got it together in space, but I think EPP Bounty Hunters and destiny adders would be the way to go for land domination. My only removal cards are Sniper…hmm, maybe I can work on that. My drains are only fair, but I'm not looking for that. I'm looking for beatdown.

I think that this deck has some real power to it. Any feedback would be nice! I hope it is much better than the first one!

   Shewski     Good comments by Waltdog. By using Mob Pts, you really need to be hiding at non-BG docking bays. You will be hard pressed to hold a BG DB intially, when only generating 3 force. Get some more activation beyond relying on your DBs, or you will be choked. Why Flagship? Use Dag Executor unless you want it to die with a since battle destiny. The Emperor isn't that good of a character here. I agree with getting rid of Imp Dom and focusing on ground or space. You are space heavy now- to have a decent space presence, you just need 4-5 ships and pilots, who should be versatile enough to help on the ground. Use V Vader. I'd use EPP Hunters as well until they make enough of their weapons practical (and affordable) to use.
