 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Hopefully most of you already know how Imperial Domination (V), Dark Deal, CC Occupation, and We're In Attack Position Now work, so I won't go into detail on those. If you need the crash course on any of these subjects, feel free to email me (kdavid@pacifier.com) or post a message for me in the discussion forums.

Assault Rifle tech - put the rifles on your Elite Squadron Stormtroopers at the West Gallery. Here's how your location layout will look: Carbonite Chamber, West Gallery, Chasm Walkway, Docking Bay, Sector, System. You can now fire the rifles among ALL FOUR SITES (see West Gallery game text), giving you excellent coverage for your Blast Points, and some emergency character removal (one time, I hit a Master Luke at the docking bay with a tracked 5... trust me, your opponent will HATE you for this!). Be sure to either give these characters some forfeit fodder backup, or keep a Trooper Assault in hand to give them immunity.

Ever want to give yourself 13 Force on turn two? Here's how to do it: turn 1, pull your two interior sites with I'm Sorry, pull docking bay with IAO, pull system or sector with objective, pull SecPrec (V) with Mob Point (after using it to grab Executor, of course!), and play all of them. Don't worry about guarding SecPrec with a character... if your opponent comes to Cloud City, they'll be regretting it soon enough.

With Deal, CC Occupation, and WIAPN on table, you have some pretty nasty turn-by-turn damage going on. 22 per turn, to be exact. Ouch.
