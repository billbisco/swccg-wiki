 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I have several possibilites to flip: I can control two docking sites. I can have a system and a site. Or if I've got four of my agents out, I can control whatever I like. This deck should be able to stay both in space and on the ground. But if I'm missing something, please let me know what you think!

Some card choices:
- I don't have any Outer Rim Scouts, so I'll just have to do with the agents I've got.
- Kashyyk is just too good not to have in here. I'll pull my systems in the classical way: Imp. Command - Piett - Merrejk - Systems.
- Stormtrooper Garrison: With 4 imperial leaders in here, the guys in white will do their best to protect them.
- Blizzard 4 will pull Mara or Vader for me.
- Battle Deployment can help me tremendeously on the ground, while WIAPN will smoothen out things in the air. Together they'll help my destiny too.
- 3 Imperial Commands should be sufficient for my needs.
- With 3 Dark Jedi, the 2 I have you nows should be quite helpful.
