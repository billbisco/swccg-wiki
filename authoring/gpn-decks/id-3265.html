 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The Mon Calamari Star Cruiser should be Home One but I can't find one.
I use haven on Rendezvous Point to get my corvettes for free and then my horde of ships is no match for any imperial fleet. IF its a land based deck i can force drain quite a bit once the obj. is flipped.
I have the organized attacks in their because the corvettes used to be x-wings but I changed to corvettes they seemed more friendly.
Leia is their to help me retrieve what ever I may have lost. The oranized attacks may be exchanged for corellian slips because talon rolling my falcon is annoying.
The few maneuvers allow me to get the destiny draws I want. So when shooting a x-wing cannon at saber 1 I know I will be ok.
Overall the decks runs smooth but I keep running out of room for stuff I want to put in. Any help would be greatly appreciated thnx.

   catman421     This deck looks good. Conciter replacing millenium falcon with gols squadron 1, it works better with general calrissian. Also you should replace the mon cal with any of the unique ones. A thing to keep in mind with haven, u can play it at rendezvous point, get things free, or you could play it closer to the main systems, like sullust and not have to pay to move things. Also, if your opponent goes after you there, you get an extra battle destiny, a benifit that you wont get a rendezvous point since the only dark starships that can move there are very weak.
   big_chief04     This deck is kinda out of date. First of all you need to put in better versions of your cards. V luke is the bomb. Artoo in red 5 or even virtual red 5 are both tons better than old red 5. I can see you will control space, but you will most probably be drained to death. Your drains arent very good, and you will have to pay for them. Try putting in some undercover spys and other drain blocking cards. Projection and menace fades are great choices for HB against darkside LSC. Without most of these cards, you will prolly lose against a good drain deck. Not bad though.
   tyranus     Some great pilots and ship in there. I can't help but think it would run better with some (not loads) ground forces. If you could try and maintain presence at a BG site you could play Menace Fades, not pay for drains, and other things too (Rebel planners, Marmor etc). Cool deck.
   Revelation     You'll win the space fight in the end, but expect your opponent to put up formidable resistance. Also, make it (V) Luke Skywalker, he's much better.
