﻿Important Stuff:

I saw this objective and I knew, it had the potential to be a powerhouse. I mean what is better
than ending the game by totally depleting your opponent’s life Force? Ok, enough of my
bantering, to the strategy. You only start off with one site, so start using your effects to pull
things. Begin landing your troops lets you pull docking bays, which are nice, and Conduct your
search let’s you pull the Endor Back Door which is a nice draining site as well as good force
activation. Use your objective to pull Emperor and use The Throne Room to pull the Emperor’s
Power( make sure you check it is in there while you are pulling your sites). Watch how your
opponent deploys and react accordingly; winning battles is the key to converting Luke. If your
opponent is a coward and will not deploy Luke, Deploy Vader to a battleground and make that
fool loose 3 force during each of your turns with Your Destiny. This one card can really hurt a
deck that does not play with Luke or against someone not willing to part with his precious jedi.
One big player on land, is Boba Fett Bounty Hunter at the same site as Darth Vader. These two
can really rock the house. I have won many a battles with these two even when outnumbered.
Space is important for you can pull out some very nice force drains with Admiral Chiraneau.
Pulling cards such as Force Lightning with Emperor Palpantine not only gets you the cards you
need but let’s you see what else is in there and how the destiny draw is. This is an important
aspect to determining how you play in response to your opponent. Force Drains are nice but
battling is the best for every time you win a battle your opponent has to place a card on
Insignificant Rebellion. For every card on Insignificant Rebellion you add three when trying to
convert Luke; very, very crucial if you want to Convert Luke. Here are some card choices:

Royal Guards:, You ask me, why two unique royal guards and I say Why Not? When each are
armed with a force pike, they add two battle destiny and can make your opponents hit and power
zero. Good for a fight or defending Death Star Two.

Moff Jerjerrod: When on Death Star II, he prevents opponents spies from deploying, forcing them
to use 8 force to move with the docking bay, enough said.

Crush the Rebellion: lets you pull I Have You Now which is nice when you need some more
battle destiny, and it prevents your opponent from drawing more than two battle destinies on
mobile sites; keeps DSII safer.

Emperor’s Power: Let’s you add a battle destiny anywhere just by losing a force from top of
reserve deck. Although the force lose sucks, adding a battle destiny anywhere could turn the
battle to your side making them lose another force and you one step closer to converting Luke.

Rise, My Friend: Let’s you move Luke as a captive of Vader from anywhere to the DSII Docking
Bay. Key card when you are ready for the final strike in ruling the galaxy.

The Rest of the cards that I chose are pretty much straight forward in accomplishing what i need
to do in order to pull this thing off. I have found that the destiny is either really, really good or
really, really bad at times. Drop! Was put in to try and fix this a bit. I hope you like my attempt
at this objective and PLEASE GIVE ME ANY ADVICE AND SUGGESTIONS OF HOW TO
MAKE IT BETTER(RATE MY DECK) THANK YOU!!!!!!!!!!


 The Last 5 Comments
   Barrysneverwrong     This deck is well-thought out and strikes quickly, which is crucial in a tournament environment. The only thing preventing me from giving this 5 sticks is the sometimes horrible destiny. The deck is destiny intensive, with the added battle destiny and weapons, not to mention depleting opponent's life force. I suggest adding Twi'lek Advisor to guarantee some high draws, or Shocking Revelation. Activate most of your deck, and play these to give yourself an edge when trying to convert Luke. A very strong deck.
