 Back to Top 
The set up is like a standard speed deal. get deal out by second turn by pulling folks w/ket malis and sites w/ sorry. if you can set up a merc pilot at a site or the cc sector, that's an extra bd at all the battles at cc sites (two if both.) This is just a trick that I think would be cool to pull off. basically, if you get ambushed at a site in general, ghhk and then ambush next turn. the three sites are in there for a reason. One sends people to the weather vane which is where I set up my destroyer droid or whoever. another is +2 power for the folks w/ a LS. and the Carbon chamber is in there for all too easy.

The problems I've had,
-Not getting Ghhk in opening hand and getting ambushed at a site when I'm not ready
-having drains cancled
-facing insane space fleets
-Not having access to They must never again leave this city.(I really want to try to get this deck good w/out this card)

I gues that's it, please don't be too harsh

Vader- Thanks for the rating. Look at the last location on the list. It says Cloud City. This is the cloud city sector. Also, Bane is in there for his other function. That's why I play Firepower v.

Cam- Thanks for the rating. I don't think slip sliding away v helps very much, since I can only do it during my move phase. I'll definately work towards that space package though. I have looked at that speed deal deck on decktech and I'll probably try to make stuff better there. Plus, I've playtested this deck. It definately can't hold its own against any good LS decks. Lol.
