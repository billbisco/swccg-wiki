 The Description
In all my years of playing, I dont think i've seen one deck based on musicians. This is a very fun musicians deck. When u add all the bonusues, this is my highest averaging power deck!

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
On your first turn, if u dont have him in your hand, search out Ak-rev so that u can get all your musicians out. hopefully u can get greeta out right away so that all your musicians deploy cheap. Use the musicians gametext to your advantage (play Lirin Car'n last so that he doesnt affect u, use Dodo to make sure u draw what u want). with all your musicians out, u have a very powerful force b/c of umpass-stay (especially if u get sunsdown and tooex on table). if your opponent seems willing to come after u, move your force to lars moisture farm and play both presence of the force and a jedi lightsabe there to drain. put your weapons and portable generators on ur warriors. if he attacks u at lar's farm, between all the bonuses from there, the generators, and the weapons themselve-not to mention the high destiny of this deck-the card precision targeting will really hurt your opponent. if your opponent isnt willing to come after u, chase him down with elis helrot. one last thing i cant help but mentioning: destroyed homestead! who doesnt play with luk or obi-wan? gives your opponent a little extra incentive to come to you.

   LordHoban     Definitely a fun deck, but I have actually seen a musician deck before (my friend, Thomas, used to play weird decks like Musicians and Ewoks, before he moved on to more hard hitting decks, though every now and then he went back to them -and didn't do too bad with them, either). I've also played against it, so I do have some, hopefully, helpful suggestions, though it's been awhile. <p> First of all, you need Rayno Vaca, from Theed expansion. Uncommon character, 0 deployment, your characters move free FROM his site. So, in essence, all you'll need is 1 force for him, he might even count himself, thus making it so your movement is free which is a must when you have so many characters dependant on sticking together. <p> Second of all, why not start with the Jabba's Palace Enhanced Objective, Court of the Vile Gangster? Allows you to pull docking bays, independant starships for defense, and gives your opponent 'incentive' to come to Tatooine, otherwise he loses 1 force at the end of each deploy phase if not at a tat battleground site. You also get more activation to start, and can get dbs out for further activation. A couple more power characters would help (since with Court, BHs now have forfeit +2 and immune to goo nay tah). I'd throw in at least a 4-Lom with Gun and Jabba (SE, aliens deploy-1 to same site, makes Scum immune to alter if at Audience Chamber) or Mighty Jabba, or original Jabba (which adds 1 to forfeit of all your unique aliens). <p> And, if you do not use Court, start with Jabba's Palace, expand the Empire on it, which means you can have all the JP locations out first turn (including audience chamber). A Gailad, Jabba, and a couple more defensive characters is recommended. I'd also add Power of the Hutt and Ephont Mon. Twi'lek it, POTH gets you Hutt Influence out of your deck and Mon (who is great spy defense) next turn. Plus, a Look Sir Droids might be recommended because of the off chance someone has A Gift in their deck or you may want to make 2-1B die. <p> Now, you have 1 major problem. You are too dependant on 1 location. The biggest helper you have is the element of surprise, but once that fades, most players will be able to work around it. This means you need some strong hit and run characters to counteract this. Prevent them from just sitting at every other site to force drain you, make them think twice before leaving someone unprotected. <p> There's so much, but for now, I guess that's it. If you want, we can talk further on the boards. I gave it a 2 for being a fun deck, but it still needs a lot of work to be truly effective.
