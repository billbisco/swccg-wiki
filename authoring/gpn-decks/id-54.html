 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I know most people will look at the deck and think it is unnecessary to have so many Senators. But I use them for my political effects to cancel force drains, add two to a force drain at a battleground, and add three to my battle destiny. It works well with my double senators so I can always have senators in my hand. I also retrieve 9 force from my lost pile in the middle of the game with Squabbling Delegates, which is a main part of the deck and crucial to winning the game with a large amount of life force left. Tikkes is probably the second most important senator for my force generation (+2 for every battleground I control) But my most important Senator is Lott Dod. He sets up my perfect hand by taking a card from my force pile every turn. This way it lets me get my Senators out quickly and have senators in my hand to use my Political Effects. Yeb Yeb would be my favorite senator though, every turn I can look at the top card of my opponents reserve deck. If I don’t like the card I lose one force from my hand (That I will retrieve later) to make it lost. But lets not forget about Edcel Bar Gane, he prohibits my opponent from drawing more than 1 battle destiny at a battleground (and we all know how the light side loves there destiny adders)

I will usually always have my two Controls in my hand by the time my opponent deploys a character, this protects my political effects. I get a good drain out of the system of Naboo and controlling the Naboo sites, plus with certain senators they add to the force loss of my opponent. If my opponent decides to come to Naboo I will be ready for him with my high power characters and my powerful interrupts, such as Dioxis (if my opponent is going to bring it, he/she will come with some Jedi) this way I can hurt them badly. If need be I can Disarm my opponent and then operate on them with Dr. Evazan (which you cannot do with the combo, that is why I choose to have the original Dr. E in). I have a card in here also that cancels Obi Wans Journal (Card being: Blow Parried) *Note: Obi-Wan’s Journal prevents me from Disarming characters with Unique lightsabers, and I am seeing them in more and more decks as time goes on.)
