 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
start with the objective, the junkyard, mos espa, prepared defenses, you cannot hide forever, power of the hutt, and no escape. you will always flip first turn, because of all your low deploy characters. since you can pull watto and anyone of the guys from power of the hutt first turn, you can get out search and destroy too. you cause alot of damage REAL fast, and with your extraordinary amount of characters, you will be able to confront them until the end.

one problem i found was jedi: so i will list ways this deck can handle them..
1) vibro.ax-simply remove them from the battles.
2) 4lom-cancel immunity
3) bossk- capture them
4) id just as soon kiss a wookie- bounce them,
and then come back and beat the crap out of
them
5) lightsabers-always good with 2 destiny

or you can just avoid them.. theres only so many of those pesky jedi running around.


if anyone says that i will lose to space, you dont understand the concept of this deck.
hidden base cant cancel my drains because of hutt influence, and i will cause more damage than you can heal from. with battle order, you will be able to limit their drains, and search and destroy will still hit them.
the trick is to get them to come to the ground. they will be overwhelemed.

as always, i will be glad to answer any questions. i still collect admirals orders (over 800 now!!), so send me an email if you got any.
