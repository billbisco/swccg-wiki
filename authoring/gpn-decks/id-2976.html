 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Basically I use Prepared Defenses to get out Inconsequential losses, No Bargain, and Molator (V). The main things I have to get out are Jabba, Tatooine Occupation, Den of Thieves, and Scum and Villainy. The other effects give you some options to cause trouble. Scum and Villainy is obviously the most essential element because you can retrieve force just for initiating a battle. Basically Tatooine Occupation allows you a lot of times to force drain and cause extra losses (up to 3). Of course the non-unique characters and Den of Thieves allows you to ignore at least for a time, force drains by your opponent. Any suggestions about interrupts to add for extra destiny draws? Should I add more non-unique characters? I especially need some suggestions to dealing with those Jedi. I have added cards that increase deploy but those don’t work against Qui-Gon. I need some more suggestions about what to add to counteract these threats because this is the only real threat, besides crazy force draining in space, to this deck, that I can see. Thanks for comments.

   grouchy old uncle owen     get an objective: the one that seems to fit this deck best would be Court of the Vile Gangster, which boosts your bounty hunters' forfeit, protects them from goo nee tay, allows you to start some good sites, and allows you to get some starships up to the system to protet your occupation (you desperately need starships, by the way, if you plan on playing the system and occupation). i'd drop the nonunique aliens and bargaining table and go for something a little more realistic, like space. also, i personally believe that a capture-oriented deck should never actually try to imprison captives or carry them around for long periods of time. i'm a big fan of the capture-during-battle, so you basically double your power by removing their character (using ECC Ig-88 w/Riot Gun and EJP Bossk w/mortar gun, and hidden weapons), then placing that captured character in opponent's used pile unless you have the coolest capture-related card in your hand: Human Shield. don't lose your guys, lose theirs! it's soooo cool, even though you can't use the "used" part in a scum deck b/c no imperials, the lost part still works and saves your bh's from attrition. anyway, i hope these are suggestions you can use.
