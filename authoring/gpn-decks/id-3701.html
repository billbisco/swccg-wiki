 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Strategy: Flip objective as quickly as possible. Then blow the death star to oblivion. I use Red Squadron so I can retrieve force after the star is gone. Order To Engage forces my opponent to fight, S-foils boosts power. Down With The Emperor! makes deployment for imperials nearly impossible. Incom Corporation boosts power. Punch It! is used with Lando In Falcon for an extra 2 destiny. Organized Attack boosts power. Hyper Escape allows me to avoid a beatdown. A Few Maneuvers boost maneuver. Houjix / Out Of Nowhere cancels battle damage and adds battle destinies. The Signal is a recyclable 5 and can grab effects.
