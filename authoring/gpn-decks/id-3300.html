 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck relies on speed and huge amounts of force loss to take out your opponent quickly. First turn, grab all that u can. Deploy as many locations as u get and draw if u need the guys to occupy the base. Once EBO is set up, avoid space battles at all cost. Spread out and drain; use alternatives to fighting, hyper escape, (my favorite, but it gets grabbed all the time), and houjix to protect from beatdowns. Ur opponent will be losing plenty to drains so he will probably try to stop as many as possible. (unless he's someone who "doesnt need starships, in that case hes dead by turn 5) if he spreads out in space, drop the falcon and flatten him. if hes playing a very space heavy deck (enough so that the falcon isnt enough to really hurt even with his force split in five) then he almost certainly has enough systems of his own for u to run to.
Card selections: plenty of anti drain in here. send in your high power characters if your opponent tries to spread out; its especially evil when u deploy to a site whre hes not expecting u like executer docking bay b/c of ur spies. (move them back to hoth with his docking bay if he trys to overwhelm your force, who doenst have docking bays in their deck?) use the ice storms to keep the opponent off hoth. it could be worse will stop u from losing much force, and figrin d'an and all wings report in will retrieve if thats not enough.

thanks for the suggestions shewski, but im afraid that, aside from maybe being able to download the (V) cards (ive had trouble doing so), i dont have any of the cards u suggested

Some of you character choices are a little suspect. Why QG, JM? He is deploy 8 unless your opponent is playing Naboo? I think your best bet is to do a Wokling/Strike Planning start, and maybe for the third effect start V Klor Slug or V Rycar (so you can deploy those Undercovers quicker!)
