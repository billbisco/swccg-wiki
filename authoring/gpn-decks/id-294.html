 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
OK, don't get the idea that I've got Ewoks on the brain or something just 'cause my only two deck submissions have involved Ewoks. This deck is a modification of an old-school Spaceport Speeders deck write-up I found somewhere else. Except I've added a few twists. The basic premise of this deck is to get a quick and bloody beatdown on your opponent. To do this you usually deploy Luke's speeder or a cheap droid at Mos Eisley and generate force by using the Spaceport Speeder interrupt. You can do this several times per turn and get 20-30 force (even though you are only generating force from Mos Eisley and Lars farm) The force you generate can then be used to deploy Mains and Toys for abeatdown against your opponent using all sorts of destiny adders. I have improved the old school deck by...firstly, upgrading characters from R3 - specifically Lando and Chewie (awesome destinies); secondly, including anti-barrier tech, since Rebel/Imperial Barrier is so prevalent (read: Desparate Reach); thirdly, instead of droids and Luke's speeder, which are unique, I have elected to go with Ewok gliders which are non-unique. Using the gliders has LOTS of advantages: better destiny (5), deploy for one force, can be pulled with Sound the Attack (another destiny 5). The overall destiny of this deck is INSANE (around 5 or so), which helps with battle destinies and Spaceport Speeder force generation. Once you generate a lot of force with the Speeders, you can search for your mains with the Tunnel Visions. If your opponent decides to not come and fight you, you just park ole Owen and Beru at the farm and Harvest his butt into oblivion. THAT'll sucker him to you. And, YES, Monnok will hurt you bad, possibly. This is designed to be a quick strike deck, so don't expect games to last long - if they do, you are in trouble (maybe). Also, if he's running TIES, well...on to the next game, just try to drain at the sites and Harvest if possible.

Comment on comment: actually, Spaceport speeders is restricted, not unique (can only be played 3 times per turn...3 dots)
