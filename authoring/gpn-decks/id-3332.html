 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Ok, hopefully you got either (v) luke, red 5, or one of the all wings in your opening hand. Use them to pull luke first turn and get himself a friend every turn.
Pull the DDDTA shield and use it to pull legendary starfighter. Hold onto Leg StarF the whole game until you need to use it.

First turn:
Deploy SPDB and put luke there for 3 force unless they saved a force to barrier him, if they did, then just deploy him to Tatooine for 4.
Pull Kessel, dont depoy it until you get most of your ships out, or until you know if the opponent is playing heavy space or not.

Second turn: pull dash and outrider with luke(if possible, if not anyone else will do.) Set up starships at tatooine. Deploy Home one DB for more force. Go ahead and lose interrupts to early ground drains, you can use them later.

Mid game, get set up on ground, use tunnel visions to get needed cards. Usually put han and chewie and melas at cantina, and everyone else at a DB. If opponent is playing heavy space, then use han up there. Han and chewie are almost safe together at a DB, Life debt gives them 5 destines, plus their 2 weapons and a fallen portal.

Some games you'll only be able to control one ground location. Use luke and tatooine celebration to retrieve at least two each turn, while draining for 3 at kessel, you might want to play the combo "control and tunnel vision in your deck because control cancels enter the bureucrat if its played on kessel.

ITTL is awesome with drains at DBs as well as an immunity booster.

Fusion generator supply tanks (v) cancels the annoying lateral damage on outrider and is a useful stat booster.

Setting up on DBs usually works with this deck while retrieving with celebration in most matchups. Barrier and fallen portals help prevent beatdowns.

Throw away on the edges so you can easily track a six with mirax and have 5 retrieval when you need it.

Match ups:

X-wing cannons take out zuckuss and friends, so their not a problem.

The only other space deck that can pose a problem is imperial command decks with many admirals and chimeara and other SDs. Just Make sure you grab imperial command with allegations, and dont spread your forces to thin.

If the opponent plays combat response, be wary of baron fel and get all you maneuvar boosting cards on table asap.

Against huntdown or BHBM, I put luke in a patrol craft on ground, or sub out 2 cards for OOC combo.

Like I said before, tatooine celebration works throughout most matchups, and Legendary starfighter wins lots of games.

If I missed anything, tell me and I will post it. Thanks
