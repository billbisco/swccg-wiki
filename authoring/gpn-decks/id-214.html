 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Basically drain at the interior Naboo sites and go to where he is.
With the Jedi chamber out within 4 turns you activation will go up and
what ever your opponent gives you, you should be able to dominate there
battle grounds. Use the Jar-Jar Electropole for combat cards.
Use Jar-Jar to get rid of some hit squads i.e. P-59 or P-60
Use Mace (at JCC) and Padme for direct damage.
Use Depa for tech
Use Ki-Adi for the objective
i.e. at a naboo site.. but just as good to opponents site.

Destiny Wise
5s= 9
6s= 7
7s= 6

Matchups Vs :-

My Lord Is That Legal
Use the Senators for hit and run at the GS. With Darth Maul at Desert
landing site you can win this as you can just drain away and wait till
he comes to you with Maul.. in this deck type you will never really sea Lord Maul
but beware if you do.

No Money, No Parts, No Deal!
Could Be tricky. ensure that you have 2 force every turn. BUT try and take
over Mos Epa and secure that site. put Qui with saber here to fight watto.


Let Them Make The First Move
With 3 Strike blocked in this deck you should fare well against them.
use clinging to the edge for maximum effect with a few well tracked 7s and 6s
With only one jedi to worry about try and get him away using Maul`s Demise
then using the remaining jedis for hit and runs you can effective win the
game... but beware of the dark side....
