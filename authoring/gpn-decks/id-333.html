Ok I know you're all going to go off about the senate stuff. You'll say"it is way too card intensive and not worth it and blablablabal". Well, it takes 7 cards for senate, and it saves me 4 or 5 duplicates b/c of Lott Dod's drawing ability. and of course that isnt the only advantage. It adds huge activation(with senate hovercam) and a drain of 1. Besides sidious works best at a coruscant location so I have to have one anyway.
