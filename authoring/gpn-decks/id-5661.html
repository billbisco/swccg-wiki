 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Average Destiny: 2.7

First turn: Deploy a docking bay. If possible, deploy Lando, Chewie or Threepio. Draw.

Deploy a docking bay every one of your turns. Use The Signal to pull and deploy Civil Disorder (V), followed by Goo Nee Tay.

Deploy characters to Tatooine, use their game text or Sai'torr Kal Fas to pull and deploy their matching weapons.

Free Han, flip objective as soon as possible using EPP guys, crazy Lando.

Spread out characters at battle grounds, protect Han with Ben Kenobi, and begin to profit-damage and force drain.

The object of each battle should be to "hit" as many of opponent's characters as possible, and in every battle you start, you get to retreive a character. This process, or kind of "retreival" battle, makes your characters expendable.

After several of these "retreival" battles, opponent's character supply should wear thin, allowing you to continue to profit-damage, Force drain. Opponent's lack of characters will put him into evasive action, giving you the chance to totally wipe out his line of characters!

Pull A Gift using Don't Do That Again, deploy it on Threepio for an anti-alien environment.

Use Launching The Assault to pull Home One. Use Mantellian Servrip to keep Home One alive by forfeiting characters -- characters which will be retreived by Civil Disorder (V) -- directly from hand.
