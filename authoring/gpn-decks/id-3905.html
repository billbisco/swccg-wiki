 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
One of my better CCT/MFD decks. Here is the strategy.

1.) Put sites in this order CC: Landing Platform, CC: Security Tower, CC: Carbonite Chamber. This allows you to drop Mosep to the Security Tower then proceed to drain for two a turn (on the sites on either side) and it will all be lost from opponents Reserve Deck.

2.) Freezing should come fairly quickly.

Basic Strategy through Turn...(any time you get a Ugnaught in hand deploy where you have presence to back it up. This set-up assumes you draw the worst possible hand.)

Turn 1: Take East Platfrom from Reserve Deck. Get a BH to CC: ST to pick up the captive. Take a Prepare The Chamber into Hand via Carbonite Chamber Console.

Turn 2: Take Tat: Docking Bay into hand. Take Ugnaught into hand. Drop Ugnaught and possibly another character to CC: CC

Turn 3: Take Tat: Audience Chamber into hand. Take Carbon-Freezing into Hand. Place Carbon-Freezing on CC: CC

Turn 4: FREEZE CAPTIVE...Here are the bonuses..

CC: CC = +2 (for controlling)
CCC = +3
Prepare the Chamber = +2
Ugnaught = +1

Total = +8

Might want to wait until Turn 6. By then you can have 2 more Ugnaughts out for a +10 and you whatever you draw will freeze (all 0's are pulled). I would suggest starting the Podrace right away. That makes waiting until Turn 6 even more productive. You should have almost 24 by turn 6. Provided you get decent draws (you need to draw 5 2's and a three, you get a +2 to each draw from the Podracer). Meaning you potentially could force your opponents to lose 14 Force in one turn (6 from Boonta Eve Podrace and 8 from Carbon-Freezing)
