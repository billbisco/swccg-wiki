 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
A Few Positive Notes:
1) Plenty of trackable interrupts, a skill I’m still trying to develop
2) High retrieval for hard hitting decks
3) Tech for all types of DS Decks
4) Heavy drains that can be spread evenly over Cloud City
5) Decent space to hold a Big Blue Deck

A Few Downers:
1) Site-Clearing Characters are few and far between in classic play, but I’m willing to tough it out
2) I’m not big on EPPs, but perhaps an Obi EPP?
3) Location choices, hmmm…need some help with that…
4) Grugnak vs. PoLR?

A Few Card Explanations:
Uncontrollable Fury {great Hunt Down, LSC, or BHBM tech}
Mechanical Failure {great Walker tech}
Lightsaber Proficiency {great drain tech}
Interrupt choices {weapons, high ability characters, “psych out” grabbers, and trackable destiny}

Any changes that could be made? Thanks for looking!

PEGD
