 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The basis of this deck is to sit at the docking bays and drain, but since the opponent is (typically) going to be unwilling to let you do this, I have several contingency plans.

The first is if the opponent decides to sit in space and drain. (If your opponent is likely to use Hidden Base, or this tactic in general, put in a couple more <> Docking Bays). What you need to do is get out Imperial Decree and control the two rebel base docking bays. Then get the <> DB’s down and reduce their drains. Then you can just sit there for your heart’s content, draining away. Although this may be boring, it wins the game.

The 2nd is if your opponent decides to evade you. In this case use Pinned Down to keep the opponent from reacting away from your troopers with weapons. This strategy can be deadly against QMC because even the infamous Path of Least Resistance forces the opponent to pay for running away.

The third situation is if your opponent tries to battle it out. With characters like Palpatine, Tarkin, Mara, the (V) Probe Droids and all of the troopers, this is probably the most enjoyable tactic. (V) Assault Rifles are an extremely effective weapon to use with Imperials, and unless your opponent packs (V) K’’lor’slug, they will be crying for mercy. The (V) Rifles deploy and fire for free, fire at same and adjacent locations for +1 destiny, and cause the target to be lost. Deadly.

Card Explanations:

Sergeant Tarl: A trooper who is also an ISB agent

Mobilization Points: Helps boost force generation

Imperial Decree: Cancel all opponent’s force drain bonuses

Imperial Academy Training: Helps out non-unique troopers and adds to battle destinies

(V) Imperial Domination: Retrieve those Imperials

Masterful Move: Pull Ghhhk and Monnok and do some good tracking

Trooper Charge: Makes your troopers power +2 and immune to attrition. Used 5.

Blast Points: Deploys a weapon on a trooper from your reserve deck. You can also add one battle destiny when your trooper fires a weapon.
