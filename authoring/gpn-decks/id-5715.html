 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Blow away Alderaan. Move DS to a central location (3-5). Deploy Piett and Motti on an ISD at the DS (with Deflector Shields if neccesary). ISDs deploy -6 (Piett, Motti, TUPITU and KDY). Move them wherever they're needed. Use Thrawn, Boba, Xizor and Guri to add destinies where applicable. WIAPN makes any ISD with a pilot immune to attrition < 6. Very good battle deck, ISDs are refreshingly cheap. Weak against force drains from Watch Your Step, need help fixing that.
