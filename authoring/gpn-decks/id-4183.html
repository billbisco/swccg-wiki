 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Basically, I get Luke, Han, and Chewie to their ships at kessel as fast as possible. the other pilots in ships are secondary. I use the Raiders on skiffs to take the land. The reason I use skiffs instead of PT crafts is they have 2 more power for one more force, and the racing ones deploy as reacts. I use the interupts for their various uses. Darklighter spin + Luke in R2 in Red 5 + few manuevers gives you a battle destiny of 8. I don't use more than one of each combo, because I don't really use the second use, and they have lower destiny. Electrobinoculars are there so I know what to make x for On the Edge. Late in the game I try to perform a Kessel Run from Ralitir w/ a captain for a quick 10 retrieval. That's pretty much it. I want to put in Tat cellebration, but I don't have it and don't really focus on ground much. Please keep in mind when rating that this deck has dominated my 3 star Court deck 5 times.
