 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This is a more fun than serious variant of matching pilot Hidden Base. The Bravos are not used too much IMO, so they make a great candidate to build a deck around. You can drain enough in space in case you get into a race, but you should be able to smack around anyone brave enough to challenge the Bravos.

RP (V) helps you get flipped faster. Basically you want to deploy 2 systems per turn until you can flip. I include 3 systems under the SE so you can have a different HB marker for every game. You can run less, but it is best to use at least 1 of the 3 systems to speed the flip.

I used every Bravo except 5, since I didn't like that card's GT as much as the other ships/pilots, since it helps vs droids ships mainly. I also include other Ep1 Ships for some flavor (and Lat Dmg immunity!). Rounding out space are some strong classic ships.

Characters are pretty understandable- Rayc is to help w/ the Run, Artoo BLD is to cancel destinies, and I included a small ground force in case the opponent thinks I'm all space. Red 5 (V) costs more than combo, but it lets me cancel that jerky Mist Hunter's GT.

A fun side strat uses Proton Torps to cause direct damage. Rebel Artillery is the easiest way, but you can couple them with the AO Let's Go Left (Thanks, DDM!) to cause 2 damage w/ every Torp hit. Take This! Makes it easier to get the right destiny draw.

You can rack up the power by using some of the trickery interrupts but also by using various personas of Ric and destiny adders like SWM.

The rest of the interrupts are pretty standard space fare.

Have fun with it! Thanks for looking!

   Darth Hoth     Looks pretty good. I would choose either Ric Olie or Ric Olie, Bravo Leader and either Queen's Royal Starship or Bravo 1 instead of using both. As Wedge pointed out, you're using both Artoo, BLD and Artoo in Red 5. If you want to keep Artoo, BLD in, use Red 5(V), it's text is much better than Artoo in Red 5 and you won't be wasting a card slot. Get rid of Whoooo! in favor of another All Wings Report In combo. The text is more open (doesn't confine you to an N-1) and you get the added bonus of the Darklighter Spin part. Having only two effects, I don't think that We Wish To Board At Once is necessary. I would drop it in favor of another Stay Sharp! Make a few changes and it could easily be a 4.
