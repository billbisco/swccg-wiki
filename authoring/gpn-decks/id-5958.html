 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Ok, so I’ve not played this yet but I’m looking for comments to get it ready to play.

Basically it’s all about going for big force drains on Kashyyk – using the Operatives and Ithorians to make each site a good drain of 3.

Ideally I’d hold back with my Wookies and Mains and spread thin on Kashyyk with Operatives and Ithorians to go for the flip / large force drain and to tempt my opponent into using his Defensive Shield Leave Them To Me. I’ll avoid a beat down with the Houjix and then hammer down the Wookies, mains and weapons to deal a beating of my own.

If need be then Obi, Luke and Han are ready to jump down elsewhere to do some killing on my opponent’s planet sites.

I’ve put Restricted Deployment and H’nemthes in there to slow down any further invasions of Kashyyk and to wipe up any characters he’s brought down and left vulnerable. I’ve got Romas in there to sort out any over confident reacting cards slowing the force drains.

If anybody can suggest a way to get Chewbacca out early then I could reduce the number of Wookies to make space for other stuff.

Nightfall, the Droids, and Slight Weapons Malfunction are in there to increase the pain when he comes down. The Superficial Damage will re-cycle my weapons and make up forfeit for the Operatives while my opponent occupies a Subjugated Planet location.

It’s desperately lacking and space defence so some cheap, hard hitting suggestions would also be appreciated – the deck is definitely vulnerable to Battle Order but I don’t have Han, Chewie And The Falcon and wouldn’t want to restrict the characters like that.

Cheers guys.

   LSJK     First, a few questionable cards: 2X – 3KPR X 2, RA-7, Disruptor Pistol X 3 ( I would take all of them out and replace them with operatives). Second, when I played operatives (I'm assuming you are using the old rules) I used all the generic sites and 20 or more operatives. You don't have to bait people, that's not the point of playing operatives. I usually had a bunch of cheap ability 3 characters (or Jedi if you can get them out) and sat them at the same site as one or two operatives so you get two battle destinies. I also used Menace Fades and a space fleet so you can make all their force drains -1 and all their bonuses cancelled. Then they have to come to you becasue they lose so much force and can do nothing to you, but they really can't with out getting crushed in a battle. Some cheap space fleet cards are Bright Hope (V), Tantive IV (V), Red Leader in Red 1, Gold Leader in gold one. I also used Home One just for fun. Hope this helps.
