 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Standard TDIGWATT deck, nothing fancy. Just drop and drain. Crush The Rebellion and Bad Feeling Have I help for holding the sites, while Early Warning Network can deploy TIEs from reserve deck as free reacts. Start an exterior site for Blizzard 4, which pulls Vader free and allows for a quick way to get CC Occupation or Dark Deal going. Colo Claw Fish for Bossk. It may seem like too many starships, but it's easy to hold Bespin and some TIEs can be moved to Bespin: Cloud City for some power boosts at the sites. CC Troopers are cheap, fast ground power and help increase the character count from the previous version, allowing for CC Occupation and Dark Deal to get out much faster. Also, Den Of Thieves gives me some drain cancellation. Let me know what you think.

Note: I took out Imp Command and one CC site and added Short-Range Fighters and Return To Base.
