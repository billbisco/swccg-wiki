 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Get the death star to Alderaan and destroy it as quickly as possible (hopefully by turn 4). Then I start playing Star Destroyers. KDY boosts immunity, and can be pulled using Motti. Motti also boosts power. Along with fighter cover, star destroyers armed with weapons are power +8.
After I destroy Alderaan, I attack Yavin. Once I destroy Yavin, I play A Day Long Remembered. All Rebel characters and starships are deploy +8. By this time, my opponent has already lost 16 force from the CPIs. Losing Hoth will finish the job. If I am unable to destroy both Alderaan and Hoth, my star destroyers can force drain and battle. R3-T6 @ Central Core boosts the star's hyperspeed. Maul, P-59 and P-60 go around destroying ground forces. The admirals boost the effectiveness of star destroyers and grab one AO. Tarkin, Palpatine and Vader provide protection to the death star. FWKTIL (V) deals direct damage to my opponent. ADITF (V) Allows for retrieval. SMOK allows for more retrieval. Imp. Reinforcements provides specific retrieval (starships and imps), FL kills characters when Palpatine is and LR deals more damage.

   Shewski     Agree with most of the below, you really need to get activation up pre flip, so Merrejk is important. Without it you make a slow deck even slower. Imp Commands will do the trick. SYCFA Flip is pretty slow to get running, but if you can pull off ADLR you should be ok.
   LordHoban     Looks good, but yeah, depending on blowing Yavin or Hoth may not be such a good idea. Death Star Assault Squad might be cool to put in since you can pull it with the objective. More unique Star Destroyers, a couple Emergency Deployments (v) and basically what has already been said below. I'd certainly like to play you over Cardtable sometime, and see how well it works. e-mail me if interested, kereej@yahoo.com ... I need to build a new LS deck, however.
   Rickynumber24     It seems pretty good, but it seems you are relying on your opponent to furnish systems. Also, your opponent only loses double the number of force icons on the light side when losing a rebel base, so blowing away alderaan does no dammage, and if you use your hoth and your yavin, they only do two dammage each. Also, from my experience, a lot of decks have obi-wan in them, so you won't always get away with ADLR. (I've had particularly bad luck in that area, but maybe it's the decks I play against.) But on from the negative, you don't need that many CPIs. If you draw it the first time, you can blow all 3 planets with only one of them. If you pull your first one, you'll need to get that one or another one into your deck to draw later. Also, you get your best bonuses with unique star destroyers, so maybe put some more uniques in. Remember to play ADLR on yavin before blowing it away. One last thing, I'm personally a bit excessively paranoid of MBO (probably because I play it) when playing this type of deck, so I like to put some ties and guns in (which would be helped by your AO), but I don't know what to put them in for. Many people just play without them, though. Good luck with your deck!
   Blackmoon     I would have rated it a '4', but there are too many possibilities for SYCFA to ever build the perfect Death Star deck. This one looks great so far, but there are a few changes I would make. I don't think you need the extra CPIs (because of AMVCO and the D* Gunners), so it shouldn't be too hard to find room for some slight alterations. First, I would make room for Commander Merrejek to pull systems, because they all need to be on the table to make the most of your deck. You might also want to add Return To Base to keep the star destroyers alive a little longer, plus you will be able to retrieve more force by redeploying them. And when playing star destroyers, Deflector Shield Generators (v) are always handy. Overall, I think this deck would be very succesful in a lot of matchups.
