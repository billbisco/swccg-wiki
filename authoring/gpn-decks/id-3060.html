 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Basically, this is my attempt to create a semi-competitive Jawa deck. The Jawas can block drains, get decent power and forfeit, and be retrieved once per turn.

I have been inspired by some other Jawa decks on this site, thanks to the authors for some creative ideas.

Geezum and Tessek are to be sent to the Audience Chamber as soon as possible, helped by You Will Take Me To Jabba Now. Geezum will pull the sites, which in turn can pull a Jedi, which can get their weapons with Saitorr. The pilot characters are there to hold Tatooine for Menace Fades. I considered placing in Tatooine Celebration as my retrieval strategy, but I like the reliability of Reflection, Figrin, and Luke (V).

Underworld contacts is probably the linchpin of this deck. It allows no drain modification by the opponent when you have aliens w/ two different card titles at the same site- Stick a Jawa and a unique Jawa and you have it- Plus the Jawa gets the drain bonus from Tessek at the Chamber.

Please let me know how you think this could be improved. I have most cards except EPP, Coruscant, and some rares from SE, DS2, Endor, Theed Palace, and Ref3. Thanks!

EDIT:

I only have one Utinni!, but I do have another copy of Aved Luun. I like the idea of the Tatooine Celebration, as I mentioned. Maybe I should remove my second copy of Thedit and switch her into it, and then axe Menace Fades to put in Tat. Celebration. I also just got a box of DS2, so now I have new copies of Wedge, Luke, and Lando to work into my decks.
