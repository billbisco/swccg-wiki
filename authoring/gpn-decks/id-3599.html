 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First turn, pull a gunner if you don't have the superlaser. Otherwise grab the WR. Primary concern is getting the DS moving.
Next turn grab the Superlaser with the Gunner. If it is in the force pile, it slows you down, but grab another gunner and you will have it by next turn. While you do this, it's useful to fortify the DB with one of the numerous 3/3 characters to get a destiny (and forfeit fodder if they get a destiny since the gunners are important). Try to flip quickly, though my biggest concern is the fact that I actually have very low destiny in this deck and therefore tend to go for a +8 or more often before firing. (excessive? Yes, I know. I'm paranoid about missing, though OE and ID help) Unfortunately, if they do a full-fledged invasion of the D* you'r screwed unless you get Tarkin and/or Vader to fight them off w/extra firepower.

Specific cards:

Ties and guns: I'm illogically paranoid of peope blowing the death star and need to feel I can do something.

Shield Generators: I hate those b-wing decks which shoot everything in sight.

Hell to pay: could probably go. It's in there since I start OE (mostly for the destiny boost. If you give me a good alternative I might put it in.

A Day Long Remembered: Destiny 6, really nice if you pull it off against a rebel-heavy deck. Too bad it doesn't come with the force loss that the light one does (in the form of blowing the death star)

The Empire's back: pull Tarkin (or Vader or Motti)

Any suggestions would be nice.

Edit: correctly identified WIAPN, V'ed TEB, Kicked Needa for Ozzel

Thanks for the tip about using Uncertain. A little unsure on what to cut though. (It's always harder to cut from your own deck where everything seems so dear to strategy even if it isn't.)

   Shewski     SYCFA flip is very tough to pull off. LH hit the nail on the head when he said you need more superlasers. Your red is extrememly lacking- definitely add more of that. Some that I recommend are Put All Sections On Alert and Imperial Commands. I think LH was referring to my deck, where flipping was not my main focus, just a pleasant helper so I can convert my cards in hand into life Force. Drop some space and focus either on flipping (and the EBO like bonuses) or using FWKTIL.
   LordHoban     There's another deck on here that deals with Blowing up the Death Star, you might want to check that one out as well. I'd say, throw in more 3/2 characters, as they'll be immune to Goo Nay Tah. Ozzel is a good example, from Hoth, 0 deploy for 3 power, 2 ability (and then, Imperial Arrest Order to give then even better forfeits). Make sure you're using The Empire's Back (v, not normal) or a second in there, to get those characters out faster and/or its subfunction that allows more loss of force. Looks like a pretty solid force, but maybe something that can help you get through your deck faster? A couple Sense/Uncertain is the Future combos, or just the latter card, might help that and insure the Superlaser (which normally you'd expect 2-3 in a deck like this) get out faster. Other than that, I'd say go at it and have fun!
