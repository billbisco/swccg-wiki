 The Description
This is your average Bounty Hunting Deck, based around the power of Jabba's Palace. Its a revised version of an old deck that I love, using the new bounty hunters with weapons. They're new to me anyway, as I'm just getting back into the game. The deck is probably not as competitive as many other posts here, but its a blast to play and I'd like your help to make it more of a tournament beater.

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The deck is straight forward in its principals. Get Gailid onto the table and start hammering away with drains, while chasing any big name rebels that decide to hit the block and dragging them back with Bounty for retrieval. The ornament helps out here as well, getting back force loss from systems and other drains that weren't stopped by the Den.

The deck doesnt have a presence in space, so it becomes all the more important to protect the ground.

Nabrun Lieds is big in my area so the Lobels in the deck really make a difference. They sit, alone usually, holding the non-battleground sites, while bounty hunters guard the Palace and Chamber. Jabba makes your resources go a long way, giving you more activation and cheaper costs. Gailid is really the centerpiece of the deck and it's important that he be protected. He is the target 90% of the time for the one Armor in the deck.

Between Bad Feeling and No Bargain deploy costs are sky high for the opponent, and None shall pass shuts down most attempts to breach the dusty fortress. Most spies are rebels, so the majority of the time you really only need one to protect non-battlegrounds, and they serve well to help stem the flow of enemy troops when the battlegrounds are threatened.

The deck does lack some versatility, despite the power of the bounty hunters, big drains, and hardcore retrieval.

Any help would be greatly appreciated. As I said, Im getting back in so there are probably plenty of cards I haven't even heard of. Bare in mind I dont use virtual cards at all, please.

Thanks all.

   Shewski     Welcome to the site! This deck looks fun but is not something you would want to take to a tourney. Four big things have changed from when you left. Starting interrupts have continued to be made, and one call Prepared Defenses (death star 2) lets you put 3 Effects on the table to start the game. Second, Imperial Arrest Order (Endor) has neutered Nabrun, so there is no need to run 8 Lobels, or any at all. Objectives are cards that give you goals to complete, and once completed, give your bonuses. All of the are focused on events, and there are 2 focusing on Jabba's Palace. Also, cards called Defensive Shield were made in Reflections 3. They are basically defensive effects that you can play from outside your deck. One is like There Will Be Hell To Pay, and grabs an interrupt- NSP would be its most likely target. There also have been a few cards that cancel NSP made. Anyways, your basic idea still works, but there are card that make you pay 3 Force for a drain if you have no space, and cards like Menace Fades or Honor of the Jedi (DS2 effects) that slow or outright cancel your drain bonuses. Come on over to the Rating Feedback Thread (RFT) and chat with us. I think your best bet is to focus more on an objective and then to use some of the new V cards made that help BHers out immensely.
