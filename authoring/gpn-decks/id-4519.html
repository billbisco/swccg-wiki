 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Strategy: Have fun!!!! :)

Between Utinni!, Aved Luun, Nar Shaddaa Wind Chimes, and the sandcrawlers the jawas simply fly out of the deck. Use you will take me to jabba now to get out the other aliens. Tessek or Arleil Schous are key, once one of them is out the non-unique jawas all deploy free. Occupy the exterior sites w/jawas and sandcrawlers while keeping kalit and tessek safe at the audience chamber. Battle at the battlegrounds w/ the power 5 jawas! (+2 from kalit, +1 from sandcrawler, +1 from being on Tatooine, become power 6 at jawa canyon!)
If you your opponent runs from the super jawas, drain. Tessek adds one to each force drain and Deneb Both is the ultimate mosep (drains come from their force pile if possible!). Underworld contacts prevents the opponent from stopping drains up to 14 force per turn!

Card selections: *jawa guns/superficial provide forfeit fodder, *Geezum gets all the locations out, *old ben keeps the important aliens alive, *radar scanner lets me see my opponent’s hand, and really puts the hurt on other fun (jawa/tusken raider) decks, *neb dulo makes the jawas a bit harder to shoot

Enjoy!!!
