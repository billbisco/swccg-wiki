 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
If you can’t figure it out basically, please don’t review, no offense or anything, though. Essentially, use ED(V) to get out Carbon Chamber Console ASAP and then you can start using SD(V) to get out MP for Executor, TMNALTC for free deployment of it, Ast. Shortage (V) cuz it’s great, Krayt Bones (V) to eat people, AAA for damage, BFHI for extra deploy. And then you should know how Deal works.
