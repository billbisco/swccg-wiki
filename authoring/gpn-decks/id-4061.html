 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Basically what you want to do early game is activate everything, and draw like hell. Play sites when you pull 'em, put high-destiny stuff back when you find it, etc. Once you get 2-3 Vaders in hand, you can start setting up the ultimate denial deck.
Here's how the "denial" works:

3 Undercover spies
2 characters who satisfy all battle damage & attrition
1 character who hides from battle
1 character with immunity to attrition
1 starship with immunity to attrition

You place these guys down at locations with big drains for your opponent, and block away. Sidious and the Infiltrator are the riskier pair to play on table (since you can still take battle damage), so hold them for last. While you're blocking all of their force drains, they'll be constantly losing force to Visage and your never-ending Vader's Obsessions. EPP Vader does a few hit-and-run assaults (don't forget to use Evader and I Have You Now!), the battle droids are another heavy-hitting combo, and Praji/Tagge pull out Elsek, Avarik, and Jonus whenever you need a spare.

Card choices -
TC-14; Play him as a prelude to a big strike against their Jedi. POOP him to cancel their immunity, them play the battle droids and watch their big Jedi guys die to attrition.

Vader's Obsession; More like my own obsession. I've always wanted to use this card mainly for retrieval purposes, and that's what I'm doing here. With a tracked 6 and 7, you'll be causing damage of (and retrieving) quite a bit. Sure the objective gets POOPed... it's not like I'm using it for anything important, anyways! >:)

After Her!; Extra retrieval. Let them have a site where they can drain for 1... as soon as they use it, play this card and drop your beatdown squad.


You don't have any drain sites of your own, so all the damage throughout the game is only going to be caused by Obsession, Visage, and battles. Have fun! >:)

David Kangas
