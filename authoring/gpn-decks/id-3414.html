 The Description
I really appologize for my first entry, but I couldn’t get my submissions to work, so I tried anything that could possibly spark a submission, and of coarse, the one I didn’t want to work, did.
This Dark Side deck is great for players that like to do more than one thing at a time. The droids help you to give nice and big force drains, where as the Jedi help create invincibility with in the game. Everything wlse (ranging from bounty hunters to capital starships) creates contraversy between opponents. Over all, this deck has helped me win 3 tournaments, and I hope you guys give it a nice rating. Also, I believe that people will be able to use this deck to their advantage, as I have.

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First, you want to start out with Tatooine: Jabba’s Palace, giving you a nice force generation of 3. During your deploy phase, it also lets you deploy 1 Jabba’s Palace site from your reserve deck. The site you would want to take out would be Jabba’s Palace: Entrance Cavern. This lets you deploy Bib Fortuna for free, and if you wait another turn or so, you can deploy Thok & Thug and get a force drain of 1 there. The once that is done, no one can attack Bib because of the Gamoreans that you put down. Also, they can’t move, from the same site to another Jabba’s Palace site, away from Bib Fortuna. This creates a nice little trap to keep your opponents characters there in a pretty tight lock-down. If you give the Gamoreans there a Vibro-Ax, your opponents must beware, because they can be placed out of a battle. Now, if you have Twi’lek Advisor in your hand, you can use it to obtain the Reactor Terminal, which lets you recycle any amount of cards from your hand into your uesed pile. Even though by now you should have many, many, many droids in your hand, DO NOT RECYCLE THEM BACK INTO
YOUR USED PILE!!!!!! If you have Ev-9D9 in your hand, then she can help you get out
Jabba’s Palace: Droid Workshop, where your force drains are plus one when with
Ev-9D9, and your droids’ deploy cost is minue one. Once all of your droids are out, you will have a force drain of over 20!!!(including the one from Thok & Thug). In just a few truns, you are ALMOST garunteed a win. Then, if you opponent tries to foil your plans (which he most likely will TRY to do), simply apply Jedi to the needed area. If you deploy Tatooine: Desert Landing Site, you can get your Darth Maul out of your hand for free, and you can move him to the designated area. Just for fun, if you (for some strange reason) want the game to go a little slower, then pull out the bounty hunters and wreak some havoc. Also, Capital starships are a plus when trying to annoy your foe. So, in conclusion to my strategy for this dark side deck, you are ALMOST garunteed a surefire win in just a few simple moves!! The Rebels don’t stand a
chance!!!!!!HAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!

   Cam Solusar     I give you a three plus one to make up for the one that I gave you on your accidental post. (Sorry about that :), you should probably edit it to say that it was accidental) This deck looks good, but it is vulnerable to Menace Fades and Great Warrior. Menace Fades you can suspend by battling it out in space, but Great Warrior could be harder to deal with. I would advise including one or two more battleground locations where you could drain in a pinch. The fact that you have won three tournaments with this deck shows that you have been successful in bypassing these strategies (if you've faced them), but there is always a first time for everything. Good luck with this deck in the future!
   Shewski     I agree with the below. Plus, Honor Of The Jedi will hurt as well since your drain is not at battlegrounds. In this age of defensive shields, you MUST have an adaquate space force, or else you will be slowed down incredibly.
   swcironecg     Unfortunately, should your opponent play either Menace Fades or Great Warrior, all your force drain bonuses would be cancelled and your drain of 20 would be a drain of zero. However, this can be fixed. Deploy Rodain (V) to the Droid Workshop and he will prevent your force drain bonuses at that site from being cancelled. But then your opponent could play Ultimatum and limit the damage to 2. Building a big drain at one site just doesn't work anymore. Also, you need more ground forces in my opinion (more Vaders and Mauls would be good). This deck looks like it would be fun to play, but doesn't appear to be up to tournament standards.
