 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The basic strategy of this deck is to force the Light Side to come to fight on Tatooine. I do this by draining with Tusken Raiders and luring the enemy into what they will think is an easy kill. All I want the enemy is to deploy characters at sites so I can make them missing and useless. When they deploy either to force drain or attack my seemingly helpless Raiders I can use cards such as Ng’ok or Imperial Barrier to prevent them from battling immediately. Next turn I can deploy a Sandwhirl, making the enemy characters missing, while my Tusken Raiders can continue draining unopposed. If my opponent decides to attack en force with Jedi I can take them out with Thermal Detenator or Naboo Blaster Rifle+ Jodo Kast. Also, I can knock away their lightsabers for the battle with the useful Gaffi stick. Hidden Weapons is in their just in case I really need help in a battle and I think I can still put up a decent fight if I have to. But that’s not the objective of this deck. The objective is to make characters missing. This has two major benefits: 1. Your opponent cannot deploy any more of that card if unique (very nice to deal with those Jedi) 2. You can cause force damage with a card called Exhaustion, which causes the opponent to lost 1 force for each missing character. Intruder Missile are in their for destiny and I would add Colo Claw Fish but I don’t have. Any suggestions? Should I add a Vader or two?

   Cam Solusar     I don't think you have enough sand people to cover all of the locations you have. Its also possible to find missing characters without special cards, all it takes is to form a search party. (designate a certain number of characters to be in the search party, draw destiny, if destiny + 1 for each member of the party (2 if scout) is >5, randomly 'find' one of your missing characters.) Its pretty simple to 'find' missing characters. I would advise you to stick to the Jabba's Palace theme that you had in your previous deck. This deck is creative but it is not hard to beat its strategy. Sorry! -Solusar
