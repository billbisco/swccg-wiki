Hidden Base

Order to engage


 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The strategy for this deck is centered around simple force drains and battles.

I basically pull my systems with Hidden Base and order to engage with the signal (although that card isn't quite as necessary)
The key to the deck is spreading the fighters (it doesn't matter what they are)and draining. When your opponent tries to counter your drains with a fleet, throw down five or six Out of nowheres and kick serious butt in a battle.

I know I'm missing something. Like I said before, I don't have the cards yet, but with online trading and cheap ebay auctions that shouldn't be that hard. I just want to know what the obvious flaw that I'm missing is.
