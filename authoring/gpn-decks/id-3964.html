 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Alright, this deck did fairly well at the tournament I went to recently. All the games where very close, I lost them all but only by very small margins (-6, -12, etc.) I want to get this deck up to tournament level. It you'll notice there are two cards missing. I took out R2-D2 and Dagobah: Bog Clearing. There are some cards I was thinking of putting in...I really would like some input so let me know what I need to do. Also Def. Shields are coming.

Cards I was thinking of putting in...

Honor Of The Jedi
Somersault
I Feel the Conflict
Sorry About The Mess

   swcironecg     I agree with Shewski. Also, you need some battlegrounds to occupy so that you can satisify Come Here You Big Coward. A few more On The Edge would also be good. I would use Luke With Lightsaber x2, Daughter Of Skywalker, and Leia, Rebel Princess. This gives you different options for who to train and what to do with the other Skywalker. Check out tyranus's "Higher Learning" deck for some ideas.
   Shewski     I won't give this above a 3 unless I get a full strategy section with every incarnation. Just cut and paste it from old ones and update it =) You need to get Daughter of Skywalker in instead of Leia (RP). Use EPP and R3 characters to save on card space. Maybe even use EPP Luke to train. Use copies of A Jedis Resilience to keep lightsaber characters alive. You only need a little space force since you can pull combos out that you need with test 4. Forget about Garbage and Jedi Leviatation. Honor is good to add, the other ones I would not. I'd add in some buddy interrupts at least Life Debt and r2 in red 5 for the powerful 0 or 7 detiny function.
