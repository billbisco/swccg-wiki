 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Use Heading for the Medical Frigate to use Superficial Damage, Battle Plan and Squadron Assignments. Then just try to overwhelm...
No big complicated strategy that needs just the right cards at just the right time.
Then make it up as you go along, not the best strategy but it seems to work.


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     Two sabers for a beginners deck. What cards do you have at your disposal? On of the most basic things is to use an objective, and/or starting interrupts and a starting effect. This deck would be fun to play (like the preconstructed ds2 decks are) but would get killed in a tournament. Keep trying- if you want more specific tuning, post on the discussion board and I (as well as others) will be happy to help.
   Revelation     Correction (no offense meant): No strategy. I gave this the same rating I would give a preconstructed, just one higher because you yourself said this was a beginners deck. To start off with, use virtual Luke Skywalker and put in Artoo-detoo in Red 5. Wedge Antilles, RSL would be better than commander, and the SE pilots and ships would make a good addition. To help remedy your weak ground, start with Strike Planning to pull Solo, Madine, and Chewie off the bat. Also use more systems if you're going to play with twenty-five starships.
