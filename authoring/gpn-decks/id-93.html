﻿

For some of the other reviews: Take out: This absolutely Right, Strangle, Anakin Skywalker. Put in Fire fightX3 and 1 more ewok catapult. take out RACING skiff and put in SKIFF. It holds 6 pass. instead of just 3. Put mains aboard and react like crazy! If you have 2(on the edges) trade for 2 X firefights

Overview:
Always start with Strike Planning, Wise Advise, and YISYW. Use Strike P. toget out Han and Crix-
Crix to get Beezer(and other scouts), to deploy a device or weapon, and use the asp-707 to put those
back on top. Auto matic 7’s!!!
On the first turns get Madine, Han, Beezer, and Wuta.(Madine and Han before activating on
opponents turn and yours !!!!!!)
Also lose YISYW to get Close air support.
Now that the first turns are over CONTROL ENDOR!!!!!!! Get out scouts with Crix! I know comes
in handy when Han ”accidentaly” is left alone at a site! Hi Leia, and what do you have with you? Mr.
Lightsaber! Get over here ”Chop, Chop!”
These starships are here for protection of Tala twins. Nobody needs to lose those Battle bonuses
from Close Air Support!!
Keep R2-D2 at any interior scomp link. Why? R2 says: if at a SCOMP LINK when opponent draws
ANY destiny of: 1-3: Activate 1 force----------4-6 may draw the top card of reserve deck into hand.
Can’t argue with that! He IS a SPY so he’ll deploy anywhere, leave him alone at an interior site to
gaurd against TRAMPLE. Remember because he is a spy your characters may deploy where he’s
at! Any ?’s? D-mail me!

If this deck is done properly you can draw 4 destinies and add 1 to each and cancel one of your
opponents.
