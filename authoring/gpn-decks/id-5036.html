 The Description
With the help of Cam, Shewski, and most especially challenger, I completely changed about a third of my previous QMC deck listed a few posts below this one. I kept some of the elements of me in it, but I was looking for a better rounded tourney deck that could face open play, yet stay classic. Some of the cards suggested I can’t get a hold of at this time, and I’m not transforming it into open format. So, here it is!

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
With anything that you create yourself, you feel that it is awesome and it is the best that you can do. However, this is not always the case. I’ve been working on this deck since eighth grade, and I’m still not finished with it. I work on it, and then I put it up on the boards…let it get batted around a bit, and then I take it back to the drawing board. This is probably the fourth rendition of my QMC deck. Thanks to challenger, Cam, and Shewski who gave my deck a rigorous raking. I couldn’t do everything you guys suggested…some because of my card base, some because, like any personal deck, you want to keep some things for yourself as a sort of inner satisfaction (i.e. SABACC). :D

Well, let’s just begin with the start of the game. 99.9% of the time I’ll probably start KTEOF, Wokling (v), and Squadron Assignments. However, if I’m playing Senate, I’ll start Scrambled Transmission (v). If they play anything related to Cloud City, I’ll drop Sai’torr Kal Fas (v) for added back up, and maybe even Lando’s Not A System, He’s A Man. It just depends on the situation. Wokling (v) can pull ALL of my non-starting effects, but it will usually pull Cloud City Celebration for nice retrieval.

On to character selection… I can see the point of using EPPs in a deck like this. It would save me the card slots by removing character weapons and Sai’torr Kal Fas (v), and I could put more characters/bullet effects/interrupts. But seriously, I like it that my characters have GAME TEXT, and more than just a weapon. I can drop LS,JK by himself and do some serious damage. Obi-wan can move characters like Kitik Keed’kak (v) away from the location. Daughter of Skywalker adds to my activation and can also take on Mara Jade, TEH on her own. Orrimaarko/Corran Horn equals the death of any Imperial spy, and Corran can smack on Bounty Hunters. All the smugglers can hold a convention and open up a can of whoop @$$, and they probably won’t get touched with a +2 defense bonus from Talon Karrde. Cloud City Lando is great for Sabacc and LNAS,HAM, and if I control all of CC, he’ll be a power of 8. :D Grugnak is a constant PoLR, and BoShek can pull any starfighter with Squassin, and he can pull Obi’s Journal. Haash’n/Thaneespi/Ackbar equals no Lateral Damage, no destiny if opponent doesn’t have ability >6, +8 power, +1 hyperspeed, +1 armor to the Home One. Who doesn’t like that?

I think my fleet is a sound fleet. I kept the Liberty because it has its own Bacta Tank, and the forfeit 9 can come in handy. R2 in R5 was a good suggestion, challenger, it makes my fleet more mobile and it opened up a card slot for all those interrupts you suggested. Home One & Company is a no brainer…it can kick when it is fully loaded. Outrider + Super Falcon = Two battle destiny, their power is minus whatever the Outrider equals…yeah, not pretty. The only thing I wish I had to tie this up is Wedge, RSL/Red Squadron 1/X-wing Laser Cannons. Gotta get that…

This deck has lots of red, and I’m going to get more for it. I need some Off The Edges…and I’m fixing to get my hands on a copy! For signature and novelty, I have Cloud City Sabacc/Casino retrieval. I haven’t lost Sabacc in the last ten or eleven rounds, so it is basically solid retrieval. The SATM/BPs are boosters for my blasters, and I can snipe with my lightsabers. OOC/TTs are good recyclers and, as challenger says, can get rid of Visage and pesky cards in the lost pile. With high ability characters, Alter and Sense combos are feasible to counter the red wave. Clash of Sabers is necessary to hold characters in place for beatdown, while PoLR can be used to runaway or react. The Bith Shuffle/Desperate Reach kills tracking immediately and is good for tracking on your own. ;) For the effects…I chose Mechanical Failure because it is good tech against Walkers. Uncontrollable Fury is good tech against BHMB and Hunt Down, CC Celebration is for constant retrieval, while Lightsaber Proficiency adds to my force drains and the power of any lightsaber-holding character.

I know that EPPs are the thing these days, but I seriously enjoy having the real characters and their weapons. Plus, I don’t have the EPPs… Obi-wan’s Journal is just icing on the cake…blocks a weapon destiny (SPARRING!) and denies removing the drain ability from the lightsaber. It also doesn’t allow the character to be Disarmed, which even though you don’t see too much anymore, it could be a nasty surprise.

I hope this deck is much better than the last three times I have posted this deck. I’ve played it, and it plays very smoothly. I can flip in two-three turns easily, and I can be getting the cards I need soon after. I don’t have too many changes to make, maybe a five card switch out. I’m serious…after reading my replies, I overhauled my red section and dropped some of my “veteran” characters and starships that have never seen the inside of my collector’s box. What do you guys think?

Thanks!
PEGD

   challenger     Ok, here I am again. I understand that you want to stay Classic, but given that you’re playing in Open with very Open decks like DS Senate, I just don’t get it. Near as I can figure it, you’re trying to give yourself a challenge. Keeping in mind you’re going to stay Classic with this deck (you could really boost it by adding Open cards, though. REALLY boost it), I won’t recommend cards that aren’t Classic legal, on the other hand, I WILL point out problems you will run into with Open decks. So, without further ado, let’s get typing. First, let’s examine your start cards. You have six available. Obviously, you’re going to start Squassin and KTEOF. So you have one more choice out of four. This is where we have the problem. It appears to me that you have TWO effects that are important for your current strategy- Lando’s Not A System, and Sai’tor (V). Given that I’m going to suggest axing sabacc later on, let’s just stick with Sai’tor (V). If you don’t start it, you have all those weapons floating around in your deck. If you do start it, then it makes sense, but then you don’t have your Senate bullet, and you don’t have Wokling (V) either. If you can’t start Wokling (V) in any games, then there’s no point having it in your deck. So now it’s Strans (V) vs. Sai’tor (V). Granted, you can probably get out Strans (V) with KTEOF, and probably reasonably quickly, so I won’t take up dispute with that. However, if you start Wokling (V) over Sai’tor (V), you get an easy CCC pull, but by the time you get out Sai’tor (V) it probably won’t be of as much use. Once again I would recommend EPPs to overcome this dilemma, but if you’re not doing that, just ax Wokling (V). Also Ineffective Maneuver (V) couldn’t hurt, especially since you’re not using EPPs. Shields you could use would be Ultimatum (vs. Walkers), Affect Mind (V) vs. Combat, and you know, other stuff based on preference. Moving on to locations. I’m glad to see you’ve added the docking bay (trust me, you’ll be glad you did), but might I just ask, where is the West Gallery? It’s a great site for QMC. Not only do you get 2/1 generation, but opponent drains –1 there, AND your blasters there can fire at the docking bay! I would cut the Casino for the West Gallery. But I need the Casino for sabacc!, you protest. Well, cut sabacc! You’re playing in Open here, PEGD. Everybody’s going to pop We’ll Let Fate-a Decide, Huh? on you and your sabacc suddenly ain’t so good. Besides, even if for some reason they don’t play that shield on you, they can just grab CCSabacc and after two fun games it’s curtains for that strategy. So, I really think kicking sabacc on a train headed to Quebec would be in your benefit. Moving on to characters. The first thing I notice is the Mon Cal pilots. Liberty’s OK to stay in, but I don’t think Home One should stay. It’s just too expensive. Again, I think you should go with matching pilots/ships instead, especially since you start Squassin. The next thing I notice is Orrimaarko. Yeah, making your opponent lose 2 is cool, and his stats are pretty good, but I think other people would serve you better. Arconas are good for QMC, for example, since they’re cheap people who can Celebrate for you and also flip your objective, holding a site with only minimal risk of getting beaten down (PREM Sense or virtual Stop Motion). Next thing I have a problem with is Daughter Of Skywalker. Truly not the Leia I think you should use. I mean, this is another one of the RST characters you’ve put in here (remember how many scouts your original deck had?)! A good replacement would be Leia RP like Shewski said, but I had something else in mind. Since you’re staying Classic, you can’t have Valorum or Palpatine hit-and-running in the Senate, right? What’s another good anti-Senate character? Leia (V)! She can get rid of Orn Free Ta or Lott Dodd for you, plus, in case Xizor’s holding down a site, she can cancel his text and eliminate him. She also works on Tarkin. It would be hilarious if you included a Bacta Tank, and instead of having a hit-and-run Valorum you had a hit-and-run Leia (V) in the Senate! Of course, since she doesn’t have politics, the only effective way to do that is to track high destiny. So, wrapping it up, I think Leia (V) is a good choice. Next thing I see is Obi-Wan Kenobi. While I’m a little iffy about him, if you just want a strong character, I guess he works. Next problem: LSJK. While he himself isn’t a problem, running R2 in R5 is. If you play Artoo in R5, you play Luke (V). Simple, yes? If you don’t play R2 in R5, you play LSJK. In a nutshell, either ax R2 in R5 or switch LSJK to Luke (V). Next problem: Lando Calrissian. Since I hope you’ll heed my words and cut sabacc, you need somebody else. Since you’re staying Classic (sigh), you either can have JPSD Lando (w/ ax), or ECC Lando (w/ blaster). Ordinarily, JPSD I think would be better, however, since ECC Lando adds a destiny on CC with an alien, he just may be your man. I think it would be a simple matter of preference. Arconas are great in QMC, too, for the aforementioned reasons. Talon Karrde, like Orrimaarko, doesn’t seem to fit in a deck like this. Or essentially any deck besides WYS. To wrap up the character section, whatever personas you use, you need some more mains. Even if you’re not trying to go beats, I can see people fighting you on Cloud City, and you running out of characters. That wouldn’t be cool. So, add a few more mains. Moving on to starships. I would procure a Wedge RSL and Red Squadron One ASAP. He’s just good, especially if you have some other Red Squads in there (like Luke (V)). Given that you’re staying Classic (again sigh), you can’t add Dolphe/Bravo 2 or Ric/Queen’s Royal, so let’s see how else you can strengthen your space. The SE Red Squadron ships would probably be a good choice, even though they don’t draw battle destiny by themselves, having a power destiny is pretty close, and if you get a couple grouped together you can be sizzlin’! So maybe add some SE Red Squad ships/pilots (another reason Wedge RSL would rock da house). If you’re playing a fair number of X-wings, probably add an X-wing Laser Cannon, and them may as well add Power Pivot. Another option is playing some of the DS2 ships and pilots, namely the Green Squads, I think this is another thing that goes to preference. Just to say a little more about Home One, I maintain my opinion that it’s too expensive, however, if you want to keep it, I guess it works okay. The thing I have a problem with, again, is the exorbitant cost of getting it and its pilots down (you must have Haash’n or Lateral Damage will destroy it), and I can see a Punishing One (V) (of course with some other ships backing it), that deploys for TWO force, annihilating at least two of your pilots. One more battle with it in there and the Home One is clam chowder. Moving on to interrupts. Again, ax the sabacc. I know I recommended 2 Clash Of Sabers, but now looking again I see you’ve only got three lightsabers in there (2 if you switch LSJK to Luke (V)), so get rid of one of them. You could use another Path Of Least Resistance, just because it’s so great! Two works, I suppose, but you can’t go wrong on a destiny 5 interrupt that lets you teleport characters. Since you do have some lightsabered characters, add A Jedi’s Resilience. And once more I recommend Escape Pod (V) x2 and Grimtaash. I don’t think you realize how awesome putting those three cards in is. I’ll tell you: It’s awesome! Several times I’ve taken away Deal’s Executor before they deploy it with these cards (used Grim pulled by Epod), and used Grimtaash is just plain awesome, how can you go wrong with two VERY easily recyclable/trackable 6s? Epod (V) is my favorite card to track, just because it’s so easy to track it. I think you can get rid of one each OOC and TBS combo. I know Coruscant’s hard to come by, but you really should get a Free Ride combo in there. If you really don’t think you can do it, regular Endor Celebration works, too, it’s just not really recyclable. But you can really use an Endor Celeb of SOME form to combat Deal. Sure, modify your space a little and it’s pretty good, but still Deal can probably take over Bespin. Which means Occupation. Which means an Endor Celeb is good. Just for VERY easy tracking purposes, and because you need to make up for your lack of 6s with only 1 AJR, throw in A Few Maneuvers x2. It’s sort of handy, but mainly it’s a NON-SEARCHING used destiny 6. Ownage. Since you’re not running many beats character, a little more evasion would probably be a good idea, so maybe a couple Dodges or Blast The Door, Kid!, or Narrow Escape, etc. As I said before, you could use a couple Off The Edge, but you know that. And, I agree with Shewski that Slight Weapons Malfunction x1 is probably a good idea. Moving on to effects. They look fine, however, since you’re not running many beat characters, even with throwing in a few, Hiding In The Garbage (V) could REALLY come in handy. As I said in my TRM strat, it’s a late-game opponent wrecker. Bacta Tank is up for consideration, but I think it’s probably too expensive. However, there are some cheap people you have, so it might work. If you add the Tank and Docking And Repair Facilities, imagine the frustration of your opponent when he takes out one of your ships, and both the ship and the pilot come back! Also, you could use Honor of The Jedi, which, put simply, is just a plain good card. While you’re not running a choke theme, Goo Nee Tay is a fun throw-in just because it can be frustrating to your opponent. Sure, it’s better in a choke deck, but it’s just a fun card to throw in if you feel like it. Moving on, your weapons and devices look fine. Moving on…well, that’s the end of the line. I hope this review helped, and now I’m worn out…again : )
