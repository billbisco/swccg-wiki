 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I like this deck because of all the ways to retrieve.
1) Winning the podrace. I will usually win because most other people do not play racing. Secon, if they do have Sebulbas Pod, I have a lot of high destiny interrupts.
2) If I win the podrace I pull I Dit It with Anakins Pod and place Boonta Eve Podrace out of play.
3) All Wings Report in allows me to retrieve for the amount of X- Wings I have on table. Unfotunatly, I have no way to grab this but I usually will get it in my hand.
4)Putting Reflection on Leia. This is dicey because I only have 1 Leia and she is relativily weak.
5) Finally I have 1 Figrin Dan. I will win the wager most of the time and I can just stick him in a T- 47.

The second reason I like this deck is it has preety good force drains. Average, with EBO out it drains about 9 a turn.

I prefer X-Wings because 2 can hold a site by themselves. If I have Incom Corporation down and EBO down than I have 11 power and a destiny chance.

Now for the playing:
Before the Game:
I like to pull I Dit It to get a 0 out of my deck.

Turn 1:
I start by pulling a site ( preferably the war room for activation) and Echo Base Garrison. Echo Base Garrison can grab Luke, Wedge and Zev.

Turn 2:
I pull another site and Echo Base Operations. I will have initiated the Podrace by now. If I do not have 3 Echo sites down than I try to refrain from playing characters.

Turn 4:
Pull the last site, deploy, and consolodate. From here on it is the fun of draining and retrieving.

Matchups:
Where I play, I see alot of BHBM and Hunt. These are the toughest matchups because all they do is invade echo base. That is why I have the speeders. With Garison don the speeders have excellent immunity and cannot be targeted by weapons.

Dark Deal is a simple matchup because they usually dont bother you. You can get EBO set up faster than Dark Deal and you have alot better retrieval from them.

Those are the main decks played in the area. I have to watch out for Come Here You Big Coward, Bttle Plan, and Secret Plans. My biggest pronlem is Battle Plan but the T- 47 partially solves that.

Thanks alot for looking.
