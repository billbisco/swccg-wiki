 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Ok, now before you go nuts on me for the crappy cards, remember that this is just for fun and more importantly, it's to THWART MY FRIENDS!!! hehe... Well, the idea is to get EV909 out and drain for as much as much as 25 a turn from the ONE location in the deck. I need to add another EV 909. The None Shall Pass and Hutt Smooches are just there to help regulate the battles and a LOT of my friends use spies. This is NOT a confrontational deck. I repeat NOT CONFRONTATIONAL. I have the Hunters and such in there just to make sure no one tests my authority at my one site. Most of my characters are Droids and cost next to nothing so the low force activation shouldn't hurt much. Well... posts are welcome!

   LordHoban     This thing is pointless. As previously mentioned, even in a fun game, your mega drain can easily be limited to 2 or stopped altogether, and you really don't have the force base to do much fighting, means you can't even fulfill any conditions that could allow you to get around these stopped cards or kick off opponent's characters. If you want to make it work, first off, don't start with that location. Second, best bet is probably either base it off the CC Darkside objectibe (This Deal Is Getting Worse All The Time), OR, start with Jabba's Palace (though You Can Either Profit By This will screw you over) and use Twi-lek to start with Expand the Empire, which will get all your locations out first turn (at least 2 2/1 locations, around 2-3 1/0 locations), and you might as well throw in Gailiad and Mosep, to put the hurt down. More None Shall Passes, some more fighting capability and at least 2-3 EVs. Some Destroyer Droids if you got 'em. Maybe a Presence of the Force to make the droid place a battleground. You might also use Surprise to move Expand over to the Auidience Chamber and pull 3 aliens out of your deck. That's all for now... but the point is, as is, it doesn't stand much of a chance at all and is too easily averted.
