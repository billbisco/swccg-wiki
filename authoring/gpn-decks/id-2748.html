well this is the deck that i took seccond in the vegas dpc with. I have played this deck in about 30 games and it has lost twice in the time. well on to the stragety...

First and formost is you have to get the jedi counsel up and running. It is imperitive that you get the counsel out first or seccond turn. If you do not you will have problems with activation for the rest of the game. one of the great strengths of this deck is that usually by the seccond turn i can generate 11 force plus whatever my opponet gives me. the other reason that the counsil is so important is because of the game text that the counsil members have great game text when it comes to battle espically plo koon. His ability to cancel and cause a redraw is invalabule rember he can do that to you or your opponet. and Depa can prevent your opponet from drawing up. but oce you have the counsel up and running you really do not have any problems with force generation forthe remainder of the game.

next there is the podrace. i don't think that i need to tell people how good i did it and draw there fire are togather. Plus the force retrieval and loss is not to bad.

The entire point of the deck is to battle plan and simple. you have to be agressive when you play this deck if you are not then you will loose horribly. assuming that you get the counsel chambe rup and running early you will pretty much be able to do anything that you need to do to win your battles and cross vader.

all i can tell you as far as what to do in certian situations is that this deck is built for battle. All you have to do i be able to shut down what ever it is your opponet is trying to do. you need to know what the game text of the cards do. tht has to be the most important part of playing this deck. you need to know what your card can do.

Explination of cards:

The starting luke - I start luke skywalker rebel scout because in my area people do play always thinking with your stomach. Also he is very good against a bring him deck.

loosing track- this card is amazing and i'm relly suprised people don't play it more first off you can damage their podracer which means that they will not stack racedstinys on their pod. Also the ability to put a low destiny card on top of there reserve deck is very good. Not only will your character have a better chance for survial bt you have a greater chance of stacking a card on i feel the conflict and crossing vader for the win.

Under attack - this card is amazing. plan and simple it is extramly fun to under attack blizzard 4 what more can i say.

Sorry about the mess- Well the combo is good i will grat you that but i really need the destiny of 4 and in my expierence with this deck most of my oppones onl leave 1 character at a sight. (because of goo nee tay)

Liberty - I am playing this card because i usually generate enough force to play both the liberty and the falcon in the same turn plus when the liberty dies it goes to the used pile. Iwas think about the spral but i rally have no retrieval except for the podraceing.

well that pretty much i hope you have good luck with it as i have.
