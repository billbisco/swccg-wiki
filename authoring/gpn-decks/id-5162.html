 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Ok, 1 card needs to go. First Turn: Against Mob Points, play DDTA. What can I say after that? Not much of a turn-by-turn can you get with TRM, but use Threepio to cycle your deck for the stuff you need. Basically, you’re just shutting down your opponent. Restrict his activation, track (ton of tracking available in this deck), and your characters keep coming back. Search for the H1 db when you need to see what's in your deck. Use VV(V) to set up some stuff for drain 3 at Kessel. A Tremor In The Force is good for a once-per game uber-choke. And hey, if he plays the YCHF shield, that’s a busted shield, right? I originally had Frozen Assets and Scramble in here, but I thought I was going overboard. The Oola/Civil Disorder combo is deadly. Basically, just have an answer to everything he tries to do to you, and you should be well set to do stuff whatever his deck is. Of course, the only problem is I have 63 cards : ). Please help.

   icebreath     Have an alternate for Colo Claw Fish in case they beat you to it. Get rid of Kessel. Think about dropping a Lando, as a lot of dark side decks are going to be playing him, too. Why Valorum? Toss Speak, Lost In The Wilderness, Were You Looking For Me, Visored Vision (V), Alter combo, Escape Pod (V), Sense & Recoil x1, ICBW (unless you add a copy of It's A Hit! for anti-It's Worse), Civil Disorder, Tremor, Hiding (V), HCatF. That gives you 4 spots... fill it in with Han w/ Blaster, Chewie (V), Life Debt, and something else for retrieval (Reflection, perhaps?). Happy now? >;) Looks good.
   Cam Solusar     Take out Oola, (V) Garbage, Valorum, Leia's Blaster, Kessel, Clash of Sabers, (V) Visored Vision, 1x Sense combo, and Grimtaash. Replace 1x Princess Leia with (V) Leia and replace Alter Combo with Ep1 Alter (to cancel political effects). I don't know why you are playing Insurrection Combo with only one DB, so use (V) Wokling instead and use it to for extra activation and to pull revolution. Looks like a good start.
   Shewski     Phew... that's a lot to cut out! I really don't like the R2 start to cycle away since it is so card intensive. Too bad seeking an audience (non V) is not startable.
