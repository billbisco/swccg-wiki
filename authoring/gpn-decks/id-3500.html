 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Strategy: Use Hidden Base and Insurrection to pull the systems and the docking bay. BoShek (V) should be the first fighter you put on table, he is considered a "matching pilot" for any of the Special Edition fighters, so he gets the immunity to attrition from their game text. Use him to get out the Fusion Generator Supply Tanks (V), deploy it on the docking by once you get some scouts out to protect it. Use Signal to get out your effects. Strikeforce protects your drains, Bacta Tank saves scouts, and Thrown Back is for desitiny stacking. Once you control the docking bay and at least one system, with Hidden Base flipped and Menace Fades on table you should be able to limit the damage your opponent can do. Multiple copies of Take The Initiative and Fallen Portal allow you to easily defend the docking bay. Insurrection adds to forfeit of most characters. Midge and I'll Take The Leader can add to your drain at the docking bay. Panic pulls the X-Wing Laser Cannon and provides a fighter 'react' in a pinch. The Gold figher pilots add to drains at Anoat and Kashyyyk. Basically just drain in space and limit the damage opponent does with Hidden Base and Menace Fades. Let me know what you think.
