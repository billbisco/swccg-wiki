 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Between Rieekan, Luke, Echo Base Garrison, and Manuevering Flaps (V), you should have no trouble getting Rogue Squadron set up on Hoth. Then just play T-47 Battle Formation, add on the direct damage from the objective, and retrieve a few force with Frostbite (V), and you should be well on your way to victory. Thanks to all that helped build the deck!

NOTE: I added Houjix to the sideboard so that if you want you could add it in to counter Sunsdown & To Cold For Speeders, which could really put a hurt on this deck.
