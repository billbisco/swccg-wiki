 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The basic strategy is to use Prepared Defenses as a starting interrupt and use it to deploy Battle Order, Inconsequential Losses and Combat Response. I usually use one of the systems as a staring location due to the large number of starships in the deck. Then it is just a case of using weapons as forfeit in battles and playing it as I go, the deck is very mixed so I have no overall strategy, any help with this would also be really useful. Thanks.

Thanks for the comments guys, okay I'll tell you some alterations that I'm planning to make to the deck and I'd be grateful for feedback on them. Firstly I'll remove the Victory-Class Star Destroyers, replace them with 6 Unique ISD, along with Grand Admiral Thrawn and Praji (V) and other similar matching pilots, etc. I'll rmeove most of the Courst stuff and use SYCFA as am objective, thus I'll add Primary Ignition, Death Star and Superlaser to the deck, along with a Death Star Gunner. With the removal of the ground from Court I'll add instead a few more Imperial ground attack, for example a couple more AT-STs and more AT-AT pilots etc. to suppport the ground aspect, I might also add a copuple of Stormtroopers, hey they're rubbish but what sort of all Imperial deck would it be without them!?! I'd appreiciate any comments on this new plan of attack for my deck, all of these changes will be possible as soon as I have completed by radical change in the cards I own (ie buying a lot more!)

   Shewski     You would probably lose by 20-30 cards in a given tournament. Most Tournament level decks can set themselves up full in a few turns, where your deck (as is) needs to draw your locations, etc. to get going. I would try to find an objective to build upon- Court of the Vile Gangster or Set Your Course would be my suggestions, like Hoth said. You really just need to work on focusing on either space or ground. Will you win through battles or drains?
