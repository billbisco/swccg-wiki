Strategy
The main focus of this deck is to drop biker scouts, bikes and sites early. The objective can grab to site and scouts (non-unique imps) and the scouts can grab bikes using their game text. Characters like Guri, Evazan & Baba and Tarkin provide for a beatdown group. Vader and Maul add to this group and to force drains with their sabers. P-59 and P-60 and more weapons and provide a nasty shock for your opponent when they hit, since they both cause instant force loss. TC-14 reduces the effectiveness of jedi, first by causing their movement cost to increase by 2 when at the same site and also TC-14 can be placed out of play to reduce a jedi’s power to zero and cancel its immunity to attrition. Zuckuss in MH, Dengar in PO and Bossk in HT provide space cover, require opponent to have ability greater than 6 to get any battle destinies, no immunity to attrition, +1 to your total battle destiny for each opponent’s starship and extra destinys. The victory SDs back-up the bounty hunters and add to total power on the ground. The biker scout gear gives the scouts a higher defense value and also adds 2 to the destiny of scout blasters. Search and destroy adds to the scouts forfeit and potentally cause your opponent to lose force each turn. First strike cause force loss for your opponent and retrieval for you. Presence of the force adds to your activation and drains. Organa’s Necklace adds to force drains. Yavin 4: jungle is in the deck so the necklace can be played. Use elis helrot to transport your character from and back to ralltiir to get it. Maul strikes adds a battle destiny when maul battles. Alter is for effect removal, collateral damage is cause havic before a battle, evader cancels revolutions, monnok cuts opponents hand down, ghhhk prevents battle damage, those rebels wont escape us cancels nabrun lieds, sense cancels interrupts, uncertain is the future recycles hands back into reserve deck. Sniper can pickoff a few characters before a battle. Limited resources keeps a lot of cards in your opponents hand or does a lot of damage. Its worse stops it could be worse, adds to damage and provides a great destiny. Dark Jedi Presence allows for imps to double their power when a dark jedi is present. Twi’lek advisor allows you to grab effects/ Nevar Yalnal provides a great destiny and eliminates spies. The scout blasters give the scouts some thing to shoot with, a good destiny too. The sabers add to drains.

   swcironecg     Your character mix is good, but you either need more copies of those characters or some other strong characters (Boba Fett, other bounty hunters, maybe walkers) in case of a beatdown, especially if your opponent plays Ounee Ta. Take out the Necklace, there are easier ways to add to drains, even if it means just deploying some Dark Jedi Lightsabers. Since you have biker scouts, High Speed Tactics would be a great way to add a destiny. The Scout Blasters won't hit much, but add in Imperial Academy Training and for each biker scout armed with one in battle your battle destiny will be +1, or just take them out to make room for other cards. You have good ships, but as with the character to few in my opinion. If your opponent is playing with a large space fleet they could grab control of Ralltiir early in the game, preventing you from flipping. Maybe add some star destroyers for more power. Dark Jedi Presence should be taken out, You only have two imperial other than the scouts (whose power 1 isn't worth doubling) and one of then is a jedi (vader) and won't be affected by the bonus if he is the jedi the card targets (though with Maul things could get nasty). Take out It's Worse, if your opponent doesn't use It Could Be Worse then that card is useless, at most you should have one. I hope I haven't rambled on too much, but with some changes this deck could be really good, like all Ralltiir Ops decks!
