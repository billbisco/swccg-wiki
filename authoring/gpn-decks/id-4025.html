 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck wants to pull off as many kessel runs as possible. I don't have many different personas of mains, so that's why I'm using Chewbacca, Protector. Most of the effects have had success in the past. Um, Hyper escape is there, since there aren't many starships. In case of an ambush. there's high force drains in space. Projection makes them drain less at a site. Ultimatum stops any super drains. r2 in red five just plain kicks butt with luke in it. that's about all I can think of. Please tell me everything that this deck needs. I hope to have it tournament worthy as soon as there's a tournament in Ohio. That probably won't be for a while though. Please help me.
I don't have a clue how to work this objective, but it must kick butt for it to be illegal in Classic Format. Please tell me I can do something here. Thanks

   Shewski     WYS can be deadly when used correctly. As for starting effects, I'd use Sqad Assign, Insurrection & Aim High, and either YISYW & Staging Areas or Wokling (V). Use Cor. Tatooine if possible. Your characters should be smugglers of all shapes and sizes. I'd use Chewbacca (V) since you can pull some beatdown cards like Life Debt with him, but it's mainly up to you. Captain Han is a good one to use. If you want to go killer, forget the Kessel run aspect (and Leebo). Palace Raiders in Patrol Craft rule the ground, Smugglers in ships rule the skies. Use Melas as well. Ground, the PRs can react for free in Patrol Craft and add destiny. Use Insurrection to pull the many Tatooine DBs (Mos espa, 94, <>) and use Fallen Portals to defend. You can also pull some 1/0 DBs for generation. You want to use Bacta tank (and anti SAC) to protect your guys. Use Tunnel Visions to pull your guys, and then abuse them from the lost pile. Same with On The Edge. In Space, use I'll Take the leader to buff up your immunity and Tatooine Celbration to retrieve. once you can hold it, Pull Kessel and drain for 3. Legendary Starfighter is great, since you are playing with r5 and the falcon. All Wings Report In & Darklighter Spin (pulled by V Luke), will get the bulk of your fleet out quickly. Good luck!
