Strategy Overview:
Choose three effects to start with . These are optional on which to use if need be! Use Strike P. toget out Han and Crix- Crix to get Chewie (and other scouts), to get wuta, to get sites, to flip the Objective!
On the first turn get Madine, Han, Chewie, and Wuta.(Madine and Han before activating or on opponents turn and yours !!!!!!)
Now that the first turns are over CONTROL ENDOR!!!!!!! Get out scouts with Crix! And get out The Bunker. If opponent is force draining you lose ability 1 or 2 scouts you can get them back if you flip, DO NOT lose too many though!
I know comes in handy when Han ”accidentaly” is left alone at a site! Hi Leia, and what do you have with you? Mr. Lightsaber! Get over here ”Chop, Chop!”
These starships are here for protection of Tala twins. Nobody needs to lose those Battle bonuses from Close Air Support!!
Independence may be changed for Liberty (it goes to the used pile if lost in a battle).
Where I am at, Miyoom Onith is big! Don’t no why. Well use shocking Info. it get’s the onith out! Or if opponent isn’t using MO use it as a recyclble 5!!
Keep R2-D2 at any scomp link. Why? R2 says: if at a SCOMP LINK when opponent draws ANY destiny of: 1-3 Activate 1 force----------4-6 may draw the top card of reserve deck into hand. Can’t argue with that! He IS a SPY so he’ll deploy anywhere, leave him alone at an interior site to gaurd against TRAMPLE. Remember because he is a spy your characters may deploy where he’s at! Disregard the JK lightsaber!!!!!!
