 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
start HFTMF for Insurrection combo, seekin an audience, and staging areas. occupy AC and one of the other two(if not both) to flip. drain at the sites you control, added to by Tessek. cancel to force drains a turn with the objective and Bargaining Table.use Revolution and Obis Apparition to choke opponents activation, while using Careful Planning and staging areas, etc, to raise yours. win by causing tons of direct damage and a good unmodifiable drain at the tat sites(again, added to by Tessek, Lightsaber Pro).
