 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Turn 1: u should be able to get the DS2 docking bay out, maybe with jerjerrod there. With 4 advisors, the docking bay, and IAO that’s 6 cards that can get it. If u cant get it, draw and deploy it Turn 2: Deploy jerjerrod, grab and deploy the first sector, draw Turn 3: grab and deploy the 2nd sector, start to occupy ds2, draw Turn 4: grab and deploy the last sector, deploy TTO, deploy more space, by this time u should have gotten either nemoidian advisor or one of the 2 battleground systems, deploy one, deploy ground, move DS2 to that system. Now everything is set up. If ur lucky with ur opening hand or what u draw, it can be possible to set up everything a turn early. Once u reach this point, ur opponent should be losing at least 5 to TTO, u are draining in space, and draining on the ground. If he doesn’t come after u soon, he’ll be gone awfully soon. In space u have battle destiny’s galore (also as much as +4 total from the baron), extra power from the dreadnaughts and all power to weapons (each tie is total power +3), and some nice weapons. On the ground Dr. Evazan is murder with the weapons. I should be able to survive his attempts at clearing me out.
Card Selections: the superlaser can kill off annoying capital starships (i.e. defiance), relentless pursuit either makes a hit starship immediately lost or cancels one of my opponents starships power-very useful, sullust is a good drain, reactor terminal puts cards from my hand back in life force so I don’t have to worry about drawing up cards I need in my life force, inconsequential losses can keep me from forfeiting an important character by forfeiting weapons instead, lots of twi’lek advisors get my needed effects out quickly

responses: Darth Hoth: thank u for the comment on Desparate Counter. id never even heard or the card, but ill try to get it. i might consider trading out some ground for SDs, but i really need blaster rack to guarantee getting the weapons out.


 The Last 5 Comments
   swcironecg     The best way to play DSII: pull Mobilization Points/ You Cannot Hide Forever with Operational As Planned. Use it to pull a system, then make it lost to pull Desparate Counter. Deploy that, then pull Imperial Arrest Order and Combat Response. Pull the DSII: Docking Bay, put Jerjerrod there and he's safe. Pull a Spaceport Docking Bay and drop it at the system you pulled with Mob Points. Now you have a place for the DSII to go and do 3-4 damage a turn if you can occupy the site and get We Shall Double Our Efforts on table. Add Fighters Coming In to get force drain +1 at both docking bays. Use Scythe Squadron to defend the DSII. Darth Hoth and Shewski make good points about ground. Good luck.
   Shewski     With the desperate counter comment in mind, your deck could rate a 3. Also, your ground should be to satisfy Battle Plan or quick beatdown early. The bulk of your damage should come from space. Consider using No Escape to make the TTO damage stick. I think your only site should be a DB, perhaps the Endor one. For better ground effiecncy use EPP Mauls. Then DR E can operate with some lightsaber strikes, and forget his gun and Blaster Rack. More space would help.
   Darth Hoth     I would suggest starting Desperate Counter. It lets you pull Combat Response and IAO, plus it subtracts from opponents maneuver at the sectors. You seem a little weak on air, I would suggest using 2-3 SDs. Your ground force is more than enough for this deck, lose a few of them. My suggestion would be to loss 1 of the Vaders and the Lord Maul. If you go the Desperate Counter route, drop some of the Twi'lek Advisors. The Blaster Rack (V) can only used for 2 weapons, it's not worth the slot. The way this is built, I would say it's not really the sort of thing I would use for DS2, maybe if you started Endor Operations.
