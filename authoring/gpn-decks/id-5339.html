this is my first deck post so give me lots of suggestions.

an entire legion of my best troops means u are drawing 8's when u draw battle droid blaser rifle

the phantom menace is to make maul a killing machine

begin landing your troops to pull docking bay and forfiet bonus

get out droid starfighters or droid controll ship to prevent early space beat down

if they dont deploy to naboo you get establish controll out to drain.

naboo occupation is for force denial

if they do deploy to naboo which usually occurs in the first 3 turns with a typical obi w/saber or some other stupid dude then hit em with oh switch off, stunning leader, and self destruct mechanism the look on the other guys face makes your day :P

use the unique dudes game text
SSA-306=smugglers and spies dont deploy cheap and dont add destiny
SSA-1015=if jedi with u power bonus and they dont deploy cheap
3B3-888= enemy is defense value -3 and easy hit
3B3-1204=anti jedi immunity to attrition
OWO-1 with backup= a good beat-em-up-stick
maul= ur own personal beat em up stick

pull daultay dofine and use him to pull activate the droids for retrieval or activation

blaster rack for weapons recycle along with ur droids on droid racks

the admirals orders is like a permanant tallon roll

matchups:
throne room mains woah ur toast but you may pull it off if you stick to ur guns and are very careful

speeders easy win they wont deploy to you once beat em down with any character they come after u. lay down some establish control for some major drains:P

hidden base watch out for ur dude in the sky if they know what theyre doin youll get a run for ur money a good strategy would be to attack if they come down with some pissy dude within the range of ur ship

profit no idea drain like mad im guessing.
