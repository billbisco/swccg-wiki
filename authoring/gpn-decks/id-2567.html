 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This is a deck that I am playing for a couple of years. It won me a lot of games and some great battles. It has never converted Vader, because by that time ussually the opponent was death or out of vaders.

This deck can deliver a punch. My last tournament (continental) I beatdown a LSC squad (Lord Maul with Saber, Premiere Vader with Saber, Boba BH, Grand Moff Tarkin, Admiral Ozzel and Mara Jade) with four characters. His total power was 42 and mine was 83!

You need to win battles, so use Colo Claw Fish and Jedi Presence.

Naboo: Docking Bay is to counter LSC decks.
