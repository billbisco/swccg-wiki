 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The idea is basically the same, capture and retrieve force only this time around it might be easier. I've added Prepared Defenses to start and Ket Maliss (V). This allows you to deploy some characters and sites quick. You can also use Jabba's Palace to pull out sites quickly as well. I have also added Greedo (V). He has a much better forfeit value and allows you to pick up Oo-ta Goo-ta, Solo? quickly. Beedo is in there to enhance Greedo as well in which both can be pulled out using Ket Maliss (V). I also changed Djas Puhr to (V). He is now immune to attrition <5 with a weapon. I also added Boba's Slave I and his Rifle. You might want to replace Lyn Me with a Rodian (V) to help you pull out your bounty hunters even quicker. Use Jabba's Through With You to put weapons on your guys and Oo-ta Goo-ta, Solo to hit them where it hurts. With the docking bays being pulled out with Ket Maliss (V) moving around is not a hassle.

   catman421     You have a very good start here. Try using COTVG, its an excellent platform. If you use COTVG, ditch the imperial starships and replace them with bounty hunter ships. They can be pulled with the objective. Also ditch the non-tatooine locations. They are of no benifit to you. Add rancor pit, sarlacc pit, and each creature. Because of COTVG, feeding characters to those two creatures causes big force loss to opponent. Also drop the imperial blasters and blaster rifle. They're useless. You could also, as a side strategy, run podraces. It provides great retrieval and force loss. You also should put in, FIMA, tat. occupation and scum and villany. Occupation and scum damages your opponent, retrives for you and your characters deploy cheaper. As youve seen, shields help alot.

   Bantha Bob     I think the idea for this deck was fairly well thought out, and should do OK in play. I have always liked bounty decks because it gives DS a chance to do some retrieving. My suggestions would be, if you are not going to use bounty hunters with permanent weapons switch your Jabbas Through With You cards out for virtual Oo-ta Goo-ta Solo?. Also you may want to start with Prepared Defenses instead of Twi'lek so you can put down All Wrapped Up and Inconsequential losses at the start. Court of the Vile Gangster, virtual Ket Malis, and Fear Is My Ally may also be some cards you want to work into this deck.
