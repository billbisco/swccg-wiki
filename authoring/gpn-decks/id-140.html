 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Basically, this deck relies on two things, first after you draw your hand, pull the executor, the 2 sites, and hopefully out of your hand or your force pile(which you usually draw up) you'll get either TMNALTC, or an effect puller. get dark deal out and done within the first 2 or 3 turns at latest. if you can't do it, at least get CC Occupation out. use Imp Commands to pull generals and admirals(chiraneu lets the executor drain for 2)
maybe even alert my star destroyer to make it completely immune if you cant get guri up there fast.
other than that, its up to you, thats my basic strategy, the admiral's orders are there for more direct damage and battle destiny cancelling.
