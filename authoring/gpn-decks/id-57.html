 The Description
This is a deck that I originally saw posted on another fan site, the CCG portion of theForce.net. I've modified it some, but not nearly enough. It needs a mechanism for getting locations out (perhaps Wuta), and better attention to dealing with spies. It's a fun concept deck, which can be very powerful if a good starting game is on your side.

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This is effectively a Force draining deck, with plenty of battling power, should the opponent be foolish enough to attack you on Endor. The plan is to have as many Force icons at each Endor site as possible, with Sister Skywalker and the gentle Ithorians up to that task. Tessek at Jabba's Palace: Audience Chamber adds yet another Force drain where you have non-unique aliens, and Kazak adds an extra drain to each site with another Ewok. It's not hard to quickly amass four or five in Force drains per site. Endor Celebration makes battling an especially painful option for your opponent, and the noble Lumat, as his race's rep, provides both some Force drain and battle destiny protection.

The Republicans are there to hold down the Tatooine fort--Bo Shuda won't be cancelled as long as there're no Rebels in the palace, and they don't qualify. Transmission Terminated fights off a Hunt Down deck's primary annoyance and The Signal is there to get out Bargaining table to fend off the opponent's inevitable drains. I've also included a Dresselian Commando to sacrifice just in case the opponent fields a Carbon Chamber Testing deck.

Watch out for spies and Tatooine Maul; they can severely limit your Force draining capabilities. It might be wise to include Anakin's Podracer too, just in case your opponent plops down Sebulba and Watto's Box. This deck's downfall is that it can be slow starting, especially if you don't get enough Endor locations out quickly. And it's also a little low on destiny.
