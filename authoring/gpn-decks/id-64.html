 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Why Merc Armor? Its Great Deplor for free on Leia then courage her jedi presence and lightsaber prof on her she can make high power the deck is to fight now run away unless absolute need be.

Ok i start off with the Objective The Guest Quarters and my three effects are keeping the empire out forever squadren assignments and the third one varies.

Card Choices.

Insurrection-Elis Helrot WONT TOUCH me NO MORE!
Jek Porkins and Red 6-Draw Destiny if not able to otherwise.
Jedi-I play QMC to conquer not to hide with my tail safe.

Stratedgy. Establish my Drains with the lightsabers. Set up so i can run luke over and/or path obi wan then courage and jedi presence. Use the journal to not be hit and forfeit to the tank. Sense their Shut him up or down. Flip fast but safely and try to establish the celebration. Use edge cards for back up retrieval while threepio tracks.


Against Huntdown Podracing- Try to win the race and out duel Vader with Luke and Retrieve as much as you can with your edge cards.

Against Dark Deal- Let them drop sites to get the deal out and concentrate on the space. Hit the space early while its week then drop your sites to cancel the deal... An easier matchup than most.

You May Start Your Landing- Establish Menace Fades and Honor fast. Use suicide Qui Gonn to hit then go to the tank. Do Not let your self get h by the cold storm.

Court Of The Vile Gangster-Auto-Win That Deck Just Sucks Nowadays.
