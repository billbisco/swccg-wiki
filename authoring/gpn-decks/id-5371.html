 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Alright...

-Starting-

First Strike - retrives 1 when you initiate (for a total of 3 with S & V out). Plus makes opponent pay 1 force for playing an Interrupt during a battle you initiate.

All Wrapped Up - Helps to flip the obj. fairly quickly.

Power of the Hutt - lets you pull 3 cards from the deck (Bib, Jabba's Barge, Hutt Influence). Could be interrupted as pulling 4 cards because of Barge's text (..you may immediately deploy Passenger deck from Reserve Deck)

-Locations-

Tatooine: JP - I included two so that I could get a copy quicker. Good generation + the ability to pull the other JP sites.

Is Tatooine: Jabba's Palace considered a Jabba's Palace site?

JP: Rancor Pit - duh..for Rancor.

JP: Lower Passages - move between here and ANY JP site (HUGE), plus good activation.

JP: Entrance Cavern - Bib deploys for free. Nice place to set Mosep to maximizes his text.

JSB: Passenger Deck - Good force activation + non-battleground.

-Characters-

Mosep - most broken card ever..LOL. Stick him with some backup at Entrance Cavern with Audience Chamber on one side and Tat: JP on the other. Stick Galid at the AC and you have a drain of 3 (4 if Tat: JP is considered a JP site) of the top of the opponents Reserve Deck.

MJ, TEH - thinking of making this another Arica. Wadda ya think?

-Devices-

Mandalorian Armor - use on a character, in order to let you play Hidden Weapons.

-Interupts-

S/UITF and A/CD - worried about playing so many of these with so low ability character. Curious as to thoughts on this one...

-Effects-

Hutt Influence - good card IF the following is true...

Are all Jabba's Palace sites considered to be Tatooine battlegrounds?

If so then this card is really nice. Otherwise, it's just so-so.

-General Strategy-

Plays like ya think. Get a good hold on AC pref with Bib, Jabba, and Jodo. Get Scum out, capture and flip. Use the obj. to get the Indy starships out if you need them. Use NSP and IB to create massive beatdown opportunities.


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     A decent build, but Court is ultimately a limited decktype. Here's what I would do to improve it: 1) Use Jabba's Influence (V)- huge, gets out tons of your characters to speed your flip. I might use it over POTH. 1) use LLL (V) to get out T JP (which is a JP site) and use that to get out your other JP battlegrounds (which are Tatooine battlegrounds). Characters are pretty good... I'd only use Mighty Jabba. Boelo is Huge w/ him, where is he? Use R3 Bib since he's better at holding the AC. Run some more droids, like ECC 4 Lom, Guri, Xizor, P droids.... you can also use Stinger & Virago in space. I don't think the armor or Aurras gun is worth it. I would get rid of the SAC. Add in Jabba's Through w/ You. If you are concerned about activation, use Sec Precautions (V) on the AC, pullable by Mob Pts combo. Anyways, a decent build pre v6, but you need to take advantage of Influence (V) to make a more competitive deck.
