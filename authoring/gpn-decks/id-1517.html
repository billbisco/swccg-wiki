STRATEGY:

Well this is a senate deck, so apparently you want to take control of the senate. ALWAYS go for the first turn flip with Lott Dod and someone else(it really doesn't matter with who). Get out your political effects, and go from there. If they play space, get out the junkyard and set Watto and Maul with his saber there, and enhance the drain every turn to make it a drain of 4. Do that and cancel their drain of 2 at Naboo every turn. Combine this with the drain of 1 in the senate and Yeb Yeb(try and get Yeb Yeb out early in this match-up to try and make them lose some systems), you shouldn't lose. If you are playing against senate, this deck DOES NOT lose the senate. Me and my friend(Corey Faherty) have tested this deck against all possibilities of light side senate, and somehow this deck always takes the senate. We even tested it against a hardcore senate deck, with 23 characters with politics(15 are senators, 3 of each one), 3 of every senate interrupts, and all 4 political effects, and this dark side senate always took control of the senate. Against Hyperdrive, top their Junkyard(TECH!), put Watto and Maul with his saber there, and just sit there and drain. If they do come and battle, they don't even get a battle destiny, so it's kinda hard to win(for them). You think Qui-Gon's Lightsaber may cause problems?? WRONG. Set up a destiny of 4 or more(it's not that hard with senate) and drop Aurra with Quietly Observing and STEAL the saber. Now not only do they get no destiny at their converted Junkyard, but it's your 2 sabers to their 0! Complete lockdown. If they try and run away and drain somewhere else, either beat them up with the remaining characters or just throw Kedar down to block the drain.

Some card choice explanations:

Why 2 Junkyards?
Well mainly because Hyperdrive is popular in my area, so I need to get it out fast. Plus, if they try and top the site after you convert it, you can just convert it again.

Why bother with Aurra and Quietly Observing?
If you are asking this question, then apparently you don't know the HUGE advantage you get if you steal their ONLY saber so it's your 2 sabers to their 0! It's just a game breaker.

Why all 4 Political Effects?
They are KEY to a good senate deck. Adding 3/Subtracting 3 from destinies is HUGE. It can help cause even more battle damage, and prevent you from losing anything to attrition. And of course, canceling drains and adding to drains is good.

How do I deal with the I Did It! problem?
Well, we all know how you have to lose 2 force to keep force BEFORE you put back your senators. How do I get around this? Wipe Them Out Immediately lets me activate a force during their turn, then use The Point Is Conceded to activate a 2nd force. Now you have the 2 force needed to put your senators back whenever you want, even with I Did It! on table.

This senate deck is the best available in Episode 1 in my opinion. It can shut down ALL possbile light side decks right now, by the many different methods listed above. I have yet to see anything better(even on Decktech.net).


By Brandon Schele
