 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck design is simple. Deploy characters and starships as soon as possible to flip, using the objective to pull both sites, then deploy more characters and starships to solidify your forces. About two characters at a site and three starships at the system should be enough, but if you face a space-heavy deck then deploy more starships. Don’t deploy the Falcon unless necessary, the three force a turn to keep it on table can be costly. Between
Organized Attack, The Signal, Keeping The Empire Out Forever, and Nar Shaddaa Wind
Chimes you should be able to get any card you need. With Traffic Control and your cards
searching abilities (don’t draw cards, it’s a waste of force and has the potential to stack doubles up in your hand) you should be able to keep doubles out of your hand, preventing a Monnok beating. You can’t play Nabrun Leids during a battle, but you can play it before to move your characters in/out of the battle zone if your opponent just deployed a large attack force; you can also use it to send your characters on Cloud City to an opponent’s site for some mayhem. A strong system defense is necessary to keep Cloud City Celebration on the table, and its force retrieval is crucial; at max this deck only does four damage to your opponent each turn, your goal is get enough retrieval and
cancellation with Cloud City Celebration, Bargaining Table, Nar Shaddaa Wind Chimes,
Projection Of A Skywalker and It Could Be Worse so that you can out last your opponent.
Please let me know what you think of this deck, thanks.
