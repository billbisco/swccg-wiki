   star wars ccg player #2     There are not enough sights in this deck. People will be able to force drain you to death. You can't force drain enough in return. And my B-wings would kill you Saber Squadron. Good Luck; I would like to play you some time
   Cam Solusar     I think you have way too many characters. Take out the Saber Squadron pilots and their ships and put in some ships like IG-88 in IG-2000 or Zuckuss in Mist Hunter. These ships are more powerful, have hyperdrives, and take up less space. Try to take out some of the characters who don't work as well with your objective, such as The Emperor and Xizor, and, as the other review says, put in some interrupts to help your characters. Another good addition might be additional copies of Ominous Rumors and Establish Secret Base. You never know, but there is always the chance that they may get canceled in some way, and that might destroy your strategy. (If you can get a copy of Colonel Dyer, he can prevent Ominous Rumors from being canceled while he is at the Bunker) Watch out for decks that have lots of characters to spread around. I once blew apart a deck similar to this with a simple trooper deck containing mostly commons and uncommons. Your deck looks much better than the one I played, though. Good Luck! -Solusar
