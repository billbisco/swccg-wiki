 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I usually begin with Coruscant: Docking Bay, Twi'lek Advisor and Strategic Reserves but that can be changed. You can substitute Twi'lek Advisor for Prepared Defenses and deploy the other 3 effects. It's your choice. I use Strategic Reserves because I usually get some of the Royal Guards or Stormtrooper Cadets in hand. Pulling out sites shouldn't be a big problem. There are plenty of them in this deck and I usually end up pulling out 2 or 3 by the third turn. Sometimes more. There is good space protection with the Battleship and Bossk in Hounds Tooth while your troops can be guarded with the AAT. The characters have plenty of power and ability so pulling battle destiny won't be a problem either. There's plenty of protection with Self-Destruct Mechanism, Defensive Fire, Trooper Assualt and Ng'ok for those who like to deploy many at once.
