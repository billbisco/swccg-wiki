Basically flip the objective then blow up the Death Star. Use Heading for the Medical Figate to get out Squadron Assignments, Do, or Do Not & Wise Advice (Anti-Jedi Power Effect), and Sai'torr Kal Fas. Everything else is rather obvious as to its use (in my opinion). I thinking of useing a different Leia so suggestions on that are welcome (I have every version of her except Jabba's Palace ;-).

   superdavis     Not a bad swing at MBO. There are a few questionable card choices that I can see though. Rebel scouts are a little underrated, so it's good to see a few of the better ones in your deck (or any deck for that matter). The problem is that, if you're going to have a limited (in size anyways) ground strike force, you should consider adding characters with a little heavier staying power. (Tatooine Qui Gon comes to mind.) Bacta Tank helps; 2-1B would make it better. But keeping in mind your chocie of a ground force, if you want to keep it, I would suggest adding a Lt. Page to make your guys come down cheaper, and take a few of your weapons out. Use the Naboo blaster rifle (from the Coursucant set) since it's cheaper to play, has a much higher destiny than blaster rifles or blasters (get the standard blasters out of here) and you add 1 if a leader is firing. (You have quite a few leaders in the deck already.) To make up for the quantity of weapons, use more uniques (Chewie's Bowcaster instead of the generic version, for example) and play with cards like Saitorr Kalfas (V), Panic (V), Rebel Scanners, Crack Shot (anything hit may not satisfy attrition), Rebel Artillery (adds to Force loss if you hit or control with a weapon and MUCH better 7 destiny than Bionic Hand) and Slight Weapons Malfunction (adds a destiny if you fire a nonlightsaber weapon, ANY nonlightsaber weapon) to make impact of your scouts and weapons a little heavier. Why Dash and no Outrider? If you want to hold down those heavy Force drains, you'll want him in his ship, not to mention something a little more intimidating than a generic Nebbie freighter; use Home One and make room for an Admiral Ackbar. Also consider (V) BoShek since he can deploy with any starship using Squad Assignments game text. (He's matching pilot for ANY starship.) Judging by the deck, budget is a consideration, but BoShek, Panic, Slight Weapons Malfunction are all nonrares. Another good nonrare would be a targeting computer from Premiere, which lets you fire your X Wing Cannon more than once per turn as well. If you have it, use Thrown Back instead of Traffic Control. It acts like both a permanent Grimtassh AND Traffic Control (though in a somewhat limited capacity). Heading For the Medical frigate only works on ONE effect in this deck. . . the rest either don't drop for free or aren't permanently immune to alter (if memory serves), so I'm wondering what the tech is there. Not a bad premise overall. I might have to make my own version and post it so you can take your turn over analying MY deck. ;)
