 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The goal is to beatdown your opponent and feed them to the Rancor. There are twenty
characters in this deck so you shouldn’t have a difficult time getting them out to control locations. Use the Audience Chamber to get Jabba out so you get extra forfeit and force generation. Use objective + Mob Points to get lots of activation. The force generation in this deck is huge and it helps with whole beatdown plan.

Space: pull the Executor with Mob Points first thing in the game. Then when you have
enough force, drop it with a bounty hunter ship (ZiMH is good) to your opponents
weakest system for a good beatdown.

Capturing: These are all the cards that let me capture characters: All Wrapped Up, IG-88
With Riot Gun, and Hutt Smooch. It should be enough since with All Wrapped Up on
table all I need to do is beatdown my opponent so they lose characters and have a bounty hunter present. Don’t forget to drop off captives to the dungeon before you feed them to retrieve two force and any bounties.

Any comments would be helpful. I am especially looking for opinions on what interrupts and effects to include. If your going to make comments about the character selection, keep in mind that these are the strongest characters I have and if you’re going to suggest anything stronger I probably don’t have it. Thanks for reading.

   Shewski     I like the start. Do you own a Sarlacc? It is good to trap/suprise players on Tatooine. Mob Points is a good idea, but I believe that it is not a good idea for generation. Against a smart player, they will suspend it immediatly after you pull anything with the Dont Do That Again shield. My only knock on a COTVG is that is needs to force players to deploy. The 1 force loss is not that hard to shrug off, unless you can add to the hurt. So assume that they will avoid you- can you still win? Maybe not if they are playing Battle Plan- you are draining little to pay the tax. I'd suggest using V Beseiged (as it lets you capture ships/characters in space) instead of secret plans, since the def shield does the trick usually. Why Kir Kanos? Very confused by his presence. I'd suggest ditching him and adding Snoova or Djas Puhr to make capturing a little easier. I'm not a big SAC fan, so maybe add some we have a prisoners, and definitely more elis Helrots- he is still the fastest way to complete a bounty. The best thing to do would be to run a JP drain deck, focusing on Mosep/Gailid, and then run a Tatooine Occupation to let the hurt pour in. Your opponent is sure to come to the desert planet then, and if not, hopefully you can win before they do. If that is the case consider using Hutt Influece so they can't modify your force drains. Good luck!
