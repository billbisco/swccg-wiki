 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
this deck looks like it will do really well, but i need some help tweaking it. here's the strategy:

1. get the war room out on the first turn and pull either kuat or rendili depending on if you get kuat drive yards or rendili stardrive, respectively.

2. imperial command can pull admiral piett who can pull either commander merrejk(who can pull batteground systems) or we're in attack position now.

3. holonet transmission can pull any imperial depending on the situation: if you need the death star, then you can pull the emperor, or if you need power, pull mara jade.

4. it is key to get kuat drive yards out using either twilek advisor, motti(V), or just pulling it. this gets your star destroyers to kuat.

5. rendili stardrive is obviously for the weapons so they can deploy and fire free and +1 to each weapon destiny.

6. once you starting activating a lot of force, focus on getting a lot of star destroyers and fear will keep them in line(V) out. finally, get the death star to parsec 4 for the most effectiveness of fwktil(V).

7. force drain from here on out with an occasional deployment of vader and his minions.

[Moderator's Note]
ICSH works only for starship weapons fired from a starfighter, so the destiny adding function would be null and void. The deployment from reserve functions still works.

   Rickynumber24     Looks pretty good. Pretty much what Shewski said, but I would add that the Oppressive Enforcement non-combo is better in this situation IMO because it boosts the destiny of all the SDs, which are ones and twos to start with. With the guns you have there, I Can't Shake Him (does the same thing as Heavy Fire Zone except that it is unique and you can use it to add a destiny if you fired a starship weapon) could be devastating. To add to the weapon theme, you could use Crossfire and Inconsequential Losses (mentioned also by Shewski) to add power and save your SDs to fight another day in the face of attrition. Your ground force is more than adequate in my opinion, and if this is more of a space deck (which I think it is) I would use Darth Vader(V) for his general usefulnes in space. Just my opinion, though.
   Shewski     I think you should start Crossfire instead of Something Special Planned. Once you get your combos, you can ditch Mob pts to get Inconsequential Losses. You are going to have some ridiculous power on your SDers. With Motti and a weapon, they will be +7 power! Wow! The only thing that stinks is Motti not being able to be pulled with Command. Ground looks good, actually a little heavy. I'd remove some for more space domination. Adm Chiraneau works great draining in space. You can use Heavy Fire Zone to get some Turbolasers out and into play. Two reactor terminals are not needed- I like to try and play with out them, but 1 might help out. You can get some other 2/1 systems that are closer to parsec 0 so your fear damage works better. Use Rendili, Kuat, Nal Hutta, and Carida. Those 3 should let the hurt pile on. You may want to use A Bright Center to the Universe if LU (V) is popular in your area.
