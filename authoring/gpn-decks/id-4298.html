 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This is my Profit deck that I am trying to get ready for a tournament. You all know the basic strategy so I will go over the majority of my card choices.

Tatooine: City Outskirts: Let's you pull a Jedi from Reserve Deck plus it's 2 LS icons and 1 DS. Granted Qui-Gonn, Obi, and Luke With Stick are the only Jedi's in this deck, I still think that it is useful if just for the activation alone.

Tatooine: DB 94: To get the hell off of Tatooine if I have too. I am considering taking it out in favor of a more useful card and/or location.

Jerus Jannick: P4 A2 and if forfeited from Leia's site satisfies ALL battle damage and attrition...'nuff said

Lando Calrissian Scoundrel: P6 A3 Adds a battle destiny + if opponent intiates you retrive and add destiny number to power. Tyte.

x2 Master Luke: Looking to put x3 Masters in but need to get a hold of another. The -2 to Tatooine is nice..big thing of course is the ability to return an alien to opponents hand.

x2 Leia, Rebel Princess: Self-explanatory

Leia With Blaster Rifle: Needed to fill a character slot...seriously considering taking her out in favor or another location or possibly another character

x2 Lando With Vibro-Ax: Probably cutting this to only one...all the +'s of LC, S minus the retrive thing. Plus he gets to exclude. Pretty nice all around

Artoo and Threepio With His Parts Showing: Just in for A Gift (Threepio is nice for tracking and putting unusable cards back)

Deneb Both: Nice card. When deployed lets you take top two cards of Reserve Deck into hand, plus if you Force Drain at same or adjacent site it must come from opp. Force Pile.

Geezum: To get sites out plain and simple

Skiff: In to allow quick reacts across table.

Out of Commission/Transmission Terminated: Places one card in opp. Lost Pile out of play, recirculating 5, great for placing on Colo Claw Fish

Tunnel Vision: Destiny is better than combo counterpart. Good for finding cards if you need them quickly.

Plz give useful and honest feedback. I need to get this deck ready for a tournament that takes place in a little over 2 weeks.

Thanks in advance. Lates Y'all

   Shewski     Are you using Han (V)? I really like using Rycar (V) to pull LS Profs, as well as Saitorr and Insurrection combo. For locations, I'd use the 3 Tatooine DBs and use Fallen Portal to defend them. You can only pull Obi or Qui, EPP Luke is not a Jedi. Your character selection is interesting. I'd run 1 Leia RP and 1 Leia (V). I'd run all Master Lukes, and use a TAT Obi and Ben Kenobi. I'd run 1 angry lando and 1 ax lando. Skiffs are ok, but not needed. Use more unique weapons when using Saitorr, like Obis and Luke's Saber (so you can deploy 4 sabers). Forget Suprise Assasult- run some Run Luke Runs. I'd also add some effect pullers. Your OOC/TT combo isn't in your cardlist- did you forget it? Beggar is always fun to throw in. See Wedge231's deck for some ideas.
