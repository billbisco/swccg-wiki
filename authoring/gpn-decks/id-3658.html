 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I have tried my hand at several decks and somehow have not come across one that works. Due to my lack of gameplay for a few years I didn't realize some of the rules that had been changed and implented so one deck in particular was a definate screw-up, which I plan to change. I hope this deck is up to standards. It looks good and I just spent some time with it. It has not been play tested yet, as I have just created it from an amalgam of cards. I hadn't usually used D-Shields but have been told that my decks are very susceptible to them. I have added the D-Shields to this deck. Some that I have incorporated are: A Tragedy Has Occurred, Aim High, and Another Pathetic Life Form (which works well against Tatooine based decks with alot of aliens or MKOS/FAI decks). The Camp also works well with those types of decks. It's all basically centered around your troopers with plenty of surprises like Aim High (reg effect) and Rebel Ambush. The big mains with their toys are in their for added protection. Tell me what you think, please.
