 The Description
This (classic) multi-strategic deck includes bounty-hunting, Jabba's Palace (without Jabba!), force-draining droids, blizzard walkers on tatooine, and more Tusken Raiders than you can shake a gaffi stick at!

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck may seem confusing at first, but it seems to work quite well once you get used to it. Jabba's Palace should be the starting location with Prepared Defenses as a starting interrupt. All Wrapped Up and Combat Response can be played immediately via Prepared Defenses' text. Combat Response should help you to get the bounty hunters and their ships into the game, while All Wrapped Up will help out with the bounties themselves.The Audience Chamber can be used to find Fett or Zuckuss, then use Combat Response to get them going. EV-9D9 can sit at the Droid Workshop with the other droids in the deck, force draining with a mighty bonus. The Tusken Raiders should easily be able to hold their ground on Tatooine; they'll get plenty of help from the AT-ATs. The whole Ion Cannon with We Have A Prisoner idea usually won't work every time, but it doesn't have to. We Have A Prisoner, Dengar's Modified Riot Gun, Zuckuss' Snare Rifle, Bounty, All Wrapped Up, and Aiiii! Aaa! Agggggggggg! all add a fancy bounty hunting twist to the deck. Make sure to use Jabba's Palace's game text every turn as you will need the force and the locations themselves to win. Happy hunting/tusken raiding/blizzard walking/droid workshopping!!
