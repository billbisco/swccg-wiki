 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Kills Harvest easily, limits WYS (control Tatooine Early and drop battle deployment) to one destiny draw, and your destinies are incredibly high. Gall+Scout Blaster = one less character in a battle. High Speed Tactics versus Patrol Craft with Palace
Raiders = no more palace raiders. Greeta is the rep because she can pull a Rodian once per turn (including a copy of herself). Could really do some serious damage. Some drain potential versus pure space, Search and Destroy for numbers. Not really concerned with numbers due to high destiny biker scout troopers. Abyssin Ornament to retreive with the biker scouts.
