 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
On first turn get Palpy to Throne Room with Emperor's power and get the DS2 docking bay out with IAO. Get the Endor Landing Platform on your second turn. Keep drawing cards until you get Vader, and get him out to Endor. Get a docking bay every turn until you run out. You'll need the force. This deck is built on high power cards with immunity that can win battles. Not cause a ton of force loss, but just win the battle so the light side must put a card on Insignificant Rebellion. Vader doesn't need his saber to duel Luke, and he isn't there to fight. He is there to hide and stay out of the way, while still causing Your Destiny to work, and then grab Luke and sprint to the Throne Room. The disruptor pistol and Dr. Evazan are there so that a hit character won't be able to use his power and you can win the battle. The deck isn't big on draining. To turn Luke you need to find the enemy and fight him. Since the deck requires you to use lots of force Battle Order is there so you don't have to use one to start a battle (which you will do a lot of). Leave Them To Me is only there so that if you loose something really important you can get it back (use the shield against LU) which is why I only need one Empire's Back (V). Using IAO after 3 turns your cards will generate 8 force by yourself to get all those high priced bounty hunters and Jedi out. The deck isn't as destiny strong as it should be, but hopefully you'll be able to win enough battles to make it irelevent. If Luke isn't in your opponents deck then this turns into a beat down deck with the strong characters and starships as well as Your Destiny and Emperor's Power (loose from Reserve Deck).
