<<The Strategy>>

The basic objective of this deck is to force drain and cause direct damage, retrieve, and battle when necessary.

First turn I pull Roche, an Asteroid Field, Big One, and the Asteroid Cave. On subsequent turns I pull the rest of the Asteroid Fields and the remaining systems. The way I play this deck really depends on the type of deck I go against, of which there are three general categories:

All Space:

This confrontation will probably mostly be battling, with some Kessel and Rycar’s Runs thrown in for good measure. It will probably be a close game, so ideally I will play smart and get some beatdowns in if the opponent leaves his Executor alone or something like that. Wedge, Dash and Theron Hett in their ships would make a good battle squad, with a Freighter Captain in a YT-1300 for forfeit fodder.

Ground deck with a compact but strong space force:

This game will probably be a tough one. Decks like COTVG and TDIGWATT, just to use a couple examples, usually concentrate on ground but have space forces adequate to give you a bad day. This game might start out early by taking out the opponent’s space force, with the latter part of the game dedicated to a drain/direct damage race. This is also where Obi-wan will come in handy, doing some hit/run to take out weak parts of his/her ground forces.

All Ground:

These types of decks SHOULD be pretty easy to take out, but I’m not going to get overconfident. Most decks tend to have at least SOME space, but for those that don’t my plan would be to just spread out and drain, keeping control of the Asteroid Fields and sitting Obi-wan at the Asteroid Cave to fulfill Battle Plan. So far I haven’t seen any non-space decks other than my ISB, but I’ll keep the possibilities open.

Card Selection

(V) Asteroids Do Not Concern Me:

Pulls asteroids and Rycar’s Run, causes direct damage when you control asteroid fields

(V) Red 5:

Why not R2 w/ Red 5?
Well, first of all it has higher maneuver for the asteroids, and it can also cancel annoying game texts like (V) Mist Hunter or the old ZiMH.

Closer?!:

With this card you can pull off Rycar’s Run in two turns, and it also has a nice high destiny to track.

Lost Relay:

Can cause some extra direct damage against the opponent; also has a neat picture.

Asteroid Cave:

To fulfill Battle Plan


This is my first attempt at a Hidden Base deck. Please rate accordingly. ;)

   Shewski     Good comments by Ricky, but I'd stay away from CEC. For your start, I think you should go with YISYW combo, as it can pull either SA or Battle Order. This also frees you to start another card. I'd at least use Tibrin instead of one of your systems in order to make sure you have some emergency retrieval in case you lose your hidden base. I'd rather see your forget about the smugglers and just have good pilots, but that's me. I agree that you need to have some good maneuver to play in the 'roids. I'd use they'd be crazy to follow us as well, just to make you 'roids hurt some more. I also used Ineffective Manuever (non V) to have some fun/direct damage with the opponent's effects. Good luck!
   Rickynumber24     Looks pretty good. I would use something other than the YT-1300 transports, though. Their maneuver is really low. You have Correllia and a lot of freighters, though, so maybe you want to use Correlli Engineering Corporation, since freighters tend to be fairly expensive ships for their stats. Also, if you want another matching pilot combination, how about Hobbie and his ship? They are maneuver 6 together and he goes well with X-wing cannons. Finally, with all the high-maneuver ships you might want Darklighter Spin or perhaps the combo version to get your ships as well. Luke can pull it.
