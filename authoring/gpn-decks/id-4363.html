 The Description
Just a Light Side Deck I created out of mostly DSII cards. I'm not a top notch player, so I'm open to critcism. The main focus of this deck is to completely dominate space with unique cards and matching pilots.

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
There really is no "strategy" to this deck. Just get all the systems out there fast and control Aquaris and generate 10 Force ASAP to get Home One out. Launching the Assault is also key in getting Home One out fast. Squadron Assignments helps yank out the key pilot/starfighter combos fast. TTFOAHC is a pseudo Houjix that is a great destiny 5 to recycle. A Few Manuvers is in there to aid the fighters and to get the destiny 6s back. Superficial Damage is good to avoid losing all the weapons and recycle them to the Used Pile. Of course, if worse comes to worst, Hyper Escape and Careful Planning are in there to either get the much needed Force to fire weapons or to turn tail and flee as fast as possible, plus the fact that they are both destiny 5 and Used Interrupts.


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     This deck will be great for pickup games, but isn't ready for the modern tournament environment. That being said, Hidden Base would be a great way to go with this objective. I'd say to pick a ship type (mon cal, A wing etc) and focus on it, especially with helper interrupts/effects. Lock (V) is excellent, who can be pulled with Riekkan (V). Look into getting some defensive shields, EPPs, and other good Ref 3 cards (Han Chewie & Falcon) if you want to bring this deck up to speed.
   Cam Solusar     This deck looks like a great start to some really interesting strategies. Given what you have here, your best option is probably doing a Hidden Base deck with plenty of planets where you can force drain (Coruscant, Kessel, Kiffex, etc.) Kin Kian is good for adding some extra hurt. If you do end up using this type of strategy I would recommend a couple copies of It Could Be Worse and taking xcshort's advice of removing some weapons. The other option is to use the effect Slayn and Korpil facilities to retrieve force by hitting ships and vehicles. That strategy is a little harder to pull off but I think it is more fun than the draining strategy. If you do that I would recommend using mainly B-wings with Intruder Missles, Ion Cannons, and some Concusion Missles along with Hit and Run and Steady Aim. All of the cards that these strategies require are found in the Death Star II and Special Edition expansion sets, so if you want to pursue these ideas I would advise trying to get those two sets. This deck has lots of potential. Keep up the good work! :)
   xcshort     I'm going to pose my comment in the form of a question. How are you going to lure them into space? I do see that you have a few characters, mainly Luke that can make a mess of a ground force. Not only that but too many weapons. I would drop some of the weapons and a few of the character and ships for more interrupts. The superfical damage is a good idea if you're running many weapons but it won't help if your opponent gets large ground based draining. Put in Kessel and a lower system, a smuggler like Han, Chewie, or Wedge and Kessel Run for some retrival. If you want to stay almost solely in space.
