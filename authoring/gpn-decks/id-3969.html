 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Turn one: deploy Mara and her stick.
Turn two: back her up / keep the early force drain and look for Scum and Villainy
Turn three on: capture and destroy
You want to try to turn up a Fett/Kast early to deal with quickly deployed power cards using Hidden Weapons. You can turn them up with double back/rodian (V). Capturing characters is easy w/ all wrapped up just force them to “forfeit” characters by mowing them down.

Card choices:
-The Cloud City Fett- I like this version because he adds to the forfeit of his captives (if he feeds them to the Sarlacc the opponent loses force = the captives forfeit and if you collect a bounty you retrieve = captives forfeit)

-Rodian (V)- he ca pull bounty hunters! What more could you want? Also its an alien and at each sarlacc feeding I retrieve force = number of aliens present.

-Mobquet A-1 Deluxe Floater- for moving captives around also it’s forfeit 5!

-Defensive Fire & Hutt Smooch- I’ve been having spy trouble.

-Hidden Weapons- if your asking that what are you doing rating decks? Go play the NEW Star Wars game so that you don’t overload your brain!

-Two Sarlaccs?- precautionary
