 The Description
From the author of BHBM v2.3, DBO v1.0 and the editor of Player's Preconstruct #8!
This is my first AOBS try. Unlike my previous decks this one only includes cards that I own. AOBS was the first objective I ever got, but I only got Xizor last week. The deck really should have a second one, I found this out when in my first game with this deck I lost him to EPP Han and Chewbacca (V) in battle on the 4th turn. I know I should add some V cards but I was to lazy to look through them. They'll probably make it into v1.1 Comments and critisisim apreciated.

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I'm always really bad at this part. Allright, the deck mixes AOBS with Scum and bounty hunting. With the use of docking bays and very few other sites it's pretty easy to get from anywhere to the Dungeon. That's what the generics are for, and IAO will get everything out. On the first turn use IAO to get a generic bay to Coruscant, move Xizor and flip before the LS does anything unless Luke came out at the start of the game. AWU makes it easy to capture, and with all the docking bays and high deploy cards Mobilazation Points was an easy choice. The deck has a good split between ground and space. Damn, I'm blanking on what to say next. I know there's more! If I think of it I'll post it. Also, if you ask any questions in your reviews I'll answer them hear.

Thrawn: I can't put in more characters because these are the only BSAs I have except for a couple crappy information brokers, and Palpatine who cancels S&V. I don't have Greedo. The deck is built around capture. It's primarily a capture deck, with AOBS and Scum being secondary.

Shewski: Like I said with Thrawn, these are the only available characters I have. The Dr. E combo isn't legal in an AOBS deck. We Have A Prisoner is there because there are lots of disruptor pistol which cause the character to become lost imediatley and not forefitted where they can be taken by AWU.
