 The Description
I've gotten alot of great ideas for Profit decks from GPN's deck area. It seems to be one of the most enjoyable to play since you get to use so many mains. This version is a collaboration of several popular styles and a few ideas that I thought I'd put in to TRY and improve the deck's functionality. Improvement on Profit is tough because it's already been given so many good ideas. I don't know how good of a job I've done, but I think this deck should work out well (or at least be fun to play).

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
The first thing I tried to improve were the deck's destiny draws. Rather than having The Signal search for effects, I put in Were You Looking For Me and Threepio with his Parts Showing. I thought this would be a good idea because TWHPS can be replaced by See-Threepio, allowing an extra 3 force to be retrieved (well, actually 2 when you consider that TWHPS would be lost). Those extra two force can really come in handy; especially if Han somehow makes his way to the lost pile. With "Were You Looking For Me?", you can pull both versions of Threepio into your hand and play one after another- getting the 2 force retrieved right when you need it most. Also, my main R2 is the virtual version. It allows some pretty good bonuses while with Threepio.
I also decided on putting in Yoda's Gimer Stick and Leia Rebel Princess- these two make for a nasty cancel combo.
In general, this deck has alot of good searching potential and can set up some powerful force drains/Be Destroyed damage. 3x Master Luke makes for a quick release of Han (or a guard of Han if you'd prefer to wait to release later). I don't know what else to say. I've tested it a few times and it seems to work pretty well- which should say alot when you take into consideration the fact that I'm not a very skilled player.

Thanks for all the advice!
