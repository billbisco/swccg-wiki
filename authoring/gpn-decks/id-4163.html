Is the activation high enough? I could probably put in Home One: DB. And Mos Espa DB would be in there for sure if I had it.

3 Lukes? One for beatdown, one for retriaval and one for BHBM. Talon Karrde will replace a raider as soon as I get him.

There’s not much retrieval in here. Tatooine Celebration will get a slot soon. And some Harvests along with Owen/Beru could probably be useful too. Kessel Runs would be a bonus, but I don’t depend on completing them.

   Shewski     It's a good start! I'd add the other Tat DB, Mos Espa DB. The three Lukes are interesting, mostWYS just run Luke (V). Is normal Chewie (V)? He should be, so he can pull life Debt. Axe Leebo. Don't worry about Talon Karrde as well. Swap in Capt Han of normal Han. Lando w/ Gun stinks! Use Crazy Lando. Also be sure to add in ANH Wedge (he's a smuggler) and RS1. I don't think that you are taking full advantage of you interrupt abuse. Add more Cont. Tunnel Vision, an On the Edge, houjix, etc. Strikeforce/Planet is not really needed- Menace does the trick. Ditch the Kessel Runs. Tat Celebration/LUke V should give you plenty of retrieval. Maybe add a weapons or two to take care of BH ships, can also use a Slight weapons Malfuntion to add more destiny. May want to take steps to pretect your reacting palace raiders.
