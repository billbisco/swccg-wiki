This is the deck that went undefeated in the first two days of worlds this year going 6-0 on both days combined.
It's only loss of GenCon was to the world champion on Day 3.
This deck is solid.

Some card choices:

x4 systems under your starting effect along with x5 in your reserve?

Yes, this is what gives you the potential to flip second turn. All you have to do is draw a system in your opening hand or activate one during your first turn, then draw up for it and you will have the option of flipping second turn unless you get really lucky and draw three systems in your opening hand then you can flip first turn. If you don't get a system in your hand turn 1 don't worry you will probobly flip on turn 3.
Before you flip and spread out make sure you have a speeder with matching pilot down at the Desert with Sandwhirl going as well as an evasion card so you don't get beatdown in space (Houjix & Out Of Nowhere, Hyper Escape or Rebel Barrier). Once you have that accomplished you will not have much left to do other than drain them out and an occasional counter attack.

Manuevering Flaps (v)?

This is how you hold a site with Hidden Base nowadays so you don't have to pay to drain. You simply put down a speeder with matching pilot at the Desert with sandwhirl and then you are ready to spread out fast. It's a harder location to beatdown at then the forest because only walkers can go there and the Desert is pullable with Kirdo III also. If they control the Sandwhirl you can Tibrin for it back.

Rebel Planners (v)?

This card is of course what lets you pull an extra system every turn as well as making Hidden Base a lot quicker.

Corran Horn?

This is in here just in case you go up against a deck like walkers that puts Tatooine Maul in space with his ship. If he weren't in here you would never be able to kill their ship without putting your speeder pilots in space. He's also great against Huntdown.

Dash Rendar (v)?

His speeder of choice in this deck is Rogue 4. But he can pilot any speeder if you're having a tough time getting going or if you need an extra pilot somewhere, great character for the deck.

Honor Of The Jedi?

This card is money! It is great against a good number of match-ups and is pullable so it's worth including it.

Imperial Artocity (v) x2?

Oh yeah! You can pull one of them with the Don't do that again shield and this card is great against Match-ups like Walkers and FWKTIL. It's also destiny of six.

It could Be worse x1?

Yes, but be careful! If they have a ton of force saved you may be asking for it if you play it. Sense can also suck, but we all know how useful the card can be so you need at least one in the deck.

Organized Attack x3?

Some players may tell you that you only need 2 in this deck but that just isn't the best. You need to have a ship at each of you big drain systems after you flip so playing three is the way to go.

Power Pivot?

It's funny going up against CCT and watching them see their Executor turn into a 0 power ship.

Han, Chewie And The Falcon x1?

Yep. With all that CCT going around out there the best number to play is 1.

X-wing Lasser Cannon x2?

Gets the Power Pivot to work and is really dumb not to play with in HBX.

Ok this deck is pretty much played the same against every deck but I will give you the match-ups I played against anyway.

Match-ups:

Walkers-This is the deck that your play should vary the most. you do not want to put a speeder and his pilot down to the Desert untill mid/late game. You will want to pay to drain early on but this is a suprisingly easy match-up because I beat it multiple times fairly easily.

SYCFA/FWKTIL-There are a few things you can try against this deck. Probobly you best option is to play a ship or two and then wait for them to come and beat you down then play Houjix. After that you spread out and drain since they also give you the Death Star. With Imperial Atrocity (v) and your bigger ships like HCF to do some counter beats this is not that tough to beat.

CCT-You can cancel their Despair drain because of Rebel Planners (v) and you can beatdown the Executor with Power Pivot and some ships so do what you gotta do.

Huntdown- It's better to wait untill Vader is at a site before you deploy Luke in his speeder to the Desert. That way your opponent will have a tougher time playing the Dark Jedi + Blizzard 4 + I Have You Now combo on you. Just try to get out Honor and make them lose to their own Visage while you don't. If they play No Escape cancel Visage with Corran. Spread out as fast as you possibly can and drain them out before they can do too much damage to you.

Senate-Well if your're playing against Senate then you might want to pick out your own Stategy because out of the seven games I played with the deck this is the only one I lost but it was to the world champion.

I played against (Walkers x2, CCT x2, Huntdown x1, FWKTIL x1) all of which this deck beat on days 1 and 2.

You can probobly figure out the rest on your own so that's it.

Good luck-Nick
