 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
1st Turn - Activate 7 , Pull Rieekan (v) & deploy. Pull A New Secret Base which pulls 1 marker and Med Lab (v). Use Med Lab to pull 2-1b (V) (if Darkside gives you an icon to activate). Use Echo Base Garrison to pull Luke. Use Manuvering Flaps (v) to deploy Luke & Rouge 1 at 4th marker.

2nd Turn - Activate 9 to 10. Pull 1 of the speeders with Reiekan and deploy with use of Man Flaps. Delpoy other marker. Get another Speeder out if possible. Move to try and get 3 sites in control and flip Objective.

3rd Turn - Act 10 to 11 Force. Get another speeder and deploy. Use EPP's to disrupt opp objective.

4th Turn - Find Frostbite (v) and get some ships out. Sped speeders and leave force for a react. If you have to loose a card from battle, loose Luke, Zev, or Hobbie b/c they go to used pile and can be pulled out of reserve deck. Use ground forces to either support Hoth if they come there or go and battle on there turf.

With the objective causing force damage, Frostbite retrieving, and getting Lock out to lower shield so I can force drain, I should be able to put alot of pressure on my opponite.

Card Choices -

Wedge, RSL - he is a stud with Luke (2 Battle Destines and cancel 1) I could pull the Special Edition one but I can always pull the speeder with Rieekan and use Flaps.

Endor Celebration - I don't want to get killed by the Occupation cards.

Owen & Beru - Anti Watto.

Qui-Gonn - He is a tank.

Both Falcons. If I get Lando, I can put him w/ Han, or lose it if I have the other on the table.

Home One - If that is sitting on Hoth, Frostbite should be okay....
