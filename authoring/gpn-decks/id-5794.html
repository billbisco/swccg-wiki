 The Last 5 Comments
   Darth Hoth     I'm just going to touch on a few quick things, since they are rather large changes. First off, you need to come up with a longer strategy section, no one's going to review a deck that has almost no strategy. Second, the Shmi deck isn't all that great of a deck these days. I'd suggest either switching it to TRM so you get the first turn, or Profit. TRM would probably be an easier switch for you from this deck. It's nice to see you aren't afraid of interrupts, but I think you might have gone a little overboard with them. You've used so much that you've taken away from much needed weapons. If you're going to have HCATF, I'd suggest getting a little more space in, but I'd rather see you drop it all together. This deck could become a very good TRM if you were to go that route.
