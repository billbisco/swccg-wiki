 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First order of business: get out Ominous Rumors with EO first turn. After that its very simple: deploy characters to Endor to add to the drains in space. Then control and drain for 3 or 4 force at each location per turn. Use Admiral Chiraneau to add more to drains if you have him. I think I have 9 leaders in this deck so if anyone has any suggestions about how to use this ability- please tell me in your comments. I included Admiral’s Orders not only for destiny but also because of their nice game text relating to sites of the system you occupy. I do expect some of you to criticize my use of Blaster Rack with only one weapon- but I think it’s worth it with Lord Vader. Any other cards you think I should add?
