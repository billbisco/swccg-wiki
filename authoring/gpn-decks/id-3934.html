 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Coruscant is your battleground system to satisfy Don't Do That Again so you can get extra force generation from Mobilization Points. Assuming you control all of your docking bays, then you generate 12 force per turn from your locations. You only give up 2 force generation to your opponent, but still have a battleground site and system to satisfy Battle Plan. Your ships can react for free to Kuat, so it is not hard do defend. Basically the idea is just beatdown your opponent. Take over one of the sites on the planet they are playing on, drop an Outer Rim Scout there, deploy Imperial Decree, and your opponents drains should be shut down. Most card choices are self-explanatory, but if you have questions just ask. This is the first ISB Ops deck I have built, so all comments and advice are welcome. However, I would like to keep the same strategy idea of force choke and beatdowns, so try to keep advice along those lines and abstain from re-working the entire strategy of the deck, unless you think it is completely horrible.

Cam Solusar: How many agents should I pack? I have two Abyssian Ornament to pull them, should I add more of those? I can easily occupy a Coruscant, a battleground system, and that stops Don't Do That Again from suspending Mobilization Points. What cards should I take out to make room for more agents?

   Shewski     I know that 5D6-RA7 is a popular ISB choice since he shuts down drains and can't be battled. Some of the other ISB decks I have seen could use probe droids and the incinerator to retreive 2 droids per turn. I think you could stand to reduce some of your space (since you want to only hold Cor.) in order to make room for more agents, but I am not too familiar with this deck type, so take my comments with a grain of salt.
   Cam Solusar     Based on my past experience you will probably need more agents. To flip you need four ISB agents out (you have no rebel base sites). You only have six agents in this deck, so the odds of flipping are really low. Merrejk might be a good card to put in, as well as more planets. This deck activates 14 force if you control all of the DB's, but Mobilization Points is likely to be cancelled if your opponent packs the Don't Do That Again shield. This deck looks like it has a lot of potential. Good start and good luck!
