 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Pretty simple. First turn, get the COR db, then flip, arrange the cards in your force pile, and draw for a barrier or Stunning to protect Xizor. Then, next turn, drain and stuff, activate of course, arrange the cards, get db. Not much of a turn-by-turn for this deck. But as for the strategy, Emperor Palpatine has three Force Lightnings because you can look at the top cards of your deck in a battle- pick who you fry! Dr. Evazan?s Sawed-Off Blaster is good for the same reason. The Rack (V) obviously gets Dengar?s, IG-88?s, Aurra?s, and Dr. Evazan?s weapons. Your Bounty Hunters are boosted with Ket Maliss (V) and Desijilic Tatoo (V). After Her! Retrieves, obviously. Not much else to say.

   Shewski     I think you are trying to do a little too much location wise. I'd rather see you focus on DBs, since Maliss, the true AOBS helper gives you that ability. You can also run the BF Bridge for more pullable generation (swapping Twilek for WMAOP). Since you have some Dejariks, the holotable wouldn't hurt. I'd probably start Rack instead, since you want to maximize the text on Tatoo. Why are you running Emp Palpy (and giving him such support like 3x Lightning)? Once he hits the table, Ket Maliss' bonuses go away. Character selection is limited... I'd add some more BHers like Aurra/Gun, Jodo Kast, and the like. A Vigo wouldn't hurt. Also, I like using Greedo/Reegessk to max out Ket. Another option you may want to use is start All Wrapped Up, but then use multiples of Jabba's Through With You to add destiny/deploy BH guns from reserve. Oota (V) is good for getting lots out of your BHers. Drop is not needed since you can return one card to Used from hand every player's turn with the flipped objective. Plus you already have Monnok. A little space might be OK, but if you don't go with it, go with Zuckuss on the ground. If you go space, MP combo is good (just pull your planet as first action).
   pointy-eared green dude     This looks like a real powerhouse! However, there are some questions that arise in my mind. Do you think that you have enough characters? Multiple copies are okay, especially of BF,BH and such, but what about other characters like the almighty Makurth? They aren't bad, and can be lethal by themselves. Also, I see A LOT of red...strong red, mind you, just maybe too much. Keep up the good work, Rev.
   Cam Solusar     Here's what I think: The deck looks pretty solid, but there are a few things that you could do (or that I would do) that would free up some card spaces and make it better. Instead of (V)L,L,L use Mobilization Points and replace the Bunker and Forest Clearing sites with battleground docking bays. Another thing I would do is put in a 2nd Xizor. You only have one and your strategy is screwed if you lose him. I would also put in another Guri, for good measure. You can probably get rid of one each of Kintan Strider and Stunning Leader. Then I'd put in 2x ZiMH to keep space covered. If you disagree with any of this, please respond on the Ratings Feedback Thread. Good Luck and MTFBWY!
