 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
----- This deck is extremely flexible. It is based on taking cards out of your opponent’s hand making beatdown less possible, and trying to stick him with limited resources… Now I know what you all are going to say about this deck, - “scrambed transmission will kill it”… But it isn’t killed by scrambled transmission, although I can no longer use about 7 cards they can be lost (yeb yeb helps with this, look at their top card and lose your non useful card and they lose one too) and to force drains and stuff like that… There is plenty of other force drain and beatdown potential in this deck to keep it on its feet…

If scrambled transmission is not played or not out yet –
At tops (w/out monnok luck) I can take about 6 cards out of opponent’s hand (defensive fire 1, fear 2, scanning crew 1, senate obj 2) and then play limited resources for 2 or 4 more force, it works very well…and it annoy’s my opponent when they draw their cards they need into their and lose them right away…

Also the normal senate effects and senators make it easy to find cards that I want and drain big…

I added in the duel cards because they are high destiny and work for this deck if I track correctly and use yeb yeb to check if they have something tracked…and because it’s a fun unexpected twist to a senate deck…

Against LS senate my plan is to just rip the cards out of their hand and if needed maybe choking senators? Hmm… well the dueling and drains should hold up against them…I’ve never played against LS senate although playing DS senate decks for almost a year (different twists to it…drain, this, KTOD style)

The other good thing about this deck is that besides for LS senate it doesn’t have many weaknesses against other decks…standard beatdown characters, limiting their “resources” (cards in hand), dueling, and exceptionally large drains keeps this deck alive against whatever they thrown at ya…

Be nice with the ratings I took a 6 month break from playing SWCCG so I don’t know some of the new rules and stuff…
