 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This is a pretty powerful deck with a lot of movement. There are three different docking bays in this deck that allow you to move freely from site to site. Pulling your site, though might be somewhat difficult as there are no cards here to do it with so you'll have to rely on your luck of the draw to pull them out. It might take you a few turns to pull out all the ones you want. You do, however, have enough sites for good generation. You start off with 5 but if you pick up those 5 for the first two turns you'll end up with some good cards in hand, including sites. Tatooine: City Outskirts allows you to deploy a Jedi from Reserve. Tatooine: Marketplace allows you to retrieve force. Once you have your Tatooine: Docking Bay and have used Tatooine: Outskirts to pull either Obi-Wan or Luke than you are ready for a fight. Tunnel Vision and Weapon Levitation allow you to pull out weapons or something better. Organized Attack is in here for destiny more than anything else (before you ask) so that is interchangeable with anything you want. You can use the Bacta Tank to save your characters and if Redemption is down, they deploy free there. There are plenty of ways to save your characters so it will be hard for your opponent to get rid of you. You do have to worry about Shut Him Up or Shut Him Down. Once that play on your Tank you'll have to rely on pure skill. Not that you didn't have to to begin with. ;)

   Colin -Hunter     Rescue the princess in my mind is not that wonderful (and for me boring to play as it tends to beat down a lot). Having said that here are my suggestions. Get rid of chewie, Akbar, 8D8 and Commander Wedge. Try to get as many character with permanent weapons as possible (2 of each is a good start). Throw away all the weapons other than Han's heavy blaster (and use the virtual text..... please!), swap han solo for Virtual Han. If you own no good space than either don't bother or use X-wing swarms. By that I mean lot of x-wings, organised attcks, and x-wing laser cannons. If you keep the space keep the spiral (it is fantastic). Replace 8D8 with Virtual Escape Pod and play atleast 1 if not 2 ghkks. Steal the escape pods if you own none (they are uncommon or common). That will be a start for you :)
