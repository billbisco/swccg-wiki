 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
ok, before you activate, use mob points combo to pull executor and use the CC prison to pull lando (V)...

1st turn, activate at least 4 force. us 2 force and KDY (V) to pull bespin system . use 1 force and SD (V) to pull they must never again leave this city. deploy executor for free to bespin system... then pull CC: DB using CCT...if your opponent gave you at least 1 force then you should deploy lando (V) to either the prison or executor...

2nd turn, activate at least 7 force. use 1 force and SD (V) to pull despair (V) and then use 2 force and then use KDY to pull another system...you can then use force to deploy what you need....

you are pretty much setup after turn 2...now you can either spread out and drain away or you can save 3 or 4 ties and then use them for a suprise beatdown...if you can throw down 4 of them(for 8 force) at a system where the opponent has an x-wing then you have power 16 and a draw...play an APTW and boom, power 24, a draw and immunity to attrition. if you have fighter cover out and a couple sfs's on the ties, just shot once and its power +3, you don't even need to hit a ship...you just need to shoot once...its a very nice beat down...very suprising too...if you don't want to do beat downs then just put chiraneau on the executor and at all your systems(naboo,sullust and endor) your drains are +1...plus you can drop down someone to bring jabba's prize to the carbonite chamber...now you are draining there for 3, 4 if you can get vader and his saber down there...also since you have fighter cover out, if you have BFBH, DVDLOTS and Maul at the chamber they are all immune to attrition <7 and get 3 BDs...it will take alot of guys to come down and battle you off CC...

the main reason i am using CCT as the platform in this deck is so that i can deny the lightside han and lando...there are some changes that i am trying to make but i'm not sure what i want to take out...i would like to add admiral piett because he can pull my admiral's orders plus i can pull him with imperial command...i run 4 APTW because you can use them to pull your ties(lost) or boost them up(used) and plus i have a decent chance of pulling at least one of them in my opening hand...i'm also thinking of maybe taking out DVDLOTS and his saber and then maybe adding vader with stick and an all too easy since i'm gonna be sitting at the carbonite chamber anyway...i've also thought about adding cloud city occupation but i'm probably going to be only holding 1 site(chamber) and then maybe the docking bay...not sure if its worth a card slot...just let me know what you think...
