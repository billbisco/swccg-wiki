 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Start with Twi'lek Advisor and Bad feeling have I. This disrupts most LS as it adds to the mains deploy cost.

Draw till you can activate enough and then save. If your opponent deployed, kill him. If not, take over his locations, or force drain at Endor and Tatooine: Lars Moisture Farm. This is mainly against ground, but you have enough ships to take the air if needed. You should be able to hold several sites, and if your opponent gets too many cards in his hand use monnok. Limited Resources can be a life saver near the end of the game, and SAC are a must.(Why else did they remake them??)
