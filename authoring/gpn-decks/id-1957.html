 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
alright, obviously this deck needs a lot of work, but i was bored at work and needed something to do so i came up with this and decided to put it on this site and see what happens.

my goal was to use 2 virtual cards, and try to bend the words of an objective to do something really weird.

so my 2 virtual cards are boshek sai tor kalfas. sai tor obviosly pulls weapons for padme panaka and obi, while boshek does the dbl duty of being a virtual card and bending the words of the objective. since boshek is an alien i can get around the restrictions set by the objective, and he can pull hobbie's ship to use the xwing cannon. how cool is that!?!

chewie and cc lando can also get around the objective and pull the falcon at the same time. although it may be a better choice to have lando with ax and a bravo ship combo; i thought it might be cool to see the falcon in a hyperdrive deck since the falcon's hyperdrive is always crapping out in the movies.

obviously this deck would lose to combat and senate 9 out of 10 times, just because it isn't all that powerful on the ground, but if the opponent were to ignore you for a while, i think you could pull off a few turns of big drains and maybe even up the differential.

so lemme know what you think, who know's, i just might put a version of this deck together sometime.

;-)

later
