 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
First turn, pull Rieekan and Swamp and use Swamp to pull a Nudj. Next get the Farm and another Nudj (same turn if you have the Nudj in hand). If you don't get any icons from your opponent you will have to wait on deploying Rieekan. Get Tigran with Rieekan soon, to increase the deploy costs. I usually flip with Rieekan at Swamp, Tigran at Farm, and someone (or someones) who can actually fight at the Forest (so that they're immune to attrition). Set up at the Forest so that you can hold it. That should be the only battleground site you need to hold, ideally since Menace and the objective will make your opponent's drains almost nonexistant. Get whichever DSII effect is needed most with Insight.

Once you're set up, drain in space for 4 at Coruscant and Kessel. Make sure to fortify Coruscant especially well because it is easy for them to attack you there, and you get another deploy increase for imperials there. If your opponent leaves you any other systems, deploy there. Unfortunately, I haven't had a chance to play against a real space deck yet, so I don't know how well it would work against one.

R2 in Red 5 is simply to get Luke (for retrieval) out faster. It's also a destiny 7, so I can't object to that.

Organized attack is in there because it makes squadrons power +3 (they're 3 X-wings/Y-wings) and immune to attrition. And, it's a used 5.

Tala 2 is spy cover for Dantooine. It can be piloted by Blount or Corran.

Once you start getting cards out of the deck, almost all that's left is destiny 5 cards, so expect those when playing in the middle of the game if you don't know what the card is.

 The Last 5 Comments
   Cam Solusar     I like the changes that you have made. I have absolutely no experience with DBO, but this deck looks like it's fun to play. Watch out for those Court or BHBM decks playing No Escape, as well as SFS TIE's, which could give your space problems. (Grimtaash, maybe?) Other than that, great job. Good luck.
   Shewski     I'm a fan of the DBO platform, as it can be a nice suprise at the tournament scene. I'd run YISYW to get Menace Fades when you want it, plus you could run Squad Assign to get some pilots out to get more out of Strikeforce. I'd run Angry Chewie over Protector, and maybe EPP Han to do some ground hit and runs. I'd use a Falcon as a Match w/ Gen Lando (or Angry Lando is good space and ground). I'd include some B Wing Squads... Power 12 is nothing to sneeze at. I would axe Log Delay and run Goo Nee Tay- it is far more useful. Menace Fades (if you control Hoth) does shut down the Walker Garrison bonuses. If you had a little more ground to truly satisfy Menace, I think you'd have a fine deck.
