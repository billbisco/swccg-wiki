 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Strategy
========
After extensive research, I found out that Jedi Luke and Rebel Scout Luke cannot be Jedi Tested at all. Thus they were no longer a possible choice. On the other hand Master Luke isn't a Jedi just force Sensitive thus he could persona replace SoS(v) and test past 5. I am also currently see-sawing on taking Encampment / Extra Yoda / Master Luke / Luke's Lightsaber out and just putting Luke with Stick in and after completeing Test 5.. Pulling SoS(v) into hand and playing Luke with Stick down instead.

As the deck currently is, You can sacrifice Yoda's Hope to pull Levitation, Use Levitation to grab your Binocs and use Wokling to Pull Encampment and boom your good to go. You retreieve 3 force from each test up to 4. then From Test 5 you retrieve your 10 from SYIC because you replace Yoda(v) with Master of the Force.

Plus you have the on the edges and Reflection (which SoS(v) can pull) so force retreval is not a problem in this deck. THe only problem you might have is sometimes paying for your retreval but most of the time it shouldn't be a problem.

Your Beatdown squads (quiggy, Obi and Lando) are great.. plus you have Jar Jar and Corran Horn for foothold situations so that you can beatdown at a site you have no icons at.

Overall this deck is fun and seems to compete well against moderate players. I don't have a huge ammount of players in the area and I don't have really any on par with the big boys Ie. Hunter and the rest. But I think it would stand a chance against most people if played well.
