 The Description
This is a deck that mainly focuses on the destruction of Alderaan so that the opponent would have a very hard time deploying anything, and if he/she did, it would loose in a major battle immediately.

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Most of the strategy is explained in the Strategy article "Star Destroyers!!!!", but otherwise... Get the Death Star Out and blow up Alderaan. Then use A Million Voices Crying Out to retrieve Commence Primary Ignition so you can blow up Hoth for the bonuses of A Day Long Remembered. Then, with Astromech Shortage, most of your opponent's starships will cost +9!!!!! And you can deploy the Accuser for free as a react and it's immune <4 (read the other strategy for more info). Try to eliminate the opponent as quickly as possible so that they cannot do anything else but suffer your huge force drains. Also AMVCO allows you to retrieve 3 force every time you deploy a Star Destroyer after you blow up Alderaan!!!
Any suggestions or comments...

 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     Maximize your Prepared defenses! You should aim for Y4 instead of Hoth since it is closer. Pack Imp Commands to get out Admirals, get rid of the weenie bounty hunters (pack EPP ones if you must). You have no way to get your effects, especailly Day Long Remembered, which your entire deck is based upon- pack effect pullers. Use less DS sites and more DS gunners. You have a lot of systems- why not use Merrejk to get them out earlier? Pack more superlasers too. I think you definitely need to revamp this- ditch many ships and pilots, and refocus on your objectives.
   LordHoban     You have no interrupts in this deck. Only 3 effects? You need some way to get through your deck faster, maybe a couple Uncertain is the Future, so you can actually get all the needed stuff out a lot quicker. A couple Emergency Deployments (v) (premier), couple Sense/Uncertain is the Futures (Refl2) work wonders. You should also have at least 2 Death Star Gunners, and once Alderaan is blown, not depend on blowing up Yavin 4 or Hoth, just have that as a plus if you succeed. As it is, certain decks will obliterate you before you get as far as Alderaan, let alone Hoth or Yavin 4. Look at your deck, first turn you get your gunner, deploy him (hopefully to a site you have in hand), and then next turn get the superlaser. 3rd turn, you move the death star once. 4th turn, move it again. 5th turn, finally, you get to blow it up unless the opponent has invaded the Death Star by then. Also, you can't have very good destiny in this deck. At least with some cards to help you cycle through your deck faster, you can use Colo Claw Fish, put a seven on it, and with the gunner deployed you could draw a 0 and succeed at blowing Alderaan. You don't need that many starships. I'd take at least 5-7 out for the above mentioned cards, and give it a try. A good try, but it needs a bit more balance.
