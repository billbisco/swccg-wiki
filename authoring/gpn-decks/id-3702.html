 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Strategy:
Dominate Cloud City with huge amounts of power. Shoot anything that hits cloud city then feed them to a character w/saber at carbonite chamber using all too easy.

Card Selections
Characters
I use several jedi with their sabers to hit high ability characters and add to force drains. Fett and IG-88 pack repeating weapons to hit everything else.P-59 and P-60 hit characters and cause direct force loss. Xizor and Tarkin (V) provide more power. Lobot and CC Troopers also provide power and forfiet fodder.

Starships
The bounty hunter ships provide great power and abilities. Flagship Executor can be deployed for free to Bespin using They Must Never Again Leave This City (Executor is better than Flagship, I just dont have Executor). The TIEs are powerful and using TMNALTC, if lost, go back to hand.

Effects / Interrupts
Dark Deal And Cloud City Occupation are key to the deck. Both can be grabbed by the obj and provide a large amount of extra damage (DD adds 2 to force drains, CCO deals 1 for each CC site I occupy) All Too Easy freezes characters hit with sabers @ carbonite chamber (also does a 8 damage) Aiiii! Aaa! Agggggggggg! deals damage for each captured character @ a prison. Evader / Monnok can be pulled with crush the rebellion and cause cards to be lost from opponent's hand. Ghhhk / TRWEU cancels battle damage and other things, including Landing claw and reacts. Twi'lek Advisor are high destiny and grab effects. Limited resources deal damage.
