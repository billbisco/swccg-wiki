 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck use Hidden Base to slow my opponent's stratigies while I force drain my opponent to death. After the objective is flipped, I can cancel 2 drains a turn, and I can cancel another one a turn with Rebel Fleet. If my opponent is still draining for too much, I play the Battle Plan D-Shield. This way, to inflict any force drain damage, my opponent must pay 12 force. I activate enough so it will not concern me.

Card Selections
Adimial Orders
Concentrate All Fire is a key card. It adds 3 to power of any starship that fires a weapon. since there are so many weapons, I can easily add over 10 to power this way.
Characters
The Red squadron pilots (Red 7-10) and their starships can be used in any combination. I use the non-(V) version of Luke because the (V) text does not help this deck. If I was trying to destroy the death star, I would use the (V).
Starships
Artoo-Detoo in Red 5 I use because Luke can deploy for free aboard and it has a destiny of 7. Defiance is a key ship for the weapon stratagy. I use Gold Squadron 1 because it can easily deffeat a TTO deck and is cheaper to deploy than the regular Falcon.
Locations
I use Coruscant (SE) because I can drain for 3 there and Imperials are deploy +2. Chandrila and Aquaris activate huge amounts of force. Sullust is targetable by Haven.
Effects
Docking And Repair Facilites keep starships from being lost. Haven reduces deploy costs and adds a battle destiny. Rebel Fleet cancels a force drain a turn. Traffic Control allows me to draw alot and toss back what I dont want. I use Wokling (V) because of its activation bonus and retrieval. Superficial Damage allows me to sacrifice weapons to soak up battle damage.
Interrupts
All Wings Report In can grab A-wings and retrieve. The Signal can grab effects and is a recyclable 5. Star Destroyer! is a recyclable 7. Corellian Slip reduces a starship's manuver, so I can shoot it easier.
Weapons
I use Concussion Missiles because they can target anything (I did use A-wing Cannons for a while but decided that missiles can do more damage). Heavy Turbolaser Battery is important. It should only be played on Defiance. This way any capitial ship (execpt Executor) will be hit.

This deck has never lost when Ive had control of space.
