 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
This deck worked well to begin with. The only problem I found was I really wanted another lightsaber after I had lost Obi-Wan or Qui-Gon the first time. Hence the addition of Another Saber for each plus Leia’s Blaster Rifle which helps give me flexibility in that I can skip Anakin’s Saber and give it to Luke after he looses Luke’s Saber. Padme helps get Obi-Wan and his mentor out early. A Jedi’s Resilience (Just added the second one) is there to keep Obi, Qui and Luke coming back hopefully with another saber courtesy of Sai’torr Kal Fas. So you basically have 2 sabers for Luke, Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon. Of course there is always the possibility of picking another up you’ve just lost if you are able to flip your objective. So they keep coming and they are armed. J
It’s usually best to use Insurrection & Aim High to check to see what weapons are available to you before you determine a course of action. With the destiny in this deck you will rarely miss anyone, of course you have Colo Claw Fish. If not I guarantee any deck your opponent plays that revolves around weapons and uses one of the Naboo Underwater Creatures will be hurting because of your ability to cancel that.
If I can’t stack my destiny on Colo Claw Fish because my opponent started with it or Sando Aqua Monster, I guarantee you my opponent won’t be to happy with me when I kill his card. This really hurts those Assault Rifle Decks.

I want to Duel my opponents Jedi to that end I have Blast The Door, Kid (x2) and Dodge.
Move away or cancel the battle then Duel next turn with those destiny 7 Bionic Hands.
Another Interrupt I love is Away Put Your Weapon. This can really save the game when it is close. I just wish I could keep a saber or two out J.

Setting up:
Usually I like to get Obi-Wan or Luke out as early as possible, usually with a Portable Scanner, if not I would just assume bring out Qui-Gon.

So get Obi-Wan or Luke out deploy their weapon and a Scanner next turn grab Obi-Wan’s Journal w/the scanner. If they are playing Lightsaber Combat grab a Bionic Hand and have fun. Once you have another scanner out grab two a turn, watch the opponents force pile because Monnok would really suck. If you are worried just grab a Bionic Hand and a scanner.
So every deploy phase you will now have a nice card for your lightsaber combat.

Another device I really like is Wrist Comlink. Now I have Jedi that react I am draining for 3,3 and 2 (could be 3,3 and 3 if I decide to give Anakin’s Saber to Leia)none of which can be canceled, I can play BTDK or Dodge if you somehow cancel the react I am hitting anyone you throw at me (unless there is a Force Field) I am stacking either a 6 or a 7 on my Jedi for Lightsaber Combat. This deck is still undefeated and with this version I think its time to fear the Bionic Hand.

Here is a real quick note on defensive shields.
Always play: Only Jedi Carry That Weapon.

-Derek Brooks
