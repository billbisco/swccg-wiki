   TheOtherMaestro     I think that this deck shows some potential, but there are too many unnecessary cards in it. Revelation and SpideyGuy0 are correct, but I think that not only do you need more Docking Bays, you need more regular sites. This is just my whim, but you should probably stick with one or two planets and/or space areas and stick with them. (Don't go as far as I did though). Besides that, once you have done that, things should work out much better and you will be well on your way to making a really good deck.
