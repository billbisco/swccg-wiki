 The Description
I have played this Objective ever since it entered the scene of SW:CCG. In fact, other than a few petty attempts at other decks, this is the only type that I post. QMC/IO is a very versatile deck with much to offer in both classic and open format. In both classic and open formats, however, the consistent type of gameplay that is associated with QMC/IO is "hit and run". I have tweaked my deck time and again for this type of special ops..."hit and run" as I call it. Lately, though, I have felt aggressive, and so I have worked on this deck. It has some hit and run tactics and moderate retrieval, but it packs a punch with some high destiny and a few suprises. What do you think?

 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
I think this deck has a formidable ground force that, if played correctly, could most definitely hold Cloud City and win the game. I have a little bit of evasion/stalling with 2x Path of Least Resistance, 2x Clash of Sabers, and Yoda's Gimer Stick. I stop a lot of direct damage with Honor of the Jedi and Cloud City Celebration, which I can set up quickly. Some suprises...a few buddy interrupts, Revolution, Alter/Sense, Yoda w/ Gimer Stick. I wanted to add a flair to QMC...the buddy interrupts will definitely get use to add to damage, Alter/Sense not only defend my interrupts, but also my effects that matter in the late game (Revolution, Flash of Insight (v), Yoda's Gimer Stick, buddy interrupts, Lightsaber Proficiency). Yoda is something I saw in Cam Solusar's deck, and decided to give him a shot. I think he can find his niche sticking with Luke/Obi for power or with the "younglings" and fend off bad guys with his gimer stick. He's not quite the battling CGI muppet he is in Episode II, but he'll work. If you have any questions or comments, post them here! Please rate! Thanks!

PS Don't drop the Cloud City: Downtown Plaza until you know you can hold it. It can potentially backfire on you, seeing as it is heavy drain for both sides and is an exterior site, leaving it vulnerable to vehicular attack.
