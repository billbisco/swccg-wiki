This is a deck that some old-timer BBS members and I came up with, and we've been tweaking for ages. Many thanks as always to LordMaxx and ZippyDaJedi (John and Ivan)!!!!

Before you start with the Ewok-hate (I was once among you), take a look at the deck and see what it tries to do.

I have a couple of cards that will need to go out for Classic, so once again any suggestions are highly welcome!

Okay, this is a deck of combos:

Superficial Damage - Ewok Spears have a forfeit value of 3, the Catapults already have one of 4, and when they are discarded to satisfy forfeit, they go to the used pile. Great way to recycle a 7! :)

Ewok Spear:
Deploys free on a Spearman and adds 1 to power. Target a character using 1 Force. Both players draw destiny. Target's power or ferocity =0 for remainder of turn if your destiny + Ewok's Power > opponent's destiny.

The Ewok Spearman with a spear is power 2 + the number of LS icons at the site (add in Ithorian + Daughter of Skywalker).

Vine Snake
Ferocity =0 Slither =3 Selective
Habitat: planet Sites (except Hoth). Attacks by attaching to character. X=0. Every move phase draw destiny; each time destiny > ability add 1 to X. Chatacter is power -X (eaten if power = 0).

If you have never seen this happen before, I'm sure that the light is starting to come on. :) I've had Vine Snakes eat Vader, Palpy, Aurra, Mara, and my true highlight of storyline was when a Vine Snake eat Prince Xizor wearing Mandalorian Armor after being hit with an Ewok Spear!

Ewok Spear + I Feel The Conflict:
This is the real kick in the pants. In order to turn Vader - yes it does happen!!! - you need to win battles. To win battles you have to give your opponent a reason to come after you. This is the reason for Kazak, the Ithorians and Graak. Once they come after you you need to win battles. The Ewok Spear can take their largest character and make them a screaming baby for one battle. Usually this (in concert with canceling/adding battle destiny) will get you the win, and the extra card stacked on I Feel The Conflict. With the number of 5's in the deck, the magic number on IFTC is 4, 3 if you can track one of the two 7's.

This is a fun deck, but one that has a chance in a lot of decks. I'd like to add the classic shields cards. Any suggestions would be greatly appreciated!


 The Last 5 Comments
   Shewski     I've been smacked around enough by my friend's Ewok deck to know that they have some potency. Very creative to be sure. Some of your character choices I question. Why 2 Chewies? I'd rather use Corran Horn instead of R3P0, although using with Were You Looking is pretty cool. Space is tiny, I might rather avoid it altogether, but it has its usefulness with the Claws. The vine snake is pretty cool. Things that are also helpful to use are Slight Weapons Malfunction to add destiny to make winning that much easier, and possibly bargaining table to cancel some big drains. In playing my friend, I know that direct damage is a bear, so I might consider HotJ. If going that route, it may make sense to start YISYW combo to ditch for HotJ or Superficial. This is more like a 3.5, but creativity bumps it up a notch.
   100000101     A very creative and innovative deck design! It is always refreshing to see a deck that is both out of the box and competitive at the same time. The concept of using Ewoks is sure to throw your opponent off his guard (and that's always a good thing). Be careful, however, of decks that may not want to come to you. If you play against heavy damage decks like Imperial Occupation or This Deal Is Getting Worse All The Time, you may have trouble since they will want to out-damage you instead of battling. Also be careful of decks that use enclosed vehicles. Your Ewok Spear/Vine Snake trick will not work there. All in all, though, a good deck. Once again, my only suggestions are to include a way to deal with direct damage, and a way to deal with enclosed vehicles.
