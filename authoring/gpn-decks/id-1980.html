REVIEW THE DECK. PLEASE!

This deck was inspired by Brian Hunters "Slave" deck. I just made some changes to make it my own. Much props to "HuntaWarya".

My choice is to always start YCHF / MOB and IAO / Secret Plans. The third effect changes but most of the time is Battle Order / First Strike. I like this effect with this objective because while it will take your opponent a couple of extra turns so they can force drain you for free, you will be causing 2 force loss per turn. That's a big head start and they may lose a card that they really need.

For you first action use YCHF / MOB to pull the mighty Executor. I love it when this card hits the table for free. Your opponent knows it's coming but the look on their face is rewarding.

Deploy Watto. If there isn't one in your hand deploy him from your deck. Deploy another character to Mos Espa. Use Watto to pull his cube. Always place the cube in your hand and mix your cards. Then place the cube on the table and make your opponent lose 2 force using your objective.

Use IAO / Secret Plans to deploy the Death Star II: Docking Bay. Move your character at Mos Espa to the Junkyard. Stop moving your characters to the junkyard from Mos Espa when you can draw a battle destiny at both sites, then reinforce both.

Continue to reinforce the Junkyard and Mos Espa and spread out if you can. Continue to inflict serious pain on your opponent.

WATTO: Stick him in a vehicle to protect him from weapon strikes. Whenever you use his game text to search for his cube look to see if any important cards you want are in your reserve deck. If the card you want is not in your reserve deck or your hand it's in your force pile. This knowledge can help you to decide how much to deploy so you can have more cards left to draw into your hand, thus increasing you chances of getting the cards you want.

ARICA: Great to block big force drains and can kick ass too.

CHIEF RETWIN: Why don't I ever see this card in any decks? In a deck like this where all you want is to occupy a site. This dude is awesome. Deploy him to a site alone, maybe... ah... Mos Espa. He force drains turn after turn until your opponent deploys there to stop the blood loss. When your opponent does deploy there and pays 1 force to start a beatdown, you just hide Retwin from battle and you still occupy the site. If your opponent did not deploy in force he has a possible beatdown coming next turn. If he did deploy in force his characters there are basically out of the game because he can't battle you and he can't afford to move from there and allow you to force drain again.

DR. EVAZON & PANDA BABA: Put these guys with someone with a lightsaber and your opponent cringes. Put them with someone that swings and lightsaber twice and you may soon detect a foul odor coming from your opponent's side of the table.

GRAGRA, SSA-306, SSA-1015, AND BAD FEELING HAVE I: All make your opponents characters more expensive to deploy. Put 2 or more together and they could very possibly be too expensive to play.

JANUS GREEJATUS & WIPE THEM OUT, ALL OF THEM: Big help in getting back to card your opponent lost 2 force to keep you from deploying or getting to that high destiny you were tracking.

BLIZZARD 4 & TEMPEST SCOUT 4: Put Watto in one of these to protect him from weapons plus they are both destiny 4's. They also protect characters from sand storms. (I had a Jawa deck kill my Watto deck using these.) Can you imagine deploying Blizzard 4 for free using your objective then deploying Maul or Vader for free using Blizzard 4's text on the same turn? Can you say OUCH!

NO ESCAPE: First effect to pull with WMAOP. Hold on to it as long as you can so you can retrieve a good card if needed.

GURI: If it's (not she, Guri is a droid after all) with Watto it may seem as if he and his vehicle will never leave.

MAULS SITH INFILTRATOR: With Maul piloting and cloaking, you can occupy Tatooine for Tatooine Occupation, even if your opponent has his whole Starfleet there.

BROKEN CONCENTRATION: Did your opponent carefully leave 2 force so they would not have to loose 2 to your objective or leave 1 force to start a battle? Use this card to totally screw up their plans.

NEIMOIDIAN ADVISER: Go get Jabba's house then use that to deploy the Audience Chamber then use that to deploy Jabba then use that to... uhh... sorry, got carried away. Don't forget to track the 5.

Hope you enjoy this deck. If there are any questions please D-Mail me before you review.
