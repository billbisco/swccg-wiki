 The Last 5 Comments
   LordHoban     I have to ask, where are your locations? Only 5 locations in a deck like this when you have 19 ships? 15 characters? How do you plan to get those out? You can't really depend on the opponent these days, especially if they're playing the new Hoth Objective, they get a ton of locations out and give you very little to work with. That means, you need your own means of getting locations out. I'm going to comment on each individual section to give you a better idea of my thoughts. SITES Executor: Control Station Hoth: Wampa Cave Jabba’s Palace: Droid Workshop Mon Calamari --Why the Control Station? Why not a docking bay? Use your Prepared Defenses to have Imperial Arrest Order, and pull docking bays with added force generation potential with the Mobilization Points (D*2). And on that note, use regular Mobilization Points, not the combo. You get more force generation potential to be able to deploy your characters and fight off an opponent (don't count on avoiding an enemy, because they will probably either come to you, or be force draining enough not to worry, or cancel your force drains with something like Come Here you big Cower shield or Battle Order). Also, start with Krayt Bones, and Fear Is My Ally. That way, you can store a Wampa (or 2) and Bubo under Fear, and pull them if you need to cause a little havok to a Hoth player (and possibly slow them down) or someone that wants to play with you on JP. Most spies are easily eaten up by a Krayt powered Bubo. You can also pack a One-Arm (v) from SE if you have it under there, and use him to pull your Wampa Cave if against Hoth. Characters Admiral Motti Admiral Piett Aks Moe Baron Soontir Fel Commander Gherant Darth Vader, Dark Lord of the Sith Grand Moff Tarkin General Veers Tikkes Aliens Boba Fett Bossk Salacious Crumb Thok and Thug Zuckess --Aweful lot of character support since you can't get them out regurlarly. I'd remove some of the least useful characters in exchange for useful defensive interrupts you can recycle back in your deck. The Ved Captain Piett might be able to help out, but you need to get through your deck somehow, and lack of enough icons on your side will seriously be detrimental to you in the end. At least put in some more 2/0 locations. DROIDS 4-LOM CZ-4 EV-9D9 LIN-V8M x2 MSE-6 ‘Mouse’ Droid Security Battle Droid x2 --A mouse droid? Why? 0 destiny, no use. I want a useful mouse droid, but at the moment, it isn't happening, you're better off replacing it with a more useful droid. Why 2 Mine Layer droids when you only have 1 timer mine? If you're gonna use mines, use it all the way. Go crazy with it, send mines after an opponent then clearn up with your characters. Your destiny average is low, so you might want to consider upping it, if you're going with mines, go for it, but you need decent destiny to take out an opponent. I'd replace 4-Lom with his gun weilding counterpart if you have him (Enhanced JP or Cloud City, one of those). Very useful. Vehicles Blizzard 2 Tempest 1 Tempest Scout 3 Tempest Scout x2 --Good choice of vehicles, but again, you need to be able to have the force to use them. I'd recommend my favorate Tempest Scout, TS 6. Costs 3, deploys -2 as a react, moves as a react, and has 2 ability. That, and the classic Blizz Scout 4, a 4 destiny, great little beast, even better with the PC Ved version. Weapons AT-AT Cannon Bossk’s Mortar Gun Dark Jedi Lightsaber Darth Vader’s Lightsaber Timer Mine Zuckuss’s Snare Rifle --I'd try to use an original Vader's Lightsaber, much better and it adds to force drains. Again, if you're gonna go mine layer route, go overboard with it, or don't bother. It can be really fun, or not worth it at all. The Snare Rifle I'd drop, not likely you'll hit anything with its -1 and this deck's poor destiny average. STARSHIPS Avenger Chimaera Devastator Dreadnaught-Class Heavy Cruiser Executor Judicator Saber 1 Thunderflare TIE Defender Mark 1 x2 TIE Interceptor x2 Trade Federation Battleship Trade Federation Droid Control Ship Tyrant Victory-Class Star Destroyer x3 Visage --Lot of good ships, but a bit of overkill if you ask me. Stick to 6-7 ships, maximum, if you want strong defense. Just make sure you have the pilots to back 'em up and ways of dealing with certain 'pesky' LS ships and mains that'll eat your fleet alive, mainly because you don't have the force to get most of this out. You need some good interrupts, common, uncommon, whatever. Just start looking. INTERRUPTS Prepared Defences Tallon Roll --As stated above, you need more red in this deck. 2 cards is ridiculous, I've never seen a deck operate on so little red, effects or interrupts. At least ten cards in your deck should probably be devoted to red, and most decks have way more than that. Effects Disarmed You Cannot Hide Forever and Mobilization Points --I'd use regular Mob Points, it is far more effective, it pulls the Executor. Try adding Bespin system and that effect that deploys the Executor for free there, They must never leave here again, from Reflections 3. Overall, a good effort, but it just won't cut it, especially in modern day play. Long ago, before Death Star 2, even before Endor, you might have had half a chance, but decks are getter faster and faster, everything is getting pullable (which is getting out of hand). I'd recommend adding Imperial Arrest Order, put some docking bays in there, and make your system one of the ones listed on Mob Points, so you can pull it if you need to. You should find an objective that suits your needs best as well, as they tend to give extreme advantages. Tell your friend to keep at it, don't give up, because it's a long road to honing your deck building skills. If you want a fun deck to play that isn't that expensive to put together, check out Jabba's Hit Squad (I know, personal plug). It's inexpensive, has combo deployment that makes it a strong offensive deck, and can be modified to fit your droid draining as well without too much trouble and give you some needed edge. Most of all, have fun with the deck you build, no matter what. Don't conform to a deck style just because it wins, use one that you'll have a blast playing. That's my aims these days, just to have fun, because that's all that really matters.
