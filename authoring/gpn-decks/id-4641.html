 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
Bounty hunter beats & choke deck. It'll take too long trying to explain what each and every card does to contribute to this deck, so I'll just keep it down to the key cards:

Ket Maliss, Tattoo - together, bounty hunters are now forfeit +4 and defense value +2. Other Black Sun agents are forfeit +2 and defense value +1. Oh yeah, and you can pull docking bays and Greedo. And Tattoo causes Force loss that can't be reduced if you hit characters with weapons.

IG-88 (V) - drop him at Empy's site with some forfeit fodder and his Inhibitor. Once he nabs a captive, he's absolutely invincible: he's defense value 9 (5 armor +2 from Tattoo and Ket Maliss, +2 from his game text), immune to attrition < 7 (when with Empy, anyways), has a built-in Tunnel Vision, and can help your other BHers at same site take some extra captives with his Inhibitor. Ouch.

IT-O (V) - drains are +2 where you have a captive. Ouch.

PB's Hold-out - give to Jodo Kast, and it'll fire for free, be a +3 > defense value to hit, and cause forfeit = 0.

Fighter's Coming In - Presence on the system so that you can play this card, then drain +1 at your docking bays on Coruscant. Also some extra immunity for your ships.

Evader & Monnok - TRM Revo Choke protection. TRM Revo Choke absolutely kills Black Sun decks.

No Escape - stop using HotJedi, dangit!


Basically, get Xizor to the <>DB with some backup pretty quickly for an early flip, then just beat the crap out of people with your bounty hunters. Drain and direct damage, check your destinies with your objective, etc. You have to play it to understand it, methinks... (and I'm trying to type up 6 decklists at once), so I guess that's all.
