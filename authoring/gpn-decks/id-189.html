 The Strategy
 Back to Top 
It has spies to cancel Visage Of The Emperor, and interrupts like Swing and a Miss and Courage Of A Skywalker to respond to those decks. All of my characters are rebels. Thats why I use 3 Obi's and 2 Jedi Presence. I have 5 ships for the space battles. I have battleground locations for space and ground that I can drain for 2. Its an all around good deck.

I use Dagobah and Mind What You Have Learned to get Yoda to his hut for damage control and MWYHL for Sense & Alter control. I never flip the objective. Then I wait to see what my opponent does. Then I attack with force. One of the key cards in this deck is Tunnel Vision. I have two in the deck. If I get one of these cards early on I get a big Force pile going, and search it for the cards I need then. I have "The Planet That Is Furthest From" for operative decks. I have 3 Nabrun Leids because I need the ability to move around at will. This deck has been my favorite. Try it out and enjoy.
