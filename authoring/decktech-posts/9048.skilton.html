    Author: Ronald "LojeNella" Rullens

    --------------------------------

    Cards:

    ‘Starting(8) Set You Course For Alderaan/The Ulimate Power In The Universe Death Star Death Star Docking Bay 327 Alderaan Prepared Defenses (SI) Mobilization Points (SE) Oppressive Enforcement (SE) You Cannot Hide Forever (SE)

    And you now the rest..

    Enjoy
