Blaster Rack (V) is a Dark Effect from Virtual Set 1: Legacy.

Card Text

Deploy on table. During your deploy phase, if you just deployed a unique (•) character, may deploy a matching weapon on that character from Reserve Deck; reshuffle. (Immune to Alter.)

Lore

Imperial facilities, like the Death Star and garrison bases, have blaster racks at key locations to equip soldiers with weapons like blaster rifles and thermal detonators.
