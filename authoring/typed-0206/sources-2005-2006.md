# G2: 2005–2006 decklist gap hunt

All 51 saved decks are typed lists. 49 come from DeckTech (decktech.net/starwarsccg) deck pages and 2 from essentialswccg.com, all fetched from Wayback. In 2005–06 players and Michael Carr (mc9457, PC) published tournament 60s on DeckTech and linked them from the PC forum. The PC never published decklist PDFs for these years: the starwarsccg.org "Legacy decklists" pages start at 2007, and res.starwarsccg.org/Resources/tournaments/Decklists/2005/ and /2006/ have no files.

Raw files are under `raw/` (decktech/, essentialswccg/, forum/ = PC forum topics plus DeckTech tournament reports used for context). `dt_parsed.json` indexes every archived DeckTech deck page with id 32500–36150 (286 pages). `dt_list.txt` gives their Wayback captures. No 2003–2006 deck titles exist in decklist_titles.json, so none of these duplicate the wiki.

## Sources that could not be reached or had nothing

- forum.starwarsccg.org topics from before 2006 are private or deleted. Search only reaches the [History] 2006 forums: f=88 "[FY06] 2006 Championship Tournament Season" and f=203 "[FY06] Worlds Weekend 2006".
- swccgpc.com on Wayback, 2005–06: a phpNuke homepage plus 786 random forum topics. None hold a 60-card list. Its News/Archives section stops in 2004.
- res.starwarsccg.org: an S3 bucket with no listing. Guessed 2005/2006 Decklists paths return AccessDenied, which means the files do not exist.
- jasonmaas.net/swccg: the CDX index was unavailable when tried (Internet Archive rate-limiting). It could be retried.
- DeckTech is only partly archived (about 309 of about 3,600 deck ids in this range). Most 2005–06 tournament decks posted there are lost unless someone kept copies.
