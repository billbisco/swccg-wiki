# G1: 2002 (gap), 2003 and 2004 tournament decklists

All paths are under `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/89b0b907-d4b4-53cc-bc54-8fba9b724b03/scratchpad/gap/G1/`.
`found.json` has 48 entries (43 firm, 5 flagged "Caller to decide"). `extras_out_of_scope.json` lists 27 tournament
decks from 2005–2006 (plus one 2001 deck) that turned up in the same DeckTech crawl.

## Sources and how they were reached

| Source | Result |
|---|---|
| theforce.net `/ccg/sw/decks/*.pdf` | **Still live.** All 16 official 2002 Worlds top-8 decklist PDFs downloaded directly. |
| swccgpc.com (Wayback, 2002–2006, 14,947 CDX rows) | News archives, event pages, homepage (`interior.htm`) captures for 2003–2005 were read. Only deck links: 2002 Worlds (theforce PDFs), 2002 JK/YJ champions (other games, skipped), DPC Chicago 2003 top-4 PDFs. **No 2003 or 2004 Worlds decklists were ever posted on swccgpc.com.** |
| swccgpc.com `/Decks/dpcchicago/*.pdf` | Only `desaiDS.pdf` was archived. The other 7 (desaiLS, fredLS/DS, hunterLS/DS, clintLS/DS) are not in Wayback, and the live redirect to starwarsccg.org returns 404. |
| swccgpc.com phpBB forums 2003–2005 | Forum indexes and topic lists were read (about 330 topic titles). None of them is a decklist thread. Besides the forum list there was a "DPC Chicago Coverage" forum with 6 live-update threads, which I did not fetch. Fetching all 3,800 captured post URLs was too slow under Wayback throttling. |
| theforce.net CCG news history 2002–2005 (186 stories) | Results only (for example, 2003 Worlds archetypes for the top 8). No decklists beyond the 2002 PDFs. |

| forum.starwarsccg.org (FlareSolverr) | The forum starts around 2009. "Decks that Have Won Worlds" (t=83355) names archetypes only, with no lists. phpBB search flood control blocked further searches. |
| angelfire swccgpc/dconresults, DeckTech treports 2003–04, the featured pages | Results and articles only, or not fetched (see gaps). |

## Per event

**2002 World Championship (DecipherCon, 2 Nov 2002)**: 13 new. These are official PC decklist forms for the top 8, both sides:
Rippetoe LS/DS, Terwilliger LS/DS, Winkelhaus LS/DS, Fred LS/DS, Consoli LS, Chu LS/DS, Hayes Hunter LS/DS.
I skipped 3 because the wiki already has those player and side pages: Consoli DS, Shaw LS and Shaw DS. Those PDFs are saved anyway
(`raw/tfn/angeloDS.pdf`, `shawLS.pdf`, `shawDS.pdf`) and can be used to verify the existing DeckTech-sourced pages.
DeckTech duplicates: Fred LS (26675) and Rippetoe LS (28648, "card for card") match the PDFs and are noted on those entries.
Lingrell also used his NYC BHBM here (see below).

**2002 NYC Mini-Open**: 1. Lingrell DS BHBM (DeckTech 31369, headed "Dark Side Decklist for NYC Mini-Open").

**2002 Origins Open**: 1 flagged. Desai KToD Senate (28824) carries a multi-event record (Mass/Jersey Regionals '02,
NYC, Origins 8-0, FreedomCon II, Worlds, Chicago DPC). It is probably the 2003 DPC Chicago version.

**2003 DPC Atlanta (Feb 2003)**: 1. Classic-format side event, 1st place, Matt Lush LS Hidden Base (posted by Steve Marshall).

**2003 MegaCon (Orlando, 1 Mar 2003)**: 1. Lee Edwards DS SYCFA, 3rd place.

**2003 DPC Chicago (12 Apr 2003)**: 1. Desai DS, official PDF. 7 of 8 top-4 PDFs are lost (see above).

**2003 State Championships**: 9 in total. Maryland 3 (Elia LS 1st, also won Mystic Visions 2003; Lingrell LS and DS 2nd),
Georgia 1 (Rippetoe DS, 3-0), Minnesota 3 (Kienholz LS 3-0; Engelking DS 2nd "original version"; Engelking LS 2nd),
Chandrila/Italy 2 (Destefanis LS and DS, 1st), Florida 1 (Razi LS, 1st).

**2003 Origins**: 1. Matt Lush LS Senate, 4-0. The event and placement are not stated.

**2003 World Championship (Dragon*Con, 29 Aug–1 Sep)**: 3, all Day 2 or Day 3 decks. These are Chu's Day 2 DS (Deal, 4-0) and Day 2 LS (speeders);
note that his Day 3 title decks (Combat / Senate) were **not** found. The third is Gemme's DS KTOD Huntdown, used on Days 2 and 3 (8th place).
Engelking's LS from MN States was also used on Day 2 (2-2).
No official top-8 lists exist. The PC never posted them, and theforce.net only gives the archetypes.

**2004 DPC Atlanta (Masters Series)**: 1 firm and 1 flagged. Damian Loback LS Senate, 4th place (posted by John Anderson). Flagged: Kevin Elia's
"nonProfit", 3rd at DPC Atlanta and 2nd at Mystic Visions, described as "plus or minus 3 cards".

**2004 State Championships**: Washington 1 (Watabayashi LS, 1st). Illinois 1 flagged (Brownell LS MBO, an updated list, 5-1 at Normal DPC and IL States).

**2004 Origins**: 1. Justin Stratton DS Watto, the same deck Nate Meeker played. Placement not stated.

**2004 Mystic Visions**: 2. Lingrell LS winner and DS winner. Check the year on the DS entry, which was posted in Oct 2004.

**2004 European Championship (Bochum)**: 2. Lambrecht LS TRM/EBO; Destefanis DS "Hyper Solid Deal Europe" (finals).

**2004 German Open (Ralltiir)**: 1 flagged. Friedrichs LS, 1st place. He used the deck over two years, and the list was posted in 2005.

**2004 World Championship (Gen Con, Aug 2004)**: 5 firm and 1 flagged. Schele DS and LS (the World Champion; Day 3 lists with the Day 2
changes noted), Nick Jones DS and LS (Day 3), Brad Reinhold DS (Day 2). Flagged: Reid Smith DS "Effect WarZ FINAL" (Day 2 3-1, posted 2005 as a "final" version).
The runner-up Reid Smith's Day 3 decks were not found.

## Not reached and gaps
- 2003 Worlds Day 3 decks of Chu (the winner), Shaw, Hays, Winkelhaus, Gogolen, Desai and Elia, and 2004 Worlds runner-up Reid Smith's Day 3 pair.
- DPC Chicago 2003: 7 PDFs never archived.
- 22 DeckTech deck pages in the 26556–35500 range were archived only as "members only" (ids 27126, 29431, 29693, 29716, 32959,
  33176, 33244, 33694, 33786, 33830, 34147, 34256, 34340, 34399, 34494, 34846, 34952, 35013, 35138, 35143, 35206, 35459). I checked
  the 2005 captures of 5 of them: none is a tournament deck. DeckTech ids that were never captured are unknown.
- I did not fetch the DeckTech tournament reports (`/starwarsccg/treports/`, only about 5 captures for 2003–04).
- swccgpc.com forum post captures (about 3,800) were not crawled exhaustively, and the "DPC Chicago Coverage" threads (t=1290–1303) were not read.
- European sites (German, Belgian, Nordic national-championship pages) were not found. Wayback CDX became throttled from the PC IP;
  the gemp server route (plain curl) worked for the fetches.
- DeckTech text is "as posted". The card counts were not verified to 60; every `found.json` note gives the card-section line count.
