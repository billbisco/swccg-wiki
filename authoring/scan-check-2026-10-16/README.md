# Decklist scan check, 2026-10-16

Sheet transcriptions (one JSON per scan: header, numbered lines, V marks, struck lines, unsure notes) for the 79 scans behind
35 groups of identical decklists (mostly 2008 Worlds Day 2). The 59 pages from 2008 Worlds were rebuilt from these
(`tools/build2.py`, `tools/payload2.py`). 2012–2014 pairs were left alone: their second sheet says "same as yesterday"
or is the same document. Applied by Holocron Claude from Bill's PC.
