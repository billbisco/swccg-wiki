# G3: decklist gaps for 2007 (not yet covered), 2008 and 2009

Everything below comes from PC fax/scan PDFs. Nearly all of them are on res.starwarsccg.org (S3). That bucket returns 403 for a key that doesn't exist, so I found files by **guessing names** in the known pattern. One more PDF came from the swccgpc.com Wayback copy.
- Raw files: `G3/raw/*.pdf`. Per-deck page map: `G3/notes*.tsv` (event, stage, pdf, page, player, side, title). Header contact sheets: `G3/img/`.
- `found.json` holds 1,030 deck entries (one per 60-card list; when a page has both sides, the page is listed twice). None of these events or stages have deck pages on the wiki yet. For 2008 and 2009 the wiki has only Worlds; for 2007 it has MPC, Worlds, Endor, Kashyyyk, MA, NJ, ND, US East Day 2 and Australia.
- Names are as read from handwriting. Entries marked `?` or "first name only" need a ruling before publishing. The side is taken from the checkbox; where the box was blank it is shown as `?`.

## Not found / could not reach
- res.starwarsccg.org: about 600 guessed names were probed across the 2007–2009 Regional (35 planets), States (all US states + 4 provinces), Nationals, Euros, TMM, MPC and Worlds patterns. Nothing else exists beyond the files above. In particular, there is no 2009 Worlds Day 1 PDF, no 2008 Texas MPC Day 1, and no 2008 or 2009 regionals or states besides those listed.
- The 2008 Worlds D1 and the 2007/2008/2009 Worlds Team PDFs exist, but the wiki already covers those stages.
- Wayback (web.archive.org) went offline ("Temporarily Offline" / connection refused) midway, so I could not check the swccgpc.com `news/news.php?id=…` and `forums/tournaments.php?tourn_id=…` captures for typed 2008/2009 lists. swccgpc.com CDX showed only one decklist file for this era (ny08decklists.pdf). That is worth a re-check.
- I did not search the forum.starwarsccg.org decklist threads for 2008–2009, beyond the 2K-event meta thread, which contains no lists.
